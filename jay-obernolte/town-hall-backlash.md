# Rep. Jay Obernolte (CA-23): Booed Off Stage, Then Canceled Future Events

### Booed Off Stage At Town Hall, Then Canceled Future In-Person Events

**Over 200 Constituents Booed Obernolte Off Stage At A Yucca Valley Town Hall After He Defended DOGE And Elon Musk.** According to KVCR News, approximately 200 people attended the Yucca Valley Community Center town hall on February 22, 2025, where the crowd shouted over Obernolte as he attempted to speak. When asked about Elon Musk making massive federal cuts despite not being an elected official, Obernolte responded, "I'm very glad there is someone looking at all of the waste. We are in an existential crisis." Audience members repeatedly chanted "Do your job!" and "No kings!" [KVCR News, [2/24/25](https://www.kvcrnews.org/local-news/2025-02-24/yucca-valley-town-hall-turns-raucous-as-rep-jay-obernolte-defends-doge)]
- **Constituents Called Obernolte A Liar And Demanded He Address Federal Worker Firings And Joshua Tree National Park Cuts.** According to the Desert Trumpet, a constituent told the crowd, "This is a fucking food desert, and that resource is a vital resource to this community." Another asked, "What are you going to do to stop the cuts and how do you feel about an unelected nonconfirmed person making the decisions you should be making?" [Desert Trumpet, [2/25/25](https://www.deserttrumpet.org/p/town-holler-rep-obernolte-meets-resistance)]

**Obernolte Canceled Subsequent In-Person Events, Citing "Inclement Weather," As GOP Leaders Urged Lawmakers To Stop Holding Town Halls.** According to the San Bernardino Sun, Obernolte canceled his early March mobile office hours in Lake Arrowhead, with constituents questioning the excuse. NBC News reported that House Republican leaders were "urging lawmakers to stop engaging in [town halls] altogether" and suggested that "if lawmakers feel the need to hold such events, they do tele-town halls or at least vet attendees." [San Bernardino Sun, [2/28/25](https://www.sbsun.com/2025/02/28/southern-california-gop-lawmaker-booed-at-town-hall-for-supporting-elon-musk/)]
