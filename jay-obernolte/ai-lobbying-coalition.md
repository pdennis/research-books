# Rep. Jay Obernolte (CA-23): Paid AI Lobbying Coalition

### Launched Paid AI Lobbying Coalition While Leading Congressional AI Policy

**Obernolte Launched A Paid Lobbying Coalition Charging AI Startups Up To $3,500 Per Month For Access To Washington Policymakers.** According to Axios, HumanX and Humanrace Capital tapped Obernolte to launch the AI Coalition, a nonprofit registered as both a 501(c)(3) and 501(c)(6) for education and lobbying. Membership costs range from $1,750 to $3,500 per month. The group bills itself as "the first nonprofit specifically designed to provide early-stage AI companies, from pre-seed through Series B, with meaningful access to policymakers." [Axios, [10/10/25](https://www.axios.com/2025/10/10/ai-startups-dc-advocate-obernolte)]
- **Obernolte Owns A Tech Company With Sony, Microsoft, Google, And Apple As Clients While Shaping Tech And AI Policy In Congress.** According to Bloomberg, Obernolte is the owner, president, and technical director of FarSight Studios, a video game developer that counts Sony, Microsoft, Google, and Apple among its clients. He simultaneously chairs the House Science Committee's Research and Technology Subcommittee and leads congressional AI policy efforts. [Bloomberg, [8/9/24](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/newsletters/2024-08-09/how-jay-obernolte-a-video-game-developer-from-california-went-to-congress)]
