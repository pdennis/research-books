**Message:** Marshall fully embraced the disastrous Iran war, causing Kansas families, farmers, and businesses to suffer higher prices.

### Marshall Opposed The CHIPS Act

Roger Marshall is no fan of investing in Kansas’s economy. He [**voted against**](https://www.congress.gov/bill/117th-congress/house-bill/4346/actions) the CHIPS and Science Act in 2022, even when his own Kansas colleague, Senator Moran, voted in favor of the bill. When the CHIPS Act became law, Kansas stood to benefit. One proposed semiconductor plant in Wichita promised nearly [**2,000 new jobs**](https://kansasreflector.com/2023/02/02/kansas-reaches-304m-incentives-deal-with-integra-for-1-8-billion-semiconductor-project/), contingent on [**CHIPS Act funding**](https://kansasreflector.com/2023/02/02/kansas-reaches-304m-incentives-deal-with-integra-for-1-8-billion-semiconductor-project/). The CHIPS Act also [**delivered**](https://davids.house.gov/media/press-releases/davids-announces-two-regional-universities-receive-prestigious-technological) almost a million dollars in the form of a National Science Foundation workforce grant to Kansas State University. Yet, Marshall decided to vote against the interest of Kansas workers.

**Message:** Marshall voted against new jobs and workforce development for Kansans.
