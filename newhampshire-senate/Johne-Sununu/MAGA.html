Sununu Sought Trump's Endorsement, Got It, And Touted It

Sununu Sought Trump's Endorsement

When Asked About Trying To Earn Trump's Support, John E. Sununu Said, “I’d Certainly Love To Have Support From Across The Spectrum, That Includes The President.” According to Fox News, “Asked about trying to earn Trump's support, Sununu said, ‘I'd certainly love to have support from across the spectrum, that includes the president.’ But he added that ‘at the end of the day, this is about building support in New Hampshire, providing the right leadership for New Hampshire and the right message for New Hampshire.’” [Fox News, 9/19/25]

[VIDEO] John E. Sununu Said He Would “Certainly Like” Trump’s Endorsement. “Paul Steinhauser: Getting back to the president, I'm just wondering, will you be looking for his support down the road? And did you vote for him in the last presidential election, if you don't mind me asking? John E. Sununu: Yes. And I would certainly like to have his endorsement, and it would be, I think, helpful in the primary. But the more support and endorsement you can have, the stronger your overall campaign is going to be. Pleased to have the support of the Senate President, Sharon Carson. Three of our Republican executive councilors, former Governor Craig Benson, Steve Stepanek who, who ran Donald Trump's campaign here in 2024.”  [Fox News Digital, 10/24/25]

[VIDEO] John E. Sununu Said That He Would “Welcome” Trump’s Endorsement. “Matt Prichard: Are you seeking his endorsement? Would you accept it? John E. Sununu: Oh, I’d welcome his endorsement.” [NBC Boston, 10/22/25]

[VIDEO] John E. Sununu Said He Would “Welcome” Trump’s Endorsement. “Rob Finnerty: So you will seek the Trump endorsement. Is that accurate? John E. Sununu: Oh, absolutely. I’d welcome his endorsement.” [Newsmax TV: Finnerty, 10/27/25]

Trump Endorsed Sununu For Senate

Donald Trump Endorsed John E. Sununu For Senate. According to Fox News, “President Donald Trump on Sunday endorsed former Sen. John E. Sununu of New Hampshire, a longtime GOP Trump critic, over one of his first-term ambassadors, former Sen. Scott Brown, in a crucial Senate race that's one of a handful that may determine the Senate majority in the midterm elections. Sununu, who was praised by Trump as an ‘America First Patriot’ who ‘will work tirelessly to advance our America First Agenda,’ is seen by top Senate Republicans as the strongest candidate to flip the seat held by longtime Democratic Sen. Jeanne Shaheen, who is retiring at the end of this year rather than seek re-election to a fourth six-year term. ‘John E. Sununu has my Complete and Total Endorsement — HE WILL NOT LET YOU DOWN. MAKE AMERICA GREAT AGAIN — ELECT JOHN E. SUNUNU,’ Trump emphasized, in a post on social media.” [Fox News, 2/1/26]

Sununu Touted Trump's Endorsement

John E. Sununu Said “I Want To Thank The President For His Support And Thank The Thousands Of Granite Staters Who Are Supporting Me.” According to Fox News, “‘I want to thank the President for his support and thank the thousands of Granite Staters who are supporting me,’ Sununu said after landing Trump's endorsement. The endorsement will further boost Sununu, who enjoys a polling and fundraising advantage over Brown. After Trump's endorsement, the Senate Leadership Fund, which is aligned with Senate Majority Leader John Thune, said on X that ‘John Sununu is the clear choice to be New Hampshire's next US Senator, and President Trump's critical endorsement has put an end to the primary.’” [Fox News, 2/1/26]
