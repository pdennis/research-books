Texas Had The Second Largest United States Military Veteran Population

2023: Texas Had The Second Largest United States Military Veteran Population Of About 1.5 Million People. According to Hogg Foundation for Mental Health, As of 2023, approximately 16.2 million military veterans reside in the United States. Texas alone has the second largest military veteran population at around 1.5 million people. This sizable community in Texas faces unique mental health challenges as they reintegrate into civilian life, including returning to jobs, communities, homes, and families. Annually, about 200,000 service members leave active-duty and return to civilian life. While transitions are difficult for most people, they are particularly burdensome for veterans and their families as they navigate the complexities in employment, housing, social support, and both physical and mental health. Studies indicate that 44 to 72 percent of veterans experience high levels of stress during this period. Inadequate support during this transition can have severe consequences.” [Hogg Foundation for Mental Health, Viewed 3/16/26]
