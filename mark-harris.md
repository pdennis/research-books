# How To Win Against Mark Harris (NC-08)

Mark Harris has spent his time in Congress fighting to make North Carolinians’ lives harder. He voted to kick more than 45,000 of his constituents off their health coverage by supporting the Big Ugly Bill, even though even though a North Carolina hospital executive said the cuts would create a “desperate situation for the people that work here, our patients and the entire community.” Harris’ vote for the budget bill also threatened North Carolina’s Medicaid expansions, which allowed 650,000 North Carolinians to have access to health insurance. According to Harris, though, states’ Medicaid expansion was the reason Medicaid was moving towards insolvency and Republicans needed to roll back the expansion. Harris praised Trump’s tariffs, telling suffering North Carolinians that they needed to endure the “short-term pain” in order to reap the tariff’s rewards. So far, those rewards were “mega-layoffs” across the country, the downfall of North Carolina’s agricultural sector, and a $1,000 tax that American families suffered in 2025. Harris repeatedly voted to prolong Trump’s war with Iran that was causing gas and grocery prices to skyrocket for Americans, and he defended Trump’s dangerous Truth Social post threatening to use nuclear weapons against Iran.

### Harris Voted To Take Health Coverage Away From North Carolinians

- Harris [voted](https://clerk.house.gov/Votes/2025190) for a bill that would kick [17 million Americans](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2025/07/01/least-17-million-americans-would-lose-insurance-under-trump-plan/) off their health insurance, including nearly [12 million Americans](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2025/07/01/least-17-million-americans-would-lose-insurance-under-trump-plan/) off Medicaid. More than [45,000 North Carolinians](https://www.jec.senate.gov/public/_cache/files/9cfdee90-dd29-41e8-97da-b5dcdc0317cf/updated-jec-fact-sheet-on-district-level-impacts-of-health-care-cuts.pdf) could lose their health coverage because of his votes, and North Carolina’s Medicaid expansion was [at risk of ending](https://www.washingtonpost.com/health/2025/08/17/north-carolina-medicaid-cuts/) due to a lack of funding. Despite the impact it would have on his constituents, Harris called for Republicans to seek the “[maximum](https://youtu.be/oUt5byoNMsg)” funding cuts and [urged](https://x.com/RepMarkHarrisNC/status/1923053945699565937) Republicans to make “transformative changes” to the program. 
- Harris’ vote also put North Carolina’s Medicaid expansion [at risk of ending](https://www.washingtonpost.com/health/2025/08/17/north-carolina-medicaid-cuts/) due to a lack of funding. A hospital executive in North Carolina said the Medicaid cuts Harris helped introduce would [create](https://www.nytimes.com/2025/07/06/us/north-carolina-medicaid-cuts.html) a “desperate situation” for North Carolinians, and North Carolina doctors [warned](https://www.washingtonpost.com/health/2025/08/17/north-carolina-medicaid-cuts/) that the Republican Budget Bill’s cuts threatened the healthcare of the [650,000 North Carolinians](https://governor.nc.gov/news/press-releases/2025/04/09/nc-medicaid-expansion-reaches-650000-north-carolinians-enrolled-fewer-18-months-after-launch#:~:text=Governor%20Josh%20Stein%20announced%20that,industries%20essential%20to%20the%20state.) who received healthcare through the state’s Medicaid expansion.

**Message:** Harris voted to take health coverage from North Carolinians to give billionaires tax breaks.

### Harris Voted For Massive Cuts To Medicare

- Harris, who [claimed](https://youtu.be/hZQ2lx1Qi6I) Medicaid expansion in North Carolina and across the nation was pushing the program towards insolvency, voted for Republicans’ reconciliation bill, which the Congressional Budget Office estimated would trigger more than [$500 billion](https://www.cbo.gov/system/files/2025-05/61423-PAYGO.pdf) in cuts to Medicare absent future congressional action. 

**Message:** Harris worked to make health care more expensive and less accessible for North Carolina seniors. 

### Harris Was The Deciding Vote To Protect Trump's Tariffs 

- Harris praised Trump’s tariffs, [claiming](https://on.soundcloud.com/0Elixa2y2QMM17paw7) they were getting the U.S. back to a “level playing field” and were [helping](https://on.soundcloud.com/CE2WyuASy6KjEQ87zO) North Carolinians. Harris [even acknowledged](https://on.soundcloud.com/4tFBkhML2g3ySFRKi6) the tariffs were causing some “short-term pain” for North Carolina small businesses, but that their sacrifices were necessary for the “long-term game” Trump was playing.  Then, he voted four times to protect the tariffs, including in April 2025 when he was the [deciding vote](https://clerk.house.gov/Votes/202594) to protect them, dooming North Carolina families who relied on him to a trade war and [crippling the North Carolina agriculture industry](https://www.carolinajournal.com/report-warns-tariffs-could-cost-nc-farms-700m-8000-jobs/). Tariffs are contributing to [higher food prices](https://www.axios.com/local/portland/2025/09/16/trump-tariffs-grocery-prices-rise-cpi), “[mega-layoffs](https://www.commondreams.org/news/mass-layoffs-trump-economy)” across the country, and amounted to a [$1,000 tax increase](https://taxfoundation.org/research/all/federal/trump-tariffs-trade-war/) on Americans. 

**Message:** Harris told North Carolinians paying higher prices due to Trump’s tariffs to endure the “short-term pain.” 

### Harris Backed Trump's Costly War With Iran That Raised Prices For North Carolinians

- Harris defended Trump’s war with Iran, [praising](https://x.com/RepMarkHarrisNC/status/2027778482541879378) Trump’s initial strikes on Iran in February and claiming Trump only “[wants to see peace.](https://www.newsmax.com/newsmax-tv/mark-harris-iran-donald-trump/2026/03/04/id/1248362/)” When asked what he would tell North Carolinians who were worried about Trump’s handling of the conflict, Harris [dodged the question](https://youtu.be/8zewUL8qhv4) and [mindlessly praised](https://youtu.be/eCv_Mc2xUCY) Trump’s strength in taking on Iran. While Harris ardently alleged Iran posed a threat to the U.S., the war caused a meteoric rise in gas prices that [forced his constituents](https://abc11.com/post/nc-gas-prices-climb-durham-drivers-say-feel-pinch/19545521/) to pinch pennies to get by and hamstrung North Carolina's [agricultural industry](https://abc11.com/post/war-drought-north-carolina-farmers-caught-middle/18782245/). All the while, Harris voted in lockstep with his Republican colleagues to ensure Trump's war with Iran continued unchecked.

**Message:** Harris repeatedly defended a war that was making life unaffordable for North Carolinians.
