HOW TO WIN AGAINST MICHELE TAFOYA

Michele Tafoya, a MAGA acolyte with zero legislative experience, wants to bring her extremist ideas to the US Senate. Tafoya has consistently advocated for policies that hurt Minnesotans. She cheered on Trump's Iran war, even as it caused gas prices and fertilizer costs to skyrocket, harming Minnesota farmers and families. She embraced Trump's tariffs, brushing off the pain that Minnesotans feel in the rising prices of consumer goods. Tafoya also supported the One Big Beautiful Bill, which gutted food assistance and threatened to eliminate healthcare coverage for 140,000 Minnesotans. And at a time when the vast majority of voters are against the build-up of data centers, Tafoya doubled down and called for more. Clearly, Michele Tafoya does not have Minnesotans' best interests at heart.

Tafoya Supported Trump's Costly War With Iran

While Trump was bombing civilians and racking up US servicemember casualties in his Iran war, Tafoya was cheering him on. She defended his reckless war of choice, claiming it was a necessity. As the conflict was jacking up prices for gas and fertilizer, Tafoya turned a blind eye to Minnesotans' need for relief; she even suggested taking “one less trip to Starbucks,” callously pinning the cost of the war on voters' backs.

Message: Tafoya fully embraced Trump's disastrous Iran war, causing Minnesota families, farmers, and businesses to suffer higher prices.

Tafoya Embraced Deeply Unpopular Data Centers

A recent survey showed that almost two-thirds of Minnesotans opposed the construction of data centers in their communities. That hasn't stopped Tafoya from championing new data centers and dismissing voter concerns. Tafoya claimed that data centers were “going to have” to be built and should just be “accepted.” This blatant disregard for concerned Minnesota citizens comes as study after study shows the community harms of data centers, ranging from health risks to higher energy costs for everyday Minnesotans.

Message: Tafoya supported the development of destructive, costly data centers, even as a vast majority of Minnesotans opposed them.

Tafoya Cheered Cuts To Healthcare And Food Assistance For Minnesotans

Tafoya praised Trump's One Big Beautiful Bill, even as Minnesotans got battered with its consequences. Cuts to SNAP in the Big Beautiful Bill were slated to cost Minnesota over $100 million every year, and an estimated 18,000 Minnesotans had already lost food benefits since the bill's enactment. What's more, the OBBB's trillion dollar Medicaid cut endangered healthcare coverage for 140,000 Minnesotans. Still, Tafoya pledged to uphold the law in the Senate. 

Message: By backing the cruel One Big Beautiful Bill, Tafoya endorsed the erosion of critical food and healthcare safety nets for vulnerable Minnesotans.

Tafoya Complained About Fraud But Took Money From A Massive Fraudster

Tafoya made tackling fraud the centerpiece of her campaign, even claiming it was the reason she decided to run for Senate. Despite the resulting harm to working families, she supported the Trump Administration's move to cut off Medicaid funds to Minnesota—all in the name of stopping alleged fraud. Yet, FEC records show that Tafoya accepted thousands of dollars from Dean Bundtrock, a fraudster who cost investors $6 billion and paid the largest SEC fine in history for accounting fraud. 

Message: Tafoya talks tough on fraud, but behind closed doors she is happy to rake in cash from one of the biggest fraudsters around.
