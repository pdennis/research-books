### Begich Voted For Devastating Health Care Cuts 

-   Begich [enthusiastically](https://alaskabeacon.com/2025/07/04/alaskas-us-rep-begich-no-doubt-before-vote-on-big-congressional-budget-bill/) [voted](http://clerk.house.gov/evs/2025/roll190.xml) for a bill that would kick [17 million Americans](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2025/07/01/least-17-million-americans-would-lose-insurance-under-trump-plan/) off their health insurance, including nearly [12 million Americans](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2025/07/01/least-17-million-americans-would-lose-insurance-under-trump-plan/) off of Medicaid. More than [37,000 Alaskans](https://www.jec.senate.gov/public/_cache/files/9cfdee90-dd29-41e8-97da-b5dcdc0317cf/updated-jec-fact-sheet-on-district-level-impacts-of-health-care-cuts.pdf) could lose their health coverage as a result of his votes. 
-   Begich was previously [president](https://www.linkedin.com/in/nbegich/) of the board of directors at an organization that recommended [reconsidering Medicaid expansion](https://alaskapolicyforum.org/2020/09/consequences-ballooning-medicaid/), which provided more than [60,000](https://health.alaska.gov/media/kk5orhkc/medicaid-enrollment-monthly-snapshot.pdf) Alaskans with healthcare. 
-   Since 2020, Begich has been a fellow at an organization that advocated for [repealing](https://www.clubforgrowth.org/issue/entitlements/) the Affordable Care Act even though more than [28,000 Alaskans](https://www.kff.org/affordable-care-act/state-indicator/open-enrollment-marketplace-plan-selections/?currentTimeframe=0&selectedRows=%7B%22states%22:%7B%22alaska%22:%7B%7D%7D%7D&sortModel=%7B%22colId%22:%22Location%22,%22sort%22:%22asc%22%7D) rely on the law for health insurance. Alaskans have the [2^nd^ highest average](https://www.kff.org/affordable-care-act/state-indicator/average-marketplace-premiums-by-metal-tier/?currentTimeframe=1&selectedDistributions=average-benchmark-premium&sortModel=%7B%22colId%22:%22Average%20Benchmark%20Premium%22,%22sort%22:%22desc%22%7D) monthly premiums across America and by [voting against](http://clerk.house.gov/evs/2026/roll011.xml) renewing enhanced ACA premium tax credits, one Alaskan small business owner said she felt that Begich was “[punishing us](https://www.adn.com/alaska-news/2025/11/16/punishing-alaska-small-business-owners-consider-next-steps-amid-steep-rises-in-health-care-costs/)” with high healthcare costs. 

**Message:** Begich voted to take health coverage from Alaskans to give billionaires tax breaks.

### Begich Supported Massive Cuts To Medicare

-   Begich voted for Republicans’ reconciliation bill, which the Congressional Budget Office estimated would trigger nearly [$500 billion](https://www.cbo.gov/system/files/2025-05/61423-PAYGO.pdf) in cuts to Medicare absent future congressional action. Begich defended cuts saying he had “[no doubt](https://alaskabeacon.com/2025/07/04/alaskas-us-rep-begich-no-doubt-before-vote-on-big-congressional-budget-bill/)” he would vote for the reconciliation bill and called it a “[transformative victory](https://begich.house.gov/media/press-releases/congressman-begich-statement-final-passage-one-big-beautiful-bill-act).” 
-   Additionally, Begich worked at an [organization](https://www.linkedin.com/in/nbegich/) that [advocated for](https://www.clubforgrowth.org/issue/entitlements/) changing Medicare to use a premium support function that would [likely result](https://www.cbpp.org/blog/house-republican-health-plan-would-radically-restructure-medicare#:~:text=The%20plan%20would%20likely%20shift,to%20maintain%20continuous%20health%20coverage.) in beneficiaries paying more for healthcare than they do under the current system. In congress, he was a [member](https://web.archive.org/web/20250124151313/https:/rsc-pfluger.house.gov/about/membership) of the Republican Study Committee, which has advocated for raising the Social Security retirement age and turning [Medicare](https://www.cbpp.org/blog/house-republican-health-plan-would-radically-restructure-medicare) into a voucher system.

**Message:** Begich worked to make health care more expensive and less accessible for Alaska’s seniors. 

### Begich Voted To Cut Food Assistance His Constituents Rely On To Afford Groceries

-   Begich [voted](http://clerk.house.gov/evs/2025/roll190.xml) for a bill that made the [largest SNAP cut in history](https://www.cbpp.org/research/food-assistance/by-the-numbers-senate-republican-leaderships-reconciliation-bill-takes). More than [28,000 households](https://www.fns.usda.gov/data-research/data-visualization/snap-community-characteristics-congressional-district-dashboard) in Alaska relied on SNAP to afford groceries.
-   Begich defended the cuts saying they were “[protecting the integrity](https://www.facebook.com/share/p/1HyqDmykGT/)” of SNAP and “[ensuring benefits go to those who truly need them](https://www.facebook.com/share/p/1HyqDmykGT/).” Local [food assistance](https://blog.foodbankofalaska.org/food-bank-of-alaska-statement-in-opposition-to-h.r.-1) [organizations](https://www.akfoodpolicycouncil.org/defend-snap/) raised alarm bells and said cuts to SNAP would not eliminate need but will “[make that need harder to meet](https://blog.foodbankofalaska.org/snap-works-cutting-it-will-hurt-alaska), and push more families into crisis.”

**Message:** Begich voted to cut food assistance his constituents relied on to afford groceries, all to give tax cuts to billionaires.
