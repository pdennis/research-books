# How To Win Against Chuck Edwards

Chuck Edwards called Medicaid expansion a ["budget boondoggle"](https://www.citizen-times.com/story/opinion/2020/02/16/chuck-edwards-medicaid-expansion-wont-solve-north-carolinas-health-care-crisis/4748432002/) in 2020, reversed himself and called it ["fiscally irresponsible" not to expand](https://www.hendersonvillelightning.com/news/11663-edwards-explains-why-he-changed-his-mind-on-medicaid-expansion.html) in 2022, then cast the [deciding 215-214 vote for the largest Medicaid cuts in U.S. history](https://avlwatchdog.org/edwards-defends-vote-for-one-big-beautiful-bill-despite-nonpartisan-analysis-predicting-it-would-benefit-the-rich-and-harm-the-poor/) — threatening coverage for [195,000 people in his own district](https://avlwatchdog.org/edwards-defends-vote-for-one-big-beautiful-bill-despite-nonpartisan-analysis-predicting-it-would-benefit-the-rich-and-harm-the-poor/) and putting [Blue Ridge Regional Hospital at risk of closing](https://cbs4local.com/news/nation-world/governor-josh-stein-president-donald-trump-nc-hospitals-rural-big-beautiful-bill-angel-medical-center-blue-ridge-regional-hospital-mission-health). He voted twice against [ACA subsidies that 79,000 constituents relied on](https://dccc.org/do-i-keep-health-care-insurance-or-do-i-wing-it-chuck-edwards-vote-leaves-small-business-owners-suffering/), causing premiums to spike [up to 403%](https://www.charlotteobserver.com/opinion/article314472641.html) for older residents, and voted three times to [shield Trump's tariffs](https://dccc.org/chuck-edwards-votes-to-protect-trumps-cost-raising-tariffs/) that a conservative think tank says could cost NC farmers [$695 million and 8,000 jobs](https://www.carolinajournal.com/report-warns-tariffs-could-cost-nc-farms-700m-8000-jobs/). His vote cut [$250 billion from SNAP](https://avlwatchdog.org/edwards-defends-vote-for-one-big-beautiful-bill-despite-nonpartisan-analysis-predicting-it-would-benefit-the-rich-and-harm-the-poor/), costing [90,000 WNC families $70 a month](https://avlwatchdog.org/edwards-defends-vote-for-one-big-beautiful-bill-despite-nonpartisan-analysis-predicting-it-would-benefit-the-rich-and-harm-the-poor/) — three bags of groceries — while families were still rebuilding from Hurricane Helene. Edwards [accepted FEMA's denial of full reimbursement](https://smokymountainnews.com/news/item/39409-fema-decision-to-cost-wnc-millions-rep-edwards-accepts-ruling-while-governor-files-formal-appeal) while Governor Stein fought it, defended [DOGE firings of Helene recovery workers](https://828newsnow.com/news/228822-u-s-forest-service-chopped-17-nc-employees-laid-off-in-doge-blitz/) as "prudent," and when constituents confronted him at town halls, he told them to go [find the ice cream truck](https://smokymountainnews.com/archives/item/39248-edwards-dodges-questions-on-medicare-franklin-social-security-office-closing) and walked away under police escort. His McDonald's franchises have been linked to a [norovirus outbreak that sickened 300 people](https://www.dailymail.co.uk/news/article-15298711/Congressman-Chuck-Edwards-McDonalds-unsanitary-maggots.html), a [wrongful death lawsuit](https://www.dailymail.co.uk/news/article-15298711/Congressman-Chuck-Edwards-McDonalds-unsanitary-maggots.html) after a convicted felon he hired shot and killed a customer, and [fly larvae found in a burger](https://www.dailymail.co.uk/news/article-15298711/Congressman-Chuck-Edwards-McDonalds-unsanitary-maggots.html) — all while Edwards, [worth $16.6 million](https://avlwatchdog.org/edwards-defends-vote-for-one-big-beautiful-bill-despite-nonpartisan-analysis-predicting-it-would-benefit-the-rich-and-harm-the-poor/), represents one of the [poorest districts in the country](https://avlwatchdog.org/edwards-defends-vote-for-one-big-beautiful-bill-despite-nonpartisan-analysis-predicting-it-would-benefit-the-rich-and-harm-the-poor/).

### **HEALTH CARE: FLIP-FLOPPED THREE TIMES ON MEDICAID, THEN CAST THE DECIDING VOTE TO GUT IT**

- Edwards opposed Medicaid expansion for six years, [calling it a "budget boondoggle"](https://www.citizen-times.com/story/opinion/2020/02/16/chuck-edwards-medicaid-expansion-wont-solve-north-carolinas-health-care-crisis/4748432002/), then reversed himself in 2022 and called it ["fiscally irresponsible" not to expand](https://www.hendersonvillelightning.com/news/11663-edwards-explains-why-he-changed-his-mind-on-medicaid-expansion.html) and ["a lifeline to NC hospitals, especially our rural hospitals"](https://www.hendersonvillelightning.com/news/11663-edwards-explains-why-he-changed-his-mind-on-medicaid-expansion.html). In 2025, he cast the [deciding vote for the largest Medicaid cuts in U.S. history](https://avlwatchdog.org/edwards-defends-vote-for-one-big-beautiful-bill-despite-nonpartisan-analysis-predicting-it-would-benefit-the-rich-and-harm-the-poor/) — even after [co-signing a letter expressing concern about the "critical need to protect Medicaid"](https://www.charlotteobserver.com/opinion/article310209185.html) the month before.
- His vote could [trigger North Carolina's Medicaid expansion "trigger law"](https://avlwatchdog.org/edwards-defends-vote-for-one-big-beautiful-bill-despite-nonpartisan-analysis-predicting-it-would-benefit-the-rich-and-harm-the-poor/), eliminating coverage for up to 195,415 people in his district. [Blue Ridge Regional Hospital in Spruce Pine](https://cbs4local.com/news/nation-world/governor-josh-stein-president-donald-trump-nc-hospitals-rural-big-beautiful-bill-angel-medical-center-blue-ridge-regional-hospital-mission-health) and Angel Medical Center in Franklin are at risk of closing. Edwards [admitted the bill "could affect" rural hospitals](https://wlos.com/news/local/big-beautiful-bil-50-billion-rural-health-fund-support-states-amid-medicaid-changes-congressman-chuck-edwards-transformation-program-healthcare-organizations-north-carolina-department-health-human-services-hr-1-hospitals) but touted a $213 million rural health grant that experts said does ["virtually nothing" to offset $49.9 billion in Medicaid losses](https://wlos.com/news/local/big-beautiful-bil-50-billion-rural-health-fund-support-states-amid-medicaid-changes-congressman-chuck-edwards-transformation-program-healthcare-organizations-north-carolina-department-health-human-services-hr-1-hospitals).
- Edwards voted [twice against extending ACA tax credits](https://dccc.org/do-i-keep-health-care-insurance-or-do-i-wing-it-chuck-edwards-vote-leaves-small-business-owners-suffering/) that 79,000 people in his district relied on, causing a Hendersonville small business owner's premiums to [spike from $1,000 to $2,670 per month](https://www.northcarolinahealthnews.org/2026/01/19/nc-health-insurance-consumers-left-in-limbo-by-congress/) and enrollment to [drop 22%](https://www.charlotteobserver.com/opinion/article314472641.html) as older residents faced [403% premium increases](https://www.charlotteobserver.com/opinion/article314472641.html).

**Message:** Edwards called Medicaid a lifeline for rural hospitals, then cast the deciding vote to gut it — threatening 195,000 constituents' coverage and putting two hospitals in his own district at risk of closing.

### **AFFORDABILITY: BACKED TARIFFS AND CUT FOOD AID WHILE FAMILIES STRUGGLED TO REBUILD FROM HELENE**

- Edwards voted [three times to shield Trump's tariffs](https://dccc.org/chuck-edwards-votes-to-protect-trumps-cost-raising-tariffs/) from congressional oversight while the Yale Budget Lab found tariffs cost families [$2,700 to $3,400 per year](https://www.charlotteobserver.com/news/politics-government/article303520156.html) and a [conservative John Locke Foundation study](https://www.carolinajournal.com/report-warns-tariffs-could-cost-nc-farms-700m-8000-jobs/) warned they could cost NC farmers $695 million. When asked about rising prices, Edwards [dismissed them as a "change curve"](https://wlos.com/news/local/represenative-chuck-edwards-north-carolina-tariffs-president-donald-trump-economic-strategy-patience-foreign-leaders-attention-republican-government-politics-trade-prices) and called stock market losses ["emotional."](https://www.citizen-times.com/story/news/local/2025/04/07/trumps-tariff-plan-likely-to-increase-costs-for-wnc-consumers/82853122007/)
- His deciding vote cut [$250 billion from SNAP](https://avlwatchdog.org/edwards-defends-vote-for-one-big-beautiful-bill-despite-nonpartisan-analysis-predicting-it-would-benefit-the-rich-and-harm-the-poor/), costing [90,000 WNC families roughly $70 a month](https://avlwatchdog.org/edwards-defends-vote-for-one-big-beautiful-bill-despite-nonpartisan-analysis-predicting-it-would-benefit-the-rich-and-harm-the-poor/) — "three bags of groceries" — while MANNA FoodBank warned the cuts would cost WNC grocery stores [$280 million in business](https://avlwatchdog.org/edwards-defends-vote-for-one-big-beautiful-bill-despite-nonpartisan-analysis-predicting-it-would-benefit-the-rich-and-harm-the-poor/). Governor Stein called the bill ["a disgrace"](https://citizen-times.com/story/news/local/2025/07/08/big-beautiful-bill-critics-worry-about-west-north-carolina-health-food-access/84495172007) and said it could leave [1.4 million North Carolinians unable to afford food](https://citizen-times.com/story/news/local/2025/07/08/big-beautiful-bill-critics-worry-about-west-north-carolina-health-food-access/84495172007).
- Tariffs imposed a [25% duty on lumber and kitchen cabinets](https://ncnewsline.com/2025/10/29/new-furniture-tariffs-ripple-through-north-carolinas-high-point-market/) — raising rebuilding costs for Helene-devastated families — while Governor Stein warned recovery costs would ["go up, potentially dramatically."](https://www.wataugademocrat.com/news/local/nc-will-appeal-fema-ruling-to-end-100-helene-cost-share-as-state-braces-for/article_468df9bd-f370-4353-a1a9-7b18b9f6e9d5.html)

**Message:** Edwards shielded tariffs that are raising prices on everything from groceries to lumber, then cut food assistance for 90,000 families still rebuilding from Helene.

### **JOBS AND ECONOMY: DEFENDED DOGE FIRINGS AND TARIFFS THAT ARE GUTTING WNC'S WORKFORCE**

- Edwards voted three times to protect tariffs that a [Richmond Federal Reserve survey](https://www.richmondfed.org/region_communities/regional_data_analysis/regional_matters/2025/impact_of_tariffs_on_regions_firms) found caused 41% of regional firms to change their hiring plans and 47% to cancel or delay capital expenditures. A conservative think tank found the tariffs could cost [NC farmers $695 million and 8,000 jobs](https://www.carolinajournal.com/report-warns-tariffs-could-cost-nc-farms-700m-8000-jobs/), with pork producers alone facing [$430 million in losses](https://www.carolinajournal.com/report-warns-tariffs-could-cost-nc-farms-700m-8000-jobs/).
- Edwards defended DOGE as ["prudent"](https://citizen-times.com/story/news/2025/03/05/wnc-farm-service-agency-office-to-be-cut-by-doge-amid-farmer-helene-recovery/81338174007) while it [fired 17 Forest Service employees](https://828newsnow.com/news/228822-u-s-forest-service-chopped-17-nc-employees-laid-off-in-doge-blitz/) doing Helene recovery in WNC's national forests, [terminated a park ranger](https://eu.citizen-times.com/story/news/local/2025/03/04/fired-nc-national-park-ranger-hopes-to-be-reinstated-after-doge-firings/81147049007/) at Carl Sandburg National Historic Site two weeks before she completed probation, and [slashed the Farm Service Agency office](https://citizen-times.com/story/news/2025/03/05/wnc-farm-service-agency-office-to-be-cut-by-doge-amid-farmer-helene-recovery/81338174007) in Hendersonville that Helene-impacted farmers depend on for relief — with [$4.9 billion in agricultural damage](https://citizen-times.com/story/news/2025/03/05/wnc-farm-service-agency-office-to-be-cut-by-doge-amid-farmer-helene-recovery/81338174007) still largely unaddressed.
- A UNC-Asheville economist warned the tariffs would ["raise the cost of living" and stifle Helene recovery](https://www.citizen-times.com/story/news/local/2025/04/07/trumps-tariff-plan-likely-to-increase-costs-for-wnc-consumers/82853122007/), while a [WNC farmer said the federal cuts "may be the last straw"](https://avlwatchdog.org/edwards-defends-vote-for-one-big-beautiful-bill-despite-nonpartisan-analysis-predicting-it-would-benefit-the-rich-and-harm-the-poor/) for small farms.

**Message:** Edwards called DOGE firings "prudent" while Helene recovery workers lost their jobs, farms lost their aid offices, and his tariff votes put 8,000 more farm jobs at risk.

### **HELENE RECOVERY: ACCEPTED FEMA'S DENIAL AND FAILED TO DELIVER FOR HIS DISTRICT**

- Edwards [accepted FEMA's ruling ending 100% cost-share](https://smokymountainnews.com/news/item/39409-fema-decision-to-cost-wnc-millions-rep-edwards-accepts-ruling-while-governor-files-formal-appeal) for Helene recovery, saying he was "ready to move on," while Governor Stein [filed a formal appeal](https://smokymountainnews.com/news/item/39409-fema-decision-to-cost-wnc-millions-rep-edwards-accepts-ruling-while-governor-files-formal-appeal) and urged the president to reconsider. Six months after Helene, [local governments had received less than 4%](https://smokymountainnews.com/news/item/39409-fema-decision-to-cost-wnc-millions-rep-edwards-accepts-ruling-while-governor-files-formal-appeal) of the $1.7 billion in damage they claimed — with 14 of 23 receiving nothing at all.
- Edwards [helped write a $110 billion disaster bill](https://smokymountainnews.com/news/item/39409-fema-decision-to-cost-wnc-millions-rep-edwards-accepts-ruling-while-governor-files-formal-appeal) but failed to secure enough for his own state: North Carolina received only [$9-15 billion of the $60 billion in damage](https://smokymountainnews.com/news/item/39409-fema-decision-to-cost-wnc-millions-rep-edwards-accepts-ruling-while-governor-files-formal-appeal). Towns like Canton, with $11.3 million in damage and a $9.7 million general fund, now face [$1.3 million out-of-pocket bills](https://smokymountainnews.com/news/item/39409-fema-decision-to-cost-wnc-millions-rep-edwards-accepts-ruling-while-governor-files-formal-appeal).
- A [former Green Beret who led the region's largest grassroots Helene relief effort](https://smokymountainnews.com/archives/item/40215-former-green-beret-adam-smith-to-challenge-edwards-in-nc-11-primary) launched a primary challenge, saying he never saw Edwards on the ground "until President Trump showed up." Trump himself [publicly praised the challenger's relief work](https://smokymountainnews.com/archives/item/40215-former-green-beret-adam-smith-to-challenge-edwards-in-nc-11-primary) while Edwards looked on from the background.

**Message:** Edwards gave up on full FEMA reimbursement while the governor fought for it — and his own district got less than 4 cents of every dollar in damage.

### **MCDONALD'S EMPIRE: NOROVIRUS, A WRONGFUL DEATH, AND FLY LARVAE**

- Edwards' McDonald's franchise in Brevard was [identified as the source of a norovirus outbreak that sickened 300 people](https://www.dailymail.co.uk/news/article-15298711/Congressman-Chuck-Edwards-McDonalds-unsanitary-maggots.html). His wife said the restaurant reopened after "a couple of days."
- Edwards' company [hired a convicted felon with a gun assault conviction](https://www.dailymail.co.uk/news/article-15298711/Congressman-Chuck-Edwards-McDonalds-unsanitary-maggots.html) who shot and killed a 30-year-old mother, Jaclyn Reed, at one of his restaurants. The family sued, alleging the company "knew or should have known" the employee was dangerous. The case was settled out of court.
- A customer found [fly larvae in a burger](https://www.dailymail.co.uk/news/article-15298711/Congressman-Chuck-Edwards-McDonalds-unsanitary-maggots.html) at one of Edwards' locations. North Carolina health authorities confirmed the sandwich was "full of fly larvae."

**Message:** Edwards built his $16.6 million fortune on McDonald's franchises linked to a mass food poisoning, a customer's death, and maggots in the food.

- Edwards [walked away under police escort](https://smokymountainnews.com/archives/item/39248-edwards-dodges-questions-on-medicare-franklin-social-security-office-closing) when a constituent asked about Medicaid cuts to children, telling the crowd, "Let's go find the ice cream truck now." - Edwards [told the Smoky Mountain News he would only speak to media that give him favorable coverage](https://smokymountainnews.com/news/item/39409-fema-decision-to-cost-wnc-millions-rep-edwards-accepts-ruling-while-governor-files-formal-appeal) and [refused to comment](https://www.northcarolinahealthnews.org/2026/01/19/nc-health-insurance-consumers-left-in-limbo-by-congress/) as constituents faced "potentially catastrophic" premium increases after his ACA vote.
- Edwards [blamed "the failures of Obamacare"](https://avlwatchdog.org/chuck-edwards-failures-of-obamacare-led-to-missions-decline-in-care/) for HCA's mismanagement of Mission Hospital — which has been found in ["immediate jeopardy" four times](https://avlwatchdog.org/its-groundhog-day-for-hca-owned-mission-hospital-and-asheville-its-deja-vu-all-over-again/) since being acquired — and said the government has "no role" in fixing it.

**Message:** Edwards does not listen, does not answer, and does not act — he walks away from constituents and blames everyone but himself.

### **WEALTH VS. POVERTY: A $16.6 MILLION CONGRESSMAN IN ONE OF AMERICA'S POOREST DISTRICTS**

- Edwards' [net worth of $16.6 million](https://avlwatchdog.org/edwards-defends-vote-for-one-big-beautiful-bill-despite-nonpartisan-analysis-predicting-it-would-benefit-the-rich-and-harm-the-poor/) from McDonald's franchises stands against his district's [$51,884 median household income](https://avlwatchdog.org/edwards-defends-vote-for-one-big-beautiful-bill-despite-nonpartisan-analysis-predicting-it-would-benefit-the-rich-and-harm-the-poor/) — in the bottom 15% of all congressional districts. The CBO said the bill he voted for would [hurt the poorest 10% while enriching the richest 10%](https://avlwatchdog.org/edwards-defends-vote-for-one-big-beautiful-bill-despite-nonpartisan-analysis-predicting-it-would-benefit-the-rich-and-harm-the-poor/) — a category that includes Edwards himself.
- Activists [boycotted Edwards' McDonald's franchises](https://www.blueridgenow.com/story/news/local/2025/11/26/activists-boycott-rep-edwards-mcdonalds-snap-medicaid-cuts/87466340007/), with one organizer saying, "He's making money off of us while he's withholding food stamps."

**Message:** Edwards voted to enrich himself and the ultra-wealthy while cutting health care, food assistance, and jobs in one of the poorest districts in America.
