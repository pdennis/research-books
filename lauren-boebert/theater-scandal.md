# Caught on Camera Groping Her Date and Vaping at a Family Theater, Then Lied About It

**Boebert Was Escorted Out Of A Denver Theater For Vaping, Groping Her Date, And Causing A Disturbance During A Family Musical.** According to The Denver Post, Boebert and her companion were asked to leave the Buell Theatre during a performance of the touring Broadway show "Beetlejuice" after "three complaints from other theatergoers" about disruptive behavior including vaping, singing, and recording. Surveillance footage subsequently showed Boebert's date touching her chest and Boebert appearing to place her hand near his crotch during the performance. [Denver Post, [9/12/23](https://www.denverpost.com/2023/09/12/lauren-boebert-removed-beetlejuice-musical-denver/)]

- **Boebert's Campaign Falsely Denied She Was Vaping Before Surveillance Video Proved Otherwise.** According to NBC News, Boebert's campaign manager told The Washington Post that the visible smoke "came from heavy fog machines" used in the production. Surveillance video released days later showed Boebert raising her hand to her face, "and then a puff of smoke comes out of her mouth toward the person in front of her." Boebert subsequently apologized, claiming, "'I genuinely did not recall vaping that evening.'" [NBC News, [9/14/23](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/congress/surveillance-video-appears-contradict-boeberts-denial-was-vaping-beetl-rcna105246)]

- **Boebert Acknowledged She "Fell Short" Of Her Values And Blamed Her Divorce.** According to The Colorado Sun, Boebert stated, "'There's no perfect blueprint for going through a public and difficult divorce, which over the past few months has made for a challenging personal time for me and my entire family. I've tried to handle it with strength and grace as best I can, but I simply fell short of my values on Sunday.'" [Colorado Sun, [9/15/23](https://coloradosun.com/2023/09/15/lauren-boebert-apology-beetlejuice/)]
