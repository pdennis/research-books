# Fled Her District After Nearly Losing, Attacked as "Carpetbagger" by Fellow Republicans

**Boebert Abandoned Her Own District After Winning Reelection By Just 546 Votes.** According to CPR News, Boebert announced in December 2023 that she would not seek reelection in CO-3 but would instead run in CO-4, a safer Republican seat on Colorado's Eastern Plains, after her 2022 race against Democrat Adam Frisch was decided by just 546 votes — one of the closest House races in the country. [CPR News, [12/27/23](https://www.cpr.org/2023/12/27/lauren-boebert-congress-2024-election-switching-districts/)]

- **A Republican State Lawmaker Called Boebert's District Switch "Carpetbagging."** According to Axios Denver, Republican state Rep. Richard Holtorf said Boebert was "carpetbagging," stating, "'Seat shopping isn't something the voters look kindly upon. If you can't win in your home, you can't win here.'" [Axios Denver, [1/26/24](https://www.axios.com/local/denver/2024/01/26/lauren-boebert-carpetbagger-debate-4th-district-colorado)]
