# Rep. Lauren Boebert (CO-04) Can't Be Trusted on Jobs and the Economy

Rep. Lauren Boebert talks about jobs but her record tells a different story. She voted against every major jobs bill of the Biden era — the bipartisan infrastructure law, the Inflation Reduction Act, and the CHIPS and Science Act — then repeatedly claimed credit for the jobs and funding they created in her own district. She called the Inflation Reduction Act "a complete scam" even as it funded an $200 million expansion and 850 new jobs at CS Wind in Pueblo. She voted against $51.4 million for the South Bridge project in Glenwood Springs, then bragged about "securing" the funding, prompting Transportation Secretary Pete Buttigieg to ask, "In what way do you believe that your support helped this project?" Her own business record is no better: Shooters Grill lost more than $600,000 over three years, racked up eight tax liens for failing to pay unemployment insurance premiums on her workers, and she refused a court order to garnish an employee's wages — a debt that went unpaid. She voted to keep Trump's 25% tariffs on Canada while 86% of Colorado businesses say tariffs are a challenge, and Colorado's beef industry faces a $39 million revenue drop. And when Trump vetoed her own bipartisan water project — a 130-mile pipeline delivering clean drinking water to 50,000 people in southeastern Colorado — she could not persuade even her own party to override it.

---

### Voted Against Every Major Jobs Bill, Then Claimed Credit for the Results

**Boebert Called The Inflation Reduction Act "A Complete Scam" Even As It Created 850 Jobs At CS Wind In Pueblo.** According to Newsweek, "Biden said, 'Tell that to the 850 Coloradans who get new jobs in Pueblo at CS Wind thanks to this law,'" after visiting the CS Wind facility — the world's largest wind tower manufacturer — which announced a $200 million expansion directly attributed to the Inflation Reduction Act. Boebert voted against the law and called it "'a complete scam.'" [Newsweek, [11/29/23](https://www.newsweek.com/biden-boebert-colorado-inflation-reduction-act-cs-wind-1847864)]

**Boebert Voted Against The Bipartisan Infrastructure Law, Then Bragged About "Securing" $51.4 Million It Funded For A Bridge In Her District.** According to The Colorado Sun, Boebert "voted against the Infrastructure Law (H.R. 3684)" but later claimed she "'worked closely with local elected officials on both sides of the aisle'" to secure over $51.4 million for the South Bridge project in Glenwood Springs — funding that came through the Rural Surface Transportation Grant Program authorized by the law she opposed. [Colorado Sun, [6/7/24](https://coloradosun.com/2024/06/07/lauren-boebert-infrastructure-funding-bill/)]

- **Transportation Secretary Buttigieg Called Out Boebert For Taking Credit.** According to Newsweek, Buttigieg responded to Boebert's claim by stating, "'Congresswoman, in what way do you believe that your support helped this project? We chose it because it's a good project, and funded it using President Biden's infrastructure package, which you voted against.'" The White House press office added, "'One thing about Congressional Republicans ... they're going to take credit for investments they voted against.'" [Newsweek, [5/24/24](https://www.newsweek.com/lauren-boebert-called-out-white-house-voted-against-1903853)]

**Boebert Voted Against An Appropriations Bill, Then Celebrated "$20 Million For Important Water And Infrastructure Projects" It Funded In Her District.** According to Newsweek, Boebert posted that "'10 out of 10 of our requests were successfully funded'" from the Consolidated Appropriations Act she voted against. X's Community Notes flagged the post, stating, "'While Congresswoman Boebert may have worked with local stakeholders, it is important to note that she voted against the passage of the legislation providing the funds.'" [Newsweek, [3/27/24](https://www.newsweek.com/lauren-boebert-fact-check-voted-infrastructure-funding-1881675)]

### Ran a Restaurant That Lost $600,000 and Stiffed Workers on Unemployment Insurance

**Boebert's Shooters Grill Lost More Than $600,000 Over Three Years Before Its Landlord Refused To Renew The Lease.** According to The Daily Beast, Boebert's financial disclosures showed Shooters Grill lost more than $242,000 in 2018, $143,000 in 2019, and $226,000 in 2020 — more than $600,000 in total. The restaurant closed in July 2022 after the building's new owners declined to renew the lease. [Daily Beast, [7/11/22](https://www.thedailybeast.com/is-it-last-call-for-lauren-boeberts-gun-restaurant/)]

**Boebert Owed Nearly $20,000 In Tax Liens For Failing To Pay Unemployment Insurance On Her Workers.** According to Colorado Newsline, Boebert's Shooters Grill accumulated eight liens from the Colorado Department of Labor and Employment for nonpayment of unemployment insurance premiums between 2016 and 2020, totaling $19,552.86 including interest and penalties. She paid them off just weeks before being elected to Congress. [Colorado Newsline, [2/4/21](https://coloradonewsline.com/2021/02/04/rep-boebert-paid-off-nearly-20k-in-state-tax-liens-in-2020/)]

**Boebert Refused A Court Order To Garnish An Employee's Wages And Never Paid The Resulting Lien.** According to the Colorado Times Recorder, a Garfield County court assessed a $2,578 lien against Shooters Grill after Boebert was "'unresponsive to written requests to deal with the matter'" and "'skipped two hearings arranged by the court.'" As of June 2021, there was no record of Boebert settling the debt. [Colorado Times Recorder, [6/8/21](https://coloradotimesrecorder.com/2021/06/co-court-has-no-record-of-boebert-settling-debt-owed-from-her-refusal-to-garnish-employees-wages/37194/)]

**Boebert's Restaurant Gave 80 People Food Poisoning At A County Fair.** According to The Denver Post, 80 people contracted Clostridium perfringens food poisoning from pork sliders served at the Rifle Rodeo in June 2017. Colorado health officials confirmed the outbreak was linked to the pork sliders. [Denver Post, [6/22/17](https://www.denverpost.com/2017/06/22/food-poisoning-rifle-rodeo/)]

### Supported Tariffs That Are Devastating Colorado Jobs and Agriculture

**Boebert Voted To Keep Trump's 25% Tariffs On Canada While Colorado Families Face Up To $2,400 In Higher Annual Costs.** According to CPR News, Boebert voted to keep the tariffs in place, telling the outlet, "'I think President Trump is doing just fine on the tariffs issue.'" Fellow Colorado Republican Jeff Hurd broke with Boebert to vote against the tariffs. The Yale Budget Lab estimated the tariffs cost the average household up to $2,400 per year. [CPR News, [2/11/26](https://www.cpr.org/2026/02/11/u-s-house-votes-against-trumps-tariff-on-canada-with-help-of-hurd-and-five-other-republicans/)]

**Eighty-Six Percent Of Colorado Businesses Surveyed Said Tariffs Were A Challenge, And Manufacturers Reported Costs As High As $2 Million.** According to The Denver Post, a state report found that "'86% of the businesses view the tariffs levied this year as challenges and only 14% see them as beneficial,'" with manufacturers "'incurring costs as high as $2 million.'" Colorado's effective tariff rate has increased sevenfold since last year. [Denver Post, [11/30/25](https://www.denverpost.com/2025/11/30/tariffs-colorado-business-agriculture/)]

**Colorado's Beef Industry Faced A $39 Million Revenue Drop And 265 Fewer Jobs From Tariffs.** According to The Denver Post, a state report projected tariffs caused a "'$39 million revenue drop'" in the beef industry in the first half of the year compared to 2024, along with "'265 fewer jobs'" and an "'$80 million decline'" in economic output statewide. Beef is Colorado's top agricultural export. [Denver Post, [10/23/25](https://www.denverpost.com/2025/10/23/colorado-agriculture-tariffs-beef/)]

### Failed To Deliver on Her Own Signature Water Project

**Trump Vetoed Boebert's Bipartisan Arkansas Valley Conduit Bill, And She Could Not Persuade Her Own Party To Override It.** According to CPR News, Trump vetoed the Finish the Arkansas Valley Conduit Act — a bill that passed both chambers unanimously — which would fund a 130-mile pipeline delivering clean drinking water to 50,000 people in 39 communities across southeastern Colorado. Only 35 Republicans voted to override, falling 40 votes short. Boebert said she was "'disappointed to see the lack of leadership, the amount of people that will fold.'" [CPR News, [1/8/26](https://www.cpr.org/2026/01/08/house-trump-arkansas-valley-conduit-veto-override-fails/)]

- **The Water Project Would Have Addressed Carcinogenic Contamination Affecting 50,000 Coloradans In Boebert's Own District.** According to CPR News, Chris Woodka of the Southeastern Colorado Water Conservancy District stated, "'This isn't a frivolous project. It's a project that meets federally mandated standards for water quality to ensure that 50,000 people are drinking clean, not carcinogenic, water.'" Over $200 million has already been invested in the project. [CPR News, [12/30/25](https://www.cpr.org/2025/12/30/trump-vetoes-arkansas-river-valley-conduit-funding-bill/)]

### Impact on Everyday People

**Colorado Ranchers Said Tariff-Driven Cost Increases Made It Harder To Stay In Business.** According to The Denver Post, equipment costs surged — "tractor tires jumped from $1,500 to $5,000 each" — and global fertilizer prices rose 15%, with much of the supply sourced from tariff-affected Canada. Ranchers who finally achieved fair returns as beef prices climbed 14% now face the threat of imported Argentine beef undercutting those gains. [Denver Post, [10/23/25](https://www.denverpost.com/2025/10/23/colorado-agriculture-tariffs-beef/)]

**Colorado Rural Hospitals Were Already Laying Off Workers As Medicaid Cuts Boebert Voted For Loomed.** According to CPR News, Arkansas Valley Regional Medical Center in La Junta was "'laying off approximately 5% of its workforce'" and "'previously closed its obstetric services in April,'" citing "'cost increases, inflationary pressures, and declining reimbursement rates.'" The hospital is one of six rural Colorado facilities at risk of closure. [CPR News, [6/27/25](https://www.cpr.org/2025/06/27/la-junta-hospital-lay-offs-medicaid-cuts/)]

**San Luis Valley Potato Farmers Lost An Estimated $150,000 Per Crop Circle Due To Tariff-Driven Market Instability.** According to the Governor's office, the cost of production for potatoes in the San Luis Valley has hovered from "'$8.00 to $11.00 per hundredweight'" while return pricing sat at "'$5.00 to $6.00 per cwt,'" and with a typical yield of 50,000 cwt per circle, "'farmers are losing an estimated $150,000 per circle.'" [Colorado Governor's Office, [11/20/25](https://www.colorado.gov/governor/news/governor-polis-releases-new-report-detailing-how-trumps-tariff-taxes-are-squeezing-colorado)]
