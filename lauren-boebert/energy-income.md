# Concealed Husband's $1 Million in Energy Industry Income While Pushing to Loosen Drilling Rules

**Boebert Failed To Disclose Her Husband's Nearly $1 Million In Consulting Fees From A Major Colorado Oil And Gas Company.** According to The Colorado Sun, Jayson Boebert was paid $460,601 in 2019 and $478,386 in 2020 by Terra Energy Partners, "one of the largest producers of natural gas in Colorado," for consulting services. Lauren Boebert did not report this income on her financial disclosures when she ran for Congress in 2020, and the payments only came to light in August 2021 through amended disclosures. [Colorado Sun, [8/19/21](https://coloradosun.com/2021/08/19/lauren-boebert-husband-terra-energy-productions/)]

- **Boebert's Name Appeared On Business Documents For Her Husband's Oil And Gas Consulting Firm.** According to The Colorado Sun, Lauren Boebert's "name appears in business documents filed with the state for the oil and gas consulting firm run by her husband, Boebert Consulting," which received the nearly $1 million from Terra Energy Partners. She had simultaneously been pushing to loosen federal drilling regulations as a member of Congress. [Colorado Sun, [8/20/21](https://coloradosun.com/2021/08/20/lauren-boebert-husband-jayson-oil-gas-consulting-terra-energy/)]
