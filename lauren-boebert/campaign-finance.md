# Repeatedly Used Campaign Donor Money for Personal Expenses

**Boebert Used Campaign Funds To Pay Her Personal Rent And Utility Bills.** According to NBC News, Boebert's campaign acknowledged in an FEC filing that $6,000 in payments for rent and utilities were a "personal expense of Lauren Boebert billed to campaign account in error." The FEC had previously warned that "if it is determined that the disbursement(s) constitutes the personal use of campaign funds, the Commission may consider taking further legal action." [NBC News, [9/22/21](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/congress/rep-lauren-boebert-improperly-used-campaign-funds-rent-utilities-n1279976)]

**Boebert Paid Herself Over $22,000 In Mileage Reimbursements Without Providing Any Documentation.** According to CPR News, Boebert's campaign paid her $22,259 in mileage reimbursements in 2020, which would have required her to drive 38,712 miles — despite attending few publicly advertised campaign events during the pandemic. Campaign finance expert Kedric Payne of the Campaign Legal Center called the amount "'unheard of in my review of monetary reimbursement.'" Boebert's lawyers never submitted any mileage log to the FEC. [CPR News, [2/7/21](https://www.cpr.org/2021/02/07/what-we-know-about-lauren-boeberts-campaign-payments-to-herself-for-driving-38000-miles/)]

**Boebert Faced A New FEC Complaint For Spending Over $3,300 In Campaign Funds On A Trip To See Kid Rock In Texas.** According to The Washington Times, FEC filings showed Boebert's reelection committee spent $925 on tickets at AT&T Stadium in Arlington, Texas — the weekend Kid Rock was hosting a Rock N Rodeo event — and $2,455 at a 4-star hotel nearby. American Muckrakers PAC filed an FEC complaint alleging the trip constituted prohibited personal use of campaign funds. [Washington Times, [12/9/25](https://www.washingtontimes.com/news/2025/dec/9/lauren-boebert-used-campaign-funds-see-kid-rock-fec-filings-show/)]

**Boebert Admitted She "Under-Reported A Lot Of Stuff" To The FEC.** According to Salon, Boebert told the Colorado Times Recorder, "'I am doing the work of the people. I had to make those connections. And really, I under-reported a lot of stuff.'" Her campaign treasurer separately wrote to the FEC that "'there was no intent to try to report contributions illegally as I just plain forgot to watch for large donations.'" [Salon, [8/27/21](https://www.salon.com/2021/08/27/lauren-boebert-admits-to-underreporting-fec/)]
