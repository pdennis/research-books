# Rep. Lauren Boebert (CO-04) Can't Be Trusted on Health Care

Rep. Lauren Boebert has consistently voted against legislation that would lower health care costs for Colorado families — opposing insulin price caps, Medicare drug price negotiation, and veterans' health benefits — while voting for the largest Medicaid cuts in American history, threatening coverage for an estimated 140,000 to 230,000 Coloradans. In her own 4th Congressional District, nearly 45,000 children rely on Medicaid, and Prowers Medical Center in Lamar is among six Colorado rural hospitals at risk of closure due to the cuts she championed. Boebert has separately voted to strip federal funding from a rural hospital in Cheyenne Wells and a public health facility in Fort Morgan — both in her own district. Despite promising "I will never vote for legislation that will leave Coloradans without health care coverage," Boebert's record tells a different story.

---

### Voted for Massive Medicaid Cuts While Her Own Constituents Depend on the Program

**Boebert Voted For The Largest Medicaid Cuts In History, Threatening Coverage For Up To 230,000 Coloradans.** According to The Colorado Sun, all four Colorado Republicans in the House, including Boebert, voted for the One Big Beautiful Bill, which the Congressional Budget Office projects will cause "11.8 million more people" to "lose health insurance coverage" nationwide, with "$1.2 trillion in cutbacks to Medicaid and food stamps, primarily through new work requirements." [Colorado Sun, [7/3/25](https://coloradosun.com/2025/07/03/colorado-votes-big-beautiful-bill-congress/)]

**Roughly 16 Percent Of Boebert's District Relies On Medicaid, Including Nearly 45,000 Children.** According to the Colorado Children's Campaign, "nearly 45,000 children" in Boebert's 4th Congressional District were "enrolled in Medicaid" in 2024, and over 9,000 households with children received SNAP benefits — all of which are threatened by the cuts she voted for. [Colorado Children's Campaign, [5/22/25](https://www.coloradokids.org/statement-the-big-beautiful-bill-will-cause-big-problems-for-colorado-kids/)]

**Boebert's Medicaid Vote Puts Prowers Medical Center In Lamar At Risk Of Closure.** According to CPR News, Prowers Medical Center is among "six Colorado rural hospitals at risk of closure," and the proposed federal cuts "could mean $10.4 billion in reduced reimbursements to Colorado hospitals over five years." Nearly half of Otero County's population relies on Medicaid. [CPR News, [6/27/25](https://www.cpr.org/2025/06/27/la-junta-hospital-lay-offs-medicaid-cuts/)]

**Boebert Dismissed Concerns About Medicaid Cuts By Focusing On Transgender Care.** According to Westword, when confronted about the bill's Medicaid provisions, Boebert stated, "'Out-of-touch Democrats in Colorado and D.C. would rather these tax dollars be used to absolutely chop and cut our children and our adults,'" deflecting from the estimated 140,000 to 230,000 Coloradans who could lose coverage. [Westword, [5/29/25](https://www.westword.com/news/boebert-evans-face-denver-protest-after-snap-medicaid-cuts-24668981/)]

### Voted Against Lowering Prescription Drug Costs

**Boebert Voted Against The Inflation Reduction Act, Which Capped Insulin At $35 And Allowed Medicare To Negotiate Drug Prices.** According to the DCCC, the Inflation Reduction Act "did not receive a single vote from Republicans in Congress — including Lauren Boebert," despite its provisions capping insulin costs at $35 per month for Medicare recipients and allowing Medicare to negotiate prescription drug prices for the first time. [DCCC, [8/16/23](https://dccc.org/one-year-ago-today-lauren-boebert-voted-against-relief-for-working-families/)]

**Boebert Voted Against The Affordable Insulin Now Act, Which Would Have Capped Insulin Costs At $35 For All Americans.** According to NPR, the House passed the Affordable Insulin Now Act on March 31, 2022, by a vote of 232 to 193, with only 12 Republicans voting in favor. Boebert was among the 193 members who voted against capping the price of insulin at $35 per month. [NPR, [3/31/22](https://www.npr.org/2022/03/31/1090085513/house-passes-bill-to-cap-insulin-prices)]

### Voted Against Veterans' Health Care

**Boebert Voted Against The PACT Act, Then Defended Her Opposition To Health Benefits For Veterans Exposed To Burn Pits.** According to The Hill, Boebert was one of 88 House Republicans who voted against the PACT Act, which "expands eligibility for Department of Veterans Affairs health care to veterans who served in the post-9/11 era" and "creates a framework for establishing presumptions of service connections related to toxic exposures." Boebert defended her vote by saying, "'I'm not spending $600 billion forever.'" [Hill, [8/13/24](https://thehill.com/homenews/house/4864310-boebert-pact-act-vote/)]

### Voted Against Her Own District's Health Infrastructure

**Boebert Voted To Strip Federal Funding From A Rural Hospital And Public Health Facility In Her Own District.** According to a report on the vote, Boebert voted for "an extreme amendment to the appropriations bill" that sought to "strike local project funding," including $2,030,000 for Keefe Memorial Hospital in Cheyenne Wells and $3,041,000 for a Northeast Colorado Health Department facility in Fort Morgan — both in her 4th Congressional District. Over 70 House Republicans voted against the amendment. [Eileen for Colorado, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://www.eileenforcolorado.com/news/lauren-boebert-votes-to-kill-hospital-and-public-health-projects-for-her-own-district)]

### Called for ACA Repeal Without a Replacement

**Boebert Campaigned On Repealing The Affordable Care Act, The Only Law Protecting Pre-Existing Condition Coverage.** According to The Denver Post, Boebert criticized her Republican predecessor for "failing to repeal the ACA while in Congress," stating that Republicans "'had an opportunity in 2017 to repeal Obamacare.'" Despite promising "'I will never vote for legislation that will leave Coloradans without health care coverage,'" Boebert offered no replacement plan to protect the more than 159,000 Coloradans who receive health insurance through the state's ACA marketplace. [Denver Post, [8/16/20](https://www.denverpost.com/2020/08/16/boebert-mitsch-bush-3rd-congressional-health-care-insurance/)]

### Impact on Everyday People

**Colorado Rural Hospitals Are Already Laying Off Workers With Medicaid Cuts Looming.** According to CPR News, Arkansas Valley Regional Medical Center in La Junta — a 25-bed critical access hospital — is "laying off approximately 5% of its workforce" and "previously closed its obstetric services in April," citing "'cost increases, inflationary pressures, and declining reimbursement rates.'" The hospital is one of six rural Colorado facilities at risk of closure. [CPR News, [6/27/25](https://www.cpr.org/2025/06/27/la-junta-hospital-lay-offs-medicaid-cuts/)]

**A Boebert Constituent Said Her Son Would Die Without Medicaid.** According to Westword, Dawn Caldwell of Highlands Ranch confronted Boebert and Rep. Evans at a protest, stating, "'My son will die without Medicaid. This will make life almost impossible for most people with disabilities.'" An estimated 10.3 million people nationwide would lose Medicaid coverage by 2034 under the cuts Boebert voted for. [Westword, [5/29/25](https://www.westword.com/news/boebert-evans-face-denver-protest-after-snap-medicaid-cuts-24668981/)]

**Eighty-Five Percent Of Colorado's Rural Hospitals Already Lack Sustainable Profit Margins.** According to the Colorado Times Recorder, "43 of Colorado's 88 acute-care hospitals operate in rural areas" and "85% of rural hospitals lack sustainable profit margins." The proposed cuts threaten a system that "deliver[s] nearly 5,000 babies annually and stabilize[s] 310,000 emergency patients" in rural Colorado. [Colorado Times Recorder, [2/11/25](https://coloradotimesrecorder.com/2025/02/why-medicaid-cuts-threaten-health-care-for-everyone-in-colorado/67740/)]
