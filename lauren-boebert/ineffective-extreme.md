# Passed Zero Bills in Her First Two Years, Ranked Dead Last in Bipartisanship

**Boebert Introduced 41 Bills In Her First Congress And Enacted Zero Into Law.** According to GovTrack, during the 117th Congress (2021-2022), Boebert introduced 41 bills and resolutions but none were enacted into law and none were even reported out of committee. She ranked at the 0th percentile among House Republicans for bipartisan collaboration, cosponsoring bills from the opposite party just 2% of the time. [GovTrack, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://www.govtrack.us/congress/members/lauren_boebert/456805/report-card/2022)]

### Made Anti-Muslim Remarks About a Colleague, Then Refused to Apologize Directly

**Boebert Called Rep. Ilhan Omar A Member Of The "Jihad Squad" And Implied She Was A Terrorist.** According to The Denver Post, video emerged of Boebert telling a Colorado audience about sharing an elevator with Omar. Boebert said she saw a Capitol Police officer running toward them and said, "'Well, she does not have a backpack — we should be OK.'" Omar denied the encounter ever took place. Boebert apologized "to anyone in the Muslim community" but refused to apologize directly to Omar, instead doubling down on her rhetoric during a subsequent phone call. [Denver Post, [11/26/21](https://www.denverpost.com/2021/11/26/lauren-boebert-ilhan-omar-anti-muslim-comments-apology/)]

### Said the Church Is "Supposed to Direct the Government"

**Boebert Told A Church Congregation She Was "Tired Of This Separation Of Church And State Junk."** According to The Denver Post, Boebert told the congregation at Cornerstone Christian Center in Basalt on June 27, 2022 that "'the church is supposed to direct the government'" and that "'the government is not supposed to direct the church.'" She called the separation of church and state "'junk'" based on "'a stinking letter'" that "'means nothing like what they say it does.'" PolitiFact rated her claim about the founders' intent as false. [Denver Post, [6/27/22](https://www.denverpost.com/2022/06/27/lauren-boebert-church-state-colorado/)]

### Tweeted "Today Is 1776" On January 6 and Disclosed Pelosi's Movements

**Boebert Tweeted "Today Is 1776" On The Morning Of The Capitol Riot And Later Tweeted About Pelosi's Location.** According to Newsweek, Boebert tweeted "Today is 1776" on the morning of January 6, 2021, a phrase widely interpreted as a call for revolution. During the breach, she tweeted that "The Speaker has been removed from the chambers," drawing scrutiny for potentially disclosing security information. Jan. 6 protest organizers later told Rolling Stone they had met with Boebert ahead of the insurrection. [Newsweek, [1/11/21](https://www.newsweek.com/lauren-boeberts-tweet-history-scrutinized-amid-questions-her-capitol-insurrection-role-1562255)]
