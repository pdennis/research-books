# Personal Conduct: Arrests, Son's Felony Charges, Restaurant Scandals

### Has Been Arrested or Summonsed at Least Four Times

**The Denver Post Reported Boebert Had "An Unusually Long" Arrest Record "For A Congressional Candidate."** According to The Denver Post, Boebert "has been arrested and summonsed at least four times over the past decade for petty crimes." Incidents include a 2015 arrest at a music festival for disorderly conduct — during which she claimed "'she had friends at Fox News'" — and a 2016 arrest after her truck wound up in a ditch and she repeatedly failed to appear in court. [Denver Post, [8/27/20](https://www.denverpost.com/2020/08/27/lauren-boebert-arrests-colorado-3rd-mitsch-bush/)]

### Son Arrested on 22 Criminal Charges Including Five Felonies

**Boebert's Eldest Son Tyler Was Arrested On Multiple Felony Charges And Over 15 Additional Offenses For A String Of Thefts.** According to CPR News, Tyler Boebert, then 18, was arrested in February 2024 in connection with vehicle break-ins and property thefts in Rifle, Colorado. Charges included "four felony counts of criminal possession of identification documents" and "one felony count of conspiracy to commit a felony." He later pleaded guilty to attempted identity theft and received a deferred sentence. [CPR News, [2/28/24](https://www.cpr.org/2024/02/28/rep-lauren-boebert-son-tyler-arrested-multiple-felony-charges/)]

- **Tyler Boebert Was Subsequently Charged With Misdemeanor Child Abuse.** According to Westword, Tyler Boebert, then 20, was cited in July 2025 for criminal negligence related to an incident involving his young son. Lauren Boebert described it as "'a miscommunication on monitoring my young grandson that recently led to him getting out of the house.'" [Westword, [7/27/25](https://www.westword.com/news/lauren-boeberts-son-now-facing-child-abuse-charge-25141015/)]

### Restaurant Poisoned 80 People, Then She Defied COVID Health Orders

**Boebert's Restaurant Gave 80 People Food Poisoning At A County Fair.** According to The Denver Post, 80 people contracted Clostridium perfringens food poisoning from pork sliders served at the Rifle Rodeo in June 2017. Colorado health officials confirmed the outbreak was linked to the pork sliders. [Denver Post, [6/22/17](https://www.denverpost.com/2017/06/22/food-poisoning-rifle-rodeo/)]

- **Boebert Defied Colorado's COVID Public Health Orders And Had Her Restaurant's License Suspended.** According to CPR News, Boebert reopened Shooters Grill for dine-in service in May 2020 in defiance of Colorado's Safer at Home public health order. Garfield County served a cease-and-desist order, which Boebert ignored, and her food service license was suspended. [CPR News, [5/14/20](https://www.cpr.org/2020/05/14/rifle-restaurant-shooters-grill-defies-a-cease-and-desist-order-to-keep-serving-diners/)]
