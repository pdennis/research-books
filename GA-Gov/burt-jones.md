# How To Win Against Burt Jones

Burt Jones supported federal legislation that stripped health care from hundreds of thousands of Georgians, set up future cuts to Medicare, cut SNAP benefits, and rescinded tax credits meant to support clean energy jobs and projects in Georgia. Jones praised Trump’s “One Big Beautiful Bill,” and opposed Medicaid expansion. He also lauded Trump’s “Department of Government Efficiency,” even as its cuts affected thousands of Georgia federal workers and those reliant on Social Security. Jones praised Trump’s tariffs as they crushed Georgia’s economy, which was 7^th^ in the nation for net exports, ignoring the [stock market crashing](https://www.npr.org/2025/04/04/nx-s1-5352362/markets-selloff-dow-trump-tariffs) and increasing food prices. Jones also supported Trump's war with Iran even as it drove up gas and fertilizer prices across Georgia. Jones supported Georgia’s 2019 abortion ban and attended events with extreme anti-abortion organizations. As a state senator and lieutenant governor, Jones used his power to attempt to overturn the 2020 presidential election, and to advance legislation that would benefit his family’s business. In addition, Jones used his wealth to enrich his own campaigns.

### Burt Jones Supported Devastating Health Care Cuts

-   In 2025, Jones [supported](https://x.com/burtjonesforga/status/1958522567804755995) Trump’s [“One Big Beautiful Bill,”](https://www.congress.gov/votes/house/119-1/190) which is estimated to kick [17 million Americans](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2025/07/01/least-17-million-americans-would-lose-insurance-under-trump-plan/) off their health insurance, including [nearly 12 million Americans](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2025/07/01/least-17-million-americans-would-lose-insurance-under-trump-plan/) who rely on Medicaid. More than [624,000 Georgians](https://www.jec.senate.gov/public/_cache/files/9cfdee90-dd29-41e8-97da-b5dcdc0317cf/updated-jec-fact-sheet-on-district-level-impacts-of-health-care-cuts.pdf) could lose their health insurance as a result of the bill. More than [1.5 million Georgians](https://www.kff.org/affordable-care-act/state-indicator/marketplace-enrollment/?currentTimeframe=0&selectedRows=%7B%22states%22:%7B%22georgia%22:%7B%7D%7D%7D&sortModel=%7B%22colId%22:%22Location%22,%22sort%22:%22asc%22%7D) were enrolled in Affordable Care Act Marketplace health insurance plans.
-   Jones [opposed Medicaid expansion](https://soundcloud.com/abpac/burt-jones-shelley-winter-show-7825/s-fCfqjO9xyOv?si=84116e2a0fb840d2acc00111a347c5a2&utm_source=clipboard&utm_medium=text&utm_campaign=social_sharing) and touted similar work restrictions in [Georgia’s Pathways Program](https://www.npr.org/sections/shots-health-news/2025/07/28/nx-s1-5466338/georgia-work-requirements-medicaid-pathways), which cost an additional [$54.2 million](https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-25-108160) to implement. By 2034, more than [93,400 Medicaid recipients](https://www.jec.senate.gov/public/_cache/files/9cfdee90-dd29-41e8-97da-b5dcdc0317cf/updated-jec-fact-sheet-on-district-level-impacts-of-health-care-cuts.pdf) in Georgia were expected to lose coverage due to Trump’s tax bill. 

**Message:** Burt Jones supported stripping away health care from hundreds of thousands of Georgians to fund tax cuts for the wealthy.

**Message:** Burt Jones supported Trump’s tariffs and ensured Georgia families will pay more for groceries.

### Burt Jones Supported Stripping SNAP Benefits From Georgia Families

### Burt Jones Supported The War In Iran Even As The War Led To Increased Costs In Georgia
- Burt Jones [repeatedly](https://x.com/burtjonesforga/status/2039703893915885978) [supported](https://x.com/burtjonesforga/status/2027868815669727384?s=20) [Trump’s war in Iran.](https://x.com/burtjonesforga/status/2027742422369779926?s=20) Jones even claimed he was [“confident”](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_5nvoEynEPc) in Trump’s claim that prices would magically come down soon and argued Trump “knows the situation in Iran the best.”

- Trump's war of choice with Iran [drove up](https://www.ajc.com/business/2026/05/metro-atlanta-gas-prices-are-up-50-since-start-of-iran-war/) [Georgians'](https://gasprices.aaa.com/?state=GA#state-metro) [gas prices](https://www.walb.com/2026/04/08/experts-warn-war-with-iran-could-raise-costs-georgia-fuel-prices-leading-way/) and [squeezed](https://www.cbsnews.com/atlanta/news/georgia-farmers-delivery-drivers-diesel-gas-prices-iran-war-impact/) [Georgia farmers.](https://www.13wmaz.com/article/news/local/georgia-farmers-brace-for-price-spike-as-iran-war-rattles-fertilizer-markets/93-9f41de8a-5449-403b-8edc-f38512da9b75) [Fertilizer](https://www.walb.com/2026/04/08/experts-warn-war-with-iran-could-raise-costs-georgia-fuel-prices-leading-way/) costs soared and Georgians braced for higher utility bills as the war prolonged. The Iran war [disrupted](https://www.walb.com/2026/04/08/experts-warn-war-with-iran-could-raise-costs-georgia-fuel-prices-leading-way/) shipping at Georgia ports and slammed Georgia exporters with uncertainty and increased insurance costs. The war also [led](https://www.walb.com/2026/04/08/experts-warn-war-with-iran-could-raise-costs-georgia-fuel-prices-leading-way/) to Georgia airlines raising their baggage fees and experts warned of reverberating price shocks from increased jet fuel prices.

**Message:** Burt Jones backed Trump’s war with Iran even as it drove up gas and fertilizer prices across Georgia and squeezed Georgia farmers and families.

### Burt Jones Used His Power And Wealth To Benefit Himself And His Family

-   Jones had a record of ethics controversies, including using his wealth to bolster his own campaign, and using his power as lieutenant governor to financially benefit his family’s business. 
-   In 2023 and 2024, as Georgia lieutenant governor, Jones was [“one of the most powerful supporters”](https://www.ajc.com/politics/burt-jones-family-could-benefit-from-new-hospital-overhaul/V6FQ67MLBNAHJNL7YPDOZ6SEBQ/) of legislation that would have benefited his father’s $10 billion project. Jones’ father, the [president of Interstate Health Systems](https://www.ajc.com/politics/burt-jones-family-could-benefit-from-new-hospital-overhaul/V6FQ67MLBNAHJNL7YPDOZ6SEBQ/), had purchased 250 acres of land and planned to build a 450,000-square-foot hospital as part of the project. Jones supported legislation twice that [rolled back restrictions on new hospital construction](https://www.ajc.com/politics/burt-jones-family-could-benefit-from-new-hospital-overhaul/V6FQ67MLBNAHJNL7YPDOZ6SEBQ/), which would have allowed his father’s project to move forward more quickly. 
-   During his 2022 lieutenant governor campaign, Jones [failed to disclose](https://www.ajc.com/politics/lg-candidate-jones-didnt-report-private-campaign-flights-in-disclosures/WRXXGMJZHNCTDG2ZLHXBKYJGIE/) his use of his family’s private jet for at least eight one-way flights that corresponded with campaign events he attended. Also during this campaign, Jones [misused funds](https://apnews.com/article/2022-midterm-elections-georgia-campaigns-government-and-politics-campaign-contributions-5bab8a2810c1ba96fbb9b31cd2040def) from his state senate campaign and was required to pay a $1,000 fine and reimburse his senate campaign $8,000.
-   In his 2025 gubernatorial campaign, Jones [loaned himself $10 million](https://apnews.com/article/burt-jones-republican-georgia-lieutenant-governor-2026-62ac32083e0792cd55a5014e4a5e8bc8) to his campaign committee. One of his primary opponents, Chris Carr, [filed a complaint](https://apnews.com/article/georgia-governor-2026-carr-burt-jones-lawsuit-19086a4662858f519b1ed3b0af963a71) calling for an investigation into the source of those funds, as [Jones’ 2022 financial disclosure](https://georgiarecorder.com/2025/12/04/carrs-challenge-to-jones-10m-campaign-loan-dismissed-by-ethics-panel/) showed he had only $700,000 in cash and securities. Carr’s complaint was ultimately dismissed after it was determined that Jones’ loan did not qualify as [“an illegal or excessive contribution.”](https://georgiarecorder.com/2025/12/04/carrs-challenge-to-jones-10m-campaign-loan-dismissed-by-ethics-panel/)

**Message:** Burt Jones had a record of ethics controversies, including using his wealth to enrich his own campaign, and using his power as Georgia lieutenant governor to increase his family’s wealth.
