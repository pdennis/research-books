Overview

Rick Jackson faced scrutiny for his companies’ multi–million-dollar payments to settle claims they exploited workers and breached labor laws despite Jackson’s campaign promise to create jobs and bring “business solutions to Georgia just like President Trump.”

In 2022, Jackson Nurse Professionals agreed to a nearly $2 million payout after being accused of violating California’s Unfair Competition Law and the Fair Labor Standards Act. In a federal suit filed in 2017, nursing employees accused Jackson Nurse Professionals of failing to pay overtime wages and wages owed at the time of employment termination. The company allegedly also unlawfully collected wages from employees who did not work the minimum hours required under their contracts.

Jackson Nurse Professionals Allegedly Failed To Pay Overtime Wages And All Wages Owed At The Time Of Employment Termination, And Collected Wages From Employees Who Did Not Work The Minimum Hours Required Under Their Contracts

Nursing Staff Alleged Jackson Nurse Professionals Violated California’s Unfair Competition Law And Violated The Fair Labor Standards Act. According to the Order Re: Final Approval of Class Action Settlement in E. Howard Musgrove v. Jackson Nurse Professionals, LLC et al, "E. Howard Musgrove (‘plaintiff’ or ‘Musgrove’) filed this action, on behalf of himself and others similarly situated, against Jackson Nurse Professionals, LLC (‘Jackson’ or ‘defendant’) on September 6, 2017.1 (See Dkt. 1, Complaint). On December 11, 2017, plaintiff filed the operative First Amended Complaint (Dkt. 27, ‘FAC’), asserting claims for: (1) failure to pay overtime wages, Cal. Lab. Code §§ 510, 1194; (2) violations of California’s Unfair Competition Law (‘UCL’), Cal. Bus. & Prof. Code §§ 17200, et seq.; (3) waiting time penalties, Cal. Lab. Code § 203; (4) violations of the Fair Labor Standards Act (‘FLSA’), 29 U.S.C. §§ 201, et seq." [Order Re: Final Approval Of Class Action Settlement, E. Howard Musgrove v. Jackson Nurse Professionals, LLC et al, 2:17-cv-06565-FMO-SS, U.S. District Court Central District Of California, 6/24/22]

Nursing Staff Alleged Jackson Nurse Professionals Failed To Pay Overtime Wages And Failed To Include Per Diem Payments And Bonuses In The Regular Rate Of Pay When Calculating Overtime Wages. According to the Order Re: Final Approval of Class Action Settlement in E. Howard Musgrove v. Jackson Nurse Professionals, LLC et al, "E. Howard Musgrove (‘plaintiff’ or ‘Musgrove’) filed this action, on behalf of himself and others similarly situated, against Jackson Nurse Professionals, LLC (‘Jackson’ or ‘defendant’) on September 6, 2017.1 (See Dkt. 1, Complaint). On December 11, 2017, plaintiff filed the operative First Amended Complaint (Dkt. 27, ‘FAC’), asserting claims for: (1) failure to pay overtime wages, Cal. Lab. Code §§ 510, 1194; [...] based on Jackson’s failure to include per diem payments and bonuses in the regular rate of pay when calculating overtime wages. (See Dkt. 27, FAC at ¶¶ 16-18, 33-63)" [Order Re: Final Approval Of Class Action Settlement, E. Howard Musgrove v. Jackson Nurse Professionals, LLC et al, 2:17-cv-06565-FMO-SS, U.S. District Court Central District Of California, 6/24/22]

Nursing Staff Alleged Jackson Nurse Professionals Violated Labor Code And Failed To Pay All Wages Owed At The Time Of Employment Termination. According to the First Amended Complaint in E. Howard Musgrove v. Jackson Nurse Professionals, LLC et al, "Third Cause of Action 14. That the Court declare, adjudge and decree that Defendants violated Labor Code sections 201, 202 and 203 by willfully failing to pay all wages owed at the time of termination of employment of Plaintiff and other class members who are former employees of Defendants; 15. For statutory wage penalties pursuant to California Labor Code section 203 for Plaintiff and all other class members who are former employees of Defendants as of September 6, 2014; and 16. For such other and further relief as the Court may deem equitable and appropriate." [First Amended Complaint, E. Howard Musgrove v. Jackson Nurse Professionals, LLC et al, 2:17-cv-06565-FMO-SS, U.S. District Court Central District Of California, 12/17/17]

Nursing Staff Alleged Jackson Nurse Professionals Failed To Pay Correct Premium Overtime Rates And Unlawfully Collected Wages From Employees Who Did Not Work The Minimum Hours Required Under Their Contracts. According to the First Amended Complaint in E. Howard Musgrove v. Jackson Nurse Professionals, LLC et al, "Second Cause of Action 10. That the Court declare, adjudge and decree that Defendants violated Business & Professions Code section 17200, et. seq. by (1) failing to pay Plaintiff and other class members the correct premium overtime rates for all hours worked in excess of 8 hours per day and/or 40 hours per week in violation of Labor Code section 510, and (2) collecting wages from Plaintiff and other class members when they did not work the minimum number of hours required by their Assignment Contracts in violation of Labor Code section 221. 11. For an accounting for and restitution of the wages unlawfully withheld and retained by Defendants during the class period; 12. Fort reasonable attorneys’ fees and costs of suit incurred herein pursuant to Code of Civil Procedure section 1021.5; and 13. For such other and further relief as the Court may deem equitable and appropriate." [First Amended Complaint, E. Howard Musgrove v. Jackson Nurse Professionals, LLC et al, 2:17-cv-06565-FMO-SS, U.S. District Court Central District Of California, 12/17/17]
