Overview

In an unearthed audio from Jackson's Columbus rally in August 2026, Jackson told an attendee he feared Democrats would “cheat” during the November election and said Governor Kemp would deploy the National Guard to “watch” the election he was trying to win. Jackson's comments came amid concerns Trump could use the military to interfere with elections across the country.

Rick Jackson repeatedly pushed election denialism and undermined the results of the 2020 election. He called the 2020 election “totally ridiculous” and “terrible,” and attacked Georgia Secretary of State Brad Raffensperger as “Judas” for not overturning the 2020 election. Jackson also accused Fulton County of “cheating on voting” for the last 40 years and trying to “keep the truth in dark” after a federal subpoena sought personal data of election workers and volunteers during the 2020 election.
