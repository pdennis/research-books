# Rep. Andy Ogles: Dismissed Gun Violence After The Covenant School Shooting

### Ogles Dismissed Gun Violence In His District After Six People Were Killed At The Covenant School

**Ogles Posted A Christmas Card Of His Family Holding AR-15s, Then Called Gun Control "Ridiculous" After A Mass Shooting In His District.** According to NBC News, a 2021 photo showed Ogles and his family posing with AR-style rifles in front of their Christmas tree. On March 27, 2023, a shooter killed six people — including three nine-year-old children — at The Covenant School in Nashville, within Ogles's district. Days later, Ogles dismissed gun control, stating, "The idea that guns are the problem here is ridiculous." [NBC News, [3/28/23](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/politics-news/rep-andy-ogles-whose-district-includes-nashville-criticized-gun-filled-rcna76961); Mediaite, [3/29/23](https://www.mediaite.com/crime/the-idea-that-guns-are-the-problem-here-is-ridiculous-congressman-representing-nashville-school-in-mass-shooting-scoffs-at-gun-control/)]

- **Ogles Defended The Photo As A "Public Service Announcement" On Proper Gun Handling.** According to CBS News, when asked about regretting the photo, Ogles responded, "Why would I regret a photograph with my family exercising my rights to bear arms?" and characterized it as "a public service announcement on how to properly hold and display a weapon." [CBS News, [3/28/23](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/tennessee-andy-ogles-responds-critics-christmas-card-family-guns/)]
