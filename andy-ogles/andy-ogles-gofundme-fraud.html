Two days after the stillborn death of his son in February 2014, Andy Ogles created a GoFundMe called "Lincoln's Place" using a photo of the dead baby to solicit donations for a children's burial garden that would include 20 burial plots for needy families, a life-size statue of Jesus, and benches surrounded by flowers. Donors contributed nearly $25,000, which GoFundMe confirmed Ogles received. The garden was never built. No statue was erected. No benches were placed. No burial plots were purchased — even though the cemetery where Ogles's own child is buried sold plots for $1,200 each, meaning Ogles had enough money to buy 19 of the 20 plots he promised. Both the cemetery manager and a funeral director say they offered to work with Ogles but he never came back. In 2015, Ogles blamed "government bureaucracy," but there is no evidence any regulation would have prevented the purchase of cemetery plots for charitable purposes. When NewsChannel 5 confronted Ogles in 2023, he refused to answer questions, got into a truck, and slammed the door. He later issued a statement claiming the money "evolved" into direct support for families — but provided no receipts, no names, and no documentation of any kind. The parallels to disgraced Rep. George Santos, who ran a GoFundMe for a disabled veteran's dying service dog and then kept the money, were noted by multiple outlets.

Ogles Used A Photo Of His Stillborn Child To Raise Nearly $25,000 For A Garden That Was Never Built

Ogles Created A GoFundMe Two Days After His Child's Death, Promising A Burial Garden With A "Life-Size Statue Of Jesus" And Benches For Grieving Families. According to NewsChannel 5, "two days after the loss of the child in 2014, Ogles himself used a photo of his stillborn baby for a GoFundMe for something he called 'Lincoln's Place.'" The GoFundMe implored, "Help us help other families. No family should have to bury their child; no child should be alone." It promised a "burial garden" that would "create a place for Lincoln's new play friends," "a life-size statue of Jesus watching over the children," and "benches for families to sit while surrounded by flowers." [NewsChannel 5, 3/15/23]

Donors Gave Nearly $25,000 — And GoFundMe Confirmed Ogles Received The Money. According to NewsChannel 5, "people responded to Ogles' emotional plea, donating almost $25,000. GoFundMe confirms Ogles got the money." [NewsChannel 5, 3/15/23]

There Is No Burial Garden, No Benches, And No Statue Of Jesus At The Cemetery Where Ogles's Child Is Buried. According to NewsChannel 5, "in the cemetery where Lincoln is buried, we checked from one corner to another. And just like the tipsters had feared, there is no children's burial garden, no benches where families can sit, and no life-size statue of Jesus." [NewsChannel 5, 3/15/23]

Ogles Had Enough Money To Buy 19 Of The 20 Burial Plots He Promised — But Never Came Back

Cemetery Plots Cost $1,200 Each, Meaning Ogles Had Nearly Enough To Fulfill His Stated Goal Of 20 Plots. According to NewsChannel 5, Ogles told donors on Facebook "that he wanted to raise enough money to purchase 20 burial sites that could be donated to families who lose their children." The cemetery where Ogles's child is buried sold plots for $1,200 each in 2014, "meaning Ogles had enough money to buy 19 plots, almost enough for 20." [NewsChannel 5, 3/16/23]

  • The Cemetery Manager Said He Offered To Work With Ogles But He Never Returned. According to NewsChannel 5, cemetery manager William Puckett "said he offered to work with the family to make their dream come true. 'We could have started out with what was reasonable and worked up from there,' Puckett told NewsChannel 5 Investigates. However, he said, Andy Ogles never returned to work out the plan." [NewsChannel 5, 3/16/23]

  • A Funeral Director Who Planned To Help Ogles Said He Never Followed Through. According to NewsChannel 5, funeral director Pam Stephens of Williamson Memorial Funeral Home stated, "there is absolutely no cemetery, given space available, that would not have worked with them. Back at that time, we could have helped them do something, but they never came back to us." [NewsChannel 5, 3/16/23]

Ogles's Excuses Shifted Over Time And He Never Provided Any Documentation

In 2015, Ogles Blamed "Government Bureaucracy" For Not Building The Garden — But There Was No Evidence Any Regulation Prevented Him. According to NewsChannel 5, Ogles told The Tennessean in 2015, "What we found over the past year as we have been going through this process is that burials are heavily regulated." He insisted at the time that none of the money had been spent. However, NewsChannel 5 found "there is no evidence that any government regulation would have prevented the purchase of several cemetery plots for burying children." [NewsChannel 5, 3/15/23]

When Confronted In 2023, Ogles Refused To Answer Questions And Fled To His Truck. According to NewsChannel 5, when reporter Phil Williams asked, "What did you do with the money that people donated for Lincoln's Place?" Ogles ignored the question. Williams pressed, "Congressman, this doesn't have to be a story if you just offer some evidence it went for a good cause." Ogles "drove away without answering any of our questions." [NewsChannel 5, 3/15/23]

  • Ogles Later Changed His Story, Claiming The Money "Evolved" Into Direct Financial Support For Families — But Offered No Proof. According to NewsChannel 5, Ogles issued a statement claiming "the purpose evolved from a memorial to direct financial support for families covering the cost of funeral expenses and other needs for their children as opportunities to help arose." Ogles did not provide any receipts, names, or documentation. [NewsChannel 5, 3/16/23]

  • Ogles Later Claimed $25,000 "Wasn't Enough" — But He Could Have Bought 19 Of 20 Promised Plots At $1,200 Each. According to NewsChannel 5, Ogles stated in his response that "what we raised wasn't enough for our original goal." However, at $1,200 per plot, $25,000 would have covered nearly all 20 burial sites Ogles had publicly promised to purchase. [NewsChannel 5, 3/16/23]

Donors Demanded Answers And At Least One Had To Demand A Refund

A Donor Said She Had "Gnawing Questions" About Where The Money Went And Demanded Ogles "Tell Us Where The Money Went." According to NewsChannel 5, one donor said she wanted "to believe that that money went for something good" but Ogles's other challenges with the truth left her with "gnawing questions about what happened to these funds." When asked what her message to Ogles would be, she stated, "Tell us what you did! Tell us where the money went!" [NewsChannel 5, 3/15/23]

  • One Donor Who Demanded His Money Back Received A Refund — But Others Were Not Given That Option. According to NewsChannel 5, "one donor, who said he asked Ogles directly what he did with the money. When he couldn't get a straight answer, he insisted on getting his money back and he got it. Other donors, however, say they were never given that option." [NewsChannel 5, 3/15/23]

Multiple Outlets Drew Direct Comparisons To Disgraced Rep. George Santos

The New Republic Said Ogles "Appears To Have Kept Money Raised Through A Charitable GoFundMe Event," Making Him The Second Freshman Republican In Hot Water Over Dubious Fundraising. According to The New Republic, "Tennessee Representative Andy Ogles appears to have kept money raised through a charitable GoFundMe event, making him the second freshman Republican member of Congress in hot water over dubious fundraising efforts." The article noted Santos was accused of swindling $3,000 meant to help save a disabled veteran's dying service dog. [New Republic, 3/15/23]

Salon Reported Donors Were Demanding To Know "Where The Money Went" And Drew Direct Parallels To Santos. According to Salon, "Donors raised questions about what happened to the money they donated to Rep. Andy Ogles, R-Tenn., intended for a child burial garden." Ogles "drew comparisons to disgraced Rep. George Santos, R-N.Y., after he was caught inflating his resume." [Salon, 3/16/23]

Ogles Attacked The Reporter Instead Of Providing Evidence

Ogles Accused NewsChannel 5 Of "Exploiting The Death" Of His Child — But The Reporter Noted All Donors Contacted The Station First. According to NewsChannel 5, Ogles issued a statement saying, "I knew politics could be ruthless, but I never expected it to sink to such disgusting lows as to have a reporter calling around to my close friends asking about the death and burial site of our beloved son, Lincoln." NewsChannel 5 responded: "That claim is false. All the donors with whom NewsChannel 5 spoke reached out to us, we didn't call them." [NewsChannel 5, 3/16/23]
