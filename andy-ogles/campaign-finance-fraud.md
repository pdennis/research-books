# Rep. Andy Ogles: Fabricated A $320,000 Campaign Loan And Faces An FBI Investigation

### Ogles Fabricated A $320,000 Campaign Loan And Now Faces An FBI Investigation

**Ogles Reported A $320,000 Personal Loan To His Campaign That He Later Admitted Was Only $20,000.** According to NewsChannel 5, Ogles's campaign filed a July 2022 Quarterly Report stating it had received a $320,000 loan from Ogles on April 15, 2022. In a press release, Ogles boasted of raising $450,000 including the six-figure personal infusion. On May 15, 2024, Ogles admitted the actual amount was only $20,000, stating, "While we only needed to transfer $20,000, unfortunately, the full amount of my pledge was mistakenly included on my campaign's FEC reports." [Axios, [5/23/24](https://www.axios.com/local/nashville/2024/05/23/congress-tennessee-ogles-amends-campaign-finance-reports)]

**The Office Of Congressional Ethics Voted 6-0 To Recommend A Full Investigation, Finding "Substantial Reason" Ogles Violated Federal Law.** According to Tennessee Lookout, the OCE board voted unanimously to call for a closer look at Ogles's finances, finding "there is substantial reason to believe that Rep. Ogles omitted or misrepresented required information in his finance disclosure statements or FEC candidate committee reports." The board also found Ogles "apparently exercised control over his campaign finances, to the exclusion of his campaign treasurer and manager." [Tennessee Lookout, [1/2/25](https://tennesseelookout.com/2025/01/02/u-s-house-ethics-board-calls-for-more-investigation-of-tennessee-5th-district-congressman/)]

**The FBI Seized Ogles's Cell Phone The Day After His 2024 Primary Victory As Part Of A Fraud Investigation.** According to The Washington Post, FBI agents executed a search warrant on Ogles in August 2024, seizing his personal cell phone as part of a criminal investigation into campaign finance fraud. A month earlier, agents had also seized his personal email account. [Washington Post, [8/6/24](https://www.washingtonpost.com/national-security/2024/08/06/andy-ogles-fbi-phone-search-campaign-finance/)]

- **Ogles Stalled The FBI Investigation For Over 14 Months By Claiming Congressional Privilege.** According to the Nashville Banner, FBI agents were blocked from examining the seized evidence for more than 14 months while Ogles's legal team argued agents should not be able to access any material relating to his congressional business. The federal magistrate handling the dispute resigned without ruling, leaving the case for another judge. [Nashville Banner, [10/24/25](https://nashvillebanner.com/2025/10/24/any-ogles-fbi-phone-warrant-ruling/)]

- **Career Federal Prosecutors Withdrew From The Investigation After Trump Took Office.** According to NewsChannel 5, in January 2025, acting U.S. Attorney Rob McGuire filed a notice withdrawing two assistant U.S. attorneys from the case. The investigation was transferred to the Department of Justice in Washington, D.C. [NewsChannel 5, [1/25](https://www.newschannel5.com/news/newschannel-5-investigates/career-prosecutors-withdraw-from-federal-criminal-investigation-of-gop-congressman-andy-ogles)]

**The Campaign Legal Center Filed A Formal FEC Complaint Alleging Ogles "Deliberately Filed False Reports To Conceal Fake Loan."** According to the Campaign Legal Center, the complaint alleged Ogles "deliberately filed false reports disclosing a fabricated $320,000 loan" during his 2022 primary, and that the discrepancy "artificially inflated campaign cash-on-hand figures during a competitive primary race." [Campaign Legal Center, [4/23/25](https://campaignlegal.org/document/clc-alleges-andy-ogles-deliberately-filed-false-reports-conceal-fake-loan)]

- **Ogles's Financial Disclosures Contained Over $1 Million In Discrepancies.** According to the Campaign Legal Center, Ogles's reports appeared to include over $1 million in financial discrepancies, including the $320,000 reported personal loan and a previously undisclosed $700,000 line of credit from FirstBank. [Campaign Legal Center, [1/10/24](https://campaignlegal.org/update/rep-ogles-1-million-financial-discrepancies)]
