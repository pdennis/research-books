# Rep. Andy Ogles: Pushed A Trump Third Term And Attacked The Courts

### Ogles Proposed A Trump Third Term And Impeached Judges Who Ruled Against The Administration

**Ogles Introduced A Constitutional Amendment To Allow Trump To Serve A Third Term.** According to Newsweek, Ogles filed a resolution on January 23, 2025, to amend the 22nd Amendment, arguing Trump "has proven himself to be the only figure in modern history capable of reversing our nation's decay." When asked if he was "willing to break the Constitution," Ogles acknowledged the challenge but said it forced a necessary conversation. [Newsweek, [2/3/25](https://www.newsweek.com/trump-third-term-constitutional-amendment-andy-ogles-2025020)]

**Ogles Called Judges Who Blocked Tariffs "Woke Activist Judges" And Said He Wanted Trump To Have A Third Term "To Reset These Courts."** According to Newsweek, Ogles stated on Fox Business Network, "I'd love for Trump to have a third term so we can reset these courts and actually have folks who follow the law and the Constitution versus their ideological positions." He was responding to a unanimous Court of International Trade ruling that blocked Trump's global tariffs. [Newsweek, [5/29/25](https://www.newsweek.com/andy-ogles-trump-third-term-reset-courts-tariffs-2078645)]

**Ogles Introduced Articles Of Impeachment Against Two Federal Judges Who Ruled Against The Trump Administration.** According to Wikipedia, on February 24, 2025, Ogles introduced an article of impeachment against Judge John D. Bates after he ruled against the administration, and a month later filed an article of impeachment against Judge Theodore Chuang. [Wikipedia, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andy_Ogles)]
