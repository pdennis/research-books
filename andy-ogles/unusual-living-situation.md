# Rep. Andy Ogles Lives In A $3.7 Million D.C. Townhouse Owned By His Top Donor

Nashville car magnate Lee Beaman has played an extraordinary number of simultaneous roles in Rep. Andy Ogles's political career: top individual donor, campaign treasurer (on FEC filings Beaman now disputes signing), sole funder of a super PAC that backed Ogles's 2022 primary, owner of the $3.7 million Capitol Hill townhouse where Ogles lives alongside House Speaker Mike Johnson and an evangelical pastor running an unregistered influence operation, and — as of 2025 — a Trump nominee for the TVA board of directors whose Senate confirmation has been delayed by questions about his entanglement with Ogles's campaign finance scandal. No single donor-to-officeholder relationship in Congress combines this many overlapping financial, residential, and political dependencies. The arrangement raises serious questions about whether Ogles is receiving an unreported gift in the form of below-market rent, whether Beaman's dual role as treasurer and super PAC funder violated federal coordination rules, and whether Ogles's legislative agenda — which aligns with Beaman's public policy advocacy on fuel efficiency standards, federal employee protections, and election certification — is being shaped from inside the house where he sleeps.

### Beaman Funded Ogles's Career From The Beginning

**Beaman Pledged To Raise $4 Million For Ogles Before He Even Ran For Congress.** According to the Nashville Post, "Lee Beaman, the owner of Beaman Automotive group and a regular conservative donor, has announced he plans to raise at least $4 million in a federal super PAC to support Andy Ogles' run for Senate." Beaman stated, "Having worked with Andy Ogles during his service as State Director of Americans for Prosperity, I know first-hand his tireless commitment to conservative issues." The Senate bid did not materialize, but Beaman continued backing Ogles when he ran for the House in 2022. [Nashville Post, [4/17](https://www.nashvillepost.com/beaman-raising-4m-for-ogles/article_f492bf30-149d-580e-a7b0-b3a7fb43662c.html)]

**Beaman Served As Ogles's Campaign Treasurer On FEC Filings During The Period Of The Fabricated $320,000 Loan.** According to an FEC audit referral, Lee Beaman was listed as treasurer of Andy Ogles for Congress from April 6, 2022 through August 24, 2022 — the period during which Ogles reported a $320,000 personal loan that he later admitted was only $20,000. [FEC Reports Analysis Division, [3/27/23](https://www.fec.gov/files/legal/adrs/1112/1112_01.pdf)]

- **Beaman Disputed Under Oath That He Ever Served As Treasurer Or Approved The Reports.** According to NewsChannel 5, three separate financial disclosures filed with the FEC during Beaman's tenure were reportedly signed by Beaman attesting to their accuracy. However, Beaman stated in written responses to the Senate Committee on Environment and Public Works, "Mr. Ogles asked me in a text message to serve as his campaign treasurer, a position I thought was an honorary position. When I did not respond either way, he assumed I agreed to serve. A couple of months later, I saw my name listed as treasurer in a political advertisement. At that point, I told him I could not serve." Beaman stated that he was certain he "never signed or compiled any reports, and I had nothing to do with any campaign bank accounts." [NewsChannel 5, [12/16/25](https://www.newschannel5.com/news/newschannel-5-investigates/longtime-political-supporter-of-congressman-ogles-disputes-claims-made-in-his-campaign-reports)]

**Beaman Was The Sole Donor Of $50,000 To A Super PAC That Spent $24,000 On Ads Backing Ogles In The 2022 Primary.** According to the Tennessee Lookout, "Conservative Nashville car magnate Lee Beaman serves as treasurer to Maury County Mayor Andy Ogles' congressional campaign, but he was also the sole donor of $50,000 to Volunteers for Freedom PAC, which put $24,000 to an ad buy backing Ogles. That necessarily means Beaman coordinated with the PAC, which is shady at best and illegal at worst." [Tennessee Lookout, [8/19/22](https://tennesseelookout.com/2022/08/19/stockard-on-the-stump-beamans-past-adds-questions-to-ogles-campaign)]

**Beaman Has Maxed Out Personal Donations To Ogles Across Multiple Election Cycles.** According to FEC records, Beaman gave $5,800 in the 2022 cycle and $6,600 in the 2024 cycle (plus $6,250 and additional $3,300 contributions), making him one of Ogles's largest individual donors over his congressional career. [FEC, [Viewed 2/18/26](https://www.fec.gov/data/receipts/?committee_id=C00811844&contributor_name=beaman)]

### Ogles Lives In Beaman's D.C. Townhouse Alongside Speaker Johnson And An Unregistered Influence Operation

**Ogles Was Observed Living In Beaman's $3.7 Million Capitol Hill Townhouse, Which Doubles As Headquarters For A Secretive Political Influence Project.** According to ProPublica, "Rep. Andy Ogles, a Tennessee Republican who is among President Donald Trump's most aggressive allies in Congress, has been at the house on multiple days over the past two weeks, according to people who live in the area. Video reviewed by ProPublica showed Ogles leaving the townhouse with bags on Feb. 27. As he left, he locked up the front door and pocketed the keys to the house." The six-bedroom home is also where House Speaker Mike Johnson has been staying. [ProPublica, [3/6/25](https://www.propublica.org/article/roommates-steve-berger-mike-johnson-andy-ogles-right-wing-influence-townhouse)]

- **The Townhouse Is The Base Of Operations For Pastor Steve Berger's "Ministry Center" That Claims To Translate Biblical Teaching Into Policy.** According to ProPublica, Berger "has said his goal is to 'disciple' members of Congress so what 'they learn is then translated into policy.' He has claimed to have personally spurred legislation, saying a senator privately credited him with inspiring a bill." Berger's nonprofit, Ambassador Services International, runs on a budget of approximately $1 million per year and files no lobbying reports. [ProPublica, [2/28/25](https://www.propublica.org/article/mike-johnson-evangelical-pastor-steve-berger-roommates)]

- **Beaman And Berger Publicly Advocated Together On Specific Policy Changes, Including Reducing Fuel Efficiency Standards For The Auto Industry.** According to ProPublica, "He and the donor who owns the house, Lee Beaman, have publicly advocated together for numerous specific policy changes, including a bill that would make it easier to fire federal employees and a regulation that would reduce fuel efficiency standards for the automotive industry. After the 2020 election, they both signed a letter declaring that President Donald Trump was the rightful winner and calling for Congress to overturn the results." [ProPublica, [2/28/25](https://www.propublica.org/article/mike-johnson-evangelical-pastor-steve-berger-roommates)]

- **House Ethics Rules Prohibit Discounted Rent As An Improper Gift, But Ogles's Rental Terms Have Not Been Disclosed.** According to ProPublica, "Members of Congress are allowed to live anywhere, as long as they pay fair-market rent, experts said. Discounts on rent are generally seen as improper gifts and prohibited by House ethics rules." A Johnson spokesperson said the speaker "pays fair market value in monthly rent," but Ogles did not respond to requests for comment about his arrangement. Two-bedrooms in the neighborhood rent for as much as $7,000 per month. [ProPublica, [3/6/25](https://www.propublica.org/article/roommates-steve-berger-mike-johnson-andy-ogles-right-wing-influence-townhouse)]

### Ogles's Legislative Agenda Aligned With Beaman's Business Interests And Policy Advocacy

**Ogles Co-Sponsored A Bill To Repeal The Fuel Economy Standards That Regulate Beaman's Car Dealership Industry.** According to Congress.gov, Ogles was one of four co-sponsors of HR 2779 (118th Congress), introduced April 20, 2023, "to repeal the corporate average fuel economy standards." CAFE standards require automobile manufacturers to meet fuel efficiency targets — a direct regulatory concern for Beaman, who owned nine car dealerships operating under 13 different franchises and brands. ProPublica had reported that Beaman and Berger "publicly advocated together for [...] a regulation that would reduce fuel efficiency standards for the automotive industry." [Congress.gov, [Viewed 2/18/26](https://www.congress.gov/bill/118th-congress/house-bill/2779); ProPublica, [2/28/25](https://www.propublica.org/article/mike-johnson-evangelical-pastor-steve-berger-roommates)]

- **Ogles Also Offered An Amendment To Defund Enforcement Of Biden's Proposed CAFE Rule.** According to the Competitive Enterprise Institute, "The House is expected to consider an amendment offered by Rep. Andy Ogles (R-TN) to the Transportation and HUD spending bill (H.R. 4820) that would prohibit funding for the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration's proposed rule 'Corporate Average Fuel Economy Standards for Passenger Cars and Light Trucks for Model Years 2027-2032 and Fuel Efficiency Standards for Heavy-Duty Pickup Trucks and Vans for Model Years 2030-2035.'" [Competitive Enterprise Institute, [11/7/23](https://cei.org/news_releases/amendment-prohibiting-funding-for-biden-administration-fuel-economy-regulations-would-benefit-consumers/)]

- **Ogles Introduced A Third Bill Targeting EV Policy, The "Free Market Drives Itself Act," To Eliminate The Biden Administration's Electric Vehicle Working Group.** According to Congress.gov, Ogles introduced HR 8737 on June 13, 2024, the "Free Market Drives Itself Act." According to the Washington Examiner, the bill aimed "to eliminate the Electric Vehicles Working Group, which makes EV recommendations to President Biden's administration, in an effort to reduce EV spending." In the 119th Congress, Ogles also co-sponsored HR 312, the "Restoring Vehicle Market Freedom Act of 2025," and HR 311, the "Restoring Fuel Market Freedom Act of 2025." [Congress.gov, [Viewed 2/18/26](https://www.congress.gov/bill/118th-congress/house-bill/8737); Washington Examiner, [6/21/24](https://www.washingtonexaminer.com/policy/energy-and-environment/3054631/ogles-bill-curb-ev-spending/)]

**Ogles Introduced The "End The Deep State Act" Twice To Make It Easier To Fire Federal Employees — The Other Policy Change Beaman And Berger Advocated For.** According to Congress.gov, Ogles introduced HR 6558 on December 1, 2023 (118th Congress, 13 cosponsors) and reintroduced it as HR 697 on January 23, 2025 (119th Congress, 8 cosponsors). According to a press release from Ogles's office, the legislation "classifies federal employees who hold policy-centered roles as 'Schedule Policy/Career' employees. This ensures that the President is able to remove federal workers who intentionally undermine the President's policy goals. This legislation also repeals a Biden-introduced rule protecting insubordinate employees from losing civil service status." ProPublica had separately reported that Beaman and Berger advocated for "a bill that would make it easier to fire federal employees." [Congress.gov, [Viewed 2/18/26](https://www.congress.gov/bill/118th-congress/house-bill/6558); Ogles Press Release, [2/4/25](https://ogles.house.gov/media/press-releases/rep-ogles-codify-president-trumps-efforts-end-deep-state); ProPublica, [2/28/25](https://www.propublica.org/article/mike-johnson-evangelical-pastor-steve-berger-roommates)]

- **Ogles Also Introduced Three Bills Imposing Permanent Hiring Freezes At Federal Agencies.** According to Congress.gov, Ogles introduced the MATCH Act (HR 3527), the ZIPPO Act (HR 3528), and the FIRE Act (HR 3529), all on May 18, 2023, targeting the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau, the U.S. Agency for Global Media, and the Bureau of Alcohol, Tobacco, and Firearms, respectively. Ogles stated, "Imposing hiring freezes in agencies that never should have been created to begin with is just the first step." [Congress.gov, [Viewed 2/18/26](https://www.congress.gov/bill/118th-congress/house-bill/3527); Ogles Press Release, [5/19/23](https://ogles.house.gov/media/press-releases/rep-ogles-introduces-3-bills-stop-hiring-federal-bureaucrats)]

### Beaman's TVA Nomination Stalled Over Questions About His Role In Ogles's Campaign Finance Scandal

**A Senate Democrat Called Beaman "Not Just Unqualified, But Unfit" For A Federal Appointment Because Of His Ties To Ogles's Campaign Finance Investigation.** According to the Knoxville News Sentinel, Sen. Sheldon Whitehouse stated, "There is an ongoing investigation into his role in false statements made about campaign contributions, where loans were mischaracterized as donations at a time when he was the treasurer. And there's just a cloud of confusion about 'Was he the treasurer, or was he not the treasurer?' 'Did he know about this, or did he not know about this?'" [Knoxville News Sentinel, [12/10/25](https://www.knoxnews.com/story/news/politics/2025/12/10/senate-committee-defers-vote-on-lee-beaman-trump-nominee-tva-board-of-directors/87689809007/)]

- **The Senate Committee Postponed Beaman's TVA Vote And Trump Had To Renominate Him In January 2026.** According to the Leaf-Chronicle, the Senate Committee on Environment and Public Works deferred the vote on December 10, 2025, and the year ended without a confirmation. Trump renominated Beaman on January 13, 2026. [Leaf-Chronicle, [1/14/26](https://www.theleafchronicle.com/story/news/politics/2026/01/14/trump-renominates-nashville-businessman-lee-beaman-for-tva-board/88176508007/)]

- **Whitehouse Questioned Whether Speaker Johnson — Beaman's Other Tenant — Had Stalled The House Ethics Investigation Into Ogles.** According to NewsChannel 5, Whitehouse stated, "The non-partisan office of Congressional ethics found evidence of wrongdoing but, despite OCE's recommendation to subpoena witnesses, the investigation has been stalled since another of Beaman's tenants, Mike Johnson, became speaker of the House." [NewsChannel 5, [12/16/25](https://www.newschannel5.com/news/newschannel-5-investigates/longtime-political-supporter-of-congressman-ogles-disputes-claims-made-in-his-campaign-reports)]
