# Rep. Andy Ogles (TN-05): Can't Be Trusted on Health Care

Rep. Andy Ogles has spent over a decade fighting to strip health care from the people he represents. Before entering Congress, he led the Americans for Prosperity campaign that killed Insure Tennessee -- Gov. Haslam's plan to expand Medicaid and cover over 300,000 uninsured Tennesseans with $1 billion in federal funding. In Congress, his very first bill sought to repeal the Inflation Reduction Act, which capped insulin costs for seniors at $35 a month and placed a $2,000 annual limit on out-of-pocket drug costs for Medicare recipients. He has openly declared he wants to "be the guy that kills" the Affordable Care Act, which 643,000 Tennesseans relied on for marketplace coverage in 2025. He voted for the "One Big Beautiful Bill," which the Congressional Budget Office projected would leave nearly 12 million Americans uninsured through Medicaid cuts -- and on the morning of that vote, called Medicaid recipients who would lose coverage "losers in their mama's basement." Nashville's mayor estimated 62,000 residents of Ogles's own district would lose medical coverage under the bill. Despite all this activity against health care protections, Ogles has not sponsored a single bill to expand health care access, lower prescription drug costs, or address the uninsured -- his only sponsored health care bills are abortion restrictions. His health care record reveals a consistent pattern: attacking protections his own constituents depend on while offering no replacement.

### Ogles Led The Campaign That Killed Medicaid Expansion In Tennessee

**Ogles Led The Americans For Prosperity Campaign That Killed Insure Tennessee In 2015.** According to NBC News, Andy Ogles served as Americans for Prosperity's Tennessee state director and led a six-week campaign against Gov. Bill Haslam's Insure Tennessee proposal, which would have used over $1 billion in federal Medicaid expansion funding to cover more than 300,000 uninsured Tennesseans. AFP ran radio ads warning conservatives that legislators might "vote for Obamacare," paired photos of Republican legislators with Obama's image on social media, and chartered a bus to bring over 100 AFP activists in red shirts to pack the February 3, 2015 committee hearing. The bill died in committee on February 4, 2015. [NBC News, [2/5/15](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/elections/rebuke-tennessee-governor-koch-group-shows-its-power-n301031)]

- **Ogles Dismissed Insure Tennessee As "Simply Obamacare With A Pretty Wrapper."** According to the American Journal of Managed Care, Ogles stated, "We're opposed to Insure Tennessee. It's simply Obamacare with a pretty wrapper." He also said, "Obama's policies have eliminated jobs across the country, driven down wages, and served as a hindrance to our nation's economy. Today Tennessee took a stand against Obama's failed policies and over-bearing federal mandates." [American Journal of Managed Care, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://www.ajmc.com/view/why-did-tennessees-medicaid-expansion-plan-fail-in-a-word-obama)]

### Ogles's First Bill Targeted Seniors' Drug Cost Protections

**Ogles Introduced His First Bill To Repeal The Inflation Reduction Act, Which Capped Insulin At $35 For Seniors.** According to a press release from Rep. Ogles's office, "Rep. Ogles Introduces First Bill: The Inflation Reduction Act of 2023 to Repeal Democrats' Record Spending." The Inflation Reduction Act of 2022 authorized a $35-per-month cap on insulin copayments for Medicare recipients, as well as an annual $2,000 total limit on out-of-pocket drug costs. Ogles's repeal bill had 20 original co-sponsors and was endorsed by Americans for Prosperity. [Rep. Ogles Press Release, [2/2/23](https://ogles.house.gov/media/press-releases/rep-ogles-introduces-first-bill-inflation-reduction-act-2023-repeal-democrats)]

**Biden Called Out Ogles's Repeal Bill During His 2023 State Of The Union Address.** According to Wikipedia's account of the 2023 State of the Union address, President Biden referenced the bill during his speech without naming Ogles directly. Ogles took credit for being targeted, tweeting, "You know you're doing something right for Americans when @POTUS targets you in his SOTU." [Wikipedia, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_State_of_the_Union_Address)]

### Ogles Vowed To Destroy The Affordable Care Act

**Ogles Declared He Wanted To "Be The Guy That Kills" The Affordable Care Act.** According to WKRN Nashville, Congressman Andy Ogles posted on X, "I will NEVER fight for Obamacare. I want to be the guy that kills it." This statement came as enhanced ACA subsidies were set to expire at the end of 2025, threatening coverage for hundreds of thousands of Tennesseans. [WKRN, [12/25](https://www.wkrn.com/news/tennessee-news/it-puts-your-back-against-the-wall-aca-subsidy-deadline-looms-for-tennessee-families/)]

- **643,000 Tennesseans Enrolled In ACA Marketplace Plans In 2025.** According to the Sycamore Institute, 643,000 Tennesseans enrolled in ACA Marketplace plans in 2025, more than twice the number enrolled in 2020 before Congress enacted the temporary subsidy increase. An estimated 142,000 to 203,000 Tennesseans could forgo insurance coverage as a result of premium increases following the subsidies' expiration. [Sycamore Institute, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://sycamoretn.org/aca-marketplace-subsidies/)]

- **Tennessee ACA Premiums Expected To More Than Double After Subsidy Expiration.** According to the Sycamore Institute, average enrollee premiums could more than double after enhanced subsidies expired, with insurers proposing 2026 increases ranging from 0.3% to 41%. Small business owners, gig workers, rural residents, and older adults earning over 400% of the federal poverty level face the greatest increases. [Sycamore Institute, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://sycamoretn.org/aca-marketplace-subsidies/)]

### Ogles Voted For Massive Medicaid Cuts

**Ogles Voted For The "One Big Beautiful Bill," Which CBO Projected Would Leave Nearly 12 Million Americans Uninsured.** According to Yahoo News, U.S. Rep. Andy Ogles joined Tennessee GOP lawmakers in a "yes" vote on the reconciliation bill on July 3, 2025. The measure passed 218-214. Ogles declared the legislation "made history" and would "unleash our economy," but did not address the bill's health care provisions. The Congressional Budget Office projected the bill would leave nearly 12 million Americans uninsured through Medicaid cuts. [Yahoo News, [7/3/25](https://www.yahoo.com/news/u-rep-ogles-joins-tn-224912125.html)]

**Ogles Called Medicaid Recipients "Losers In Their Mama's Basement" The Morning He Voted To Cut Their Coverage.** According to the DCCC, before voting for the One Big Beautiful Bill, Ogles appeared with personality Benny Johnson and called over 30,000 TN-05 residents at risk of losing Medicaid "losers in their mama's basement." DCCC spokesperson Madison Andrus responded: "Mask off -- Andy Ogles simply hates his constituents." [DCCC, [7/3/25](https://dccc.org/losers-in-their-mamas-basement-this-is-what-andy-ogles-thinks-of-his-constituents-he-just-voted-to-kick-off-medicaid/)]

**Ogles Pushed For Medicaid Work Requirements, Dismissing 15 Million Recipients As Not Working.** According to Just The News, Ogles identified Medicaid as a key sticking point in the reconciliation bill, stating, "Part of the issue, or the sticking point, is Medicaid." He argued that "you have 15 million people on Medicaid that are able-bodied, meaning that they can have a job, and they have no income; they're not working." [Just The News, [5/25](https://justthenews.com/government/congress/rep-andy-ogles-says-medicaid-one-issues-holding-trumps-one-big-beautiful-bill)]

- **Tennessee Has Not Expanded Medicaid, Leaving An Estimated 95,000 Residents In A Coverage Gap.** According to the Center on Budget and Policy Priorities, approximately 95,000 Tennessee residents are in the Medicaid coverage gap -- earning too much to qualify for TennCare but too little to qualify for marketplace subsidies. Tennessee is one of ten states that has not expanded Medicaid under the ACA. [Center on Budget and Policy Priorities, [4/3/24](https://www.cbpp.org/sites/default/files/4-3-24health-factsheet-tn.pdf)]

### Ogles Repeatedly Used Health Care As A Political Weapon

**Ogles Introduced Anti-Mask Mandate Amendments To At Least Six Appropriations Bills.** According to C-SPAN, Ogles introduced amendments to prohibit funding for COVID-19 mask mandates across multiple appropriations bills in 2023 and 2024, arguing that "policies involving mandatory mask implementation are not about safety nor are they about science. Mask mandates are at their core about control." He introduced nearly identical amendments to Energy and Water, Interior, Labor-HHS-Education, and Defense appropriations bills. [C-SPAN, [10/26/23](https://www.c-span.org/video/?634231)]

**Ogles Claimed Masks Were Ineffective On The House Floor While Citing Mischaracterized Research.** According to C-SPAN, Ogles stated on the House floor that "Fauci's acknowledged that he was winging it, that the six-foot margin was made up. We now know the efficacy of masks didn't work." He repeatedly cited epidemiologist Tom Jefferson's Cochrane review, which Jefferson himself cautioned did not prove masks were ineffective -- only that the randomized trial evidence was inconclusive. [C-SPAN, [6/13/24](https://www.c-span.org/video/?643569)]

**Ogles Introduced Legislation Opposing Gender-Affirming Care For Minors.** According to C-SPAN, Ogles stated on the House floor that "the hypersexualized left is not satisfied with its month of sexual pride. Instead they're going after our children and it's coming to a neighborhood near you from drag schools in schools to gender affirming care," while introducing two bills related to children and gender identity. [C-SPAN, [6/20/23](https://www.c-span.org/video/?629118)]

**Ogles Cosponsored Anti-Vaccine Legislation.** According to Congress.gov, Ogles cosponsored the "End the Vaccine Carveout Act" (HR 4668, 119th Congress), sponsored by Rep. Gosar, with 29 cosponsors. He also cosponsored the "No Vaccine Mandates in Higher Education Act" (HR 3044, 119th Congress). [Congress.gov, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://www.congress.gov/member/andrew-ogles/O000175)]

### Ogles Sponsored Zero Bills To Expand Health Care Access

**Ogles's Only Sponsored Health Care Bills Were Abortion Restrictions.** According to Congress.gov, across the 118th and 119th Congresses, Ogles sponsored only three health-related bills: the Ending Chemical Abortions Act of 2023 (HR 5806), the Preventing Forced Abortions Act of 2024 (HR 8985), and the Ending Chemical Abortions Act of 2025 (HR 629). He has not sponsored a single bill to expand health coverage, lower drug costs, address the uninsured, improve rural health care access, or strengthen Medicare or Medicaid. [Congress.gov, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://www.congress.gov/member/andrew-ogles/O000175)]

### Impact On Everyday People

**Nashville Mayor Estimated 62,000 TN-05 Residents Would Lose Medical Coverage Under The Bill Ogles Voted For.** According to the Nashville Banner, Nashville Mayor Freddie O'Connell cited CBO projections showing that 62,000 residents of Tennessee's 5th congressional district would lose medical coverage, 32,000 would lose food assistance, and 12,000 would lose housing assistance under the reconciliation bill Ogles supported. [Nashville Banner, [6/9/25](https://nashvillebanner.com/2025/06/09/ogles-criticism-impact-nashville/)]

**Ogles's Own Constituents Confronted Him Over Medicaid Cuts At A Spring Hill Event.** According to WPLN Nashville, at a September 2025 Americans for Prosperity event in Spring Hill, constituent Teri Mai stated, "The cuts to Medicaid are going to have horrendous effects on all of us who live in the smaller, rural counties. He needs to know that we don't support him, not everybody supports him." Another constituent, Sarah Lowery, said, "He does not respond, he does not hold town halls. He does not answer any concerns his constituents have when they call him." Even Ogles supporter Larry Hardesty expressed concerns: "There are a lot of things unanswered [...]I'm a little concerned about the turmoil that stirred up over that." [WPLN Nashville, [9/7/25](https://wpln.org/post/amidst-a-backdrop-of-protesters-rep-andy-ogles-defends-one-big-beautiful-bill-at-spring-hill-event/)]

**Tennessee Health Care Advocates Warned Of Affordability Crisis For Nashville Families.** According to NewsChannel 5 Nashville, Michele Johnson, Executive Director of the Tennessee Justice Center, stated, "I'm really worried about families and how they'll navigate this. And not just families, I'm worried about our healthcare infrastructure." A 35-year-old man earning $65,000 annually would see his monthly premium jump from $376 to $544 -- a $168 monthly increase -- after subsidies expired. [NewsChannel 5, [11/3/25](https://www.newschannel5.com/news/state/tennessee/davidson-county/tennessee-health-care-advocates-concerns-over-rising-costs-and-expiring-subsidies-for-affordable-care-act-insurance-plans)]

**Seven Rural Tennessee Hospitals Have Closed Since 2010, With More At Risk.** According to Public News Service, seven rural Tennessee hospitals have permanently closed since 2010, with nine more at risk. Judy Roitman, Executive Director of the Tennessee HealthCare Campaign, noted that Kentucky's Medicaid expansion has improved hospital financial stability, while Tennessee has refused to expand: "That's the key to our stability is actually having the funds coming in to treat these patients." At least 330,000 Tennesseans would gain coverage if the state expanded Medicaid. [Public News Service, [3/28/24](https://www.publicnewsservice.org/2024-03-28/health/medicaid-expansion-could-save-tn-rural-hospitals-from-closing/a89561-1)]

**An Estimated 476,000 Additional Tennesseans Would Be Eligible For Coverage If The State Expanded Medicaid.** According to AARP, an estimated 476,000 additional Tennessee residents would be eligible for health coverage -- including 72,000 uninsured people ages 50 to 64 -- if the state opted to expand Medicaid under the Affordable Care Act. Tennessee would have received an additional $1.5 billion in federal funding during 2024 and 2025 if it had expanded. [AARP, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://states.aarp.org/tennessee/improving-access-to-health-care-for-tennesseans)]
