# Rep. Buddy Carter (GA-01): Can't Be Trusted on Jobs and the Economy

Carter voted against every major job-creating law of the past decade — the Bipartisan Infrastructure Law, the CHIPS and Science Act, and the Inflation Reduction Act — then took credit for the jobs and projects those laws delivered to his district. He praised Trump's tariffs as "home-run economic policies" while the Port of Savannah, which supports 609,000 Georgia jobs, saw cargo volumes drop 9.6% and Georgia's $28 billion poultry industry faced retaliatory trade barriers. He voted to gut the IRA clean energy tax credits that attracted the $7.6 billion Hyundai Metaplant to Bryan County — the largest economic development project in Georgia history, expected to create 8,500 jobs — even as two other clean energy factories in his region canceled plans and took nearly 1,000 promised jobs with them. He carries a 9% lifetime score from the AFL-CIO, authored a 23% national sales tax that economists call regressive, and dismissed concerns about cutting nearly $200 billion from SNAP while 1.4 million Georgians rely on food assistance.

### Voted Against Every Major Job-Creating Law — Then Took Credit

**Carter Voted Against The Bipartisan Infrastructure Law, Then Touted Its Benefits At The Georgia Chamber Luncheon.** According to the Democratic Party of Georgia, Carter "tout[ed] the benefits of President Biden's Bipartisan Infrastructure Law after voting against the popular, job-creating measure last year alongside every single Georgia Republican." The $1.2 trillion law funded upgrades to Georgia's ports, roads, broadband, and airports. [Georgia Democrat, [8/9/22](https://www.georgiademocrat.org/buddy-carter-touts-president-bidens-bipartisan-infrastructure-law-after-voting-against-it/)]

- **A Brunswick News Reader Called Out Carter For Taking Credit For Port Funding He Voted Against.** According to a letter to the editor in The Brunswick News, the paper "quoted Rep. Buddy Carter at length" saying "'It is critical that we invest in this necessary resource'" about Brunswick port upgrades — even though Carter "voted against the bill" that funded them. The reader noted the coverage "implicitly credits Rep. Carter with securing the funding" while failing to interview Senators Ossoff and Warnock, who actually voted for the law. [Brunswick News, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://thebrunswicknews.com/opinion/letters_to_editor/giving-credit-where-credit-is-due/article_e28d8ead-3273-5888-aad8-9c655d3d9f0d.html)]

**Carter Voted Against The CHIPS And Science Act, Called It The "CHIPS And Stagflation Act."** According to a press release from Carter's office, Carter opposed the $280 billion CHIPS and Science Act, calling it the "CHIPS and Stagflation Act" and stating, "the government cannot be in the business of picking winners and losers." The bill, which invested in domestic semiconductor manufacturing and scientific research, passed the House 243-187 with 24 Republicans supporting it — Carter was not among them. [Rep. Buddy Carter Press Release, [7/28/22](https://buddycarter.house.gov/news/documentsingle.aspx?DocumentID=10476)]

**Carter Voted Against The Inflation Reduction Act, Then Took Credit For The $7.6 Billion Hyundai Plant It Funded.** According to Canary Media, Carter voted against the Inflation Reduction Act in 2022 and again voted to gut the IRA in May 2025, yet his district is home to the $7.6 billion Hyundai Metaplant in Bryan County — the largest single economic development project in Georgia history, expected to generate approximately 8,500 jobs — which Hyundai executives credited to IRA domestic production tax credits. The League of Conservation Voters named Carter in its "Clean Energy Hypocrisy Report." [Canary Media, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://www.canarymedia.com/articles/politics/buddy-carter-georgia-reconcilation-bill-solar-evs)]

### Defended Tariffs That Hurt His Own District

**Carter Praised Trump's Tariff Policies As "Home-Run Economic" Wins While Port Of Savannah Lost 9.6% Of Its Cargo Volume.** According to a press release from Carter's office, Carter credited "targeted tariffs with generating billions in revenue while protecting American industries." Yet according to The Washington Post, the Port of Savannah — which handles over 4.6 million container units annually and supports 609,000 Georgia jobs — saw container volumes fall 9.6% in June 2025 compared to a year earlier. [Washington Post, [7/29/25](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2025/07/29/georgia-port-of-savannah-statistics-tariffs/1be921c2-6c8b-11f0-aab6-8141d7095676_story.html)]

**Carter Voted To Block A Resolution Ending Trump's Tariffs On Canada.** According to GovTrack, Carter voted against H.J.Res. 72 on February 11, 2026, which would have terminated the national emergency declaration used to impose tariffs on Canadian imports. The resolution passed the House 219-211, with six Republicans crossing the aisle — but Carter was not among them. [GovTrack, [2/11/26](https://www.govtrack.us/congress/votes/119-2026/h65)]

**Retaliatory Tariffs Threatened Georgia's $28 Billion Poultry Industry And 88,000 Jobs.** According to Atlanta News First, Georgia's poultry sector employs more than 88,000 workers and generates $28 billion annually, ranking as the largest in the U.S. and seventh globally. U.S. Poultry and Egg Council President Greg Tyler warned, "'If we can't export this product, the U.S. industry will have to scale back production,'" as Canada removed U.S. poultry products from store shelves and China imposed a 15% tariff increase on chicken. [Atlanta News First, [4/25/25](https://www.atlantanewsfirst.com/2025/04/25/increasing-tariffs-could-threaten-georgias-poultry-industry-officials-warn/)]

- **Georgia Chamber Warned Tariffs Would Raise Grocery Prices And Threaten Peanut Exports.** According to Georgia Public Broadcasting, Georgia Chamber CEO Chris Clark warned that reciprocal tariffs could "make it more difficult to ship Georgia peanut products [...] around the world" and that consumers would "continue to" experience higher costs on groceries and retail goods. Georgia leads the nation in peanut production, accounting for roughly half of all U.S.-grown peanuts. [GPB, [4/14/25](https://www.gpb.org/news/2025/04/14/georgia-chamber-urges-businesses-be-proactive-tariffs-take-effect)]

### Voted To Kill The Clean Energy Jobs Boom In His Own Backyard

**Carter Voted To Gut The IRA Clean Energy Tax Credits That Attracted 42,000 Jobs And $28 Billion In Investment To Georgia.** According to a report from Senator Warnock's office, 51 new clean energy projects worth over $28 billion have been announced in Georgia since the IRA passed, expected to create nearly 42,000 jobs. Over 70% of the investments and 83% of the new jobs are in counties with median family incomes below the national median. [Sen. Warnock Press Release, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://www.warnock.senate.gov/newsroom/press-releases/senator-reverend-warnock-releases-new-report-on-42000-ga-jobs-at-risk-under-gop-tax-bill/)]

**Carter Admitted Clean Energy Tax Credits Were "Not A Red Line" Despite Signing A Letter To Protect Them.** According to E&E News, despite signing a letter with 17 other House Republicans asking leadership to take "a scalpel" rather than "a sledgehammer" to IRA clean energy tax credits, Carter admitted the issue was "not a red line" for him — suggesting he would ultimately vote to strip the credits supporting the Hyundai plant and approximately 42,000 clean energy jobs statewide. [E&E News, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://www.eenews.net/articles/green-credits-not-a-hill-to-die-on-for-supportive-republicans-2/)]

**An EV Supplier Canceled A $325 Million Factory In Statesboro, Taking 250 Promised Jobs With It.** According to The Current, Aspen Aerogels canceled plans to build a manufacturing facility in Bulloch County near Statesboro — in Carter's district — that would have created more than 250 jobs producing thermal barriers for electric vehicle batteries. The company cited softer EV demand caused by "high vehicle prices, insufficient charging station infrastructure," and government policy shifts discouraging EV purchases. [Current, [2/15/25](https://thecurrentga.org/2025/02/15/ev-supplier-cancels-plan-to-build-plant-near-statesboro/)]

- **A $2.6 Billion Battery Factory In Georgia Was Also Scrapped, Costing 720 Promised Jobs.** According to Atlanta News First, Freyr Battery scrapped plans for a $2.6 billion lithium-ion battery factory in Coweta County that would have created more than 720 jobs over seven years. The company cited climbing interest rates, falling battery prices, and corporate leadership changes. [Atlanta News First, [2/6/25](https://www.atlantanewsfirst.com/2025/02/06/freyr-battery-scraps-plans-factory-coweta-county-georgia-official-says/)]

**Carter Scored 10% On The AFL-CIO's Lifetime Legislative Scorecard.** According to the AFL-CIO, Carter carried a 10% lifetime score on their congressional scorecard tracking votes on worker pay, benefits, safety, and organizing rights. He scored 0% in the most recent session, meaning he did not vote with workers on a single key labor vote tracked by the AFL-CIO. [AFL-CIO, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://aflcio.org/scorecard/legislators/buddy-carter)]

### Authored A 23% Sales Tax That Would Devastate Working Families

**Carter Authored The Fair Tax Act, A 23% National Sales Tax That Economists Called Regressive.** According to Money, Carter's Fair Tax Act would abolish the IRS, eliminate all federal income, payroll, estate, and corporate taxes, and replace them with a 23% national retail sales tax — an effective rate of approximately 30%. While the bill includes a monthly "prebate" to offset costs at the poverty level, economists widely view national consumption taxes as regressive, hitting lower- and middle-income households hardest. [Money, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://money.com/fair-tax-act-national-sales-tax/)]

- **Yale Budget Lab: Tariffs Already Cost The Average American Household $1,800 Per Year — A National Sales Tax Would Pile On.** According to the Yale Budget Lab, the price level from all 2025 tariffs rises by 1.3% in the short run, "the equivalent of an average per household income loss of $1,800 in 2025." The burden falls hardest on the lowest-income households, who face losses more than three times greater than the top decile as a share of income. [Yale Budget Lab, [10/17/25](https://budgetlab.yale.edu/research/state-us-tariffs-october-17-2025)]

### Cut Food Assistance While Dismissing Concerns

**Carter Voted For The One Big Beautiful Bill, Which Cut Nearly $200 Billion From SNAP.** According to FOX 5 Atlanta, the One Big Beautiful Bill Act — which Carter voted for and publicly championed — cuts nearly $200 billion from the Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program through "cost-shifting to states, eligibility restrictions, increased work requirements and limits on USDA's ability to update benefit levels." [FOX 5 Atlanta, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://www.fox5atlanta.com/news/snap-benefit-cuts-big-beautiful-impact-georgia-explained)]

**Carter Dismissed SNAP Cut Concerns: "I Am Not In The Least Way Concerned."** According to FOX 5 Atlanta, Carter stated, "'I'm not in the least way concerned that people who are deserving to be on the program aren't going to be on the program,'" even as 1.4 million Georgians rely on SNAP benefits, including a disproportionate share of children, seniors, and people with disabilities. [FOX 5 Atlanta, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://www.fox5atlanta.com/news/snap-benefit-cuts-big-beautiful-impact-georgia-explained)]

### Impact On Everyday People

**Port Of Savannah Supports 609,000 Georgia Jobs — And Carter Backed The Tariffs Threatening Them.** According to The Washington Post, the Port of Savannah and Georgia Ports Authority facilities support 609,000 jobs and contribute $171 billion in sales activity annually in Georgia. Carter praised the tariff policies that drove a 9.6% cargo volume decline in June 2025 and threatened the port-dependent supply chain that employs hundreds of thousands across the state. [Washington Post, [7/29/25](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2025/07/29/georgia-port-of-savannah-statistics-tariffs/1be921c2-6c8b-11f0-aab6-8141d7095676_story.html)]

**Second Harvest Of Coastal Georgia Scrambled To Respond As SNAP Cuts Carter Voted For Took Effect.** According to WTOC, Second Harvest of Coastal Georgia — which serves 21 counties across Carter's district — distributes 25 million pounds of food annually through more than 240 nonprofit partners. Executive Director Mary Jane Crouch said, "'We're not sure what's going to happen with SNAP benefits, so — you know — we're here.'" [WTOC, [10/23/25](https://www.wtoc.com/2025/10/23/second-harvest-food-bank-steps-up-help-families-impacted-by-potential-snap-funding-cuts/)]

**Nearly 39% Of Households Below The Poverty Line In Carter's District Experienced Food Insecurity.** According to Coastal Democracy, District 1 "consistently shows higher food insecurity rates than national averages," with grocery prices rising 25.6% from February 2020 to July 2025 — outpacing overall inflation of 21.6%. Lower-income households spent approximately one-third of their after-tax income on food. [Coastal Democracy, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://savannahindivisible.org/depleted-food-banks-and-food-insecurity)]

**Atlanta Community Food Bank CEO: "We Would Essentially Have To Double Our Volume Overnight."** According to FOX 5 Atlanta, Kyle Waide, CEO of the Atlanta Community Food Bank, stated, "'Our demand has increased by 60% over the last three years,'" and warned that to replace lost SNAP funding, "'we would essentially have to double our volume overnight, and obviously, that's not possible.'" [FOX 5 Atlanta, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://www.fox5atlanta.com/news/snap-benefit-cuts-big-beautiful-impact-georgia-explained)]
