# Rep. Buddy Carter: Threatened To Cut Social Security And Medicare

### Threatened Social Security And Medicare

**Carter Said "Everything Is On The Table" When Asked About Cutting Social Security And Medicare.** According to the Democratic Party of Georgia, when running for House Budget Committee chair, Carter was asked if he would rule out cuts to Social Security, Medicaid, and Medicare. He said "No" and added that cuts were "something, certainly, that [the GOP Budget Committee] is going to be looking at." [Georgia Democrat, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://georgiademocrat.org/rep-buddy-carter-supports-cuts-to-social-security-medicare-and-medicaid/)]

**Carter Named Social Security, Medicare, And Medicaid By Name As Programs He Would Like To Cut.** According to the Democratic Party of Georgia, as a Senate candidate, Carter called out Social Security, Medicare, and Medicaid by name as specific programs he would target for cuts. [Georgia Democrat, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://georgiademocrat.org/buddy-carter-targets-social-security-medicare-medicaid-by-name-for-cuts/)]

**2024: Carter Hedged On Social Security On C-SPAN, Implying Future Beneficiaries Could See Cuts.** According to C-SPAN, Carter stated "72% of the budget is Social Security, Medicaid and Medicare. Social Security can be fixed without cutting benefits to those who are getting it now" — the phrase "those who are getting it now" implying that future beneficiaries could face reductions. [C-SPAN, [11/14/24](https://www.c-span.org/video/?652106)]

### Authored A 23% National Sales Tax That Would Devastate Working Families

**Carter Authored The Fair Tax Act, A 23% National Sales Tax That Economists Called Regressive.** According to Money, Carter's Fair Tax Act would abolish the IRS, eliminate all federal income, payroll, estate, and corporate taxes, and replace them with a 23% national retail sales tax — an effective rate of approximately 30%. While the bill includes a monthly "prebate" to offset costs at the poverty level, economists widely view national consumption taxes as regressive, hitting lower- and middle-income households hardest. [Money, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://money.com/fair-tax-act-national-sales-tax/)]

- **Yale Budget Lab: Tariffs Already Cost The Average American Household $1,800 Per Year — A National Sales Tax Would Pile On.** According to the Yale Budget Lab, the price level from all 2025 tariffs rises by 1.3% in the short run, "the equivalent of an average per household income loss of $1,800 in 2025." The burden falls hardest on the lowest-income households, who face losses more than three times greater than the top decile as a share of income. [Yale Budget Lab, [10/17/25](https://budgetlab.yale.edu/research/state-us-tariffs-october-17-2025)]

### Voted Against The PACT Act For Veterans

**Carter Voted Against The PACT Act Expanding Healthcare For Veterans Exposed To Burn Pits.** According to GovTrack, Carter voted Nay on the PACT Act (HR 3967) on March 3, 2022. The bill, which expanded healthcare and disability benefits for veterans exposed to burn pits and toxic substances during military service, passed the House 256-174 with only 34 Republicans joining all Democrats in supporting it. [GovTrack, [3/3/22](https://www.govtrack.us/congress/votes/117-2022/h57)]
