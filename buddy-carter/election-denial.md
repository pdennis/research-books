# Rep. Buddy Carter: Voted To Overturn The 2020 Election, Then Called Protesters "Insurrectionists"

### Voted To Overturn The 2020 Election, Then Called Protesters "Insurrectionists"

**Carter Voted Against Certifying The 2020 Election Results Hours After The Capitol Attack.** According to The Current, Carter voted to object to the electoral votes from both Arizona and Pennsylvania on January 6, 2021, maintaining his objections even after the violent insurrection at the Capitol. He was among only a handful of Georgia Republicans who refused to certify the results. [Current, [1/7/21](https://thecurrentga.org/2021/01/07/carter-objection-to-biden-certification-was-about-state-process-not-trump/)]

**2026: Carter Called Peaceful Church Protesters "Insurrectionists" And Demanded They Be Placed "In Handcuffs."** According to a press release from Carter's office, Carter introduced a House resolution condemning demonstrators who protested at a Minnesota church and demanded: "I want to see these insurrectionists in handcuffs." He described the roughly three dozen peaceful demonstrators as a "mob" acting "in defense of pedophiles, murderers, and rapists." [Rep.
