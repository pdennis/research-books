# Rep. Buddy Carter: Violated The STOCK Act And Made Suspicious Stock Trades

### Financial Hypocrisy

**Carter Violated The STOCK Act By Reporting Stock Trades 301 Days Late.** According to Raw Story, Carter violated the Stop Trading on Congressional Knowledge Act by failing to report stock trades within the required 45-day period, filing 301 days late. He was also more than eight months late disclosing the sale of government securities for the Atlanta Development Authority valued between $15,001 and $50,000. [Raw Story, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://www.rawstory.com/raw-investigates/three-members-of-congress-violate-federal-financial-law/)]

- **Carter Co-Sponsored The Stop Insider Trading Act While Violating Existing Disclosure Requirements.** According to Quiver Quantitative, Carter publicly supported legislation to ban insider trading in Congress even as he was violating the existing STOCK Act's disclosure requirements. [Quiver Quantitative, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://www.quiverquant.com/news/Press+Release:+Buddy+Carter+Supports+Legislation+to+Ban+Insider+Trading+in+Congress)]

**Carter Sold Up To $1 Million In Ameris Bancorp Stock Days Before The Company's Earnings Report.** According to Invezz, Carter disclosed the sale of between $400,000 and $1,000,000 worth of Ameris Bancorp stock just days before the company released its earnings report. Insider Tracker flagged Carter's "history of questionable transactions" and listed him among politicians violating the STOCK Act. [Invezz, [7/26/24](https://invezz.com/news/2024/07/26/congressman-buddy-carter-sells-up-to-1-million-in-ameris-bancorp-stock-before-earnings-report-whats-behind-the-move/)]

**Carter Filed Ethics Complaints Over Campaign Account Transfer Scheme.** According to The Atlanta Journal-Constitution, an ethics complaint was filed with the Federal Election Commission and the FBI's public corruption unit alleging Carter and several Republican state legislators engaged in a scheme to evade bans on transferring money between state and federal campaign accounts. Carter kept a state senate campaign account active after announcing he was seeking election to the U.S. House. [Atlanta Journal-Constitution, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://www.ajc.com/blog/politics/ethics-complaint-targets-buddy-carter-campaign-account-transfers/AWp7L6shp1Vcg7cDXI1LmJ/)]
