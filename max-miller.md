Max Miller has voted against Ohioans to give the wealthiest Americans’ tax breaks and support an agenda that raised costs and left Ohioans behind. When he campaigned, Miller promised to fight for seniors and workers but voted to cut Medicare by $500 billion, voted for a bill that would kick 17 million Americans off their health insurance including more than 22,000 of his own constituents, and voted to cut food assistance that more than 21,000 households in his district relied on the afford groceries. Miller voted to protect Trump’s tariffs six different times, even as it became clear they were hurting Ohio businesses, manufacturers, and causing layoffs. Miller supported DOGE and was proud to support its budget cuts that harmed Ohio’s food banks, farmers, and fair housing organizations. Miller requested that the National Guard be sent to Cleveland despite crime rates going down. Miller was also no stranger to legal battles. Miller accused someone of threatening his life in an antisemitic road rage incident, but his account of events was questioned in a defamation suit – the case is still ongoing. Miller had a contentious relationship with his ex-wife and was recently accused of speaking to her in a demeaning manner and having conducted dangerous physical behavior in the presence of their child. Miller also filed a defamation suit against his ex-girlfriend Stephanie Grisham shortly after she published an op-ed about an abusive relationship she had been in with a fellow White House staffer during the first Trump administration. Miller has demonstrated time and time again that he’s wrong for Ohio’s 7^th^ congressional district.

### MILLER COURTED SENIORS AND WORKERS WHEN HE WAS CAMPAIGNING, BUT WHEN HE GOT TO CONGRESS, HIS PROMISES VAPORIZED

-   On the campaign trail in 2021, Miller promised to be [a fierce advocate](https://www.facebook.com/share/p/18EJQmV5dP/) for seniors. Since being elected, he [voted](http://clerk.house.gov/evs/2025/roll145.xml) for the Big Beautiful Bill, which the Congressional Budget Office estimated would trigger more than [$500 billion](https://www.cbo.gov/system/files/2025-05/61423-PAYGO.pdf) in cuts to Medicare absent future congressional action. Miller then turned around and [told constituents](https://www.facebook.com/share/p/1ZkcuTNu1c/) that Medicare had not been touched by the Big Beautiful Bill. 
-   On the Labor Day before his first election, Miller promised that he would [always put workers first](https://x.com/MaxMillerOH/status/1566815060525289473?s=20), but since entering congress he has only voted in favor of working people [14%](https://aflcio.org/scorecard/legislators/max-miller) of the time and he stripped federal workers of their [bargaining rights](https://clerk.house.gov/evs/2025/roll332.xml) and [other protections](https://clerk.house.gov/evs/2025/roll098.xml). 

**Message:** Miller voted against Ohio workers and seniors after promising he would fight for them.

### MILLER PROUDLY VOTED TO TAKE HEALTH COVERAGE FROM MORE THAN 22,000 OHIOANS IN THE SEVENTH CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT

-   Miller voted for a bill that would kick [17 million Americans](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2025/07/01/least-17-million-americans-would-lose-insurance-under-trump-plan/) off their health insurance including more than [22,000 of his own constituents](https://www.jec.senate.gov/public/_cache/files/9cfdee90-dd29-41e8-97da-b5dcdc0317cf/updated-jec-fact-sheet-on-district-level-impacts-of-health-care-cuts.pdf). Miller told Ohioans that his vote [secured Medicaid for the most vulnerable](https://x.com/RepMaxMiller/status/1940843203420143945?s=20) despite the reality that the bill he voted for would kick [12 million Americans](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2025/07/01/least-17-million-americans-would-lose-insurance-under-trump-plan/) off the program. 
-   Miller said the Affordable Care Act was a [failure](https://www.facebook.com/share/p/1JeyPN2X64/) and [one of the costliest pieces of legislation in our nation’s history](https://www.facebook.com/share/p/1JeyPN2X64/) regardless of the fact that it provided more than [583,000 Ohioans](https://www.kff.org/affordable-care-act/state-indicator/open-enrollment-marketplace-plan-selections/?currentTimeframe=1&sortModel=%7B%22colId%22:%22Location%22,%22sort%22:%22asc%22%7D) with access to health insurance. 
-   Miller ultimately voted to extend enhanced ACA premiums tax credits, but not before  he made sure his constituents knew he felt they were [unsustainable](https://www.facebook.com/share/p/1JeyPN2X64/) and [irresponsible](https://www.facebook.com/share/p/1JeyPN2X64/). However, his vote was too little too late, the credits expired, causing tens of thousands of Ohioans to [drop coverage](https://ohiocapitaljournal.com/2026/02/16/advocacy-group-says-more-than-113000-ohioans-are-now-going-without-affordable-care-act-coverage/) and [premiums to spike](https://www.cleveland.com/politics/2026/01/affordable-care-act-subsidies-expire-over-500000-ohioans-face-higher-health-insurance-costs.html).

**Message:** Miller proudly voted to rip away health coverage from Ohioans all to fund billionaire tax breaks. 

### MILLER BACKED TRUMP’S TARIFFS THAT MADE LIFE HARDER FOR OHIOANS

-   Miller voted six times to protect the tariffs, including in April 2025 when he was the [deciding vote](http://clerk.house.gov/evs/2025/roll094.xml) to protect them, even though they made things more expensive for [Ohio businesses](https://www.news5cleveland.com/news/local-news/cleveland-metro/cleveland-businesses-feel-impact-of-tariffs-despite-supreme-court-ruling), hurt [18%](https://signalcleveland.org/tariffs-impact-on-ohio-manufacturers-mixed-why-some-benefit-and-others-lose/) of Ohio manufacturers, and caused [massive layoffs](https://www.washingtonpost.com/business/2025/11/06/job-cuts-accelerate-october-layoffs-trump/) across the country. Consumer products like coffee, sugar, and appliances got as much as [33% more expensive](https://www.cleveland.com/news/2026/03/price-patrol-one-year-later-what-100-everyday-prices-reveal-about-tariffs-inflation-in-northeast-ohio.html) after 100 days of Trump’s tariffs. 
-   Even after it was clear how Trump’s tariffs and trade policy devastated Ohioans, Miller [sent a letter](https://adriansmith.house.gov/media/press-releases/smith-daines-support-trump-administrations-engagement-agricultural-trade) to the Trump Administration that commended their ongoing trade efforts.

**Message:** Miller was the deciding vote to make Ohioans pay higher prices.

### MILLER VOTED TO CUT FOOD ASSISTANCE FOR OHIOANS WHO RELIED ON IT TO AFFORD GROCERIES

-   In 2025, Miller [voted](http://clerk.house.gov/evs/2025/roll190.xml) for a bill that made the [largest SNAP cut in history](https://www.cbpp.org/research/food-assistance/by-the-numbers-senate-republican-leaderships-reconciliation-bill-takes). His vote threatened food assistance for more than [21,000 households](https://www.fns.usda.gov/data-research/data-visualization/snap-community-characteristics-congressional-district-dashboard) in the 7^th^ congressional district. Miller has always been skeptical of the program his constituents rely on to afford groceries; in 2023 he tried to [limit access](https://www.usatoday.com/story/news/politics/2023/03/14/snap-benefits-food-stamps-eligibility/11466782002/) to SNAP and claimed program recipients [make SNAP a lifestyle](https://www.facebook.com/share/p/1AvAWR5VXB/).

**Message:** Miller voted to cut food assistance that Ohioans relied on to afford groceries all to fund tax breaks for millionaires.

-   Miller initially thought DOGE had some [very exciting ideas](https://www.notus.org/congress/doges-big-dreams-meet-congressional-reality) and then [proudly voted](https://www.facebook.com/share/v/18e9SvLENu/) to codify its cuts. He supported the agency even as its cuts hurt Ohio’s [food banks](https://www.nbc4i.com/news/local-news/columbus/doge-cuts-hitting-ohio-farmers-food-banks/#:~:text=The%20move%20cuts%20about%20$420,million%20dollars%20in%20federal%20funds.&text=%E2%80%9CWhat%20it%20really%20helped%20us,through%20the%20program%20last%20year.), [farmers](https://www.nbc4i.com/news/local-news/columbus/doge-cuts-hitting-ohio-farmers-food-banks/#:~:text=The%20move%20cuts%20about%20$420,million%20dollars%20in%20federal%20funds.&text=%E2%80%9CWhat%20it%20really%20helped%20us,through%20the%20program%20last%20year.), and [fair housing organizations](https://www.cleveland.com/realestate-news/2025/03/cleveland-fair-housing-nonprofit-among-those-suing-doge-over-unlawful-cuts.html). DOGE also caused [mass layoffs,](https://www.cbpp.org/research/social-security/reassignment-wont-fix-the-largest-ever-social-security-staffing-cut) [longer wait times](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2025/03/25/social-security-phones-doge-cuts/) and [frequent website crashes](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2025/03/25/social-security-phones-doge-cuts/) at the Social Security Administration, hurting Ohio seniors’ ability to access services. 

**Message:** Miller was excited about cuts that hurt Ohio seniors, food banks, and farmers.

**Message:** Miller wanted the National Guard to be deployed to Cleveland even though crime was trending downward in the city.
