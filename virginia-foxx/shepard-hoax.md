# Rep. Virginia Foxx (NC-05): Called Matthew Shepard's Murder a "Hoax"

### Foxx Called Matthew Shepard's Murder a "Hoax" on the House Floor

**Foxx Called The Hate Crime Murder Of Matthew Shepard A "Hoax" During Debate On Hate Crimes Legislation.** According to NBC News, during House debate on hate crimes legislation in April 2009, Foxx stated that Shepard's death "was a hoax" and that "'we know that that young man was killed in the commitment of a robbery'" rather than because "'he was gay.'" Matthew Shepard was robbed, beaten, and left to die tied to a fence near Laramie, Wyoming, in 1998; two men pleaded guilty and testified they targeted him because he was gay. [NBC News, [4/30/09](https://www.nbcnews.com/id/wbna30505428)]

- **Shepard's Mother Rejected Foxx's Apology, Saying It Excused "Her Sentiment, Her Insensitivity, [And] Her Ignorance."** According to NBC News, Judy Shepard stated, "'It's apologizing for semantics but not her sentiment, her insensitivity or her ignorance.'" Shepard added, "'I never expected it to be called a hoax.'" Foxx had written a letter of apology calling it "'a poor choice of words.'" [NBC News, [4/30/09](https://www.nbcnews.com/id/wbna30505428)]
