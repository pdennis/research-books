# Rep. Virginia Foxx (NC-05): Told Students She Has "Very Little Tolerance" for Their Debt

### Foxx Said She Had "Very Little Tolerance" For Students With Debt — After Paying $87.50 a Semester For Her Own Degree

**Foxx Said She Had "Very Little Tolerance For People Who Tell Me That They Graduate With $200,000 Of Debt Or Even $80,000 Of Debt."** According to ThinkProgress, in an April 2012 appearance on G. Gordon Liddy's radio show, Foxx stated, "'I have very little tolerance for people who tell me that they graduate with $200,000 of debt or even $80,000 of debt because there's no reason for that.'" She said she "worked my way through, it took me seven years, I never borrowed a dime of money." [ThinkProgress, [4/19/12](https://archive.thinkprogress.org/rep-virginia-foxx-on-people-with-student-loans-i-have-very-little-tolerance-for-them-408d16518ba3/)]

- **Foxx Paid $87.50 Per Semester In Tuition At UNC In 1961 — A Fraction Of What Students Pay Today.** According to New America, Foxx paid $87.50 for a full semester's tuition at the University of North Carolina in 1961, or approximately $671 in today's dollars. In-state students at UNC now pay $7,008 annually — more than three times Foxx's inflation-adjusted cost. During Foxx's seven years at UNC, tuition increased approximately 0.6% annually; since 2005, UNC students have seen annual increases of 7.2%. [New America, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://www.newamerica.org/education-policy/edcentral/virginia-foxx-paid-8750-in-tuition-in-1961/)]

**Foxx Demanded That Rep. Maxwell Frost's Remarks On Student Debt Be Stricken From The Congressional Record.** According to The Hill, during a May 2023 floor debate on student loan forgiveness, Rep. Maxwell Frost argued, "'If we legislated using the logic that you bring to this issue today, women and Black folks wouldn't have the right to vote because it would be unfair to those who never got to vote before them.'" Foxx responded by demanding, "'I demand his words be taken down,'" forcing Frost to withdraw his remarks. [Hill, [5/24/23](https://thehill.com/homenews/house/4019071-gop-lawmaker-demands-maxwell-frost-remarks-on-bigoted-logic-be-taken-down/)]
