# Rep. Virginia Foxx (NC-05): At 83, Among the Oldest Members of Congress

### At 83, Foxx Is Among the Oldest Members of Congress and Faces a Primary Challenge

**Foxx, 82, Is Among The Oldest Members Of Congress And Would Be 83 On Election Day 2026.** According to NBC News, Foxx is among Congress' 24 Silent Generation members, alongside Harold Rogers (87), Maxine Waters (86), and Nancy Pelosi (85). She has confirmed plans to seek another term in the March 3, 2026 Republican primary.
