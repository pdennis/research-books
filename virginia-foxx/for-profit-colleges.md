# Rep. Virginia Foxx (NC-05): $665,000 From For-Profit Colleges While Chairing the Committee That Oversees Them

### Foxx Collected Over $665,000 From For-Profit Colleges While Chairing the Committee That Oversees Them

**Foxx Was The Top House Recipient Of For-Profit Education Industry Donations From 2016 To 2022 While Serving On The Education Committee.** According to The New Republic, "Foxx has received over $665,000 from the for-profit education industry and $86,000 from student loan companies across election cycles" and was the leading House recipient of for-profit college donations from 2016 to 2022. [New Republic, [5/24/23](https://newrepublic.com/post/172968/gop-lawmaker-virginia-foxx-erases-maxwell-frost-student-debt-argument-record)]

- **Foxx's Legislation Would Roll Back Accountability Rules For The Same For-Profit Colleges That Funded Her Campaigns.** According to Higher Ed Dive, Foxx's College Cost Reduction Act would rescind the gainful employment rule, change college ownership regulations, and roll back the 90/10 rule — all accountability measures targeting the for-profit education industry. [Higher Ed Dive, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://www.highereddive.com/news/republican-bill-foxx-cap-student-borrowing-colleges-liable-unpaid-loans/704505/)]

- **Employees Of For-Profit Colleges Were Among The Top Donors To Foxx's Leadership PAC.** According to Republic Report, in the 2016 election cycle, "the employees and political-action committees of five for-profit companies were among the top 20 donors to the congresswoman's leadership PAC," including American Career College, Capella Education, and ECPI College of Technology, each contributing the maximum $5,000. Full Sail University employees gave her more than $36,000 over her career. [Republic Report, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://www.republicreport.org/2022/as-house-education-chair-probes-for-profit-colleges-ranking-republican-foxx-takes-their-donations/)]
