## Overview
Steil claimed he wanted to protect Social Security and Medicare, but instead he voted for a bill that would have triggered nearly $500 billion in Medicare cuts. He also defended DOGE as they weakened Wisconsinites’ access to the Social Security Administration with mass layoffs. Steil was a member of the Republican Study Committee, which advocated for raising the retirement age and increasing the cost of health care for Medicare beneficiaries.
