## Overview
Steil voted for a bill that would kick 17 million Americans off their health insurance, including nearly 12 million Americans off of Medicaid. As a result of his vote, 32,339 of his own constituents could lose their health insurance. Steil told voters his vote for the “One Big Beautiful Bill” would “strengthen” Medicaid rather than admit his vote significantly cut the program. Steil believed the Affordable Care Act had “failed,” even though the Affordable Care Act dramatically lowered the rate of uninsured low-income workers. He also voted against renewing enhanced ACA premium tax credits, which an estimated 271,589 Wisconsinites relied on to afford health insurance.
