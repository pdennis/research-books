# How To Win Against Stephanie Bice

Stephanie Bice has spent her time in Congress advocating [to repeal the Affordable Care Act](https://www.readfrontier.org/stories/horn-vows-to-protect-affordable-care-act-bice-promotes-use-of-health-savings-accounts-as-a-replacement/) and has voted against every bill that would lower prescription drug costs, including the [\$35 insulin cap](https://ktul.com/news/local/all-oklahoma-republican-congress-members-vote-no-on-bill-capping-insulin-prices) and [Medicare drug price negotiation](https://bice.house.gov/media/press-releases/bice-opposes-democrats-partisan-budget-bill). She voted for the ["One Big Beautiful Bill"](https://bice.house.gov/media/press-releases/bice-passage-one-big-beautiful-bill) that could strip Medicaid from [174,000 Oklahomans](https://www.kff.org/medicaid/allocating-cbos-estimates-of-federal-medicaid-spending-reductions-and-enrollment-loss-across-the-states/) and SNAP benefits from [131,000 more](https://oklahomawatch.org/2025/07/24/historic-cuts-to-snap-put-131000-oklahomans-at-risk-of-losing-benefits/) — while the bill delivers [over \$50,000 a year in tax cuts to the top 1%](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/05/23/house-republican-big-beautiful-tax-bill-favors-the-rich.html).

She voted against the [infrastructure law](https://gaylordnews.net/6956/news/oklahoma-house-representatives-vote-against-bill-that-includes-oklahoma-projects/) that sent $6.4 billion to Oklahoma — after [earmarking \$20 million in the same bill](https://www.normantranscript.com/news/oklahoma-delegation-opposes-infrastructure-bill-despite-earmarks/article_fafd4e98-dac1-11eb-ab8f-e76aed4c2bc9.html). She brands herself as bipartisan but votes with her party [97% of the time](https://projects.propublica.org/represent/members/B000740-stephanie-bice/votes-against-party/117), voted to [overturn the 2020 election](https://www.readfrontier.org/stories/majority-of-oklahoma-members-object-to-bidens-election-after-riot-overtakes-u-s-capitol/).

### **HEALTH CARE: PROMISED LOWER COSTS, VOTED TO GUT COVERAGE**

Bice campaigned on lowering drug costs including [insulin and EpiPens](https://www.readfrontier.org/stories/horn-vows-to-protect-affordable-care-act-bice-promotes-use-of-health-savings-accounts-as-a-replacement/), then voted against the [\$35 insulin cap](https://ktul.com/news/local/all-oklahoma-republican-congress-members-vote-no-on-bill-capping-insulin-prices) and [Medicare drug price negotiation](https://bice.house.gov/media/press-releases/bice-opposes-democrats-partisan-budget-bill). Over 450,000 Oklahomans have diabetes.

Voted for the "One Big Beautiful Bill" that could strip [174,000 Oklahomans of Medicaid](https://www.kff.org/medicaid/allocating-cbos-estimates-of-federal-medicaid-spending-reductions-and-enrollment-loss-across-the-states/) and threatens [\$8.7 billion in losses for Oklahoma hospitals](https://www.oica.org/bbb-oklahoma) — nine rural hospitals have [already closed since 2005](https://www.kgou.org/health/2025-05-22/oklahoma-health-care-authority-addresses-impacts-to-medicaid-proposed-in-house-budget-bill).

Oklahoma ACA premiums [rose 20-21% on average, with some plans spiking 60-75%](https://ktul.com/news/local/oklahoma-aca-insurance-premiums-to-rise-as-federal-subsidies-expire-medical-costs-coverage-medicaid-public-health-funding) after subsidies expired; a 60-year-old couple earning $85,000 saw premiums jump from [\$7,225 to \$30,784](https://okpolicy.org/what-oklahomans-need-to-know-about-expiring-aca-premium-tax-credits/).

**Message:** Bice promised to lower drug costs and protect coverage, then voted against every bill that did and for a bill that guts Medicaid for 174,000 Oklahomans.

### **AFFORDABILITY: VOTED FOR TARIFFS AND SNAP CUTS WHILE GROCERIES SOAR**

Bice [voted to protect Trump's 25% tariffs on Canada](https://clerk.house.gov/evs/2026/roll065.xml) as [grocery prices rose 29%](https://www.kgou.org/business-and-economy/2025-09-19/grocery-prices-have-jumped-up-and-theres-no-relief-in-sight) and Oklahoma wheat farmers faced a ["double squeeze"](https://www.kgou.org/business-and-economy/2025-04-22/how-will-tariffs-impact-oklahomas-agriculture-industry) of lower export prices and higher input costs.

Voted for [\$186 billion in SNAP cuts](https://oklahomawatch.org/2025/07/24/historic-cuts-to-snap-put-131000-oklahomans-at-risk-of-losing-benefits/) putting 131,000 Oklahomans at risk of losing food benefits, including 255,000 children statewide.
The bill she voted for gives [over \$50,000/year in tax cuts to the top 1%](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/05/23/house-republican-big-beautiful-tax-bill-favors-the-rich.html) while Bice [framed it as preventing a "\$1,200 tax hike"](https://bice.house.gov/media/press-releases/bice-passage-one-big-beautiful-bill) — never mentioning the SNAP or Medicaid cuts.

**Message:** Bice sided with tariffs that raise grocery prices and voted to cut food benefits for 131,000 Oklahomans — all to fund tax cuts for the richest 1%.

### **JOBS AND THE ECONOMY: VOTED AGAINST OKLAHOMA'S ECONOMIC INTERESTS**

Voted against the [infrastructure law that sent \$6.4 billion to Oklahoma](https://gaylordnews.net/6956/news/oklahoma-house-representatives-vote-against-bill-that-includes-oklahoma-projects/) — after [putting \$20 million in earmarks in the bill she voted against](https://www.normantranscript.com/news/oklahoma-delegation-opposes-infrastructure-bill-despite-earmarks/article_fafd4e98-dac1-11eb-ab8f-e76aed4c2bc9.html).
Championed DOGE federal workforce cuts while [30,000 OKC federal workers](https://www.foxbusiness.com/politics/doge-cuts-rippling-through-local-economies-around-us) faced layoffs — FAA workers at the Mike Monroney Center said they felt ["betrayed and abandoned"](https://kfor.com/news/local/oklahoma-city-faa-workers-axed-in-federal-layoffs-feel-betrayed-concerned-by-rhetoric/).
The bill she voted for is projected to [eliminate 1.22 million jobs nationwide](https://www.commonwealthfund.org/publications/issue-briefs/2025/jun/how-medicaid-snap-cutbacks-one-big-beautiful-bill-trigger-job-losses-states) via Medicaid and SNAP cuts. Only [16% of Oklahoma firms](https://www.kansascityfed.org/oklahomacity/oklahoma-economist/hiring-in-oklahoma-slows-but-unemployment-remains-low/) expect to add jobs in 2026 — the third-lowest reading in a decade.

**Message:** Bice votes against infrastructure and job creation for Oklahoma, champions federal layoffs that devastate OKC, and supports a bill projected to kill over a million jobs.

### **ELECTION DENIAL AND JANUARY 6: TRIED TO HAVE IT BOTH WAYS**

Bice [voted to overturn the 2020 election results](https://www.readfrontier.org/stories/majority-of-oklahoma-members-object-to-bidens-election-after-riot-overtakes-u-s-capitol/) on January 6 — earning an ["F" from the Republican Accountability PAC](https://accountability.gop/profile/rep-stephanie-bice/). She then [voted for the bipartisan January 6 commission](https://okcfox.com/news/connect-to-congress/congresswoman-bice-backs-jan-6th-commission-other-ok-lawmakers-vote-against) — the only Oklahoma delegate to do so — then [dismissed the investigation as partisan](https://bice.house.gov/media/weekly-columns/democrats-partisan-jan-6th-committee).

**Message:** Bice tried to have it every way on January 6 — voting to overturn the election, then for the investigation, then against its findings.
