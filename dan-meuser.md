# How To Win Against Dan Meuser

Dan Meuser promised his rural Pennsylvania constituents he would fight for them, but his record tells a different story. He voted against the [Inflation Reduction Act](https://meuser.house.gov/media/press-releases/congressman-dan-meuser-votes-no-vote-inflation-reduction-act) that capped insulin at $35/month and let Medicare negotiate drug prices, voted against [extending ACA subsidies](https://www.wvia.org/news/local/2026-02-14/1-in-5-pennie-enrollees-drop-health-insurance-after-expiration-of-affordable-care-act-tax-credits) that kept insurance affordable -- and after those subsidies expired, 85,000 Pennsylvanians lost coverage, with rural counties like those in PA-09 hit hardest. He then voted for the [One Big Beautiful Bill](https://www.pa.gov/governor/newsroom/2025-press-releases/fact-sheet--how-many-lose-medicaid-and-snap-as-a-result-of-recon), which will strip Medicaid from nearly 18,000 people and SNAP benefits from 8,185 people in his own district -- and when confronted with those numbers, [called them "complete, utter nonsense."](https://www.abc27.com/pennsylvania-politics/meuser-fires-back-at-shapiro-after-medicare-snap-callout/) He praised Trump's tariffs as the start of a ["golden era in manufacturing"](https://www.newsmax.com/politics/dan-meuser-tariffs-trump/2025/04/03/id/1205525/) -- but since those tariffs took effect, the U.S. has lost over 40,000 manufacturing jobs, including up to [350 at a Mack Trucks plant in Pennsylvania](https://www.cbsnews.com/philadelphia/news/mack-trucks-layoffs-lehigh-valley-pennsylvania/). And through it all, Meuser has been caught violating the STOCK Act twice -- [hiding up to $600,000 in COVID-era stock trades](https://www.legistorm.com/pro_news/2816/rep-meuser-didnt-report-lucrative-stock-buys-on-time.html) and a [$750,000-to-$1.5 million NVIDIA sale](https://www.notus.org/money/stock-act-violations-blame-financial-adviser) -- while chairing the House Financial Services Subcommittee on Oversight and Investigations.

### **HEALTH CARE: VOTED TO STRIP COVERAGE FROM THOUSANDS OF HIS OWN CONSTITUENTS**

Meuser voted against the [Inflation Reduction Act](https://meuser.house.gov/media/press-releases/congressman-dan-meuser-votes-no-vote-inflation-reduction-act), which capped insulin at $35/month for Medicare recipients and authorized Medicare to negotiate drug prices for the first time.
He voted against [extending ACA premium subsidies](https://www.wvia.org/news/local/2026-02-14/1-in-5-pennie-enrollees-drop-health-insurance-after-expiration-of-affordable-care-act-tax-credits); after they expired, 85,000 Pennsylvanians lost health coverage, with [rural counties hit hardest](https://agency.pennie.com/one-in-five-pennie-enrollees-drop-health-coverage-due-to-expired-federal-tax-credits/). A diabetic Berwick resident said she would skip doctor visits because she could not afford the $35 copay.
He voted for the [One Big Beautiful Bill](https://www.pa.gov/governor/newsroom/2025-press-releases/fact-sheet--how-many-lose-medicaid-and-snap-as-a-result-of-recon), which will strip Medicaid from 17,967 people in PA-09. Healthcare advocates [warned this could trigger hospital closures](https://www.wvia.org/news/local/2025-05-21/healthcare-activists-warn-medicaid-cuts-will-hurt-all-pennsylvanians-prompt-hospital-closures-in-region) in his region, and the [Hospital Association said](https://www.spotlightpa.org/statecollege/2026/02/pennsylvania-rural-hospitals-medicaid-cuts-closure-health/) less than half of Pennsylvania's hospitals have margins necessary for long-term stability.

**Message:** Meuser voted against every measure that would have lowered health care costs for his rural constituents, then voted for a bill that will strip coverage from nearly 18,000 of them -- and dismissed the consequences as "nonsense."

### **AFFORDABILITY: CHAMPIONED TARIFFS THAT RAISED PRICES, THEN CUT FOOD ASSISTANCE**

Meuser praised tariffs as a ["golden era in manufacturing"](https://www.newsmax.com/politics/dan-meuser-tariffs-trump/2025/04/03/id/1205525/), but the [Yale Budget Lab found](https://budgetlab.yale.edu/research/state-us-tariffs-november-17-2025) tariffs cost the average household $1,700/year, with low-income families hit three times harder. Pennsylvania had the [nation's highest grocery price increases](https://www.supermarketnews.com/finance/pennsylvania-has-nation-s-highest-grocery-price-hikes-report) at 8.2%, with rural shoppers paying 7.6% more.
He voted for the One Big Beautiful Bill, which [strips SNAP benefits from 8,185 people in his district](https://www.pa.gov/governor/newsroom/2025-press-releases/fact-sheet--how-many-lose-medicaid-and-snap-as-a-result-of-recon), and [Feeding America estimated](https://feedingamericaaction.org/wp-content/uploads/FAQ_OBBBA.pdf) the law could take 6 billion meals off the table every year.
Despite promising tariffs would "bring down inflation," Meuser [later acknowledged](https://www.newsmax.com/politics/danmeuser-economy-affordability/2025/12/08/id/1237493/) grocery prices were still rising eight months later.

**Message:** Meuser championed tariffs that raised prices on the rural families who can least afford it, then voted to cut their food assistance -- all while blaming everyone but himself.

### **JOBS AND THE ECONOMY: VOTED AGAINST EVERY INVESTMENT, THEN CHEERED THE POLICIES KILLING JOBS**

As a Problem Solvers Caucus member, Meuser backed a bipartisan infrastructure framework -- then [voted against the Infrastructure Investment and Jobs Act](https://thehill.com/homenews/house/573409-whip-list-how-house-democrats-say-theyll-vote-on-infrastructure-bill/), which delivered [$14.8 billion to Pennsylvania](https://keystonenewsroom.com/2024/02/12/heres-how-pennsylvanias-infrastructure-has-benefited-from-some-major-federal-investments/) including specific projects in his district.
He voted against the [CHIPS and Science Act](https://www.govtrack.us/congress/votes/117-2022/h404) despite his own caucus calling for its funding. The law has since brought [$172 million and 800+ jobs](https://dced.pa.gov/newsroom/leading-the-way-in-innovation-shapiro-administration-celebrates-federal-investment-of-up-to-172-million-in-two-pennsylvania-technology-companies/) to the Lehigh Valley.
Since tariffs took effect, the U.S. has [lost over 40,000 manufacturing jobs](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/jobs-manufacturing-trump-tariffs-economy/). Mack Trucks [laid off up to 350 workers](https://www.cbsnews.com/philadelphia/news/mack-trucks-layoffs-lehigh-valley-pennsylvania/) at its Lehigh Valley plant just two weeks after Meuser celebrated tariffs on Newsmax.

**Message:** Meuser voted against every bipartisan jobs bill that would have put Pennsylvanians to work, then championed the tariffs that are destroying manufacturing jobs in his own backyard.

### **STOCK ACT VIOLATIONS: SERIAL INSIDER TRADING VIOLATIONS WHILE CHAIRING OVERSIGHT**

Meuser's family made [up to $600,000 in stock purchases](https://www.legistorm.com/pro_news/2816/rep-meuser-didnt-report-lucrative-stock-buys-on-time.html) during the March 2020 COVID market crash while Congress was receiving classified pandemic briefings, then hid the trades for over a year. Alphabet stock alone may have netted the family $150,000+.
In 2025, Meuser [violated the STOCK Act again](https://www.notus.org/money/stock-act-violations-blame-financial-adviser), failing to disclose a $750K-to-$1.5M NVIDIA stock sale for nearly 10 months -- [while chairing the Financial Services Subcommittee on Oversight](https://www.benzinga.com/insights/government/25/08/47126374/pennsylvania-rep-daniel-meuser-sold-up-to-1-50m-worth-of-nvidia-stock).
Nearly half of his 2024 fundraising came from corporate PACs, including $95,000 from financial services firms he is supposed to oversee.

**Message:** The congressman who chairs the oversight subcommittee can't follow the disclosure laws himself -- and he's been caught twice.

### **PRIDE MOBILITY AND PAY-TO-PLAY: A PATTERN OF SELF-DEALING**

Before Congress, Meuser ran Pride Mobility, where workers had to [sue to recover $2.1 million in retirement savings](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/employee-benefits/pride-mobility-stock-plan-cash-lawsuit-settles-for-2-1-million) after the company invested their ESOP funds into "dismally performing" assets.
Pride Mobility was [fined for hiring undocumented workers](https://www.timesleader.com/archive/272762/stories-records-on-fine-for-illegals-gone2c-meuser-says103673) -- the largest immigration fine in Pennsylvania manufacturing history -- while Meuser now campaigns on border security.
Meuser [donated nearly $60,000 to Tom Corbett's campaign](https://www.dailyitem.com/news/135g-job-tails-60g-donation/article_3774b253-e87e-5c83-98a9-8be101ad81f0.html) before being appointed to a $135,000/year Cabinet post as Secretary of Revenue, where he oversaw an [unconstitutional lottery privatization scheme](https://www.washingtonpost.com/blogs/govbeat/wp/2014/01/02/pennsylvanias-corbett-will-drop-lottery-privatization-plan/) and a botched $130 million computer contract.

**Message:** Meuser cheated his own employees out of their retirement, hired undocumented workers while preaching border security, and bought a Cabinet appointment -- a pattern of putting himself first.
