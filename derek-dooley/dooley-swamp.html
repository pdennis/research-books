Overview

Dooley’s brother secured millions in contracts with the Georgia school system. Daniel Dooley’s childhood best friend was Governor Brian Kemp, the contracts’ signatory. Both Daniel Dooley and Governor Kemp financially supported Derek Dooley’s campaign. 

Dooley’s Brother, Daniel Dooley, Made Millions From Georgia School Contracts Inked By His Childhood Best Friend 

Governor Brian Kemp Awarded Millions In Georgia School System Contracts To His Childhood Best Friend’s Company 

Daniel Dooley Was Kemp’s Childhood Friend. According to 11 Alive, “Gov. Brian Kemp and one of Georgia's U.S. Senate hopefuls have ties to a high-profile government contractor whose company has made millions from school safety concerns. When Kemp visited Douglas County's Chapel Hill High School in May of 2019, photos taken by the governor's office show that one of the people waiting in the school was Kemp's childhood friend Daniel Dooley.” [11 Alive, 5/28/26]

Daniel Dooley Founded Centegix Months Before Kemp Was Touting The Company's Security System That Was Installed At Chapel Hill High School. According to 11 Alive, “Kemp was at the school to talk up a security system installed at Chapel Hill from a company called Centegix.  Dooley had founded the company months earlier.” [11 Alive, 5/28/26]

School Systems Across Georgia Used Centegix System And Records show The Company Made Millions From Georgia School System Contracts. According to 11 Alive, “Now, school systems across Georgia are using the Centegix system – and records show Dooley's company has gotten millions in Georgia school system contracts.” [11 Alive, 5/28/26]

Under Kemp, Georgia Offered Grants To School Districts To Help Pay For Security Systems. According to 11 Alive, “Under Governor Kemp, the state has offered grants to school districts to help pay for their safety systems. State records show the Dooley-run Centegix has received more than $27 million in contracts with Georgia school systems from 2019 through last year.” [11 Alive, 5/28/26]

Kemp And Daniel Dooley Financially Supported Derek Dooley’s Campaign 

Daniel Dooley And Kemp Poured Money Into Dooley’s Campaign. According to 11 Alive, “Federal records also show that Daniel Dooley has dropped $100,000 into Kemp's political action committee. Kemp's PAC has, in turn, poured money into Derek Dooley's US Senate campaign.” [11 Alive, 5/28/26]
