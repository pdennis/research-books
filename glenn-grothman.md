# How To Win Against Glenn Grothman

Glenn Grothman represents the congressional district with [more manufacturing jobs than almost any other in the nation](https://grothman.house.gov/news/documentsingle.aspx?DocumentID=428) -- but he has consistently worked against the workers and farmers who drive it. He ["fully" endorsed Trump's tariffs](https://grothman.house.gov/news/documentsingle.aspx?DocumentID=4367) that UW-Madison economists project will cost Wisconsin dairy farmers [\$1-2 billion in income losses](https://www.wxpr.org/food-and-agriculture/2025-05-02/the-potential-impact-of-tariffs-on-wisconsins-dairy-industry), then [acknowledged he was "not as big a fan"](https://pbswisconsin.org/watch/us-rep-glenn-grothman-on-2025-government-shutdown-politics/us-rep-glenn-grothman-on-2025-government-shutdown-politics-nqr7fz/) when the damage became clear. He voted for a reconciliation bill that the Wisconsin Department of Health Services says will strip health coverage from [276,175 Wisconsinites](https://www.dhs.wisconsin.gov/news/releases/071525.htm) and put [nearly 90,000 at risk of losing food assistance](https://www.dhs.wisconsin.gov/news/releases/052225.htm) -- then [called expanded health coverage a "nightmare"](https://crooksandliars.com/2025/12/gop-congressman-gets-meloncholy-about) on television. He personally [introduced an amendment to kick young adults off their parents' insurance](https://younginvincibles.org/statement-rep-grothman-ahca-amendment-attacks-young-adult-coverage/), voted against the [\$35 insulin cap](https://wisconsinindependent.com/health-care/new-cap-on-medicare-part-d-out-of-pocket-prescription-costs-goes-into-effect-2/), voted against the [\$6.9 billion Bipartisan Infrastructure Law](https://www.govtrack.us/congress/votes/117-2021/h369) and the [CHIPS Act](https://www.govtrack.us/congress/votes/117-2022/h404), and proposed [eliminating the mandatory day off for Wisconsin workers](https://lacrossetribune.com/news/local/wisconsin-legislators-circulate-7-day-work-week-bill/article_8a0101ec-7c11-11e3-a2c5-001a4bcf887a.html). His decades-long record of inflammatory statements -- calling Kwanzaa a fraud that should be ["slapped down"](https://captimes.com/news/local/writers/jessica_vanegeren/glenn-grothmans-anti-kwanzaa-comments-create-ruckus/article_fdbe64ec-fe07-59c8-a535-2e544f50ea76.html), saying ["money is more important for men"](https://www.politifact.com/article/2014/nov/17/conservative-congressman-elect-glenn-grothman-his-/), and telling a reporter Democrats supported Kamala Harris ["because of her ethnic background"](https://thehill.com/homenews/campaign/4785609-gop-democratic-party-kamala-harris-biden-2024-presidential-nominee/) -- makes him deeply out of step with mainstream Wisconsin voters.

### **HEALTH CARE: VOTED TO STRIP COVERAGE FROM 276,000 WISCONSINITES**

Grothman voted to advance a budget the Wisconsin DHS says will strip health coverage from [276,175 Wisconsinites](https://www.dhs.wisconsin.gov/news/releases/071525.htm), including [63,000 adults without dependents](https://www.dhs.wisconsin.gov/news/releases/071525.htm) at highest risk. He personally [introduced an amendment to kick young adults off their parents' insurance](https://younginvincibles.org/statement-rep-grothman-ahca-amendment-attacks-young-adult-coverage/) by lowering the coverage age from 26 to 23.
He voted against the [Inflation Reduction Act's \$35 insulin cap and Medicare drug price negotiation](https://wisconsinindependent.com/health-care/new-cap-on-medicare-part-d-out-of-pocket-prescription-costs-goes-into-effect-2/).
He said "we don't have the money at all, at all, at all" for expanded health coverage a -- while ACA enrollment dropped and [premiums for everyday people skyrocketed](https://www.wpr.org/health/fewer-wisconsinites-enroll-in-aca-marketplace-as-health-care-premiums-skyrocket) after the subsidies he voted against expired.

**Message:** Glenn Grothman personally tried to kick young adults off their parents' insurance, voted against the $35 insulin cap, and now calls health coverage for 276,000 Wisconsinites a "nightmare."

### **AFFORDABILITY: ENDORSED TARIFFS DEVASTATING WISCONSIN DAIRY**

Grothman ["fully" endorsed Trump's tariffs](https://grothman.house.gov/news/documentsingle.aspx?DocumentID=4367) that UW-Madison economists project will cost Wisconsin dairy farmers [\$1-2 billion in income losses](https://www.wxpr.org/food-and-agriculture/2025-05-02/the-potential-impact-of-tariffs-on-wisconsins-dairy-industry) and reduce individual farm income by [up to 50%](https://www.dailycardinal.com/article/2025/06/uw-madison-professor-predicts-new-tariffs-will-cost-dairy-farmers-a-quarter-of-their-income). China imposed a [125% tariff on U.S. dairy](https://www.wxpr.org/food-and-agriculture/2025-05-02/the-potential-impact-of-tariffs-on-wisconsins-dairy-industry) while Canada's 25% tariff remained in effect.
He then voted for the [deepest SNAP cut in history](https://www.cbpp.org/research/food-assistance/house-reconciliation-bill-proposes-deepest-snap-cut-in-history-would-take), putting [nearly 90,000 Wisconsinites](https://www.dhs.wisconsin.gov/news/releases/052225.htm) at risk of losing food assistance and costing the state [\$314 million per year](https://www.dhs.wisconsin.gov/news/releases/052225.htm) -- while [suggesting 41 million Americans on SNAP was proof of fraud](https://crooksandliars.com/2025/12/gop-congressman-gets-meloncholy-about).
He [introduced legislation to eliminate the Low-Income Housing Tax Credit](https://grothman.house.gov/news/documentsingle.aspx?DocumentID=4690), the nation's primary affordable housing program responsible for 3.6 million affordable rental homes.

**Message:** Glenn Grothman "fully" endorsed the tariffs devastating Wisconsin dairy farmers, voted for the deepest SNAP cut in history, and wants to eliminate the nation's primary affordable housing program.

### **JOBS AND THE ECONOMY: VOTED AGAINST EVERY MAJOR JOB-CREATING BILL**

Grothman voted against the [Bipartisan Infrastructure Law](https://www.govtrack.us/congress/votes/117-2021/h369) that has delivered [\$6.9 billion to Wisconsin](https://spectrumnews1.com/wi/milwaukee/news/2024/05/14/biden-administration-wisconsin-infrastructure-funding) for roads, bridges, broadband, and water -- then [introduced a bill to cancel the EV charger program](https://wisconsinindependent.com/infrastructure/electric-vehicle-owners-say-lack-of-charging-stations-harms-businesses-in-rural-areas/) it created.
He voted against the [CHIPS and Science Act](https://www.govtrack.us/congress/votes/117-2022/h404) investing $52 billion in domestic semiconductor manufacturing -- critical to the supply chains his district's manufacturers depend on.
He proposed [eliminating the mandatory day off for Wisconsin workers](https://lacrossetribune.com/news/local/wisconsin-legislators-circulate-7-day-work-week-bill/article_8a0101ec-7c11-11e3-a2c5-001a4bcf887a.html), [authored the repeal of Wisconsin's Equal Pay Act](https://captimes.com/news/local/govt-and-politics/capitol-report/capitol-report-short-lived-pay-equity-law-appears-headed-for-repeal/article_5c0ed33b-3111-53bb-bed2-4e2aff103070.html), and called for [laying off 100,000-200,000 federal workers](https://urbanmilwaukee.com/2025/10/05/rep-grothman-wants-to-lay-off-up-to-200000-federal-employees/).

**Message:** Glenn Grothman voted against every major bill creating Wisconsin jobs, tried to eliminate your day off, and wants to lay off 200,000 federal workers even as the Trump economy stalls.

### **RACIALLY INFLAMMATORY STATEMENTS: A PATTERN HIS OWN PARTY REBUKED**

Grothman called Kwanzaa a fraud that should be ["slapped down"](https://captimes.com/news/local/writers/jessica_vanegeren/glenn-grothmans-anti-kwanzaa-comments-create-ruckus/article_fdbe64ec-fe07-59c8-a535-2e544f50ea76.html), said Democrats supported Kamala Harris ["because of her ethnic background"](https://thehill.com/homenews/campaign/4785609-gop-democratic-party-kamala-harris-biden-2024-presidential-nominee/) -- drawing a [rebuke from his own party leadership](https://thehill.com/homenews/campaign/4785609-gop-democratic-party-kamala-harris-biden-2024-presidential-nominee/) -- and called Planned Parenthood ["the most racist organization"](https://www.huffpost.com/entry/glenn-grothman-planned-parenthood-racist-wisconsin_n_2552776) in the country.
He called Martin Luther King Jr. Day ["an insult" to taxpayers](https://www.politifact.com/article/2014/nov/17/conservative-congressman-elect-glenn-grothman-his-/) and [criticized the U.S. for opposing Uganda's anti-homosexuality law](https://captimes.com/news/local/writers/steven_elbow/glenn-grothman-blasts-u-s-moves-against-draconian-ugandan-homosexuality-law/article_b77f921e-b93f-565f-9b65-9c6cc25bac56.html).

**Message:** Glenn Grothman has a decades-long pattern of racially inflammatory statements so extreme that even his own party leadership rebuked him.

### **HOSTILITY TOWARD WOMEN: "MONEY IS MORE IMPORTANT FOR MEN"**

Grothman [introduced a bill classifying single parenthood as a contributing factor to child abuse](https://healthland.time.com/2012/03/14/according-to-a-wisconsin-bill-single-moms-are-a-child-abuse-threat/), said single mothers ["expect government handouts"](https://madison.com/news/local/writers/todd_milewski/glenn-grothman-single-parents-getting-bribe-to-avoid-work-marriage/article_13356840-78b5-11e4-8a54-c375f6358e8d.html), and declared ["our country is not going to survive if we continue this war on men."](https://www.salon.com/2014/04/04/wisconsin_goper_running_for_u_s_house_our_country_is_not_going_to_survive_if_we_continue_this_war_on_men/)
When shown data that college-educated women earn 80% as much as men, he responded, ["You could argue that money is more important for men"](https://www.politifact.com/article/2014/nov/17/conservative-congressman-elect-glenn-grothman-his-/) -- then [authored the repeal of Wisconsin's Equal Pay Act](https://captimes.com/news/local/govt-and-politics/capitol-report/capitol-report-short-lived-pay-equity-law-appears-headed-for-repeal/article_5c0ed33b-3111-53bb-bed2-4e2aff103070.html).

**Message:** Glenn Grothman said "money is more important for men," repealed Wisconsin's Equal Pay Act, and introduced a bill labeling single parents as a child abuse risk.
