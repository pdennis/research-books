# How To Win Against Buddy Carter

Buddy Carter is a pharmacist worth an [estimated $28-33 million](https://www.invezz.com/news/2024/07/26/congressman-buddy-carter-sells-up-to-1-million-in-ameris-bancorp-stock-before-earnings-report-whats-behind-the-move/) whose own pharmacy [distributed nearly 3 million opioid pills](https://thecurrentga.org/2022/10/31/carter-removed-from-wrongful-death-lawsuit-involving-savannah-opioid-overdose-victim/) in Chatham County while his primary opioid supplier McKesson gave him [$55,000 in campaign contributions](https://www.fec.gov/data/committee/C00543967/). He accepted [over $500,000 from the pharmaceutical industry](https://www.fec.gov/data/committee/C00543967/) while voting against [$35 insulin caps](https://www.npr.org/2022/03/31/1090085513/house-passes-bill-to-cap-insulin-prices) — calling it a "socialist plan" — and voting for [$625 billion in Medicaid cuts](https://thecurrentga.org/2025/05/15/u-s-house-panel-passes-gop-plan-that-cuts-medicaid-by-625b-adds-work-requirement/) that could leave 750,000 Georgians uninsured. He voted against [every major job-creating law](https://www.georgiademocrat.org/buddy-carter-touts-president-bidens-bipartisan-infrastructure-law-after-voting-against-it/) of the past decade — the Infrastructure Law, the CHIPS Act, and the Inflation Reduction Act — then [took credit](https://www.canarymedia.com/articles/politics/buddy-carter-georgia-reconcilation-bill-solar-evs) for the [$7.6 billion Hyundai Metaplant](https://www.canarymedia.com/articles/politics/buddy-carter-georgia-reconcilation-bill-solar-evs) those laws brought to his district. He praised Trump's tariffs as ["home-run economic policies"](https://buddycarter.house.gov/news/documentsingle.aspx?DocumentID=16222) while the [Port of Savannah lost 9.6% of its cargo](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2025/07/29/georgia-port-of-savannah-statistics-tariffs/1be921c2-6c8b-11f0-aab6-8141d7095676_story.html) and Georgia's [$28 billion poultry industry](https://www.atlantanewsfirst.com/2025/04/25/increasing-tariffs-could-threaten-georgias-poultry-industry-officials-warn/) faced retaliatory trade barriers. He [voted to overturn the 2020 election](https://thecurrentga.org/2021/01/07/carter-objection-to-biden-certification-was-about-state-process-not-trump/) hours after the Capitol attack, then in 2026 [called peaceful church protesters "insurrectionists"](https://buddycarter.house.gov/news/documentsingle.aspx?DocumentID=16292) and demanded they be placed "in handcuffs."

### **BIG PHARMA'S FAVORITE SON**

Carter accepted [over $500,000 from the pharmaceutical and health industry](https://www.fec.gov/data/committee/C00543967/) while his pharmacy [distributed 2.8 million opioid pills](https://thecurrentga.org/2022/10/31/carter-removed-from-wrongful-death-lawsuit-involving-savannah-opioid-overdose-victim/) and his opioid supplier McKesson gave him [$55,000](https://www.fec.gov/data/committee/C00543967/). He [called himself "a big fan of big pharma"](https://www.c-span.org/video/?436157) on C-SPAN and [transferred his pharmacies to his wife](https://www.ajc.com/blog/politics/buddy-carter-faces-conflict-interest-questions-over-pharmacy-bills/cS6MRQq7C7V2F4WVWURLbO/) then co-sponsored bills benefiting them.
He voted against the [$35 insulin cap](https://www.npr.org/2022/03/31/1090085513/house-passes-bill-to-cap-insulin-prices), calling it a "socialist plan," and called the IRA drug pricing law ["the worst legislation I have ever seen"](https://www.axios.com/2022/09/23/gop-drug-price-repeal-target) — opposing the first-ever Medicare drug price negotiations.

**Message:** Carter took half a million from Big Pharma, then voted to keep drug prices high for the seniors in his district.

### **CARTER VOTED TO GUT HEALTH CARE IN A STATE WITH THE SECOND-HIGHEST UNINSURED RATE**

Carter voted for [$625 billion in Medicaid cuts](https://thecurrentga.org/2025/05/15/u-s-house-panel-passes-gop-plan-that-cuts-medicaid-by-625b-adds-work-requirement/) and said he ["would actually like to see more"](https://www.democrats.senate.gov/newsroom/press-releases/house-republicans-spent-months-promising-to-not-gut-medicaid_this-morning-republicans-passed-their-tax-plan-to-pay-for-tax-breaks-for-the-ultra-wealthy-by-cutting-over-700-billion-from-medicaid-and-stripping-coverage-away-from-nearly-14-million-americans-the-largest-cut-to-healthcare-in-american-history) cuts, while Georgia is tied for the [second-highest uninsured rate](https://www.gpb.org/news/2025/09/30/gas-buddy-carter-says-he-wont-consider-restoring-medicaid-money-avert-shutdown) in the country.
He voted against [extending ACA subsidies](https://georgiademocrat.org/ajc-reps-buddy-carter-and-mike-collins-once-again-vote-against-aca-tax-credit-extension/) as premiums could spike up to [$885/month in his district](https://georgiademocrat.org/ajc-reps-buddy-carter-and-mike-collins-once-again-vote-against-aca-tax-credit-extension/), and Georgia hospitals face a [$3.7 billion revenue loss](https://www.gpb.org/news/2025/09/30/gas-buddy-carter-says-he-wont-consider-restoring-medicaid-money-avert-shutdown).
Evans Memorial Hospital [closed its OB/GYN unit](https://www.warnock.senate.gov/newsroom/press-releases/icymi-warnock-visits-rural-hospital-facing-3-3-million-in-annual-cuts-due-to-gop-tax-bill/) and faces $3.3 million in annual cuts from the bill Carter championed — and he [dodged questions](https://www.effinghamherald.net/local/qa-rep-buddy-carter-explains-support-for-one-big-beautiful-bill/) about it from local press.

**Message:** Carter voted to gut health care in a state where one in five residents already can't afford insurance — then refused to answer for it.

### **CARTER DEFENDED TARIFFS THAT THREATEN THE PORT OF SAVANNAH AND GEORGIA'S POULTRY INDUSTRY**

Carter [praised tariffs as "home-run economic policies"](https://buddycarter.house.gov/news/documentsingle.aspx?DocumentID=16222) while the Port of Savannah — supporting [609,000 Georgia jobs](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2025/07/29/georgia-port-of-savannah-statistics-tariffs/1be921c2-6c8b-11f0-aab6-8141d7095676_story.html) — saw cargo volumes [drop 9.6%](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2025/07/29/georgia-port-of-savannah-statistics-tariffs/1be921c2-6c8b-11f0-aab6-8141d7095676_story.html). Retaliatory tariffs threaten Georgia's [$28 billion poultry industry](https://www.atlantanewsfirst.com/2025/04/25/increasing-tariffs-could-threaten-georgias-poultry-industry-officials-warn/) and 88,000 jobs.
The Yale Budget Lab estimated tariffs cost the average household [$1,800 per year](https://budgetlab.yale.edu/research/state-us-tariffs-october-17-2025), hitting lower-income families more than three times harder as a share of income.

**Message:** Carter called tariffs a "home run" while the Port of Savannah bled jobs and Georgia families paid the bill.
