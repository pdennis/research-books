# Rep. Daniel Webster (FL-11): Social Security Cuts

### Proposed Cutting Social Security Benefits For Seniors

**Webster Proposed Rolling Back Social Security Cost-Of-Living Adjustments During His 2010 Campaign.** According to HuffPost, Webster said at a Tea Party forum: "'My number one priority would be to cut spending, turn off the spigot. We can do that, and the way we would do that is to roll back the budget to 2007 [...]  Does it take back some of the COLAs for the entitlement programs? Yes, it does that, too.'" The Florida Alliance for Retired Americans responded: "'For Webster to propose cuts of $100 per person to a program that has served seniors well for so long is an indication that, if he's elected, seniors will be the first ones to suffer.'" [HuffPost, [8/16/10](https://www.huffpost.com/entry/daniel-webster-gop-house_n_683293)]
