# Rep. Daniel Webster (FL-11): IBLP Ministry Ties and Extreme Social Views

### Decades-Long Ties To Controversial Ministry Whose Founder Resigned Over Sexual Harassment

**Webster Had A "Decades-Long Affiliation" With The Institute In Basic Life Principles, Whose Founder Resigned After 30+ Women Accused Him Of Sexual Harassment.** According to Talking Points Memo, Webster "has a decades-long affiliation with the Institute in Basic Life Principles, the controversial ministry whose founder, Bill Gothard, resigned last year after more than 30 women accused him of sexual harassment." TPM reported that "IBLP subjected young followers to victim-blaming 'counseling' for rape, as well as grueling work schedules at its facilities for little or no pay, requiring women to engage in gendered tasks that included scrubbing carpets on their hands and knees." [Talking Points Memo, [9/30/15](https://talkingpointsmemo.com/cafe/dan-webster-speaker-running-iblp-bill-gothard)]

- **Webster Boasted At A 2003 IBLP Conference That He "Made Every Commitment" The Organization's Founder Asked Of Him.** According to Talking Points Memo, "In a 2003 speech at an IBLP conference, 'Discover the True Qualities of Leadership,' Webster boasted of how he diligently conducts both his private and public life according to the 'commitments' he made to the principles he learned at IBLP seminars." Webster said he "'made every commitment' Gothard asked of attendees at his seminars. 'I raised my hand every time, because it absolutely changed my life.'" [Talking Points Memo, [9/30/15](https://talkingpointsmemo.com/cafe/dan-webster-speaker-running-iblp-bill-gothard)]

- **IBLP's Textbook Dictated That "The Wife Is To Submit To The Husband" And That "Equal Authority" In A Marriage Is "Satan's Goal."** According to Talking Points Memo, the nearly 400-page 1986 IBLP textbook "dictates that, as the Alan Grayson attack ad highlighted, 'the wife is to submit to the husband in order not to blaspheme God's word' and that 'equal authority' in a marriage is 'Satan's goal.'" The textbook also "warns against the use of any birth control, claiming that God 'assigns a special woe' to those who use it." [Talking Points Memo, [9/30/15](https://talkingpointsmemo.com/cafe/dan-webster-speaker-running-iblp-bill-gothard)]

- **Josh Duggar Acknowledged Webster As An Ally At An IBLP Conference Two Months After The Founder's Resignation.** According to Talking Points Memo, "In May 2014, two months after Gothard resigned from IBLP, Josh Duggar — who a year later would himself be embroiled in a sex abuse scandal — spoke at an IBLP conference in Nashville." Duggar said: "'I know Congressman Webster is here. We know that our duty is to do what God has given us to do.'" [Talking Points Memo, [9/30/15](https://talkingpointsmemo.com/cafe/dan-webster-speaker-running-iblp-bill-gothard)]

### Sponsored Bill That Would Have Trapped Women In Abusive Marriages

**Webster Sponsored A "Covenant Marriage" Bill That Would Have Barred Divorce Except In Cases Of Proven Adultery.** According to FactCheck.org, "Webster introduced the bill, HB 1585, in the Florida House of Representatives in 1990. It would have provided for a form of marriage known as a 'covenant marriage,' which could only be dissolved if adultery is proven." The bill stated: "'A divorce may be granted on grounds on adultery if the defendant has been guilty of adultery, but if it appears that both parties to the marriage have been guilty of adultery or connived at the adultery of the other, the divorce shall be denied.'" [FactCheck.org, [10/7/10](https://www.factcheck.org/2010/10/grayson-gets-it-right/)]

**Webster Opposes Abortion Even In Cases Of Rape And Incest.** According to FactCheck.org, "it's true that he would prevent women from obtaining abortions even when the pregnancies result from rape, just as the ad says. And that goes for incest as well. He has been endorsed by the Republican National Coalition for Life, which states: '[W]e have listed the Republican Congressional Candidates whose responses to the RNC/Life Questionnaire indicate they are faithfully pro-life, and do not justify abortion for babies who are conceived through rape or incest, have a handicap, or a genetic defect.'" [FactCheck.org, [9/27/10](https://www.factcheck.org/2010/09/rep-grayson-lowers-the-bar/)]
