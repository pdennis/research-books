# How To Win Against Lauren Boebert

Lauren Boebert promised ["I will never vote for legislation that will leave Coloradans without health care coverage"](https://www.denverpost.com/2020/08/16/boebert-mitsch-bush-3rd-congressional-health-care-insurance/) — then voted for the [largest Medicaid cuts in American history](https://coloradosun.com/2025/07/03/colorado-votes-big-beautiful-bill-congress/), threatening coverage for up to 230,000 Coloradans, including nearly [45,000 children in her own district](https://www.coloradokids.org/statement-the-big-beautiful-bill-will-cause-big-problems-for-colorado-kids/). She voted against the [$35 insulin cap](https://www.npr.org/2022/03/31/1090085513/house-passes-bill-to-cap-insulin-prices) and called the Inflation Reduction Act ["a complete scam"](https://www.newsweek.com/biden-boebert-colorado-inflation-reduction-act-cs-wind-1847864) — even as it funded an $200 million expansion creating 850 jobs in Pueblo. She voted against every major jobs bill of the Biden era, then [shamelessly claimed credit for the funding they delivered](https://www.newsweek.com/lauren-boebert-called-out-white-house-voted-against-1903853), prompting Transportation Secretary Buttigieg to ask, "In what way do you believe that your support helped this project?" She voted to [keep Trump's 25% tariffs on Canada](https://www.cpr.org/2026/02/11/u-s-house-votes-against-trumps-tariff-on-canada-with-help-of-hurd-and-five-other-republicans/) while Colorado's beef industry faces a [$39 million revenue drop](https://www.denverpost.com/2025/10/23/colorado-agriculture-tariffs-beef/) and 86% of Colorado businesses say tariffs are a challenge. And her personal conduct — from getting [escorted out of a theater for groping her date and vaping](https://www.denverpost.com/2023/09/12/lauren-boebert-removed-beetlejuice-musical-denver/) to using [campaign donor money for personal rent](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/congress/rep-lauren-boebert-improperly-used-campaign-funds-rent-utilities-n1279976) — makes her a liability for any district she represents.

### **HEALTH CARE: VOTED FOR THE LARGEST MEDICAID CUTS IN HISTORY**

Boebert voted for the One Big Beautiful Bill, which CBO projects will cause [11.8 million people to lose health insurance](https://coloradosun.com/2025/07/03/colorado-votes-big-beautiful-bill-congress/) — including up to 230,000 Coloradans. Roughly 16% of her district relies on Medicaid, including [nearly 45,000 children](https://www.coloradokids.org/statement-the-big-beautiful-bill-will-cause-big-problems-for-colorado-kids/). [Prowers Medical Center in Lamar](https://www.cpr.org/2025/06/27/la-junta-hospital-lay-offs-medicaid-cuts/) is among six Colorado rural hospitals at risk of closure.
Voted [against the $35 insulin cap](https://www.npr.org/2022/03/31/1090085513/house-passes-bill-to-cap-insulin-prices) and against the Inflation Reduction Act's Medicare drug price negotiation. Voted [against the PACT Act](https://thehill.com/homenews/house/4864310-boebert-pact-act-vote/) for veterans exposed to burn pits, saying, "I'm not spending $600 billion forever."
A constituent [told Boebert her son would die](https://www.westword.com/news/boebert-evans-face-denver-protest-after-snap-medicaid-cuts-24668981/) without Medicaid. Boebert responded by [deflecting to transgender care](https://www.westword.com/news/boebert-evans-face-denver-protest-after-snap-medicaid-cuts-24668981/).

**Message:** Boebert promised to protect Coloradans' health care, then voted to gut Medicaid for 230,000 people — including 45,000 children in her own district — while rural hospitals close around her.

### **AFFORDABILITY: BACKED TARIFFS AND CUT FOOD ASSISTANCE**

Voted to [keep Trump's 25% tariffs on Canada](https://www.cpr.org/2026/02/11/u-s-house-votes-against-trumps-tariff-on-canada-with-help-of-hurd-and-five-other-republicans/) while the Yale Budget Lab estimates tariffs cost the average household [up to $2,400 per year](https://budgetlab.yale.edu/research/state-us-tariffs-september-26-2025). Her own Republican colleague Jeff Hurd broke with her to vote against the tariffs.
Tariffs are devastating Colorado agriculture: the beef industry faces a [$39 million revenue drop](https://www.denverpost.com/2025/10/23/colorado-agriculture-tariffs-beef/) and San Luis Valley potato farmers are [losing $150,000 per crop circle](https://www.colorado.gov/governor/news/governor-polis-releases-new-report-detailing-how-trumps-tariff-taxes-are-squeezing-colorado). [86% of Colorado businesses](https://www.denverpost.com/2025/11/30/tariffs-colorado-business-agriculture/) say tariffs are a challenge.
Voted to cut SNAP benefits for an estimated [131,000 low-income Coloradans](https://www.denverpost.com/2025/06/02/colorado-snap-trump-tax-bill/) while defending cuts by saying, "If you're able to work, then you should go to work."

**Message:** Boebert sided with Trump's tariffs over Colorado's farmers, ranchers, and families — raising costs by $2,400 a year while cutting food assistance for 131,000 Coloradans.

### **JOBS AND ECONOMY: VOTED AGAINST EVERY JOBS BILL, THEN CLAIMED CREDIT**

Called the Inflation Reduction Act "a complete scam" even as it created [850 jobs at CS Wind in Pueblo](https://www.newsweek.com/biden-boebert-colorado-inflation-reduction-act-cs-wind-1847864). Voted against the bipartisan infrastructure law, then [bragged about "securing" \$51.4 million](https://coloradosun.com/2024/06/07/lauren-boebert-infrastructure-funding-bill/) it funded for a bridge in Glenwood Springs.
Her own business — Shooters Grill — [lost more than \$600,000](https://www.thedailybeast.com/is-it-last-call-for-lauren-boeberts-gun-restaurant/) over three years and she owed [\$20,000 in tax liens](https://coloradonewsline.com/2021/02/04/rep-boebert-paid-off-nearly-20k-in-state-tax-liens-in-2020/) for failing to pay unemployment insurance on her workers.
Trump vetoed her own signature water project — a [130-mile pipeline for 50,000 people](https://www.cpr.org/2026/01/08/house-trump-arkansas-valley-conduit-veto-override-fails/) — and she could not persuade her own party to override it.

**Message:** Boebert voted against every major jobs bill, then took credit for the results. She ran a restaurant that lost $600,000 and stiffed her workers — and she could not even deliver her own signature project.

### **CHARACTER: A TRAIL OF SCANDALS AND SELF-DEALING**

[Escorted out of a Denver theater](https://www.denverpost.com/2023/09/12/lauren-boebert-removed-beetlejuice-musical-denver/) for groping her date and vaping during a family musical, then [lied about it](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/congress/surveillance-video-appears-contradict-boeberts-denial-was-vaping-beetl-rcna105246) before surveillance video proved otherwise.
Used [campaign funds to pay personal rent](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/congress/rep-lauren-boebert-improperly-used-campaign-funds-rent-utilities-n1279976), paid herself [\$22,000 in undocumented mileage reimbursements](https://www.cpr.org/2021/02/07/what-we-know-about-lauren-boeberts-campaign-payments-to-herself-for-driving-38000-miles/), and faces a new FEC complaint for spending [\$3,300 on a Kid Rock trip](https://www.washingtontimes.com/news/2025/dec/9/lauren-boebert-used-campaign-funds-see-kid-rock-fec-filings-show/).
Failed to disclose her husband's [nearly \$1 million in energy consulting income](https://coloradosun.com/2021/08/19/lauren-boebert-husband-terra-energy-productions/) while pushing to loosen drilling rules. Passed [zero bills](https://www.govtrack.us/congress/members/lauren_boebert/456805/report-card/2022) in her first Congress and ranked at the 0th percentile for bipartisanship.

**Message:** Boebert uses her office to enrich herself and her family while delivering nothing for her constituents — zero bills passed, zero accountability, endless scandals.

### **DISTRICT SWITCH: FLED AFTER NEARLY LOSING**

[Abandoned her own CO-3 district](https://www.cpr.org/2023/12/27/lauren-boebert-congress-2024-election-switching-districts/) after winning reelection by just 546 votes and switched to the safer CO-4 seat. A Republican state lawmaker [called it "carpetbagging."](https://www.axios.com/local/denver/2024/01/26/lauren-boebert-carpetbagger-debate-4th-district-colorado)

**Message:** Boebert did not stay and fight for the people she represented — she ran to a safer seat when the voters she had let down nearly threw her out.
