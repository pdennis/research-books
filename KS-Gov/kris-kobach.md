# **How To Win Against Kris Kobach**

Kansas Attorney General Kris Kobach was an out-of-touch extremist who refused to stand up for Kansas and repeatedly attacked Kansans’ healthcare, food assistance, and voting rights. Kobach supported restricting Medicaid through work requirements, and opposed Medicaid expansion and the Affordable Care Act. He backed Trump’s disastrous tariffs that hurt Kansas small businesses and the agricultural industry. Kobach also supported handing over sensitive SNAP data to the Trump administration and wanted to impose requirements that would make it harder for Kansans to afford groceries. He refused to defend Kansas’ critical grants from DOGE cuts as the cuts hindered Kansas’ ability to provide critical services and federal workers in Kansas were laid off. Kobach repeatedly attacked Kansas’ fundamental right to abortion and attempted to threaten Kansans’ access to abortion medication. Kobach also had a record of opposing rights to the LGBTQ+ community, like their ability to adopt or foster children, and used extreme anti-LGBTQ+ rhetoric. Kobach’s support for Trump’s deployment of federal forces against the consent of governors raised concerns about whether he would protect Kansans from Trump overstepping his authority. Kobach repeatedly promoted baseless voter fraud conspiracies and helped strategize a lawsuit to overturn the 2020 election. Kobach also had a history of lining his own pockets at the expense of taxpayers on personal and partisan battles. In May 2026, Kobach neglected Kansans and prioritized a trip to Ireland instead of attending the annual Kansas Law Enforcement Memorial honoring fallen Kansas officers.

### **Kris Kobach Supported Restricting Medicaid And Opposed The Affordable Care Act**

-   In 2018, Kris Kobach [proposed](https://www.breitbart.com/midterm-election/2018/10/17/kris-kobachs-welfare-reform-drug-tests-for-food-stamps-end-benefits-for-illegal-aliens/) imposing work requirements for Medicaid recipients in Kansas, saying it was “time to move people off welfare into jobs.” Kobach’s support for Medicaid work requirements aligned with [Trump’s “One Big Beautiful Bill” and its work requirements](https://www.kff.org/medicaid/a-closer-look-at-the-work-requirement-provisions-in-the-2025-federal-budget-reconciliation-law/) that were projected to strip more than [18,000 Medicaid recipients in Kansas](https://www.jec.senate.gov/public/_cache/files/9cfdee90-dd29-41e8-97da-b5dcdc0317cf/updated-jec-fact-sheet-on-district-level-impacts-of-health-care-cuts.pdf) of their health insurance by 2034. Additionally, Kobach [proposed](https://www.breitbart.com/midterm-election/2018/10/17/kris-kobachs-welfare-reform-drug-tests-for-food-stamps-end-benefits-for-illegal-aliens/) imposing [illegal](https://www.familiesusa.org/resources/drug-testing-in-medicaid-is-illegal-and-hurts-the-people-who-need-help-most/) drug testing requirements for Medicaid recipients in Kansas. 
-   Kobach repeatedly [voiced](https://x.com/KrisKobach1787/status/1459205599711825928) scrutiny and opposition to the Affordable Care Act, claiming the healthcare law was [“foisted upon”](https://www.breitbart.com/politics/2019/07/31/kris-kobach-democrats-offer-free-healthcare-to-illegals-while-americans-forced-to-buy-obamacare/) Americans. In 2026, [nearly 193,000 Kansans](https://www.kff.org/affordable-care-act/state-indicator/open-enrollment-marketplace-plan-selections/?currentTimeframe=0&selectedRows=%7B%22states%22:%7B%22kansas%22:%7B%7D%7D%7D&sortModel=%7B%22colId%22:%22Location%22,%22sort%22:%22asc%22%7D) relied on Affordable Care Act marketplace plans for their health care.
-   Kobach also opposed [Medicaid](https://x.com/KrisKobach1787/status/1055619373229137920) [expansion](https://x.com/KrisKobach1787/status/1038466609789194240), even though it could expand access to healthcare for [150,000 Kansans](https://www.npr.org/2024/04/22/1245146972/kansas-alabama-medicaid-expansion-uninsured-health-care-obamacare).

**Message:** Kris Kobach supported stripping away health care from thousands of Kansans who relied on Medicaid.

### **Kris Kobach Backed Trump's Tariffs, Endangering Kansas’ Economy**

-   In 2026, Kris Kobach [praised](https://x.com/KrisKobach1787/status/2026490481547301203) Trump’s tariffs and claimed his tariffs returned the “tradition” of imposing tariffs on foreign imports as the primary source of federal revenue. Kobach praised the tariffs even though the  [U.S. stock market crashed](https://www.npr.org/2025/04/04/nx-s1-5352362/markets-selloff-dow-trump-tariffs) in April 2025 due to Trump’s escalating global trade war.
-   Trump’s tariffs and trade war were hurting Kansas’ small businesses and agricultural industry, including [soybean farmers](https://jcpost.com/posts/d5263f88-f64c-43b6-83f3-164df680ba65). Trump’s tariffs impacted [Kansas’ agricultural industry](https://www.wibw.com/2025/03/11/there-is-some-uncertainty-kansas-ag-anticipates-impact-looming-tariffs/) due to the large exports of grain and soybeans to Mexico. From March 2025 to February 2026, Kansas’ small business importers paid an average of [$189,000 in additional tariffs.](https://www.americanprogress.org/article/in-the-first-year-president-trumps-tariffs-have-cost-small-business-importers-306000-on-average/) The tariffs also impacted and disrupted Kansas small businesses, including [Z&M Twisted Vines Vineyard in Lawrence](https://www.wibw.com/2025/03/11/there-is-some-uncertainty-kansas-ag-anticipates-impact-looming-tariffs/), [Rochester Brewing and Roasting in Kansas City, and The Active Hamper in Parkville](https://www.kshb.com/news/local-news/missouri/kansas-city/small-business-owners-in-kansas-city-say-tariff-uncertainty-has-already-cost-them-regardless-of-court-rulings).

**Message:** Kris Kobach supported Trump’s tariffs even as the policies and the trade war hurt Kansas small businesses and the agricultural industry.

### **Kris Kobach Supported SNAP And TANF Cuts And Handing Over Sensitive SNAP Data To The Trump Administration**

In 2026, Kobach [called](https://x.com/KrisKobach1787/status/2018863990349156698) on Kansas lawmakers to force Governor Kelly to submit the SNAP data to Trump’s Department of Agriculture.

In 2024, [more than 71,000 households in Kansas](https://www.fns.usda.gov/data-research/data-visualization/snap-community-characteristics-congressional-district-dashboard) relied on SNAP to afford groceries.

**Message:** Kris Kobach supported handing over sensitive SNAP data to the Trump administration and wanted to impose requirements that would make it harder for Kansans to afford groceries.

In 2023, Kobach [blasted](https://www.cjonline.com/story/news/healthcare/2023/02/20/after-kris-kobach-letter-walgreens-wont-send-mifepristone-in-kansas/69923382007/) Walgreens for planning to distribute mifepristone nationally, resulting in Walgreens saying they would not distribute or mail the abortion-inducing medication in Kansas. Kobach also signed onto a [legal brief](https://www.cjonline.com/story/news/healthcare/2023/02/20/after-kris-kobach-letter-walgreens-wont-send-mifepristone-in-kansas/69923382007/) in support of a lawsuit seeking to block FDA approval for mifepristone to [effectively ban abortion medication nationwide](https://www.kansascity.com/news/politics-government/article272471905.html). He also filed an [amicus brief](https://www.wibw.com/2023/04/18/attorney-general-urges-us-supreme-court-reiterate-right-regulate-abortion/) urging the U.S. Supreme Court to reject the FDA’s approval of abortion-inducing pills by mail.

In 2026, [Kobach's office defended anti-abortion laws](https://www.breitbart.com/politics/2026/08/04/kansas-judge-scraps-several-pro-life-laws-state-supreme-court/amp/) [with the extreme Alliance Defending Freedom,](https://archive.is/20260804232128/https://johnsoncountypost.com/2026/08/04/johnson-county-abortion-ruling-293199/#selection-909.0-913.30) including mandatory waiting periods, disclosure on abortion pill reversal, and requiring doctors to ask patients their “most important reason" for wanting an abortion. In 2024, Kobach also defended Kansas’ ban on a [second-trimester procedure](https://kansasreflector.com/2024/07/05/kansas-supreme-court-strikes-down-state-law-banning-second-trimester-abortion-procedure/) and additional licensing requirements for abortion clinics.
