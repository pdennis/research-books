# Won't Face Voters: Told Republicans to Stop Holding Town Halls

**Hudson Instructed Fellow Republican Members Of Congress To Stop Holding Town Halls.** According to The Assembly, NRCC Chairman Richard Hudson "instructed fellow Republican members of Congress to stop holding town halls," advising them to "avoid the spectacle of booing and jeering and instead opt for tele-town halls or Facebook lives." [Assembly NC, [3/25/25](https://www.theassemblync.com/politics/richard-hudson-republicans-townhalls/)]

  - **Democrats Held An Alternative Town Hall In Hudson's District That Drew Over 1,000 People.** According to NC Newsline, Democrats organized a "People's Town Hall" in Saxapahaw, NC — in Hudson's congressional district — that drew more than 1,000 attendees after Hudson refused to hold an in-person event. [NC Newsline, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://ncnewsline.com/briefs/democrats-to-bring-a-town-hall-to-gop-rep-richard-hudsons-district/)]

  - **At His Own Tele-Town Hall, A Fayetteville Constituent Warned That Medicaid Cuts Could End Her Coverage.** According to The Assembly, Fayetteville resident Sylvia, a 16-year teacher who became disabled, told Hudson she had obtained medical assistance through North Carolina's Medicaid expansion and warned that federal funding cuts could trigger automatic rollback of the program. Hudson responded by denying Republicans would cut Medicaid, saying, "'There's been a lot of rumors that Republicans in Congress are going to cut Medicaid. That's just simply not true.'" [Assembly NC, [4/23/25](https://www.theassemblync.com/politics/hudson-town-hall-nc-doge/)]
