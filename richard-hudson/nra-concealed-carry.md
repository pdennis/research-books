# Led The NRA's Top Legislative Priority While Accepting Maximum Contributions

**Hudson Was The Lead Sponsor Of The Concealed Carry Reciprocity Act, The NRA's Top Legislative Priority.** According to CBS News, Hudson introduced H.R. 38, which would require all U.S. states to recognize concealed carry permits granted by other states, effectively overriding stricter gun laws in states like New York and California. The NRA-ILA identified the bill as its top priority and it advanced through the House Judiciary Committee on a party-line vote of 18-9. [CBS News, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/concealed-carry-reciprocity-act-showdown/)]

  - **Hudson Received The NRA's Maximum Contribution In The 2024 Cycle — The Highest Amount The NRA PAC Gave To Any Congressional Candidate.** According to OpenSecrets, among the more than 160 congressional candidates the NRA PAC contributed to, North Carolina Congressman Richard Hudson received the highest contribution of $9,900 in the 2024 cycle. [OpenSecrets, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://www.opensecrets.org/orgs/national-rifle-assn/recipients?id=d000000082)]

  - **Seventeen State Attorneys General Opposed Hudson's Bill, Warning It Would Override State Gun Safety Laws.** According to CBS News, 17 attorneys general opposed the legislation, particularly those in states with tougher gun laws, arguing that national reciprocity would override states' ability to set their own standards and effectively weaken stricter gun laws to the lowest common denominator. [CBS News, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/concealed-carry-reciprocity-act-showdown/)]
