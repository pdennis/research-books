# Political Mentor Pleaded Guilty In Federal Bribery Scandal

**Hudson's Self-Described 'Friend And Mentor' Robin Hayes Pleaded Guilty To Lying To The FBI In A Federal Bribery Case.** According to WRAL, former Rep. Robin Hayes — who employed Hudson as his district director from 1999 to 2005 and whom Hudson called his "friend and mentor" — pleaded guilty to making a false statement to FBI agents investigating a scheme to illegally funnel $2 million through the state Republican Party. Hayes had been accused of helping high-dollar political donor Greg Lindberg bribe the Republican state Insurance Commissioner. [WRAL, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://www.wral.com/former-state-gop-chair-pleading-guilty-to-lying-to-fbi-in-bribery-case/18661683/)]

  - **Lindberg Was Sentenced To Seven Years In Prison For The Bribery Scheme.** According to WSOC, Greg Lindberg — the billionaire insurance magnate at the center of the scheme — received a seven-year federal prison sentence for his role in bribing the North Carolina Insurance Commissioner. Hayes received one year of probation and a $9,500 fine; Trump later pardoned Hayes at the end of his first term.
