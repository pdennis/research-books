# How To Win Against Darrell Issa

The third-wealthiest member of Congress with a [net worth of \$283 million](https://timesofsandiego.com/politics/2025/08/12/rep-issa-ranked-no-3-richest-member-of-congress-discloses-2024-financial-report/), Darrell Issa has used his office to enrich himself while voting against the interests of the working families in his district. Before entering politics, he was [indicted for grand theft auto](https://www.npr.org/2012/04/16/150739985/house-investigator-issa-has-faced-allegations-as-well), [suspected of committing arson](https://www.npr.org/2011/01/20/133071575/Past-Haunts-Rep-Issa-Head-Of-Investigative-Committee) after increasing his factory's fire insurance by 462% weeks before it burned, and [convicted of carrying a concealed weapon](https://www.mediamatters.org/new-york-times/report-media-ignore-rep-issas-alleged-criminal-past). In Congress, he secured nearly [\$815,000 in earmarks](https://www.kpbs.org/news/2011/aug/16/conflict-interest-questions-raised-about-sd-congre/) to widen roads next to his own commercial properties, [violated the STOCK Act](https://www.capitoltrades.com/articles/congressional-stock-trades-raise-stock-act-violation-concerns-2024-10-02) by disclosing up to $175 million in Treasury bond sales approximately 500 days late, and [rented his Washington townhouse](https://www.sandiegouniontribune.com/2025/08/16/ethics-complaints-accuse-house-speaker-of-using-campaign-funds-to-pay-issa-rent/) to Speaker Johnson's campaign. He voted [17 times to repeal the ACA](https://americanjournalnews.com/darrell-issas-long-record-of-voting-to-repeal-obamacare/), told a constituent whose son has diabetes to ["hopefully" not get a condition](https://www.americanprogressaction.org/article/my-son-has-type-1-diabetes-and-darrell-issa-voted-to-repeal-his-healthcare/), and voted for the [One Big Beautiful Bill](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/08/08/trumps-spending-bill-cuts-billions-in-snap-benefits.html) that slashed $186 billion from SNAP while delivering an average [\$66,000 tax cut to the richest 1 percent](https://itep.org/tax-provisions-in-trump-megabill-national-and-state-level-estimates/). He voted to [keep Trump's tariffs on Canada](https://www.opb.org/article/2026/02/12/house-votes-to-slap-back-trump-s-tariffs-on-canada-in-rare-bipartisan-rebuke/) while San Diego households faced [\$2,400 in annual tariff costs](https://timesofsandiego.com/politics/2025/08/13/reps-expect-tariffs-to-cost-san-diego-households-2400-this-year/) and local businesses [shut down entirely](https://www.sandiegouniontribune.com/2025/10/23/its-hard-to-know-what-to-do-in-the-age-of-tariffs-san-diego-small-businesses-raise-prices-rethink-suppliers-and-settle-into-uncertainty/). And when constituents tried to confront him, Issa [refused to hold a town hall for eight years](https://www.eastcountymagazine.org/%E2%80%9Cempty-chair%E2%80%9D-town-hall-rep-issa%E2%80%99s-constituents-air-concerns).

### **HEALTH CARE: 17 VOTES TO REPEAL THE ACA AND COUNTING**

Issa voted [17 times to repeal the ACA](https://americanjournalnews.com/darrell-issas-long-record-of-voting-to-repeal-obamacare/) and explicitly called for a ["real phase out of Obamacare"](https://americanjournalnews.com/darrell-issas-long-record-of-voting-to-repeal-obamacare/) on Fox Business. He cast the [decisive AHCA vote](https://www.sandiegouniontribune.com/opinion/the-conversation/sd-darrell-issa-votes-yes-on-obamacare-repeal-acha-health-care-20170504-story.html) that would have stripped 37,000 constituents of coverage and told a mother of a diabetic child to ["hopefully" not get a condition](https://www.americanprogressaction.org/article/my-son-has-type-1-diabetes-and-darrell-issa-voted-to-repeal-his-healthcare/).
He voted for the One Big Beautiful Bill's Medicaid cuts, projected to leave [more than 24,000 constituents uninsured](https://americanjournalnews.com/darrell-issas-long-record-of-voting-to-repeal-obamacare/) and [cost San Diego County up to 15,200 jobs](https://www.kpbs.org/news/politics/2025/07/02/congressman-issa-falsely-claims-criminal-illegals-are-enrolled-in-medicaid). Two hospitals in his district face a [combined \$14 million revenue loss](https://americanjournalnews.com/darrell-issas-long-record-of-voting-to-repeal-obamacare/).
He voted against reinstating [ACA tax credits that 8,000 of his own constituents](https://americanjournalnews.com/darrell-issas-long-record-of-voting-to-repeal-obamacare/) relied on. Some saw their premiums quadruple after they expired. He justified Medicaid cuts by [falsely claiming](https://www.kpbs.org/news/politics/2025/07/02/congressman-issa-falsely-claims-criminal-illegals-are-enrolled-in-medicaid) the program enrolls "criminal illegals" -- a claim KPBS fact-checked as false.

**Message:** For over a decade, Issa has voted to take health care away from his own constituents -- then told them to "hopefully" not get sick.

### **AFFORDABILITY: $283 MILLION CONGRESSMAN VOTES TO RAISE COSTS ON WORKING FAMILIES**

Issa [voted to keep Trump's tariffs on Canada](https://www.opb.org/article/2026/02/12/house-votes-to-slap-back-trump-s-tariffs-on-canada-in-rare-bipartisan-rebuke/) while San Diego households faced [\$2,400 in annual tariff costs](https://timesofsandiego.com/politics/2025/08/13/reps-expect-tariffs-to-cost-san-diego-households-2400-this-year/). San Diego had the [highest inflation in the nation at 4%](https://www.sandiegouniontribune.com/2025/08/12/san-diegos-inflation-rate-remains-highest-in-nation-at-4/), with meat and eggs up 7.3%, medical care up 6.8%, and tuition and childcare up 9.4%.
He voted for the One Big Beautiful Bill that [slashed \$186 billion from SNAP](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/08/08/trumps-spending-bill-cuts-billions-in-snap-benefits.html), threatening [400,000 San Diegans on CalFresh](https://inewsource.org/2025/07/16/federal-cuts-snap-calfresh-san-diego-eligibility/), while giving the richest 1% an average [\$66,000 tax cut](https://itep.org/tax-provisions-in-trump-megabill-national-and-state-level-estimates/).
Local small businesses were [forced to raise prices or shut down](https://www.sandiegouniontribune.com/2025/10/23/its-hard-to-know-what-to-do-in-the-age-of-tariffs-san-diego-small-businesses-raise-prices-rethink-suppliers-and-settle-into-uncertainty/) due to tariffs, and [2,500 cross-border businesses](https://inewsource.org/2025/09/11/tariff-san-diego-de-minimis-exemption-imports/) in Otay Mesa were threatened.

**Message:** The third-richest man in Congress voted for $66,000 tax cuts for people like himself while raising costs on San Diego families and cutting food assistance for 400,000 San Diegans.

### **JOBS AND THE ECONOMY: VOTED AGAINST INFRASTRUCTURE, CHAMPIONED DOGE CUTS**

Issa was the [only San Diego delegation member to vote against](https://timesofsandiego.com/politics/2021/11/05/all-san-diego-congress-members-but-issa-vote-to-pass-1-trillion-infrastructure-bill/) the $1 trillion Bipartisan Infrastructure Law, calling it a bill that "does not build -- it destroys."
He [voted to codify DOGE cuts](https://www.naco.org/news/us-house-passes-rescissions-package) and [introduced legislation to make DOGE permanent](https://issa.house.gov/media/press-releases/issa-moves-codify-doge-law), threatening San Diego's [47,000-strong federal workforce](https://www.sandiegouniontribune.com/2025/03/02/people-are-frustrated-people-are-angry-fear-and-chaos-grip-san-diegos-47000-strong-federal-workforce/) -- the fifth-largest in the nation.
His Medicaid vote could [cost San Diego County up to 15,200 jobs](https://www.kpbs.org/news/politics/2025/07/02/congressman-issa-falsely-claims-criminal-illegals-are-enrolled-in-medicaid), and he earned a [10% lifetime AFL-CIO score](https://aflcio.org/scorecard/legislators/darrell-issa), voting against the [PRO Act](https://fox5sandiego.com/rep-darrell-issa-votes-against-the-extreme-and-inflexible-pro-act/) and against raising the [minimum wage](http://www.ontheissues.org/CA/Darrell_Issa_Jobs.htm).

**Message:** Issa voted against the infrastructure law every other San Diego member supported, then championed the DOGE cuts threatening 47,000 local federal jobs.

### **CRIMINAL HISTORY AND SELF-DEALING: A PATTERN OF CORRUPTION**

Issa was [suspected of committing arson](https://www.npr.org/2011/01/20/133071575/Past-Haunts-Rep-Issa-Head-Of-Investigative-Committee) after increasing his factory's fire insurance by 462% weeks before it burned. He was [indicted for grand theft auto](https://www.npr.org/2012/04/16/150739985/house-investigator-issa-has-faced-allegations-as-well) in a Maserati theft and [convicted of carrying a concealed weapon](https://www.mediamatters.org/new-york-times/report-media-ignore-rep-issas-alleged-criminal-past).
He [disclosed up to \$175 million in Treasury bond sales](https://www.capitoltrades.com/articles/congressional-stock-trades-raise-stock-act-violation-concerns-2024-10-02) approximately 500 days late, violating the STOCK Act, and paid only a $200 fine. He secured [\$815,000 in earmarks](https://www.kpbs.org/news/2011/aug/16/conflict-interest-questions-raised-about-sd-congre/) to widen roads next to his own commercial properties.
He [rented his D.C. townhouse to Speaker Johnson's campaign](https://www.sandiegouniontribune.com/2025/08/16/ethics-complaints-accuse-house-speaker-of-using-campaign-funds-to-pay-issa-rent/) at $2,500/month, drawing FEC and ethics complaints. He has [refused to hold a town hall since 2017](https://www.eastcountymagazine.org/%E2%80%9Cempty-chair%E2%80%9D-town-hall-rep-issa%E2%80%99s-constituents-air-concerns), prompting "Empty Chair" events.

**Message:** Issa has spent his career enriching himself at taxpayer expense -- from suspected arson to self-dealing earmarks to STOCK Act violations -- while refusing to face the voters he's supposed to represent.
