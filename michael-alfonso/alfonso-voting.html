Americans Have Been Able To Vote At 18 Since 1971 After It Was Argued The Voting Age Should Match The Age One Can Be Drafted. According to Milwaukee Journal Sentinel, “Americans have been able to vote at 18 since 1971, when the 26th Amendment to the Constitution was ratified, according to the Reagan Library archives. That amendment lowered the voting age from 21 to match the age of those who could be drafted to fight in the military, because people argued that if a citizen was old enough to serve, they should also have the right to vote at the same age." [Milwaukee Journal Sentinel, 9/23/26]
