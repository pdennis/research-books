Alfonso Claimed He “Saw” Socialism In Cuba 44 Years Before He Was Born

Alfonso Claimed He “Saw” Socialism Take Hold In Cuba, 44 Years Before He Was Born. According to Raw Story, “A 26-year-old Trump-endorsed congressional candidate claimed Tuesday he ‘saw’ socialism taking hold in Cuba, a full 44 years before he was born. Michael Alfonso, 26, is running for Wisconsin's 7th Congressional District in an Aug. 11 Republican primary, Spectrum News reported. He is the son-in-law of Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy, who held the seat for eight years.” [Raw Story, 7/28/26]

  • When Discussing The Rise Of Socialism In Cuba, Alfonso Said: “I Saw What Happens When Socialism Starts Gaining Ground Somewhere.” According to an interview with Michael Alfonso on the David Webb Show, “ALFONSO: This rise of socialism here in the United States, it's a really scary thing, especially for me and my family. My grandparents came to this country from Cuba. I saw what happens when socialism starts gaining ground somewhere. It turns into communism, and then you have to flee. But there's a big problem. There's nowhere to flee anymore. The United States is the last hope. It is why we need to defend our beliefs, faith, family, and freedom down to the last man. Truly.” [The David Webb Show, 7/28/26] (audio)
  • Alfonso Was Born In 2000, And His Grandfather Immigrated To The United States Three Years Before Fidel Castro Brought Communism To Cuba. According to Raw Story, “Alfonso was born in 2000, according to his own account. His grandfather immigrated to the United States in 1956 — three years before Fidel Castro's revolution toppled Cuba's government and brought communism to the island.” [Raw Story, 7/28/26]
