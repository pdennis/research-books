Overview

Tafoya does not believe women should have a constitutional right to an abortion. She supported the catastrophic Dobbs decision that overturned Roe V. Wade and claimed she was “completely aligned” with President Trump on abortion policy. Tafoya called Minnesota's abortion laws “barbaric" and vowed to confirm conservative, “strict-constructionist” judges in the Senate who would only further endanger abortion rights.

That stance is ‘political suicide’ that alienates a major portion of the public, Tafoya said. ‘Dumb, dumb, dumb, dumb, dumb, just stupid,’ Tafoya said. ‘Republicans, if they want to win anything in the future, better get their messaging right on abortion. ...
