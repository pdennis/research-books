Overview

Tafoya praised Trump's One Big Beautiful Bill, even as Minnesotans got battered with its consequences. Cuts to SNAP in the Big Beautiful Bill were slated to cost Minnesota over $100 million every year, and an estimated 18,000 Minnesotans had already lost food benefits since the bill's enactment. What's more, the OBBB's trillion dollar Medicaid cut endangered healthcare coverage for 140,000 Minnesotans. Still, Tafoya pledged to uphold the law in the Senate, and she even supported repealing the Affordable Care Act, which more than 139,000 Minnesotans rely on for coverage.

The Big Beautiful Bill Caused SNAP And Medicaid Cuts For Minnesotans

The OBBB Cut Medicaid By $1 Trillion, Endangering Healthcare Coverage For 140,000 Minnesotans. According to MPR News, “The new federal law includes the largest cut to Medicaid in history, reducing funding by $1 trillion. As a result, nearly 12 million Americans are expected to become uninsured over the next decade, and according to Minnesota health officials, an estimated 140,000 Minnesotans are likely to lose health coverage. These upcoming cuts come in addition to a federal hold on $243 million of Medicaid funding that the Trump administration imposed because of possible fraud in state programs.” [MPR News, 4/17/26]

The OBBB’s SNAP Cuts Were Slated To Cost Minnesota Over $100 Million Per Year, Already Having Caused 18,000 Minnesotans To Lose Their SNAP Benefits. According to the Minnesota Reformer, “The law, which President Donald Trump named the One Big Beautiful Bill Act, requires states to pay more for SNAP, which provides food assistance to low-income people and families. States with a higher rate of erroneous payments must pay a higher percentage of SNAP benefits in their state. […] Based on the 2024 payment error rate, the state projected $168 million in new costs over the next two years. With the new, higher payment error rate, that projection is now around $250 million over two years, not including increased administrative costs. […] Blaha said at least 18,000 Minnesotans have lost SNAP benefits since Trump signed the One Big Beautiful Bill Act on Independence Day last year.” [Minnesota Reformer, 7/2/26]
