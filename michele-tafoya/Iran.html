The Iran War Was Hiking Prices For Fuel And Fertilizer

Minnesota Farmers Had To Pay More For Fertilizer Due To The War. According to WDIO, “Local farmers in Minnesota are still facing higher fertilizer and shipping costs this planting season. The Middle East supplies nearly half of the world’s urea, a key fertilizer ingredient. Since the U.S.-Iran conflict began in February, oil, energy, shipping and fertilizer costs have risen sharply. The Minnesota Farmers’ Union reports that nearly 25 percent of the world’s petroleum and 30 percent of nitrogen supplies have increased in price. Due to global supply disruptions and the ongoing U.S.-Iran conflict, farmers have needed to adapt to changing costs. Jadell Cavallin with little Waldo farms says many farmers along the north shore and in Minnesota are hurting from rising costs.” [WDIO, 6/4/26]

July 2026: Minnesota Drivers Were Paying Over $4 A Gallon For Gas. According to Bring Me The News, “Many drivers in Minnesota are paying at least $4 per gallon, with gas prices on the rise again due to the ongoing war in Iran. As of Thursday, the statewide average for a gallon of regular gas is $4.04, an 18 cent increase from $3.86 a week ago, according to AAA data. Prices at Minnesota gas stations are highest on average in the Twin Cities, where motorists are shelling out $4.12 per gallon, followed by Mankato at $4.03 and St. Cloud at $4.02.” [Bring Me The News, 7/23/26]
