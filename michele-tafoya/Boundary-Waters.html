Overview

When Representative Pete Stauber lifted a ban on mining near Minnesota's Boundary Waters, Tafoya cheered him on, calling potential mining operations in the Superior National Forest “a great thing.” The Boundary Waters, America's most-visited wilderness, contains some of the country's most pristine waters and critical habitat, free of roads and human-made structures. Yet, Tafoya supported endangering this critical natural resource and economic boon for local communities with her endorsement of toxic mining ventures. Boundary Waters advocates warned that current proposed projects, including sulfide-ore copper mining, would inevitably lead to toxic sulfates contaminating the watershed. Tafoya ignored the danger, calling impending environmental disaster an “opportunity.”

Tafoya Supported Mining Near Minnesota's Precious Boundary Waters
