# How To Win Against Andy Harris

Andy Harris is a physician who has spent his career in Congress making health care harder to access and more expensive for the people he represents. He [demanded his own taxpayer-funded health insurance start immediately](https://www.baltimoresun.com/2010/11/18/andy-harris-learns-a-lesson-2/) upon taking office — then spent the next 15 years voting to repeal or undermine the [ACA that cut the uninsured rate in his district by 54%](https://www.baltimoresun.com/2017/08/27/marylands-eastern-shore-a-gop-stronghold-home-to-thousands-who-now-have-insurance-thanks-to-obamacare/). As Freedom Caucus chair, Harris pushed to [accelerate Medicaid changes](https://www.capitalgazette.com/2025/05/22/andy-harris-votes-present-on-trump-bill/) that could strip coverage from [24,000 people in his own district](https://www.thebanner.com/politics-power/national-politics/andy-harris-trump-nill-vote-REWWZFV7WVHFPGEHSPYVLVALPQ/) — in a county where [23% of residents rely on Medicaid and 41% of children are on federal health insurance](https://www.thebanner.com/politics-power/national-politics/andy-harris-trump-nill-vote-REWWZFV7WVHFPGEHSPYVLVALPQ/). He voted for the One Big Beautiful Bill's [$186 billion SNAP cut](https://www.baltimoresun.com/2025/08/21/andy-harris-big-beautiful-bill/) — the largest in history — threatening [33,613 households in his district](https://www.baltimoresun.com/2025/08/21/andy-harris-big-beautiful-bill/), then [called the work requirements a "major win."](https://harris.house.gov/media/press-releases/one-big-beautiful-bill-secures-major-wins-american-people) 

He [cheered Trump's tariffs](https://danrodricks.com/2025/04/05/trumpy-harris-confident-we-will-win-the-trade-war-and-defeat-the-globalists/) while they [crushed Eastern Shore soybean farmers](https://talbotspy.org/trump-policies-disrupt-the-eastern-shore-soybean-market-by-wilson-dean/) and [raised costs on the fertilizer and steel](https://www.capitalgazette.com/2025/03/17/anne-arundel-farmers-brace-for-increased-costs-from-expanding-list-of-tariffs/) his district's 4,600 farms depend on. He [voted to overturn the 2020 election](https://www.marylandmatters.org/2021/01/07/after-siege-congress-affirms-electoral-college-votes-harris-supports-objections-overnight/) even after the Capitol was stormed, then [tried to bring a concealed gun onto the House floor](https://www.washingtonpost.com/local/md-politics/andy-harris-gun/2021/01/21/81c7ba90-5c63-11eb-8bcf-3877871c819d_story.html) two weeks later. All the while, Harris has [accepted over \$920,000 from health care industry PACs](https://www.fec.gov/data/receipts/?committee_id=C00435974) — including a [donation from Purdue Pharma](https://www.fec.gov/data/receipts/?committee_id=C00435974&contributor_name=purdue+pharma) during the opioid crisis ravaging his district.

### **HEALTH CARE: A DOCTOR WHO VOTES AGAINST HIS PATIENTS**

Harris [demanded his government health plan start immediately](https://www.baltimoresun.com/2010/11/18/andy-harris-learns-a-lesson-2/) upon taking office while campaigning to repeal the ACA — which [cut the uninsured rate in his district by 54%](https://www.baltimoresun.com/2017/08/27/marylands-eastern-shore-a-gop-stronghold-home-to-thousands-who-now-have-insurance-thanks-to-obamacare/), more than any other district in Maryland.
As Freedom Caucus chair, he pushed to [accelerate Medicaid work requirements](https://www.capitalgazette.com/2025/05/22/andy-harris-votes-present-on-trump-bill/) that could strip coverage from [24,000 people in his district](https://www.thebanner.com/politics-power/national-politics/andy-harris-trump-nill-vote-REWWZFV7WVHFPGEHSPYVLVALPQ/), where [23% of residents rely on Medicaid](https://www.thebanner.com/politics-power/national-politics/andy-harris-trump-nill-vote-REWWZFV7WVHFPGEHSPYVLVALPQ/).
He was the [only Maryland congressman to vote against the Inflation Reduction Act](https://www.baltimoresun.com/2022/08/14/from-lower-prescription-costs-to-cleaner-power-heres-what-the-federal-inflation-reduction-act-means-for-maryland/), which capped insulin at $35 and let Medicare negotiate drug prices.
He has accepted [over \$920,000 from health care industry PACs](https://www.fec.gov/data/receipts/?committee_id=C00435974), including a [$1,000 donation from Purdue Pharma](https://www.fec.gov/data/receipts/?committee_id=C00435974&contributor_name=purdue+pharma) during the opioid crisis.

**Message:** Harris is a doctor who demands government health care for himself while voting to take it away from his own patients.

### **AFFORDABILITY: CHEERED TARIFFS WHILE FARMERS AND FAMILIES PAY THE PRICE**

Harris declared ["we will win the trade war"](https://danrodricks.com/2025/04/05/trumpy-harris-confident-we-will-win-the-trade-war-and-defeat-the-globalists/) while China retaliated with a [37% tariff on Eastern Shore soybeans](https://talbotspy.org/trump-policies-disrupt-the-eastern-shore-soybean-market-by-wilson-dean/) and a [15% tariff on Delmarva chicken](https://whyy.org/articles/delaware-chicken-industry-tariffs/), and tariffs [raised costs on fertilizer and steel](https://www.capitalgazette.com/2025/03/17/anne-arundel-farmers-brace-for-increased-costs-from-expanding-list-of-tariffs/) that his district's 4,600 farms depend on.
He voted for SNAP cuts threatening [33,613 households in his district, including 15,684 with children](https://www.baltimoresun.com/2025/08/21/andy-harris-big-beautiful-bill/), while CBO found the bill would [make the poorest Americans $1,200 poorer and the richest $13,600 richer](https://www.baltimoresun.com/2025/08/21/andy-harris-big-beautiful-bill/).
He voted to [repeal clean energy tax credits](https://taxfoundation.org/blog/big-beautiful-bill-green-energy-tax-credit-changes/) worth up to $9,000 for Maryland homeowners, calling them the ["Green New Scam."](https://harris.house.gov/media/press-releases/one-big-beautiful-bill-secures-major-wins-american-people)

**Message:** Harris cheers tariffs that crush Eastern Shore farmers and votes to cut food assistance for 33,000 families in his own district.

### **JOBS AND THE ECONOMY: VOTED AGAINST EVERY MAJOR INVESTMENT**

Harris [voted against the bipartisan Infrastructure Law](https://www.transportation.gov/briefing-room/bipartisan-infrastructure-law-will-deliver-maryland) that delivered over $7 billion to Maryland, including [$267 million in broadband](https://governor.maryland.gov/news/press/pages/governor-moore-announces-approval-for-marylands-internet-for-all-proposals-enabling-access-to-2677-million-in-federal.aspx) for underserved Eastern Shore communities.
He [voted against the CHIPS Act](https://www.govtrack.us/congress/votes/117-2022/h404), which invested in American semiconductor manufacturing, and [voted against raising the minimum wage](https://www.govtrack.us/congress/votes/116-2019/h496) to $15.
He [defended DOGE mass layoffs](https://www.cbsnews.com/baltimore/news/maryland-unemployment-job-losses-federal-workers-economy-2025/) that eliminated nearly 25,000 federal jobs in Maryland, [calling federal workers' jobs "immoral"](https://www.thebanner.com/politics-power/state-government/andy-harris-federal-workers-Z3H6CEKWE5CFTHWV4FIR4AZRNI/) and sending form letters to [23,000 constituents who pleaded for help](https://www.thebanner.com/politics-power/state-government/andy-harris-federal-workers-Z3H6CEKWE5CFTHWV4FIR4AZRNI/).

**Message:** Harris voted against billions in infrastructure and jobs for Maryland, then called his own constituents' livelihoods "immoral."

### **EXTREME: JANUARY 6 AND THE GUN INCIDENT**

Harris [voted to overturn the 2020 election results from both Arizona and Pennsylvania](https://www.marylandmatters.org/2021/01/07/after-siege-congress-affirms-electoral-college-votes-harris-supports-objections-overnight/) — even after the Capitol was stormed. He had been [summoned to the White House](https://www.baltimoresun.com/opinion/editorial/bs-ed-0714-andy-harris-january-6-20220713-p6vjpkjvprfcjpx3pum5ud2axy-story.html) to discuss overturning the election and [signed the Texas amicus brief](https://accountability.gop/profile/rep-andy-harris/) seeking to overturn the results.
Two weeks later, Harris [set off a metal detector near the House chamber while carrying a concealed firearm](https://www.washingtonpost.com/local/md-politics/andy-harris-gun/2021/01/21/81c7ba90-5c63-11eb-8bcf-3877871c819d_story.html). When an officer detected the gun, Harris tried to hand it to another congressman, who refused.
The Baltimore Sun called Harris an ["unrepentant Jan. 6 co-conspirator"](https://www.baltimoresun.com/opinion/editorial/bs-ed-0714-andy-harris-january-6-20220713-p6vjpkjvprfcjpx3pum5ud2axy-story.html).

**Message:** Harris tried to overturn the election, then tried to bring a gun onto the House floor. He is unfit for office.

### **FREEDOM CAUCUS EXTREMISM AND PERSONAL SCANDALS**

Harris was [elected Freedom Caucus chair with Trump's endorsement and Project 2025 backing](https://marylandmatters.org/briefs/maryland-rep-harris-elected-chair-of-the-ultra-conservative-freedom-caucus/). He has said he would [support a national abortion ban after approximately six weeks](https://democrats.org/news/%F0%9F%9A%A8new-project-2025-backed-rep-andy-harris-named-new-house-freedom-caucus-chair/).
He was [accused of an ethics violation for failing to disclose his wife's consulting income](https://www.washingtonpost.com/local/md-politics/rep-andy-harris-accused-of-ethical-violation-for-failing-to-disclose-wifes-income/2018/10/17/2f2f982e-d237-11e8-8c22-fa2ef74bd6d6_story.html) from his own campaign committees.

**Message:** Harris is an extremist who pushes national abortion bans while hiding his wife's income from ethics regulators.
