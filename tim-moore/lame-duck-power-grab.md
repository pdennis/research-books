# Rep. Tim Moore (NC-14): Lame-Duck Power Grab Disguised as Hurricane Relief

### Engineered A Lame-Duck Power Grab That Stripped Incoming Democrats Of Authority

**Moore Engineered A Lame-Duck Power Grab That Stripped Incoming Democrats Of Authority While Disguising The Legislation As Hurricane Helene Relief.** According to WRAL, Moore pushed Senate Bill 382, a 131-page measure "framed as Hurricane Helene relief," that transferred control of the State Board of Elections from the governor to a Republican auditor, stripped the incoming attorney general of authority to challenge legislative actions in court, restricted the governor's judicial appointment powers, and eliminated state boards led by incoming Democrats. Governor Roy Cooper vetoed the bill, calling it "a sham." [WRAL, [12/11/24](https://www.wral.com/story/with-protested-override-nc-lawmakers-sap-power-from-governor-attorney-general-other-incoming-democrats/21763004/)]

- **Moore Appeared On Steve Bannon's Podcast To Pressure GOP Holdouts Into Supporting The Override.** According to WRAL, Moore appeared on Steve Bannon's podcast before the veto override vote, "urging listeners to pressure GOP holdouts to support the override." The House voted 72-46 to override Cooper's veto. [WRAL, [12/11/24](https://www.wral.com/story/with-protested-override-nc-lawmakers-sap-power-from-governor-attorney-general-other-incoming-democrats/21763004/)]
