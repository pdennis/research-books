# Rep. Tim Moore (NC-14): Gerrymandering, Party Switch, and Moore v. Harper

### Drew His Own District and Ducked His Constituents

**Moore Ran For Congress In A District His Republican Colleagues Drew For Him, In A Map Challenged As A Racial Gerrymander.** According to WUNC, Moore filed to run in the newly created NC-14 after "the redistricting appeared strategically designed to benefit Moore's candidacy." Under the new map, North Carolina's delegation shifted from an even 7-7 split to at least 10-4 in favor of Republicans. A federal lawsuit by Black and Latino voters alleged the maps were racially gerrymandered to dilute minority voting power. [WUNC, [12/5/23](https://www.wunc.org/politics/2023-12-05/tim-moore-defends-new-congressional-map-as-he-files-to-run)]

**Moore Held Zero Public Town Halls Since Taking Office, Forcing Constituents To Host Two Without Him.** According to NC Newsline, "over a period where some North Carolina lawmakers have held as many as six town halls, Moore has held zero." Constituents in Shelby and Kings Mountain held events with a life-sized cutout of Moore after he declined invitations to attend. Janice Robinson of Red Wine and Blue said, "'Your blind eye to vulnerable citizens while giving tax dollars to millionaires will only increase our determination.'" [NC Newsline, [10/20/25](https://ncnewsline.com/2025/10/20/as-trump-opposition-grows-north-carolina-republicans-duck-town-halls-does-it-matter/)]

- **Constituents Brought A Black Casket To Moore's Office To Protest His Medicaid Vote.** According to Qnotes Carolinas, demonstrators delivered a casket decorated with signs reading "This coffin represents policy murder!" to Moore's Gastonia district office, demanding answers about his support of the One Big Beautiful Bill. Signs read "307,000 people in North Carolina will lose Medicaid." [Qnotes Carolinas, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://qnotescarolinas.com/medicaid-cuts-gastonia-protest-2025/)]

### Moore v. Harper: Tried to Remove Courts From Elections

**Moore Was The Named Petitioner In A Supreme Court Case Seeking To Remove Courts' Ability To Check Gerrymandering And Election Rules.** According to WRAL, Moore pushed the "independent state legislature" theory to the U.S. Supreme Court, arguing that "state legislatures should face very few checks and balances, with state courts being banned from ruling their actions unconstitutional." The Supreme Court rejected the theory 6-3 in June 2023. [WRAL, [6/27/23](https://www.wral.com/story/supreme-court-rules-on-Moore-v-Harper-gerrymandering-case-that-critics-say-could-harm-american-democracy/20901044/)]

### Orchestrated A Democratic Lawmaker's Party Switch To Create A Veto-Proof Supermajority

**Moore Orchestrated A Democratic Lawmaker's Party Switch To Create A Veto-Proof Supermajority And Ram Through An Abortion Ban.** According to Jezebel, "Local Republicans urged her to run as a Democrat — fooling voters in her very blue Charlotte-area district into thinking she strongly supported abortion rights — and Republican House Speaker Tim Moore and Republican Majority Leader John Bell encouraged her to run in the Democratic primary." Tricia Cotham's switch gave Republicans a supermajority used to override 14 of Governor Cooper's vetoes, including a 12-week abortion ban. [Jezebel, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://www.jezebel.com/turns-out-rep-tricia-cotham-north-carolina-abortion-t-1850691458)]

**Moore Changed House Rules To Allow Surprise Veto Override Votes When A Single Democrat Was Absent.** According to ABC11, civil rights organizations called the rule change a "shameful power grab." The new rules eliminated advance notice requirements before veto override votes, meaning "if one single Democrat is not able to be on the House floor — whether that's for a doctor's appointment, family emergency, or anything else — Moore can call a vote and override a veto." [ABC11, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://abc11.com/nc-politics-tim-moore-roy-cooper-veto-power/12754347/)]
