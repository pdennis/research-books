# Rep. Tim Moore (NC-14): Stock Trades and STOCK Act Violations

### Stock Trades and Financial Conflicts

**Moore Failed To Timely Disclose Hundreds Of Thousands Of Dollars In Stock Trades Made Around Trump's "Liberation Day" Tariff Announcement, In A Potential STOCK Act Violation.** According to Fortune, Moore "failed to properly disclose dozens of stock trades made in April 2025, missing federal deadlines required by the Stop Trading on Congressional Knowledge (STOCK) Act." The undisclosed trades included six purchases of American Airlines stock worth $90,000-$300,000 from April 1 through April 22, four Ford purchases totaling $95,000-$250,000, and Harley-Davidson trades — all made around Trump's April 2 tariff declaration. Moore did not publicly disclose the 12 trades until well after the federal deadline. [Fortune, [6/16/25](https://fortune.com/2025/06/16/tim-moore-stock-trades-liberation-day-disclosure/)]

- **Moore Serves As Vice Chairman Of The House Financial Services Subcommittee On Oversight And Investigations, Making His Late Disclosures Particularly Problematic.** According to Fortune, Moore's position on the oversight subcommittee gave him potential access to nonpublic financial information, heightening concerns about his trading activity around major economic policy announcements. [Fortune, [6/16/25](https://fortune.com/2025/06/16/tim-moore-stock-trades-liberation-day-disclosure/)]

**Moore Invested Up To $245,000 Betting Against The Economy While Publicly Touting Trump's Economic Policies.** According to Notus, Moore "poured up to $245,000 of his own cash into an exotic investment fund that earns him money when a key stock market index falters." He made six purchases of the Direxion Daily Small Cap Bear 3X Shares fund between late June and late August 2025 while simultaneously telling the public that Trump's policies are "'going to spur the economy.'" [Notus, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://www.notus.org/congress/tim-moore-investment-russell-2000-index-trump-economy)]

- **Moore Abruptly Switched From Bullish To Bearish Investments Mid-2025, Suggesting He Privately Expected The Policies He Championed To Fail.** According to Notus, from early 2025 through mid-July, Moore invested "hundreds of thousands of dollars into the Direxion Daily Small Cap Bull 3X Shares fund." After mid-July, "Moore has not invested in the bull-market fund since," pivoting entirely to the bearish fund. [Notus, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://www.notus.org/congress/tim-moore-investment-russell-2000-index-trump-economy)]
