# Rep. Tim Moore (NC-14): Zero Town Halls and Ducking Constituents

### Held Zero Public Town Halls Since Taking Office

**Moore Held Zero Public Town Halls Since Taking Office, Forcing Constituents To Host Two Without Him.** According to NC Newsline, "over a period where some North Carolina lawmakers have held as many as six town halls, Moore has held zero." Constituents in Shelby and Kings Mountain held events with a life-sized cutout of Moore after he declined invitations to attend. Janice Robinson of Red Wine and Blue said, "'Your blind eye to vulnerable citizens while giving tax dollars to millionaires will only increase our determination.'" [NC Newsline, [10/20/25](https://ncnewsline.com/2025/10/20/as-trump-opposition-grows-north-carolina-republicans-duck-town-halls-does-it-matter/)]

**Moore's Constituents Held A Town Hall In His Absence To Protest His Health Care Votes.** According to NC Voices, constituents from Moore's district held a town hall at Kings Mountain High School — their second such event — after Moore declined to attend. Attendees raised concerns about Medicaid cuts and impacts of the One Big Beautiful Bill Act. Janice Robinson, NC Program Director of Red Wine and Blue, said, "'Your blind eye to vulnerable citizens and your constituents, while giving away our tax dollars to millionaires and billionaires, will only increase our tenacity and determination.'" [NC Voices, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://ncvoices.com/your-blind-eye-to-your-constituents-rep-tim-moore-constituents-host-town-hall-in-his-absence-to-address-federal-funding-concerns/)]

**Protesters Brought A Black Casket To Moore's Gastonia Office To Protest His Medicaid Vote.** According to Qnotes Carolinas, demonstrators delivered a casket decorated with signs reading "This coffin represents policy murder!" to Moore's Gastonia district office, demanding an explanation for his support of the One Big Beautiful Bill. Signs at the protest read "307,000 people in North Carolina will lose Medicaid." [Qnotes Carolinas, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://qnotescarolinas.com/medicaid-cuts-gastonia-protest-2025/)]
