# Rep. Tim Moore (NC-14): Chicken Plant Self-Dealing and Ethics Complaints

### Used His Office To Personally Profit From A State Subsidy

**Moore's Aide Pressured State Environmental Officials On Behalf Of A Chicken Plant Moore Co-Owned, And The State Approved A $22,000 Subsidy.** According to WUNC, emails showed that "a high-ranking aide to Moore had corresponded with the North Carolina Department of Environmental Quality about a Siler City chicken processing plant co-owned by Moore," and "at the aide's request, DEQ approved the plant's participation in a state subsidy program that provided $22,000 for the repair of a leaking underground storage tank." Moore and partners had purchased the plant out of bankruptcy for $85,000 in 2013 and later sold it for $550,000. [WUNC, [Viewed 2/16/26](http://www.wunc.org/post/nc-house-speaker-s-ethics-challenged-again-after-discovery-emails-deq)]

- **A Watchdog Group Filed Two Separate Ethics Complaints Against Moore Over The Chicken Plant Deal.** According to the Campaign for Accountability, they filed complaints alleging Moore "abus[ed] his position for personal financial gain" by pressuring DEQ officials. The State Ethics Board ultimately dismissed the complaints. [Campaign for Accountability, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://campaignforaccountability.org/watchdog-files-ethics-complaint-against-house-speaker-tim-moore-for-abusing-his-position-for-personal-financial-gain/)]
