# Rep. Tim Moore (NC-14): Can't Be Trusted on Jobs and the Economy

Moore claims to be fighting for North Carolina workers, but his record tells a different story. As NC House Speaker, he sponsored HB2 — the "bathroom bill" that cost the state $3.76 billion in lost business and nearly 3,000 jobs as PayPal, Deutsche Bank, and CoStar pulled planned expansions. He used his Speaker's office to steer a state subsidy to a chicken plant he co-owned, flipping it from $85,000 to $550,000 while his aide pressured environmental regulators. Now in Congress, Moore has championed Trump's tariff agenda even as North Carolina lost 7,200 manufacturing jobs in 2025 and western NC manufacturers began laying off workers, telling constituents they should "pay a little more" for tariffed goods. He voted for the One Big Beautiful Bill Act, which the CBO found will cut incomes for the bottom 80% of households. And while publicly touting the economy, Moore privately invested up to $245,000 in a fund that profits when the stock market declines — then failed to disclose hundreds of thousands of dollars in stock trades made around Trump's "Liberation Day" tariff announcement, a potential STOCK Act violation.

### Sponsored Legislation That Cost North Carolina Billions and Thousands of Jobs

**Moore Sponsored HB2, The "Bathroom Bill" That Cost North Carolina An Estimated $3.76 Billion In Lost Business And Nearly 3,000 Jobs.** According to the Associated Press, the law would cost North Carolina "more than $3.76 billion in lost business over a dozen years." The AP tally included only projects where public records or interviews confirmed HB2 was the reason a business pulled out. PayPal canceled a 400-job expansion in Charlotte, Deutsche Bank scuttled plans for 250 jobs in the Raleigh area, and CoStar relocated a 700-job research center to Virginia. Charlotte alone lost projects totaling 2,000 jobs. [AP via PBS, [3/27/17](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/politics/ap-exclusive-bathroom-bill-cost-north-carolina-3-76-billion)]

- **PayPal's Canceled Charlotte Expansion Alone Cost North Carolina $2.66 Billion In Economic Impact.** According to CNBC, state officials expected PayPal's planned expansion to "contribute more than $200 million annually to North Carolina's gross domestic product" and to have "added $2.66 billion to the state economy" by the end of 2028. [CNBC, [3/27/17](https://www.cnbc.com/2017/03/27/bathroom-bill-to-cost-north-carolina-376-billion.html)]

- **Adidas Built Its First U.S. Factory In Georgia Instead Of North Carolina Because Of HB2.** According to CNBC, Adidas "built its first U.S. sports shoe factory employing 160 near Atlanta rather than a High Point site" as a direct result of HB2. [CNBC, [3/27/17](https://www.cnbc.com/2017/03/27/bathroom-bill-to-cost-north-carolina-376-billion.html)]

### Used His Office To Personally Profit From A State Subsidy

**Moore's Aide Pressured State Environmental Officials On Behalf Of A Chicken Plant Moore Co-Owned, And The State Approved A $22,000 Subsidy.** According to WUNC, emails showed that "a high-ranking aide to Moore had corresponded with the North Carolina Department of Environmental Quality about a Siler City chicken processing plant co-owned by Moore," and "at the aide's request, DEQ approved the plant's participation in a state subsidy program that provided $22,000 for the repair of a leaking underground storage tank." Moore and partners had purchased the plant out of bankruptcy for $85,000 in 2013 and later sold it for $550,000. [WUNC, [Viewed 2/16/26](http://www.wunc.org/post/nc-house-speaker-s-ethics-challenged-again-after-discovery-emails-deq)]

- **A Watchdog Group Filed Two Separate Ethics Complaints Against Moore Over The Chicken Plant Deal.** According to the Campaign for Accountability, they filed complaints alleging Moore "abus[ed] his position for personal financial gain" by pressuring DEQ officials. The State Ethics Board ultimately dismissed the complaints. [Campaign for Accountability, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://campaignforaccountability.org/watchdog-files-ethics-complaint-against-house-speaker-tim-moore-for-abusing-his-position-for-personal-financial-gain/)]

### Championed Tariffs While North Carolina Workers Lost Jobs

**Moore Said North Carolinians Should "Pay A Little More" For Goods If Tariffs Create Jobs — But North Carolina Lost 7,200 Manufacturing Jobs In 2025.** According to the Charlotte Observer, Moore "said 'short-term pain' from the Trump administration's tariff policies is worth it to break an American addiction to 'cheap labor and cheap goods.'" Moore stated, "'If it creates jobs here and it helps sustain jobs, maybe we ought to pay a little more for a product made by Americans.'" However, according to Carolina Journal, North Carolina's manufacturing sector lost 7,200 jobs in 2025 — a 1.6% decrease — making it one of only two job sectors to report annual losses. [Charlotte Observer, [8/16/25](https://www.charlotteobserver.com/news/politics-government/article311705979.html); Carolina Journal, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://www.carolinajournal.com/nc-loses-7200-manufacturing-jobs-says-nc-commerce/)]

- **A Conservative Think Tank Economist Said Tariffs Will Continue To Cost Manufacturing Jobs.** According to Carolina Journal, Brian Balfour, the John Locke Foundation's senior vice president of research, stated, "'As long as these tariffs are taking place, net impact will be negative in the manufacturing job sector.'" [Carolina Journal, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://www.carolinajournal.com/nc-loses-7200-manufacturing-jobs-says-nc-commerce/)]

**Trump Imposed 25-50% Tariffs On Furniture, And Moore Backed The Policy That Raised Prices On North Carolina's Signature Industry.** According to WFAE, Trump announced tariffs of 25% on kitchen cabinets, vanities, and upholstered wooden furniture — with cabinet tariffs escalating to 50% — claiming the state "has lost its furniture industry to China." Home Furnishings Association CEO Peter Theran warned that the higher costs could be passed on to consumers, which would threaten demand. [WFAE, [9/30/25](https://www.wfae.org/2025-09-30/trump-cites-north-carolina-in-furniture-tariff-announcement)]

- **Furniture Prices Outpaced Inflation As Tariffs Rippled Through The Industry.** According to CBS News, furniture prices outpaced general inflation, with "living room, kitchen and dining room furniture rising 4.6% in November from a year earlier, compared with a 2.7% annual increase in the overall Consumer Price Index." [CBS News, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/trump-tariff-increase-delay-furniture-kitchen-cabinets/)]

**Daimler Truck Announced Potential Layoffs Of 573 Workers Near Charlotte Due To Tariff Uncertainty.** According to WRAL, Daimler Truck announced a potential temporary layoff of 573 workers near Charlotte "due to reduced truck orders" driven by "customer uncertainty over economic policy and tariffs." N.C. State economist Mike Walden observed, "'Whenever there's economic uncertainty, that is not good for business.'" [WRAL, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://www.wral.com/story/new-economic-reality-nc-consumers-face-tariff-driven-inflation/22087128/)]

### Voted For Legislation That Cuts Working Families' Incomes

**Moore Voted For The One Big Beautiful Bill Act, Which The CBO Found Will Cut Incomes For The Poorest Americans While Boosting The Wealthiest.** According to the Congressional Budget Office, the One Big Beautiful Bill Act will decrease resources "for households toward the bottom of the income distribution" while increasing resources "for households in the middle and toward the top." The CBO estimated the highest 10% of earners would see incomes rise by 2.7% by 2034, while the lowest 10% would see incomes fall by 3.1%. [CBO, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://www.cbo.gov/publication/61387)]

- **Combined With Tariffs, The Legislation Would Cut Incomes For The Bottom 10% Of Households By About 7%.** According to the Yale Budget Lab, the combination of the One Big Beautiful Bill Act and 2025 tariffs "would reduce after-tax-and-transfer incomes on average among the bottom 80 percent of U.S. households, with the bottom ten percent of households seeing an average reduction of about 7 percent in incomes, while those at the top would see an increase of nearly 1.5 percent." [Yale Budget Lab, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://budgetlab.yale.edu/research/combined-distributional-effects-one-big-beautiful-bill-act-and-tariffs)]

**Moore Voted To Cut $187 Billion From Food Assistance That 1.4 Million North Carolinians Depend On.** According to the NC Institute of Medicine, the One Big Beautiful Bill Act cuts SNAP funding by $187 billion over the next decade, affecting "1.4 million" North Carolinians who receive food assistance, "including about 580,000 children, of which more than 40,000 are under 18 months old." The law shifts "$420 million" in costs to North Carolina and an additional "$65 million" in administrative costs. [NCIOM, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://nciom.org/federal-changes-to-food-assistance-in-north-carolina/)]

### Bet Against The Economy He Publicly Champions

**Moore Invested Up To $245,000 In A Fund That Profits When The Stock Market Declines — While Publicly Touting The Economy.** According to Notus, Moore "poured up to $245,000 of his own cash into an exotic investment fund that earns him money when a key stock market index falters." The fund, Direxion Daily Small Cap Bear 3X Shares, "delivers three times the inverse daily performance of the Russell 2000 Index." Moore made six purchases between late June and late August 2025 while simultaneously telling the public that Trump's policies are "'going to spur the economy.'" [Notus, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://www.notus.org/congress/tim-moore-investment-russell-2000-index-trump-economy)]

- **Moore Abruptly Switched From Bullish To Bearish Investments Mid-2025.** According to Notus, from early 2025 through mid-July, Moore invested "hundreds of thousands of dollars into the Direxion Daily Small Cap Bull 3X Shares fund" designed to profit when markets rise. After mid-July, "Moore has not invested in the bull-market fund since," pivoting entirely to the bearish fund — suggesting he privately expected the economic policies he publicly championed to fail. [Notus, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://www.notus.org/congress/tim-moore-investment-russell-2000-index-trump-economy)]

**Moore Failed To Timely Disclose Hundreds Of Thousands Of Dollars In Stock Trades Made Around Trump's "Liberation Day" Tariff Announcement, In A Potential STOCK Act Violation.** According to Fortune, Moore "failed to properly disclose dozens of stock trades made in April 2025, missing federal deadlines required by the Stop Trading on Congressional Knowledge (STOCK) Act." The undisclosed trades included six purchases of American Airlines stock worth $90,000-$300,000 from April 1 through April 22, four Ford purchases totaling $95,000-$250,000, and Harley-Davidson trades — all made around Trump's April 2 tariff declaration. [Fortune, [6/16/25](https://fortune.com/2025/06/16/tim-moore-stock-trades-liberation-day-disclosure/)]

- **Moore Serves As Vice Chairman Of The House Financial Services Subcommittee On Oversight And Investigations, Making His Late Disclosures Particularly Problematic.** According to Fortune, Moore's position on the oversight subcommittee gave him potential access to nonpublic financial information, heightening concerns about his trading activity around major economic policy announcements. [Fortune, [6/16/25](https://fortune.com/2025/06/16/tim-moore-stock-trades-liberation-day-disclosure/)]

### Impact on Everyday People

**Western North Carolina Manufacturing Layoffs Triggered A Ripple Effect Across The Local Economy.** According to WLOS, recent manufacturing layoffs in western North Carolina raised concerns "about their broader impact on the local economy, as manufacturing is one of the region's biggest employers." Clark Duncan of the Asheville Chamber of Commerce said, "'Anytime we see a cluster of layoff announcements as we have this month, it's a reason for concern.'" Manufacturing employs more than 100,000 residents across the mountains, and reduced spending by manufacturers ripples into engineering, transportation, and catering services. [WLOS, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://wlos.com/news/local/manufacturing-layoffs-western-north-carolina-wnc-raise-economic-concerns-economist-hurricane-helene-employers-region-jobs-employment-sector-concerns)]

**Three-Fourths Of Firms Surveyed Intended To Pass Tariff Costs Directly To Consumers.** According to WRAL, AllianceBernstein chief economist Eric Winograd said, "'You are starting to see scattered bits of the tariff inflation regime filter in,'" and Sarah Dickerson noted that "about three-fourths of firms locally [...] were intending to pass some of the tariff-related costs onto consumers." [WRAL, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://www.wral.com/story/new-economic-reality-nc-consumers-face-tariff-driven-inflation/22087128/)]

**Western North Carolina Food Assistance Requests Doubled As SNAP Cuts Took Effect.** According to WLOS, the Asheville-Buncombe Community Christian Ministry reported that "food assistance requests have doubled from 35 to 80 families daily" following changes from the One Big Beautiful Bill Act. Rev. Scott Rogers, ABCCM Executive Director, said, "'Our neighbors are hurting right now. They're scared, anxious, worried most about their children.'" [WLOS, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://wlos.com/news/local/western-north-carolina-residents-snap-supplemental-nutrition-assistance-program-big-beautiful-bill-department-health-human-services-united-states-department-agriculture-food-federal-eligibility-asheville-buncombe-community-christian-ministry)]

- **Approximately 187 Local Veterans Could Lose SNAP Eligibility Under The Law Moore Voted For.** According to WLOS, the One Big Beautiful Bill Act eliminated previous SNAP exemptions for veterans, and approximately "187 local veterans" in the Asheville-Buncombe area could lose eligibility. Rogers noted that "'having that kind of work that can show a paycheck or stub is a real challenge'" for residents with disabilities or inconsistent employment. [WLOS, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://wlos.com/news/local/western-north-carolina-residents-snap-supplemental-nutrition-assistance-program-big-beautiful-bill-department-health-human-services-united-states-department-agriculture-food-federal-eligibility-asheville-buncombe-community-christian-ministry)]
