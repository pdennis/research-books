# How To Win Against Rudy Yakym 
Rudy Yakym is just another loyal foot soldier for President Trump’s agenda that raised costs for hard-working Hoosiers. He voted to cut Medicaid and take health coverage away from thousands of people in his own district, broke his promise not to cut Medicare, cast the deciding vote to protect Trump’s tariffs that cost Indiana more than $8 billion. He also voted for the largest SNAP cut in history and cheered on Trump’s costly war with Iran as gas, food, and fertilizer prices surged. Hoosiers deserve a representative who will fight to lower their costs, not a rubber stamp for Trump.
# Issue Back-Ups
### **Yakym Voted Three Times To Cut Medicaid, Then Called It A Win**
- Yakym voted [three](https://clerk.house.gov/Votes/2025145) times for Republicans’ budget and reconciliation bills, ending with a bill that would kick at least [17 million](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2025/07/01/least-17-million-americans-would-lose-insurance-under-trump-plan/) Americans off their health insurance, including nearly [12 million](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2025/07/01/least-17-million-americans-would-lose-insurance-under-trump-plan/) who would become uninsured mostly because of Medicaid cuts. Nearly 28,000 people in his district could lose their coverage, and 160,800 Hoosiers in the 2nd District were enrolled in Medicaid or CHIP.
- Yakym [celebrated](https://x.com/RepRudyYakym/status/1940876471125610976?a=pfSQQrVYTaeviijbhJ8&s=20) the bill as “an incredible win for Hoosiers and the American people,” and later [claimed](https://x.com/RepRudyYakym/status/2064139288866918907?a=WM0DvUT1YDuqFoLbDEo&s=20) Republicans were “protecting Social Security, Medicare, and Medicaid by cutting out the waste, fraud, and abuse.”

**Message:** Yakym voted to take health care away from thousands of Hoosiers to pay for tax breaks for the wealthy, then called it a win.

### **Yakym Promised Not To Cut Medicare, Then Voted For Nearly $500 Billion In Cuts**
- In 2023, Yakym [promised,](https://x.com/RudyYakym/status/1674810026324815872?a=XYIhbU_ETc2rNCMpIO4&s=20) “Let me be clear, I will not support cuts to Social Security or Medicare.” Then he [voted](https://clerk.house.gov/Votes/2025145) for a bill that the Congressional Budget Office estimated would trigger nearly [$500 billion](https://www.cbo.gov/system/files/2025-05/61423-PAYGO.pdf) in cuts to Medicare, absent future congressional action.
- Yakym was a [member](https://web.archive.org/web/20250124151313/https:/rsc-pfluger.house.gov/about/membership) of the Republican Study Committee’s steering committee. RSC budgets called for [raising](https://rollcall.com/2023/06/14/conservatives-budget-plan-renews-battle-over-seniors-benefits/) the retirement age to 69 and turning Medicare into a [“premium support”](https://rollcall.com/2023/06/14/conservatives-budget-plan-renews-battle-over-seniors-benefits/) system. Raising the retirement age to 69 would cost the median-wage retiree up to [nearly $100,000](https://www.americanprogress.org/article/raising-the-retirement-age-for-social-security-would-cut-benefits-by-thousands-of-dollars-each-year/) in benefits over 10 years.

**Message:** Yakym broke his promise to protect Medicare and sided with a group that wants seniors to work longer for less.

### **Yakym Cast The Deciding Vote To Protect Trump’s Tariffs As They Cost Hoosiers Billions**
- Yakym repeatedly voted to block Congress from ending Trump’s tariffs, including in April 2025, when he cast the [deciding vote](https://clerk.house.gov/Votes/202594) in a 216-215 vote. He voted [again](https://clerk.house.gov/Votes/2025268) in September 2025 to block challenges to the tariffs, and in February 2026 he voted [against](https://clerk.house.gov/Votes/202665) ending Trump’s tariffs on Canada.
- Yakym [defended](https://www.elkharttruth.com/news/yakym-defends-tariff-threats-on-manufacturing-tour/article_b591e0dd-d526-5e05-8634-bf15ea9c2459.html) the tariffs, claiming “Canada takes advantage of us,” and [praised](https://x.com/RepRudyYakym/status/1920519977385259333?a=9_QMSCMLw5c5qAKlADI&s=20) Trump for “securing fair trade deals.” Meanwhile, Trump’s tariffs cost Indiana more than [$8 billion](https://www.axios.com/local/indianapolis/2026/09/04/trump-tariffs-costing-hoosiers-indiana-auto-parts-industry) since 2025, about $3,042 per household.

**Message:** Yakym put Trump’s tariffs ahead of Hoosier families and small businesses who then paid the price.

### **Yakym Voted For The Largest Snap Cut In History** 
- Yakym [voted](https://clerk.house.gov/Votes/2025190) for a bill that cut SNAP by $186 billion, the “largest cut to SNAP in history, and the average family could lose [$146](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/07/10/trumps-big-beautiful-bill-cuts-snap-for-millions-of-families.html) per month. More than [25,000](https://www.fns.usda.gov/data-research/data-visualization/snap-community-characteristics-congressional-district-dashboard) households in his district relied on SNAP.

**Message:** Yakym voted to make it harder for Hoosier families to put food on the table, all to give tax breaks to the wealthy.
### **Yakym Backed Trump’s Costly Iran War As Prices Surged For Hoosiers**
- Yakym celebrated the U.S. strikes on Iran as [“a bold and decisive step”](https://x.com/RepRudyYakym/status/2027734001398489553?s=20) and voted five times [against](https://clerk.house.gov/Votes/2026170) ending the war, opposing resolutions to withdraw U.S. forces from hostilities Congress never authorized.
- The war cost the Pentagon $38 billion through July, and CBO found higher energy costs drove more than 40 percent of consumer inflation in the second quarter of 2026. Indiana farmers saw diesel prices more than double and fertilizer prices jump as much as 35 percent, while gas prices rose across the state.

**Message:** Yakym rubber-stamped Trump’s war, and Hoosiers are paying for it.
