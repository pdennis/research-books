# Checked Out: Retiring and Rumored to Resign Early

**Dunn Announced He Would Not Seek Reelection In 2026, Then Fueled Speculation He Would Resign Early.** According to Roll Call, Dunn announced on January 13, 2026, that he would not seek a sixth term, saying "'the time has come to pass the torch to new conservative leaders.'" Within weeks, multiple outlets reported Dunn was considering resigning before his term ended. [Roll Call, [1/13/26](https://rollcall.com/2026/01/13/neal-dunn-republican-florida-retires-election/)]

- **House Republican Leadership Believed Dunn Would Resign By July 2026, Further Shrinking The GOP's Razor-Thin Majority.** According to Florida Politics, by February 2026 "house Republican leadership believes Dunn will resign by July," a move that would drop the GOP majority to 217-214 — the thinnest in nearly a century. Speaker Mike Johnson personally urged Dunn to stay. [Florida Politics, [2/11/26](https://floridapolitics.com/archives/779479-neal-dunns-office-continues-to-swat-away-speculation-about-potential-early-exit-from-congress/)]
