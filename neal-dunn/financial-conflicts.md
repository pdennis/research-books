# Financial Conflicts: Health Industry Money and STOCK Act Violation

### Health Care Conflicts of Interest

**Dunn Accepted Over $273,000 From Health Industry Donors While Voting Against Lowering Drug Costs.** According to OpenSecrets, the health professionals industry was the top contributor to Dunn's campaign, with donations totaling $273,641 as of December 31, 2024. During the same period, Dunn voted against capping insulin at $35 a month and against allowing Medicare to negotiate drug prices. [OpenSecrets, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://www.opensecrets.org/members-of-congress/neal-dunn/industries?cid=N00037442)]

**Dunn Founded A 50-Physician Medical Practice And A Regional Bank Before Coming To Congress.** According to his official biography, Dunn founded the Panama City Urology Center, Bay Regional Cancer Center, and the Advanced Urology Institute, "a 50-physician practice with over 400 employees and offices throughout Florida." He was also "the founding Chairman of Summit Bank, a large regional bank headquartered in Panama City." [Neal Dunn Biography, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://dunn.house.gov/biography)]

**Dunn Held Pharmaceutical And Health Products Investments Worth Over $32,000 While Voting On Health Care Legislation.** According to OpenSecrets, Dunn's 2018 financial disclosure reported pharmaceutical and health products holdings valued at $32,500, in addition to an estimated net worth of over $9.5 million. [OpenSecrets, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://www.opensecrets.org/personal-finances/neal-dunn/net-worth?cid=N00037442)]

### Ethics and Financial Disclosure Violations

**Dunn Violated The STOCK Act By Filing A Late Financial Disclosure On A MicroStrategy Stock Purchase.** According to OpenSecrets, Dunn's wife purchased between $15,001 and $50,000 worth of stock in MicroStrategy Inc. — a business intelligence and cryptocurrency firm that owned more than $40 billion worth of Bitcoin — on December 24, 2024. Dunn did not disclose the trade until March 10, 2025, approximately 76 days after the purchase and well past the 45-day federal deadline. [OpenSecrets, [3/13/25](https://www.opensecrets.org/news/2025/03/rep-neal-dunn-becomes-latest-lawmaker-to-violate-stock-act)]

**Dunn Had An Estimated Net Worth Of Over $9.5 Million While Representing One Of Florida's Poorest Districts.** According to OpenSecrets, Dunn's financial disclosures reported an estimated net worth of over $9.5 million. His district includes 13 counties that exceed the state poverty level of 12.4 percent and have household incomes below the statewide median of $71,000. [OpenSecrets, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://www.opensecrets.org/personal-finances/neal-dunn/net-worth?cid=N00037442)]
