# Staff Scandal: Chief of Staff Resigned Amid House Ethics Probe

**Dunn's Chief Of Staff Resigned In 2018 After Being Named In A House Ethics Committee Sexual Harassment Investigation.** According to The Hill, Brian Schubert resigned as Dunn's chief of staff after the House Ethics Committee announced an investigative subcommittee to determine if Schubert and his former boss, Rep. Patrick Meehan, "engaged in conduct that constitutes sexual harassment, retaliation, or misuse of official resources." Dunn had hired Schubert after Schubert left Meehan's office. [Hill, [3/1/18](https://thehill.com/homenews/house/376083-florida-reps-chief-of-staff-resigns-amid-sexual-misconduct-allegations-report/)]
