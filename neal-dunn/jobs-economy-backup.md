# Rep. Neal Dunn (FL-02): Can't Be Trusted on Jobs and the Economy

Rep. Neal Dunn has consistently voted against the interests of working families in his own district — one of the poorest in Florida, where 13 counties exceed the state poverty level. Dunn voted against the bipartisan Infrastructure Investment and Jobs Act, which funded roads, bridges, and broadband across rural north Florida, and against the CHIPS and Science Act, which invested in domestic manufacturing and high-tech jobs. He then voted to keep Trump's tariffs on Canada — tariffs that a University of Tampa economist called a "Category 5 economic hurricane" for Florida and that Florida TaxWatch warned would raise grocery bills by 10 to 15 percent and add $10,000 to the cost of a new home. Nationally, 72,000 manufacturing jobs were lost in the eight months following the tariffs Dunn defended. Meanwhile, Dunn cast the decisive vote for the One Big Beautiful Bill Act, which independent analysts found could threaten 33,000 Florida jobs and blow a $3 billion hole in the state economy through Medicaid cuts alone. As a multimillionaire who founded a 50-physician medical empire and a regional bank, Dunn has been insulated from the economic pain his votes have inflicted on the working families of north Florida.

---

### Voted Against Major Job-Creating Investments

**Dunn Voted Against The Bipartisan Infrastructure Investment And Jobs Act.** According to GovTrack, Dunn voted Nay on H.R. 3684, the Infrastructure Investment and Jobs Act, on November 5, 2021. The bill passed 228-206 with 13 Republicans joining all but 6 Democrats. The law invested $1.2 trillion in roads, bridges, broadband, ports, and water systems — including projects across rural north Florida. [GovTrack, [11/5/21](https://www.govtrack.us/congress/votes/117-2021/h369)]

**Dunn Voted Against The CHIPS And Science Act, Which Invested Billions In Domestic Manufacturing And High-Tech Jobs.** According to GovTrack, Dunn voted Nay on H.R. 4346, the CHIPS and Science Act, on July 28, 2022. The bill passed 243-187 with 24 Republicans voting in favor. The law invested $52 billion in domestic semiconductor manufacturing and $200 billion in scientific research to create American jobs and reduce dependence on foreign supply chains. [GovTrack, [7/28/22](https://www.govtrack.us/congress/votes/117-2022/h404)]

### Backed Tariffs That Hammered Florida's Economy

**Dunn Voted Against Repealing Trump's Tariffs On Canada, Which Were Projected To Raise Florida Grocery Bills By 10 To 15 Percent.** According to NBC News, the House passed a resolution to rescind Trump's tariffs on Canada by a vote of 219-211 on February 11, 2026, with six Republicans crossing party lines to support repeal. Dunn was not among them. Florida TaxWatch warned the tariffs could raise grocery bills by 10 to 15 percent and add $10,000 to the cost of a new Florida home. [NBC News, [2/11/26](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/congress/house-votes-end-trump-tariffs-canada-rcna258661)]

**A University Of Tampa Economist Called The Tariffs A 'Category 5 Economic Hurricane' For Florida.** According to Newsweek, University of Tampa economist Abigail Hall Blanco warned, "'Florida may be the Sunshine State — but this is a Category 5 economic hurricane created by policy.'" Blanco identified three groups facing losses: consumers paying higher prices, import-dependent businesses like restaurants and construction firms, and exporters facing retaliatory tariffs. Florida is the eighth-largest import destination in the U.S., receiving over $112 billion in merchandise goods from countries targeted by the tariffs. [Newsweek, [4/4/25](https://www.newsweek.com/donald-trump-tariffs-impact-florida-jobs-economy-2055385)]

**Florida TaxWatch Found Tariffs Would Add $10,000 To The Cost Of A New Florida Home And $7,000 To A Car.** According to the Business Observer, a Florida TaxWatch analysis found that tariffs would add approximately $10,000 to the cost of a Florida home and $7,000 to the cost of a car. Construction materials tariffs alone were expected to increase costs by more than $3 billion statewide. [Business Observer, [7/11/25](https://www.businessobserverfl.com/news/2025/jul/11/report-tariffs-add-to-cost-florida-house-cars/)]

**The U.S. Lost 72,000 Manufacturing Jobs In The Eight Months After The Tariffs Dunn Defended.** According to Common Dreams, federal data showed that "in each of the eight months that followed Trump's Liberation Day tariffs, manufacturing companies reduced their workforce," with a total of 72,000 manufacturing jobs lost from April through December 2025. Census Bureau data also showed construction spending in manufacturing contracted, reversing growth from semiconductor and renewable energy investments. [Common Dreams, [2/3/26](https://www.commondreams.org/news/tariffs-and-us-manufacturing)]

### Cast Decisive Vote For Bill That Could Destroy 33,000 Florida Jobs

**Dunn Voted For The One Big Beautiful Bill Act, Which Could Threaten 33,000 Florida Jobs And Blow A $3 Billion Hole In The State Economy.** According to WUSF, the Medicaid provisions in the OBBBA could "threaten approximately 33,000 jobs — half healthcare-related" and create "a potential $3 billion hole in state economy." Florida Policy Institute CEO Sadaf Knight said the provider tax ban "would really hamstring our ability to raise the dollars that we need to fund our Medicaid program." Dunn voted Yea on the bill, which passed 215-214. [WUSF, [5/30/25](https://www.wusf.org/politics-issues/2025-05-30/trump-big-beautiful-bill-cuts-medicaid-snap-what-that-means-for-floridians)]

- **The OBBBA Would More Than Double Florida's Cost Of Administering SNAP From $89 Million To $1.6 Billion.** According to WUSF, the law required states to pay 5 to 25 percent of food benefit costs for the first time, with Florida's share set at 25 percent based on its payment error rate. The No Kid Hungry Florida director said, "'These dramatic increases put millions of Florida's children and families at risk of losing a critical program.'" [WUSF, [5/30/25](https://www.wusf.org/politics-issues/2025-05-30/trump-big-beautiful-bill-cuts-medicaid-snap-what-that-means-for-floridians)]

- **The Yale Budget Lab Found The Combined Effect Of Tariffs And The OBBBA Would Cut Incomes For The Poorest Americans By Approximately 7 Percent While Raising Incomes For The Wealthiest By 1.5 Percent.** According to the Yale Budget Lab, "the combination of OBBBA and 2025 tariffs would reduce resources on average among the bottom 80 percent of households." The bottom 10 percent of households would see an approximately 7 percent reduction in after-tax income over 2026-2034, while the top 10 percent would see a 1.5 percent increase. [Yale Budget Lab, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://budgetlab.yale.edu/research/combined-distributional-effects-one-big-beautiful-bill-act-and-tariffs-0)]

**Dunn Founded A 50-Physician Medical Practice And A Regional Bank Before Coming To Congress.** According to his official biography, Dunn founded the Panama City Urology Center, Bay Regional Cancer Center, and the Advanced Urology Institute, "a 50-physician practice with over 400 employees and offices throughout Florida." He was also "the founding Chairman of Summit Bank, a large regional bank headquartered in Panama City." [Neal Dunn Biography, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://dunn.house.gov/biography)]

His district includes 13 counties that exceed the state poverty level of 12.4 percent and have household incomes below the statewide median of $71,000. [OpenSecrets, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://www.opensecrets.org/personal-finances/neal-dunn/net-worth?cid=N00037442)]

**Dunn Violated The STOCK Act By Filing A Late Financial Disclosure On A MicroStrategy Stock Purchase.** According to OpenSecrets, Dunn's wife purchased between $15,001 and $50,000 worth of stock in MicroStrategy Inc. on December 24, 2024. Dunn did not disclose the trade until March 10, 2025, approximately 76 days after the purchase and well past the 45-day federal deadline. [OpenSecrets, [3/13/25](https://www.opensecrets.org/news/2025/03/rep-neal-dunn-becomes-latest-lawmaker-to-violate-stock-act)]

### Impact on Everyday People

**Thirteen Counties In Dunn's District Exceeded The State Poverty Level, With Household Incomes Below The Statewide Median.** According to the Florida Public Health Review, Congressional District 2 stretches from the Alabama border through the Big Bend, and "thirteen of the counties exceed the state poverty level of 12.4% and have a household income less than the statewide median of $71,000." [Florida Public Health Review, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://digitalcommons.unf.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1289&context=fphr)]

**Gadsden County — In Dunn's District — Had The Highest Child Food Insecurity Rate In Florida At 35.5 Percent.** According to WUSF, Gadsden County ranked last among Florida's 67 counties for child food security, with 35.5 percent of children experiencing food insecurity — nearly four times the rate in St. Johns County (9.1 percent). [WUSF, [2/21/25](https://www.wusf.org/health-news-florida/2025-02-21/food-insecurity-major-concern-florida-children-well-being)]

**The Tax Foundation Found Tariffs Would Cost The Average American Household $1,300 In 2026.** According to the Tax Foundation, Trump's tariffs amounted to an average tax increase of $1,000 per U.S. household in 2025 and a projected $1,300 per household in 2026, representing "the largest U.S. tax increase as a percent of GDP" since 1993. [Tax Foundation, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://taxfoundation.org/research/all/federal/trump-tariffs-trade-war/)]
