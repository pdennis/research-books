# How To Win Against Addison McDowell

Addison McDowell was a [Blue Cross Blue Shield lobbyist until the day he launched his congressional campaign](https://ncnewsline.com/briefs/ballot-bites-a-lost-endorsement-and-a-new-trump-favorite-in-ncs-6th-congressional-district/) — and since arriving in Washington, every major vote he has cast has made life harder for the North Carolina families he claims to represent. McDowell voted for the [One Big Beautiful Bill](https://www.wfae.org/health/2026-01-17/changes-required-by-the-one-big-beautiful-bill-will-affect-medicaid-in-nc-heres-what-you-need-to-know), which cut [$1 trillion from Medicaid](https://www.wfae.org/health/2026-01-17/changes-required-by-the-one-big-beautiful-bill-will-affect-medicaid-in-nc-heres-what-you-need-to-know) — threatening coverage for more than 670,000 North Carolinians — and slashed [$187 billion from SNAP](https://nciom.org/federal-changes-to-food-assistance-in-north-carolina/), the largest cut to food assistance in history, while representing [one of the most food-insecure metro areas in the country](https://greensboro.com/news/local_news/greensboro-high-point-top-nationwide-hunger-list/article_88828c52-e568-11e4-9b5b-db55afd7f635.html). He told ABC News viewers to ["trust the president" on tariffs](https://abcnews.go.com/Politics/video/trust-president-rep-addison-mcdowell-tariff-impacts-121479650) that are now [costing the average household $1,300 a year](https://taxfoundation.org/research/all/federal/trump-tariffs-trade-war/) and contributed to the [closure of a 250-worker textile plant in his own district](https://businessnc.com/unifi-now-says-250-will-lose-jobs-when-rockingham-county-textile-plant-closes/). He [refused to join the bipartisan effort to extend ACA subsidies](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/congress/centrist-republicans-revolt-signing-petition-force-vote-obamacare-fund-rcna249693), causing a [29% premium spike](https://www.northcarolinahealthnews.org/2025/08/15/big-health-insurance-increases-obamacare-coverage-one-big-beautiful-bill-act/) from his former employer Blue Cross Blue Shield of North Carolina. He has [never held a single in-person town hall](https://ncnewsline.com/2025/10/20/as-trump-opposition-grows-north-carolina-republicans-duck-town-halls-does-it-matter/) to face the constituents dealing with these consequences.

### **HEALTH CARE: INSURANCE LOBBYIST VOTED TO GUT COVERAGE**

McDowell was a [registered lobbyist for Blue Cross Blue Shield of NC](https://ncnewsline.com/briefs/ballot-bites-a-lost-endorsement-and-a-new-trump-favorite-in-ncs-6th-congressional-district/) until the day he launched his campaign — then voted for [$1 trillion in Medicaid cuts](https://www.wfae.org/health/2026-01-17/changes-required-by-the-one-big-beautiful-bill-will-affect-medicaid-in-nc-heres-what-you-need-to-know) that could trigger an automatic rollback of NC's Medicaid expansion, threatening 670,000 people.
He [refused to sign the discharge petition](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/congress/centrist-republicans-revolt-signing-petition-force-vote-obamacare-fund-rcna249693) and [voted against extending ACA subsidies](https://abcnews.com/Politics/9-republicans-vote-democrats-set-house-vote-3/story?id=128999959), causing a [29% premium spike](https://www.northcarolinahealthnews.org/2025/08/15/big-health-insurance-increases-obamacare-coverage-one-big-beautiful-bill-act/) from his former employer BCBS-NC.
A Hendersonville man's monthly premium [nearly tripled from $1,000 to $2,670](https://carolinapublicpress.org/74211/nc-health-insurance-consumers-left-in-limbo-by-congress/), and a Charlotte-area couple nearing retirement faces a potential [$24,221 annual premium increase](https://carolinapublicpress.org/74211/nc-health-insurance-consumers-left-in-limbo-by-congress/).

**Message:** McDowell lobbied for the insurance industry, then voted to let them raise premiums on 800,000 North Carolinians.

### **AFFORDABILITY: SAID "TRUST THE PRESIDENT" — THEN PRICES WENT UP**

McDowell told ABC News to ["trust the president" on tariffs](https://abcnews.go.com/Politics/video/trust-president-rep-addison-mcdowell-tariff-impacts-121479650) that the Tax Foundation found amount to the [largest U.S. tax increase as a percent of GDP since 1993](https://taxfoundation.org/research/all/federal/trump-tariffs-trade-war/), costing the average household $1,300 in 2026.
North Carolina families saw [beef prices rise 16% and coffee nearly 20%](https://progressncaction.org/2026/02/the-costs-we-carry/) due to tariff-driven cost increases, and [three-in-four NC voters said tariffs would increase prices](https://www.carolinajournal.com/polls/may-2025-three-in-four-nc-voters-say-tariffs-will-increase-prices/).
McDowell voted for the [largest SNAP cut in history ($187 billion)](https://nciom.org/federal-changes-to-food-assistance-in-north-carolina/) while representing one of the [most food-insecure metro areas in the country](https://greensboro.com/news/local_news/greensboro-high-point-top-nationwide-hunger-list/article_88828c52-e568-11e4-9b5b-db55afd7f635.html), threatening food assistance for 1.4 million North Carolinians including 600,000 children.

**Message:** McDowell told you to trust Trump on tariffs — now you're paying more for groceries, health care, and everything else.

### **JOBS AND THE ECONOMY: TARIFFS KILLED LOCAL JOBS**

[Unifi closed its Rockingham County plant and eliminated 250 manufacturing jobs](https://businessnc.com/unifi-now-says-250-will-lose-jobs-when-rockingham-county-textile-plant-closes/) in McDowell's district, citing tariff-driven customer uncertainty.
North Carolina [lost 7,200 manufacturing jobs in 2025](https://www.carolinajournal.com/nc-loses-7200-manufacturing-jobs-says-nc-commerce/) — the only major sector with net losses — while McDowell defended the tariffs causing it.
McDowell voted for a bill that could [cost North Carolina 45,000 clean energy jobs](https://www.wunc.org/politics/2025-07-03/north-carolina-politicians-react-congress-tax-bill) and put [$24 billion in clean energy investments at risk](https://ncvoices.com/north-carolinas-clean-energy-gains-at-risk-as-federal-incentives-fade/).
The bill gave the [richest 1% more than $50,000 in tax cuts while the poorest 20% got just $110](https://www.americanprogress.org/article/7-ways-the-big-beautiful-bill-cuts-taxes-for-the-rich/).

**Message:** McDowell's tariffs closed a factory in his own district while his tax bill rewards millionaires at the expense of working families.

### **NO EXPERIENCE, NO MANDATE, NO ACCOUNTABILITY**

McDowell [had never held elected office before winning his seat at age 30](https://spectrumlocalnews.com/nc/charlotte/news/2025/01/09/addison-mcdowell-congress-trump-carolina) — his only experience was lobbying for Blue Cross Blue Shield and working as a constituent liaison.
He [won his primary with only 26% of the vote](https://www.wunc.org/politics/2024-03-14/mcdowell-north-carolina-house-race-walker-runoff-2024) and avoided a runoff only because his closest opponent dropped out.
He has [not held a single in-person town hall since taking office](https://ncnewsline.com/2025/10/20/as-trump-opposition-grows-north-carolina-republicans-duck-town-halls-does-it-matter/), ducking constituents dealing with skyrocketing premiums, rising grocery prices, and threatened Medicaid coverage.
He [refused to answer NC Health News' health care questionnaire](https://www.northcarolinahealthnews.org/govpack_profiles/addison-mcdowell/) during the 2024 campaign.

**Message:** McDowell won't face his own constituents because he can't defend his record.

### **BACKED DOGE WHILE HIS DISTRICT FACED JOB CUTS**

McDowell said he had [DOGE's "back"](https://www.yahoo.com/news/addison-mcdowell-speaks-fox8-big-223029803.html) while more than [35,000 jobs in North Carolina were at risk](https://ncnewsline.com/briefs/more-than-35000-jobs-in-north-carolina-at-risk-under-potential-federal-cuts-new-report-says/) under federal spending cuts.

**Message:** McDowell backed the people cutting your jobs instead of fighting for them.
