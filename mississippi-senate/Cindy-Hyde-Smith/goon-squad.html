Sheriff Bailey’s Officers Called Themselves The “Goon Squad” And Were Accused Of Torturing And Sexually Assaulting Rankin County Residents 

Five Officers From The Rankin County Sheriff’s Office Were Charged For Torturing Two Black Men 

Five Officers From The Rankin County Sheriff’s Office Entered A Home Without A Warrant And Tortured Two Black Men By Tasing Them, Beating Them And Forcing Sex Toys Down Their Throats. According to American Journal News, “In January 2023, five officers from the Rankin County Sheriff’s Office entered a home without a warrant and proceeded to torture the two men inside, both of whom were Black. The men were repeatedly tased, beaten with a wooden plank, and had a sex toy forced down their throats.” [American Journal News, 4/6/26]

  • One Of The Victims Was Shot By The Officer When The Officer Stuck His Gun In The Man’s Mouth Which Shattered His Jawbone. According to American Journal News, “One of the men was accidentally shot when an officer forced a gun into his mouth, shattering his jawbone.” [American Journal News, 4/6/26]
  • During The Attack, The Officers Were Using Racial Slurs. According to American Journal News, “The victims say the officers used racial slurs throughout the attack.” [American Journal News, 4/6/26]

All Five Officers Pled Guilty To Several Charges Including Committing A Hate Crime. American Journal News, “All five officers pleaded guilty to several charges filed against them, including committing a hate crime.” [American Journal News, 4/6/26]

Rankin County Sheriff’s Office Had Multiple Incidents Of Police Violence 

Dozens Of Rankin County Residents Witnessed Or Had Been Victims Of Sexual Violence At The Hands Of Rankin County Police Officers. According to American Journal News, “The New York Times reported that dozens of Rankin County residents had either witnessed or been victims of similar police violence. Another man said officers dragged him from his bed in the middle of the night and threatened to sodomize him with a flashlight.” [American Journal News, 4/6/26]

The Group Of Offending Officers Allegedly Referred To Themselves As “The Goon Squad.” According to American Journal News, “The group of offending officers allegedly referred to themselves as ‘The Goon Squad.’” [American Journal News, 4/6/26]

Sheriff Bailey Claimed That He Had No Knowledge Of The Abuse Despite Residents claiming That They Reported The Brutality To His Office. According to American Journal News, “Bailey said he was stunned to learn of the abuse and had no prior knowledge of it, even though several residents said they reported the brutality directly to his office. ‘I have 240 employees, there’s no way I can be with them each and every day,’ he told The New York Times.” [American Journal News, 4/6/26]

Sheriff Bailey Was Accused Of Unlawfully Spying On His Girlfriend’s Phone And Local Officials Were Accused Of Covering Up The Investigation. According to American Journal News, “ In 2016, a local district attorney said Bailey unlawfully obtained a subpoena to spy on the phone and text message records of his ex-girlfriend. Inmates At Rankin County Jail Accused Sheriff Bailey Of Having Them Do Manual Labor On His Mother's Chicken Ranch With Some Not Being Compensated At All

Inmates At Rankin County Jail Accused Sheriff Bailey Of Having Them Do Manual Labor On His Mother's Chicken Ranch With Some Not Being Compensated At All. According to American Journal News, “ Last month, several inmates at the Rankin County jail said Bailey had them do manual labor on his mother’s chicken ranch.
