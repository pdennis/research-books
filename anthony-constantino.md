# **How To Win Against Anthony Constantino**

Anthony Constantino is the Republican nominee for New York's 21^st^ congressional district who is running for Congress on a platform largely rooted in his loyalty to Donald Trump. He has been endorsed by President Trump and Speaker Mike Johnson, who led the way in the passage of the One Big Beautiful Bill, which made massive cuts to Medicaid and food assistance.

Constantino has repeatedly defended and praised Trump's tariffs and trade policies, which have raised prices for New Yorkers. He also supported Trump's war in Iran, which has resulted in New Yorkers paying more at the pump.

New Yorkers don't need another rubber stamp for Trump's agenda of less healthcare, higher prices, and a prolonged war in Iran.

### Constantino Supported Policies That Would Cut Healthcare For Millions Of Americans

-   Constantino promised to stand by President Trump, who signed into law the One Big, Beautiful Bill that kicked more than [17 million Americans](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2025/07/01/least-17-million-americans-would-lose-insurance-under-trump-plan/) off their health insurance.

**Message:** Constantino promised to be another vote in Congress to kick New Yorkers off their health care all to give billionaires tax breaks.

### Constantino Supported Trump's Agenda, Which Included Cuts To Medicare

-   Constantino promised to stand by President Trump, who [fought](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2025/may/22/house-vote-trump-tax-bill) for passage of the One Big Beautiful Bill, which would trigger nearly [$500 billion](https://www.cbo.gov/system/files/2025-05/61423-PAYGO.pdf) in Medicare cuts absent future congressional action. Nearly [4 million](https://www.kff.org/medicare/state-indicator/total-medicare-beneficiaries) New Yorkers relied on Medicare for insurance.

**Message:** Constantino would be another vote to slash Medicare for seniors.

### Constantino Stood By Trump's Agenda, Which Included Sweeping Cuts To Food Assistance

-   Constantino promised to stand by President Trump even though his One Big, Beautiful Bill was [behind](https://www.foxnews.com/politics/johnson-touts-beauty-unified-government) the single largest cut to SNAP in history.

**Message:** Constantino would be a rubber stamp for policies that cut food assistance programs New Yorkers rely on to afford groceries.

### Constantino Repeatedly Defended Trump's Costly Tariffs

-   Constantino [repeatedly](https://x.com/constantino/status/1883886023241031703) [defended](https://x.com/constantino/status/1908943420997124550) and praised Trump's tariffs that have increased prices for New Yorkers and hurt small businesses.

**Message:** Constantino would do nothing to lower costs for New Yorkers suffering under the affordability crisis caused by Trump's reckless tariffs.

### Constantino Supported Trump's Actions In Iran

-   Constantino supported Trump's actions in Iran, [claiming](https://www.wwnytv.com/2026/06/03/constantino-discuss-congressional-campaign-live-interview/) "I trust him to use his judgement." He also said he believed Trump's actions would create "a lasting peace."
