HOW TO WIN AGAINST ROGER MARSHALL

 

Marshall Supported The Disastrous War With Iran

Roger Marshall has been an enthusiastic rubber stamp on the disastrous war with Iran. In vote after vote, Marshall shot down Congressional attempts to curtail the conflict, inflicting compounding pain on Kansas families. Insidiously, Marshall has alluded to using a nuclear weapon on Iran and bragged that the US blockade was “literally starving” Iranian civilians. In Kansas, the resulting economic shock led to drastic price hikes on fuel and fertilizer, hurting farmers in the field and consumers at the pump alike.

Message: Marshall fully embraced the disastrous Iran war, causing Kansas families, farmers, and businesses to suffer higher prices.

Marshall Opposed The CHIPS Act

Roger Marshall is no fan of investing in Kansas’s economy. He voted against the CHIPS and Science Act in 2022, even when his own Kansas colleague, Senator Moran, voted in favor of the bill. When the CHIPS Act became law, Kansas stood to benefit. One proposed semiconductor plant in Wichita promised nearly 2,000 new jobs, contingent on CHIPS Act funding. The CHIPS Act also delivered almost a million dollars in the form of a National Science Foundation workforce grant to Kansas State University. Yet, Marshall decided to vote against the interest of Kansas workers.

Message: Marshall voted against new jobs and workforce development for Kansans.

Marshall Frequently Traveled To His Expensive Beachfront Vacation Home In Florida On The Taxpayer's Dime

Florida Man Roger Marshall doesn’t want Kansans to know about his carpetbagging past. Marshall presents himself as a fellow Kansan through and through, but for years he has owned a ritzy, beachfront home in Florida. What’s worse, Marshall spent thousands of taxpayer dollars on over a dozen unexplained “official” trips to the Sarasota area where his beach house just so happens to be. Conspicuously, those visits stopped after the property was damaged by a hurricane in 2024. Now, Marshall is trying to wash his hands of the whole affair, attempting to sell the home and hoping Kansans are none the wiser.

Message: Marshall spent Kansan's tax dollars on trips to his waterfront Florida getaway.

Marshall Backed Cuts To Healthcare And Food Assistance For Kansans

Roger Marshall claims he has the backs of everyday Kansans. He claimed he would protect their Medicaid.  He claimed he would make sure their hospitals were funded. He claimed he was saving Kansans’ money. But Marshall did the exact opposite. In voting for the so-called One Big Beautiful Bill, Marshall wittingly unleashed chaos and suffering in the Sunflower State. Rural hospitals lost funding; patients lost coverage; vulnerable children lost access to food. But Roger Marshall calls this “America’s Golden Era.”

Message: By backing the cruel One Big Beautiful Bill, Marshall endorsed the erosion of critical food and healthcare safety nets for vulnerable Kansans.

Marshall Flip-Flopped On The Save Our Bacon Act

Roger Marshall is as spineless of a politician as they come. For years, Marshall advocated against Proposition 12, a California rule that required Kansas farmers to increase animal enclosure sizes if they wanted to sell to the Golden State. He led two separate pieces of legislation in the Senate to override Prop 12, positioning himself as a protector of Kansas farmers and ranchers who faced regulatory burden from the rule. But when the industry turned down his pleas for a defensive ad campaign and opposing groups started targeting him with TV ads, Marshall quietly withdrew his support for his own bill. When he’s feeling the heat, it is very clear that Roger Marshall lacks the principle to stand for anyone but himself and his own interests.

Message: Marshall won't represent Kansans' best interests when he's under pressure.

Marshall Backed Devastating Trade Wars

Roger Marshall brands himself a champion of Kansas farmers. In reality, he seems pretty comfortable letting farmers suffer, on one hand with input costs rising due to the Iran War (which Marshall supports), and on the other being squeezed by tariffs (which Marshall also supports). The resulting crisis has devastated Kansas agriculture and rippled through small business balance sheets and consumer pocketbooks. Because Marshall backed tariffs, Kansas entered a recession.

Message: Marshall embraced tariffs and stood by as they wreaked havoc on farmers and drove Kansas into a recession.

Marshall Took Credit For The Bipartisan Infrastructure Law He Staunchly Opposed

Roger Marshall turned his back on Kansans when he failed to support the Bipartisan Infrastructure Law. Marshall panned the bill, called it names, and voted it down. Thankfully, the bill still passed, because Kansas saw billions of dollars invested in a sprawling rejuvenation of infrastructure across the board, benefiting Kansans from all walks of live in every corner of the state. Marshall seems to be thankful, too, because he has made a habit of taking credit for projects around Kansas that stem from the Bipartisan Infrastructure Law he so vehemently opposed.

Message: Marshall opposed vital investments for Kansas infrastructure; then, he turned around and took credit for them.

Marshall Was All-In On DOGE Cuts

Roger Marshall knows full well how extensive the damage caused by DOGE cuts was for Kansas. Marshall championed DOGE’s hack-and-slash approach to gutting federal programs that Kansas farmers and consumers rely on. He voted repeatedly to codify DOGE cuts and even cofounded the Senate DOGE Caucus. All this as farmers lost protections, Kansans lost jobs by the thousands, and veterans saw their healthcare endangered. No wonder Roger Marshall keeps running away from his rightfully-frustrated constituents. He has no good answers for his bad deeds.

Message: Marshall hid from Kansas voters as DOGE ravaged the state.
