# Rep. Andy Harris (MD-01): Can't Be Trusted on Jobs and the Economy

Rep. Andy Harris has voted against every major jobs and economic investment bill of the past decade while cheerleading the policies that raise costs and threaten livelihoods in his own district. Harris voted against the bipartisan Infrastructure Investment and Jobs Act, which delivered over $7 billion to Maryland for roads, bridges, and broadband — including broadband expansion to underserved Eastern Shore communities where 17% of residents lacked affordable internet access. He voted against the CHIPS and Science Act, which brought semiconductor manufacturing jobs to American communities. He voted against the Raise the Wage Act, which would have gradually increased the federal minimum wage to $15 an hour. Then Harris championed Trump's tariffs — declaring "we will win the trade war" — even as China retaliated with a 37% tariff on U.S. soybeans and a 15% tariff on U.S. chicken, directly threatening the Eastern Shore's $4.8 billion poultry industry and its soybean farmers, who watched prices collapse from $13 to $10 per bushel. As Freedom Caucus chair, Harris cheered the DOGE mass layoffs that eliminated nearly 25,000 federal jobs in Maryland, telling 23,000 federal workers in his own district that their jobs were "immoral" and "paid for by our children and grandchildren." When those workers pleaded for help, his office sent form letters.

### Voted Against The Bipartisan Infrastructure Law That Delivered Billions To Maryland

**Harris Voted Against The Infrastructure Investment And Jobs Act, Which Delivered Over $7 Billion To Maryland.** According to the U.S. Department of Transportation, the bipartisan Infrastructure Investment and Jobs Act provided "more than $7 billion in federal funding directly to Maryland over five years to strengthen our roads and bridges, along with our water infrastructure, broadband internet, climate resilience, and more." Harris voted against the bill, which passed the House 228-206 with 13 Republican votes. [Department of Transportation, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://www.transportation.gov/briefing-room/bipartisan-infrastructure-law-will-deliver-maryland)]

- **Harris Claimed The Bill Was Too Expensive While His District Had 273 Structurally Deficient Bridges And 2,201 Miles Of Highway In Poor Condition.** According to Fox Baltimore, Harris said "we need infrastructure, but we need to pay for it and the bottom line was this was going to add hundreds of billions of dollars to the federal debt." Maryland had 273 bridges and over 2,201 miles of highway in poor condition at the time of the vote. [Fox Baltimore, [11/15/21](https://foxbaltimore.com/morning/maryland-representatives-speak-about-the-passage-of-the-bipartisan-infrastructure-bill)]

- **The Infrastructure Law Brought $267 Million In Broadband Funding To Maryland, Including Eastern Shore Expansion.** According to the Governor's office, Maryland received approval to deploy $267.7 million in federal broadband funding, connecting more than 43,000 homes — including communities on the Eastern Shore — to high-speed internet. Governor Moore said the funding reached "from Western Maryland to the Eastern Shore and everywhere in between." [Governor.Maryland.Gov, [7/11/24](https://governor.maryland.gov/news/press/pages/governor-moore-announces-approval-for-marylands-internet-for-all-proposals-enabling-access-to-2677-million-in-federal.aspx)]

**The DCCC Said Harris "Sided With Party Bosses" Rather Than Deliver Wins For The Eastern Shore.** According to the DCCC, "Harris already proved that he would rather side with party bosses than deliver wins for the Eastern Shore." The infrastructure law funded road repairs, bridge reconstruction, broadband expansion, clean water infrastructure, and public transit modernization across Maryland. [DCCC, [11/16/21](https://dccc.org/president-biden-signs-historic-infrastructure-deal-andy-harris-voted-against-into-law/)]

### Voted Against The CHIPS Act And The Raise The Wage Act

**Harris Voted Against The CHIPS And Science Act, Which Invested In American Semiconductor Manufacturing And Research Jobs.** According to GovTrack, Harris voted "Nay" on H.R. 4346, the CHIPS and Science Act of 2022, which invested $52 billion in domestic semiconductor manufacturing and created thousands of jobs. The bill passed 243-187, with Harris among the 187 Republicans who opposed it. Harris was the only member of Maryland's congressional delegation to vote against the bill. [GovTrack, [7/28/22](https://www.govtrack.us/congress/votes/117-2022/h404)]

**Harris Voted Against The Raise The Wage Act, Which Would Have Gradually Increased The Federal Minimum Wage To $15.** According to GovTrack, Harris voted "Nay" on H.R. 582, the Raise the Wage Act, which would have gradually raised the federal minimum wage from $7.25 to $15 per hour by 2025. The bill passed the House 231-199 on July 18, 2019. [GovTrack, [7/18/19](https://www.govtrack.us/congress/votes/116-2019/h496)]

### Championed Tariffs That Crushed Eastern Shore Farmers

**Harris Cheered Trump's Tariffs While China Retaliated With A 37% Tariff On Eastern Shore Soybeans.** According to the Talbot Spy, China — which purchased 52% of U.S. soybean exports worth $12.6 billion annually — "nearly halted American purchases since May while implementing a 37% tariff on U.S. soybean imports." Maryland's Eastern Shore, particularly Queen Anne's, Caroline, Talbot, Kent, and Somerset counties, is the stronghold of the state's soybean production. The Talbot Spy reported "there is no evidence" Harris "has made any attempt to formulate a solution to assist Eastern Shore soybean farmers." [Talbot Spy, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://talbotspy.org/trump-policies-disrupt-the-eastern-shore-soybean-market-by-wilson-dean/)]

- **Soybean Prices Collapsed From $13 To $10 Per Bushel, Leaving Eastern Shore Farmers With "Minimal To Non-Existent" Profits.** According to the Talbot Spy, soybean prices fell from $13 per bushel to $10, leaving farmers with a "profit picture at this moment somewhere between minimal to non-existent." American Soybean Association President Caleb Ragland called proposed bailout funds a "Band-Aid," emphasizing that farmers need "additional markets and higher prices — exactly what the Trump trade and aid policies are closing off." [Talbot Spy, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://talbotspy.org/trump-policies-disrupt-the-eastern-shore-soybean-market-by-wilson-dean/)]

**China Retaliated With A 15% Tariff On U.S. Chicken, Threatening The Delmarva Peninsula's $4.8 Billion Poultry Industry.** According to WHYY, after Trump announced plans to raise tariffs on Chinese imports, "China added an extra 15% tariff on U.S. poultry products." The Delmarva Peninsula processed 4.6 billion pounds of chicken in 2024, with a wholesale value of $4.8 billion. Approximately 15% of U.S. chicken is exported, and products like chicken paws have "really only a serious market outside the U.S." [WHYY, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://whyy.org/articles/delaware-chicken-industry-tariffs/)]

**Tariffs Raised Costs On Fertilizer And Steel That Eastern Shore Farmers Depend On.** According to the Capital Gazette, University of Maryland research professor Jim MacDonald said the tariffs' "biggest immediate impact on farmers, to me, is going to be tariffs and therefore price increases on that key fertilizer component," noting that "we import virtually all of the potassium we use [and] Canada produces almost all of it." Steel and aluminum tariffs also increased costs for "poultry houses on the Eastern Shore or the greenhouses and things in Anne Arundel." [Capital Gazette, [3/17/25](https://www.capitalgazette.com/2025/03/17/anne-arundel-farmers-brace-for-increased-costs-from-expanding-list-of-tariffs/)]

- **Harris Dismissed Companies Harmed By Tariffs As "Globalists" Who Had "Shipped Jobs Overseas."** According to a column by Dan Rodricks, Harris expressed confidence that "we will win the trade war, there's no question about that" and dismissed companies harmed by tariffs as "globalists" who had "shipped jobs overseas." Harris represents a mostly rural Maryland district with 4,600 farms that produce soy, corn, and poultry for the international market. [Dan Rodricks, [4/5/25](https://danrodricks.com/2025/04/05/trumpy-harris-confident-we-will-win-the-trade-war-and-defeat-the-globalists/)]

### Defended DOGE Cuts That Eliminated Thousands Of Federal Jobs In Maryland

**Harris Defended DOGE Mass Layoffs That Eliminated Nearly 25,000 Federal Jobs In Maryland.** According to CBS Baltimore, Maryland lost nearly 25,000 federal jobs in 2025, with 10,300 of those losses occurring during October and November alone. The state's unemployment rate increased from 3.8% in September to 4.2% by November. Harris defended the cuts, telling constituents that "time is of the essence" and backing Elon Musk's Department of Government Efficiency. [CBS Baltimore, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://www.cbsnews.com/baltimore/news/maryland-unemployment-job-losses-federal-workers-economy-2025/)]

**Harris Called Federal Workers' Jobs "Immoral" And Sent Form Letters To 23,000 Constituents Who Pleaded For Help.** According to The Baltimore Banner, approximately 23,000 federal employees in Harris's district contacted his office with concerns about layoffs and forced office returns. Harris sent standardized emails stating "we are in desperate need to slash down our deficit and downsize the federal government" and characterized their federally funded jobs as "immoral," saying they were "paid for by our children and grandchildren." One constituent described receiving "pro-Trump propaganda" rather than substantive representation. [Baltimore Banner, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://www.thebanner.com/politics-power/state-government/andy-harris-federal-workers-Z3H6CEKWE5CFTHWV4FIR4AZRNI/)]

- **An SSA Attorney In Harris's District Disputed His Claims, Noting Her Team Had "Recently Cleared A Sizable Backlog Of Disability Cases."** According to The Baltimore Banner, a Social Security Administration attorney pushed back on Harris's characterization of federal workers, noting her team "recently cleared a sizable backlog of disability cases" while working remotely — contradicting Harris's implication that federal employees were unproductive. [Baltimore Banner, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://www.thebanner.com/politics-power/state-government/andy-harris-federal-workers-Z3H6CEKWE5CFTHWV4FIR4AZRNI/)]

- **A Single Mother In Harris's District Faced A 3-4 Hour Round-Trip Commute Or Termination Under Return-To-Office Mandates.** According to The Baltimore Banner, a Cecil County single mother hired by Health and Human Services to work remotely would need a 3-4 hour round-trip to reach her agency's nearest office location. Another couple's flexible arrangement that enabled homeschooling their two children was upended when the husband faced a five-hour round-trip commute or resignation. [Baltimore Banner, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://www.thebanner.com/politics-power/state-government/andy-harris-federal-workers-Z3H6CEKWE5CFTHWV4FIR4AZRNI/)]

### Impact on Everyday People

**Eastern Shore Soybean Farmers Watched Profits Disappear While Harris Declared Victory In The Trade War.** According to the Talbot Spy, Eastern Shore soybean farmers saw prices drop from $13 to $10 per bushel after China's retaliatory tariffs, with profits falling to "minimal to non-existent." Meanwhile, tariffs on steel and fertilizer simultaneously raised their production costs. Harris told supporters "we will win the trade war, there's no question about that." [Talbot Spy, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://talbotspy.org/trump-policies-disrupt-the-eastern-shore-soybean-market-by-wilson-dean/); Dan Rodricks, [4/5/25](https://danrodricks.com/2025/04/05/trumpy-harris-confident-we-will-win-the-trade-war-and-defeat-the-globalists/)]

**Nearly 47% Of Lower Eastern Shore Households Were Classified As Asset-Limited And Income-Constrained.** According to the Star Democrat, United Way CEO Pam Gregory and Community Foundation president Erica Joseph wrote that nearly 47% of Lower Shore households are classified as ALICE — Asset Limited, Income Constrained, Employed — and that "a missed shift to care for a sick child, a car repair, a medical bill — any one of these can tip a working family into crisis." [Star Democrat, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://www.stardem.com/opinion/columns/hunger-doesnt-take-a-day-off-on-the-shore/article_0a4c5606-65fc-4975-869d-0c24d4c82c41.html)]

**Federal Workers In Harris's District Described Being "Demoralized" And Living In Constant Fear Of Termination.** According to The Baltimore Banner, federal employees in Harris's district reported anxiety over random firings, with one worker constantly checking email fearing termination. A Black employee felt "demoralized" by the revocation of diversity, equity, and inclusion programs. Harris's office did not respond to interview requests from The Baltimore Banner. [Baltimore Banner, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://www.thebanner.com/politics-power/state-government/andy-harris-federal-workers-Z3H6CEKWE5CFTHWV4FIR4AZRNI/)]
