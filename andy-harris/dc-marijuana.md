# Rep. Andy Harris (MD-01): Blocked D.C. Marijuana Legalization for a Decade

### Personally Blocked D.C. Marijuana Legalization For Over A Decade — While His Own State Legalized It

**Harris Inserted An Appropriations Rider That Has Blocked D.C. From Implementing Legal Marijuana Since 2014.** According to The Baltimore Sun, since 2014 — the same year D.C. voters approved legal recreational cannabis — an appropriations provision known as the "Harris rider" has barred the District of Columbia from using local funds to implement a regulated adult-use marijuana market. The rider has appeared in every congressional session since, including the current one. [Baltimore Sun, [6/11/25](https://www.baltimoresun.com/2025/06/11/rep-andy-harris-and-d-c-have-a-long-personal-war-over-marijuana-and-hes-not-budging/)]

- **Harris Blocked D.C. Marijuana Decriminalization The Same Year Maryland Decriminalized It.** According to a press release from Rep. Eleanor Holmes Norton, "the hypocrisy of Representative Andy Harris' amendment that would block the District of Columbia from decriminalizing marijuana could not be clearer" given that Maryland's own decriminalization law took effect the same day. Harris's amendment imposed a $25 fine — a fraction of Maryland's $100 fine — as the basis for calling D.C.'s law "bad policy." [Norton.House.Gov, [10/1/14](https://norton.house.gov/media-center/press-releases/norton-says-harris-dc-marijuana-amendment-hypocritically-contradicts-the)]
