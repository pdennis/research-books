# Rep. Andy Harris (MD-01): January 6 and the Gun Incident

### Voted To Overturn The 2020 Election — Even After The Capitol Was Stormed

**Harris Voted To Object To Electoral College Results From Both Arizona And Pennsylvania After The January 6 Attack.** According to Maryland Matters, Harris supported objections to the certification of electoral college votes from both Arizona and Pennsylvania in the early morning hours of January 7, 2021, after the Capitol had been attacked by a violent mob hours earlier. Harris was one of 138 House Republicans to object to Pennsylvania's results and among 121 who challenged Arizona's. [Maryland Matters, [1/7/21](https://www.marylandmatters.org/2021/01/07/after-siege-congress-affirms-electoral-college-votes-harris-supports-objections-overnight/)]

- **Harris Had Been Summoned To The White House To Discuss Overturning The Election.** According to an editorial by The Baltimore Sun, President Trump summoned a select group of GOP House members, including Harris, to the White House on December 21, 2020, to discuss overturning the election results and reinforce a plan by Trump attorney John Eastman to get Vice President Pence to reject electoral results on January 6. [Editorial - Baltimore Sun, [7/13/22](https://www.baltimoresun.com/opinion/editorial/bs-ed-0714-andy-harris-january-6-20220713-p6vjpkjvprfcjpx3pum5ud2axy-story.html)]

- **Harris Signed The Texas Amicus Brief Seeking To Overturn The 2020 Results.** According to the Republican Accountability Project, Harris signed the Texas amicus brief that sought to overturn the 2020 presidential election results, voted against establishing an independent January 6 commission, and voted against holding Steve Bannon in contempt of Congress. The group gave Harris an "F" for "trying to undermine our democracy." [Republican Accountability, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://accountability.gop/profile/rep-andy-harris/)]

**The Baltimore Sun Called Harris An "Unrepentant Jan. 6 Co-Conspirator."** According to an editorial by The Baltimore Sun, Harris's "inability to appreciate the seriousness of the events of Jan. 6 ought to be regarded as disqualifying for his job." When questioned about his involvement, Harris said he was "too busy to watch the hearing" and was instead "trying to lower the price of groceries and gasoline, and fighting crime." [Editorial - Baltimore Sun, [7/13/22](https://www.baltimoresun.com/opinion/editorial/bs-ed-0714-andy-harris-january-6-20220713-p6vjpkjvprfcjpx3pum5ud2axy-story.html)]

**The Maryland Democratic Party Called Harris A "Disgrace" And Demanded His Resignation.** According to The Baltimore Sun, the Maryland Democratic Party said Harris "must immediately resign," calling him a "disgrace" and stating that Republican members of Congress were "complicit in the violent occupation of the U.S. Capitol." [Baltimore Sun, [1/7/21](https://www.baltimoresun.com/2021/01/07/following-capitol-siege-maryland-democratic-party-says-rep-andy-harris-must-immediately-resign-calling-him-disgrace/)]

### Tried To Bring A Concealed Gun Onto The House Floor Two Weeks After January 6

**Harris Set Off A Metal Detector Near The House Chamber While Carrying A Concealed Firearm.** According to The Washington Post, Harris set off the magnetometer installed after the January 6 attack while on his way to the House chamber on January 21, 2021. When an officer used a metal detector wand, "a firearm was detected on Harris's side, concealed by his suit coat." Harris then tried to hand the gun to Rep. John Katko (R-NY), who refused to take it. Harris left and returned ten minutes later without the weapon. U.S. Capitol Police launched an investigation. [Washington Post, [1/21/21](https://www.washingtonpost.com/local/md-politics/andy-harris-gun/2021/01/21/81c7ba90-5c63-11eb-8bcf-3877871c819d_story.html)]
