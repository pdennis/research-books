# Rep. Andy Harris (MD-01): Can't Be Trusted on Affordability

Rep. Andy Harris has championed the policies driving up costs for families in Maryland's 1st District while voting against every major measure to bring them down. Harris enthusiastically backed Trump's tariffs — declaring "we will win the trade war" — even as the Yale Budget Lab estimated tariffs would cost the average American household $1,700 per year, with the lowest-income families hit hardest. He voted against the Inflation Reduction Act, which capped insulin at $35 for over 8,400 Maryland Medicare beneficiaries and created billions in clean energy tax credits for homeowners. Then, as Freedom Caucus chair, he pushed to pass the One Big Beautiful Bill Act, which slashed SNAP benefits by $186 billion — the largest cut in the program's history — threatening food assistance for 33,613 households in his own district, including 15,684 with children. The same law repealed clean energy tax credits that saved Maryland homeowners up to $9,000 on solar installations. Harris called the bill's SNAP work requirements a "major win," while Maryland's Department of Human Services warned it would "hurt Marylanders trying to put food on the table." On the Eastern Shore, where nearly 47% of Lower Shore households are classified as asset-limited and income-constrained, Harris's agenda raises costs on virtually every front — groceries, energy, health care, and the fertilizer and steel his own district's 4,600 farmers depend on.

### Championed Tariffs That Raised Costs for Families and Farmers in His Own District

**Harris Cheered Trump's Tariffs, Declaring "We Will Win The Trade War."** According to a column by Dan Rodricks, Harris expressed confidence that "we will win the trade war, there's no question about that" and dismissed companies harmed by tariffs as "globalists" who had "shipped jobs overseas." Harris represents a mostly rural Maryland district with 4,600 farms that produce soy, corn, and poultry for the international market. [Dan Rodricks, [4/5/25](https://danrodricks.com/2025/04/05/trumpy-harris-confident-we-will-win-the-trade-war-and-defeat-the-globalists/)]

**Harris Posted That "Reciprocal Tariffs Will Put The American Worker First" As Markets Plunged.** According to Maryland Matters, Harris "voiced his support of the tariffs on X," stating that "President Trump's reciprocal tariffs will put the American worker first and bring fairness back to international trade." Meanwhile, Maryland Democratic lawmakers warned the tariffs would "boost prices" and "hurt the economy" as financial markets tumbled. [Maryland Matters, [4/4/25](https://marylandmatters.org/2025/04/04/as-markets-plunge-maryland-democrats-warn-tariffs-will-boost-prices-hurt-economy/)]

- **The Yale Budget Lab Estimated Tariffs Would Cost The Average Household $1,700 Per Year, With The Poorest Families Hit Hardest.** According to the Yale Budget Lab, the 2025 tariffs represent "a short-run income loss of about $1,700 per household on average in 2025 dollars." The burden on the lowest-income households was more than three times that of the wealthiest — 2.4% of income versus 0.8% — making the tariffs a deeply regressive tax. [Yale Budget Lab, [11/17/25](https://budgetlab.yale.edu/research/state-us-tariffs-november-17-2025)]

**Tariffs Raised Costs On Fertilizer And Steel That Eastern Shore Farmers Depend On.** According to the Capital Gazette, University of Maryland research professor Jim MacDonald said the tariffs' "biggest immediate impact on farmers, to me, is going to be tariffs and therefore price increases on that key fertilizer component," noting that "we import virtually all of the potassium we use [and] Canada produces almost all of it." Steel and aluminum tariffs also increased costs for "poultry houses on the Eastern Shore or the greenhouses and things in Anne Arundel." [Capital Gazette, [3/17/25](https://www.capitalgazette.com/2025/03/17/anne-arundel-farmers-brace-for-increased-costs-from-expanding-list-of-tariffs/)]

- **China Retaliated With Tariffs On U.S. Chicken, Threatening The Delmarva Poultry Industry Harris Claims To Champion.** According to WHYY, after Trump announced plans to raise tariffs on Chinese imports by 20%, "China added an extra 15% tariff on U.S. poultry products." The Delmarva region — which includes Maryland's Eastern Shore — processed 4.6 billion pounds of chicken in 2024 in a $4.8 billion industry, and approximately 15% of U.S. chicken is exported. [WHYY, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://whyy.org/articles/delaware-chicken-industry-tariffs/)]

### Voted Against The Inflation Reduction Act's Savings On Insulin, Prescriptions, And Energy

**Harris Was The Only Maryland Congressman To Vote Against The Inflation Reduction Act, Which Capped Insulin At $35 And Let Medicare Negotiate Drug Prices.** According to The Baltimore Sun, "U.S. Rep. Andy Harris, a Republican, was the only Maryland congressman to vote against the bill." The law capped out-of-pocket insulin costs at $35 per month for Medicare patients and, for the first time, allowed Medicare to negotiate prescription drug prices. An estimated 8,400 Maryland Medicare beneficiaries gained access to the insulin cap. [Baltimore Sun, [8/14/22](https://www.baltimoresun.com/2022/08/14/from-lower-prescription-costs-to-cleaner-power-heres-what-the-federal-inflation-reduction-act-means-for-maryland/)]

**Harris Voted Against Clean Energy Tax Credits That Saved Maryland Homeowners Up To $9,000 On Solar Installations.** According to the Maryland Energy Administration, the Inflation Reduction Act established two rebate programs for homeowners: the Home Efficiency Rebates Opportunity Program for "qualifying whole-home energy efficiency upgrades that reduce energy use by at least 20%" and the Home Electrification and Appliance Rebates Program for "replacing fossil-fuel appliances with electric alternatives." The residential clean energy credit provided homeowners with a 30% tax credit — worth up to $9,000 on a typical $30,000 solar installation. [Maryland Energy Administration, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://energy.maryland.gov/Pages/IRArebates.aspx)]

### Voted For The Largest SNAP Cut In History, Threatening Food Assistance For 33,000 Households In His District

**Harris Voted For The One Big Beautiful Bill Act, Which Cut SNAP Benefits By $186 Billion — The Largest Reduction In The Program's History.** According to The Baltimore Sun, the One Big Beautiful Bill Act imposed new work requirements and other restrictions that amounted to approximately $186 billion in SNAP cuts over ten years — "a 20% cut that marks the largest reduction in the history of the program." Harris voted for the final bill on July 3, 2025, after initially voting "present" in May. [Baltimore Sun, [8/21/25](https://www.baltimoresun.com/2025/08/21/andy-harris-big-beautiful-bill/)]

- **33,613 Households In Harris's District Received SNAP Benefits, Including 15,684 With Children.** According to The Baltimore Sun, Maryland's Department of Human Services reported that 33,613 households in the 1st Congressional District receive SNAP, of which 15,684 include children. The Department warned the bill would "hurt Marylanders trying to put food on the table." [Baltimore Sun, [8/21/25](https://www.baltimoresun.com/2025/08/21/andy-harris-big-beautiful-bill/)]

**Harris Called SNAP Work Requirements A "Major Win" While The CBO Found The Bill Would Make The Poorest Americans Poorer.** According to a press release from Harris's office, the Freedom Caucus celebrated "major mandatory spending cuts and reforms [...] such as work requirements in entitlement programs like food stamps." However, according to The Baltimore Sun, CBO data showed the bill would cause the bottom 10% of earners to lose $1,200 annually while the top 10% gained $13,600. [Harris.House.Gov, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://harris.house.gov/media/press-releases/one-big-beautiful-bill-secures-major-wins-american-people); Baltimore Sun, [8/21/25](https://www.baltimoresun.com/2025/08/21/andy-harris-big-beautiful-bill/)]

**Harris Voted For A Bill That Could Force Maryland To Absorb $1 Billion In Costs Or Remove 300,000 People From Coverage.** According to InsuranceNewsNet, the One Big Beautiful Bill included over $720 billion in nationwide Medicaid cuts, and Maryland state Senate President Bill Ferguson warned that the changes could force Maryland to "either absorb $1 billion in additional costs or remove 300,000 people from coverage." An estimated 188,800 individuals in Harris's district were enrolled in Medicaid, including 62,100 children. [InsuranceNewsNet, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://insurancenewsnet.com/oarticle/rural-maryland-medicaid-recipients-risk-coverage-provider-shortage-under-trump-bill); Baltimore Sun, [8/21/25](https://www.baltimoresun.com/2025/08/21/andy-harris-big-beautiful-bill/)]

### Repealed Clean Energy Savings He Calls The "Green New Scam"

**The One Big Beautiful Bill Harris Voted For Repealed The Residential Clean Energy Tax Credit After 2025.** According to the Tax Foundation, the law "repealed the residential clean energy credit for expenditures made after calendar year 2025," eliminating a 30% federal tax credit for homeowners installing solar panels, battery storage, geothermal heat pumps, and other clean energy systems. A homeowner who installed $30,000 in rooftop solar would have received a $9,000 credit — now gone. [Tax Foundation, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://taxfoundation.org/blog/big-beautiful-bill-green-energy-tax-credit-changes/)]

**Harris Celebrated Ending Clean Energy Credits As Defeating The "Green New Scam."** According to a press release from Harris's office, the Freedom Caucus praised the One Big Beautiful Bill for "ending key 'Green New Scam' subsidies" and "opening up reliable energy production." Harris characterized tax credits that saved Maryland homeowners thousands on energy bills as a "scam" and a "racket." [Harris.House.Gov, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://harris.house.gov/media/press-releases/one-big-beautiful-bill-secures-major-wins-american-people)]

### Impact on Everyday People

**Nearly 47% Of Lower Eastern Shore Households Were Classified As Asset-Limited And Income-Constrained.** According to the Star Democrat, United Way CEO Pam Gregory and Community Foundation president Erica Joseph wrote that nearly 47% of Lower Shore households are classified as ALICE — Asset Limited, Income Constrained, Employed — and that "a missed shift to care for a sick child, a car repair, a medical bill — any one of these can tip a working family into crisis." They warned that SNAP cuts would deepen food insecurity for the approximately 40,000 people across Dorchester, Somerset, Wicomico, and Worcester counties who rely on the program. [Star Democrat, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://www.stardem.com/opinion/columns/hunger-doesnt-take-a-day-off-on-the-shore/article_0a4c5606-65fc-4975-869d-0c24d4c82c41.html)]

**680,000 Marylanders Relied On SNAP For Groceries When Governor Moore Declared A State Of Emergency Over Federal Benefit Disruptions.** According to The Baltimore Banner, more than 680,000 Marylanders — one in nine residents — relied on SNAP benefits. Governor Wes Moore declared a state of emergency and allocated $10 million to food banks after federal benefit disruptions. Local grocers reported that SNAP transactions formed a significant share of their revenue, and small stores in rural areas faced potential closure without the influx. [Baltimore Banner, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://www.thebanner.com/culture/food-drink/snap-benefits-maryland-changes-JXYRMLAFTVHNHJ5XZ6MKW4NWWQ/)]

**Eastern Shore Cancer Survivor Had Credited The ACA — Which Harris Voted To Repeal — With Keeping Him Alive.** According to The Baltimore Sun, Charles Birckhead, a 58-year-old Cambridge resident and cancer survivor, said: "Obamacare sustained us, I guess you could say." Harris voted to repeal the law that cut the uninsured rate in his district by 54% — from 63,252 to 29,193 residents — the largest decline of any congressional district in Maryland. [Baltimore Sun, [8/27/17](https://www.baltimoresun.com/2017/08/27/marylands-eastern-shore-a-gop-stronghold-home-to-thousands-who-now-have-insurance-thanks-to-obamacare/)]
