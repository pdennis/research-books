# Rep. Andy Harris (MD-01): Can't Be Trusted on Health Care

Rep. Andy Harris, a physician-anesthesiologist, has built a career opposing health care access for his constituents while benefiting from government-subsidized coverage himself. Upon winning election in 2010, Harris demanded to know why his taxpayer-funded congressional health plan would not kick in immediately — even as he campaigned on repealing the Affordable Care Act that extended coverage to tens of thousands of his own Eastern Shore constituents. He voted to repeal the ACA, voted for the American Health Care Act, voted against the Inflation Reduction Act's $35 insulin cap and Medicare drug price negotiation, and as Freedom Caucus chair pushed to accelerate Medicaid work requirements that could strip coverage from an estimated 24,000 people in his district. All the while, Harris has accepted over $920,000 from health care industry PACs — including anesthesiology groups, pharmaceutical companies, surgeons, and hospital associations — plus a 2019 donation from Purdue Pharma at the height of the opioid crisis ravaging his district.

### Demanded Government Health Care for Himself While Fighting to Deny It to Others

**Harris Demanded Early Government Health Insurance After Winning Election on Promise to Repeal the ACA.** According to The Baltimore Sun, Harris asked "incredulous questions about the fact that his congressional health benefit plan wouldn't kick in on the day he's inaugurated." Harris told fellow freshmen, "This is the only employer I've ever worked for where you don't get coverage the first day you are employed." At the time, 50.7 million Americans lacked health insurance coverage entirely. [Baltimore Sun, [11/18/10](https://www.baltimoresun.com/2010/11/18/andy-harris-learns-a-lesson-2/)]

**Harris Sought Taxpayer-Funded Coverage to Bridge a 28-Day Gap While Pledging to Repeal Coverage for 30 Million Americans.** According to Physicians for a National Health Program, Harris, a physician-anesthesiologist, "demanded immediate taxpayer-financed coverage while simultaneously pledging to 'fight to repeal health-care reform.'" The ACA would have insured approximately 30 million uninsured Americans, while Republican alternatives Harris supported would have insured only about 3 million. [Physicians for a National Health Program, [11/19/10](https://pnhp.org/news/physician-congressman-andy-harris-wants-his-health-care-but-forget-yours/)]

### Voted Repeatedly to Repeal or Undermine the ACA

**Harris Voted for the American Health Care Act, Which the CBO Estimated Would Leave 23 Million More Americans Uninsured.** According to a press release from Harris's office, Harris voted for H.R. 1628 on May 4, 2017, claiming the bill "explicitly maintains, and even adds, protections for pre-existing conditions." However, the bill allowed states to waive essential health benefits and permitted insurers to charge higher premiums based on health status. [Harris.House.Gov, [5/4/17](https://harris.house.gov/media/press-releases/dr-andy-harris-votes-favor-american-health-care-act)]

**Harris Voted Against the Inflation Reduction Act, Which Capped Insulin at $35 and Allowed Medicare to Negotiate Drug Prices.** Harris was the only member of Maryland's congressional delegation to vote against the Inflation Reduction Act on August 12, 2022. The law capped out-of-pocket insulin costs for Medicare patients at $35 per month and, for the first time, allowed Medicare to negotiate prescription drug prices — a provision that has since saved seniors billions. [GovTrack, [8/12/22](https://www.govtrack.us/congress/votes/117-2022/h420)]

### Pushed Medicaid Cuts That Would Devastate His Own District

**An Estimated 24,000 People in Harris's District Expected to Lose Medicaid Under Budget Bill He Backed.** According to The Baltimore Banner, Harris voted yes on the "One Big Beautiful Bill Act," which included over $625 billion in Medicaid cuts over ten years. The bill imposed work requirements, increased eligibility checks, and incentivized states not to expand Medicaid. An estimated 24,000 people in Harris's district face losing their Medicaid coverage as a result. [Baltimore Banner, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://www.thebanner.com/politics-power/national-politics/andy-harris-trump-nill-vote-REWWZFV7WVHFPGEHSPYVLVALPQ/)]

- **Harris Dismissed Concerns About Coverage Losses.** According to The Baltimore Banner, Harris said, "What the Democrats are trying to do is create fears about people dying, us pushing people off health care." However, data shows 72% of Marylanders on Medicaid are working adults, and many others are children, seniors, or people with disabilities. [Baltimore Banner, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://www.thebanner.com/politics-power/national-politics/andy-harris-trump-nill-vote-REWWZFV7WVHFPGEHSPYVLVALPQ/)]

**As Freedom Caucus Chair, Harris Pushed to Accelerate Medicaid Work Requirements.** According to the Capital Gazette, "anti-spending hawks, led by Freedom Caucus Chair Andy Harris" pushed to have Medicaid work requirements begin "not later than Dec. 31, 2026," so savings would take effect sooner. Harris initially opposed the bill for not cutting enough, then voted yes after receiving White House assurances of additional future cuts. [Capital Gazette, [5/22/25](https://www.capitalgazette.com/2025/05/22/andy-harris-votes-present-on-trump-bill/)]

**In Harris's Home County, Over 23% of Residents Relied on Medicaid and 41% of Children Were on Federal Health Insurance.** According to The Baltimore Banner, in Dorchester County — Harris's home base — more than 23% of residents rely on Medicaid and 41% of the county's children are enrolled in a federal-state health insurance program, according to 2023 Census estimates. [Baltimore Banner, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://www.thebanner.com/politics-power/national-politics/andy-harris-trump-nill-vote-REWWZFV7WVHFPGEHSPYVLVALPQ/)]

### Fought the Law That Slashed His District's Uninsured Rate by 54%

**The ACA Harris Voted to Repeal Cut the Uninsured Rate in His District More Than Any Other in Maryland.** According to The Baltimore Sun, the number of uninsured residents in Maryland's 1st Congressional District "fell from 63,252 in 2013 to 29,193 two years later, a 54 percent decline, with no other congressional district in the state experiencing such a big decline." Approximately 41,000 Eastern Shore residents signed up for coverage through the ACA exchange, with 83% receiving subsidies to offset premium costs. [Baltimore Sun, [8/27/17](https://www.baltimoresun.com/2017/08/27/marylands-eastern-shore-a-gop-stronghold-home-to-thousands-who-now-have-insurance-thanks-to-obamacare/)]

- **Eastern Shore Cancer Survivor Credited ACA With Keeping Him Alive.** According to The Baltimore Sun, Charles Birckhead, a 58-year-old Cambridge resident and cancer survivor, said: "Obamacare sustained us, I guess you could say." [Baltimore Sun, [8/27/17](https://www.baltimoresun.com/2017/08/27/marylands-eastern-shore-a-gop-stronghold-home-to-thousands-who-now-have-insurance-thanks-to-obamacare/)]

### Accepted Health Care Industry PAC Money While Voting Against Health Care Access

**Harris Accepted Over $920,000 from Health Care Industry PACs Over His Career.** According to FEC records, Harris received approximately $921,000 in health care industry PAC contributions from 2007 to 2025, including approximately $327,000 from medical associations, $206,000 from surgical specialty PACs, $177,000 from anesthesiology groups (his own specialty), $85,000 from pharmaceutical and biotech companies, $47,000 from dental PACs, and $43,000 from health care industry and managed care PACs. Top donors include the American Society of Anesthesiologists PAC (~$82,000), the American College of Radiology PAC (~$68,000), the American Orthopaedic Surgeons PAC (~$67,000), and the American Medical Association PAC (~$63,000). [FEC, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://www.fec.gov/data/receipts/?committee_id=C00435974)]

- **Harris Accepted a $1,000 Donation from Purdue Pharma's PAC in July 2019, During the Opioid Crisis.** According to FEC records, Harris accepted a $1,000 contribution from "Purdue Pharma Inc. PAC (Purdue PAC)" on July 22, 2019, even as Purdue Pharma faced thousands of lawsuits over its role fueling the opioid epidemic. Maryland's Eastern Shore was among the regions hardest hit by the crisis. Maryland later secured a $90 million settlement from Purdue Pharma and the Sackler family. [FEC, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://www.fec.gov/data/receipts/?committee_id=C00435974&contributor_name=purdue+pharma)]

- **Harris Accepted Approximately $34,000 from Emergent BioSolutions, Which Was Later Involved in COVID Vaccine Contamination.** According to FEC records, Harris accepted approximately $34,000 from Emergent BioSolutions PAC between 2011 and 2023. The Gaithersburg, Maryland-based biodefense company was later involved in a COVID-19 vaccine manufacturing contamination scandal at its Baltimore plant that forced the destruction of millions of doses. [FEC, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://www.fec.gov/data/receipts/?committee_id=C00435974&contributor_name=emergent)]

### Medicaid Cuts Threaten Rural Health Care on the Eastern Shore

**Budget Bill Harris Backed Could Force Maryland to Remove 300,000 People from Coverage or Absorb $1 Billion in Costs.** According to InsuranceNewsNet, the proposed legislation included over $720 billion in nationwide Medicaid cuts. Maryland receives approximately $8.5 billion annually in federal Medicaid funding, and state Senate President Bill Ferguson warned that a full 50-50 federal-state cost split could force Maryland to "either absorb $1 billion in additional costs or remove 300,000 people from coverage." [InsuranceNewsNet, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://insurancenewsnet.com/oarticle/rural-maryland-medicaid-recipients-risk-coverage-provider-shortage-under-trump-bill)]

**Rural Health Leaders Warned Medicaid Cuts Would Worsen Already-Dire Eastern Shore Health Disparities.** According to InsuranceNewsNet, Jonathan Dayton, executive director of the Maryland Rural Health Association, said there are already "lower life expectancies in rural areas and higher rates of chronic diseases" and that Medicaid cuts would "exacerbate these disparities." On the Eastern Shore, four physicians retired without replacements, leaving patients waiting a year for colonoscopy appointments. [InsuranceNewsNet, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://insurancenewsnet.com/oarticle/rural-maryland-medicaid-recipients-risk-coverage-provider-shortage-under-trump-bill)]

Harris faced "repeated interruptions while attempting to explain his healthcare positions in a hall filled largely with critics." But ending the law would be disastrous." Joana Weaver, a 49-year-old Salisbury resident with cerebral palsy, added: "Everybody recognizes that we need to fix this [...]  but to take it away from people who need it is not good either." [Baltimore Sun, [8/27/17](https://www.baltimoresun.com/2017/08/27/marylands-eastern-shore-a-gop-stronghold-home-to-thousands-who-now-have-insurance-thanks-to-obamacare/)]
