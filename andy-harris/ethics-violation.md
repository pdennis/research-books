# Rep. Andy Harris (MD-01): Ethics Violation -- Wife's Undisclosed Income

### Accused Of Ethics Violation For Failing To Disclose Wife's Income From His Own Campaign

**Harris Was Accused Of An Ethics Violation For Failing To Disclose His Wife's Consulting Income.** According to The Washington Post, the Maryland Democratic Party filed an ethics complaint with the Office of Congressional Ethics alleging that Harris failed to report more than $26,000 in consulting income his wife, Nicole Beus Harris, earned from his campaign and GOP political committees through her firm, Indy's Services. The complaint alleged Harris "paid his wife thousands of dollars from his campaign, then tried to hide it from government ethics regulators." Harris amended his filing after the omission was flagged.
