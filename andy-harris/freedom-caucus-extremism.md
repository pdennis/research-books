# Rep. Andy Harris (MD-01): Freedom Caucus Extremism and National Abortion Ban

### Freedom Caucus Chair Backed By Project 2025, Sponsored A National Abortion Ban

**Harris Was Elected Freedom Caucus Chair With Trump's Endorsement And Project 2025 Backing.** According to Maryland Matters, hard-line conservatives elected Harris chair of the House Freedom Caucus in September 2024. The DNC described Harris as "a Trump-endorsed, Project 2025-backed chair" tasked with "pushing a Project 2025 legislative agenda." [Maryland Matters, [9/17/24](https://marylandmatters.org/briefs/maryland-rep-harris-elected-chair-of-the-ultra-conservative-freedom-caucus/)]

**Harris Said He Would Support A National Abortion Ban If A Fetus Has A Heartbeat.** According to the DNC, Harris said he would support a national abortion ban after approximately six weeks of pregnancy. Harris also cosponsored the Life at Conception Act, a national abortion ban that threatens IVF protections. [Democrats.org, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://democrats.org/news/%F0%9F%9A%A8new-project-2025-backed-rep-andy-harris-named-new-house-freedom-caucus-chair/)]
