# Rep. Darrell Issa (CA-48): Arson Suspicion and Criminal History

### Arson Suspicion and Criminal History

**Issa Was Suspected Of Committing Arson After Increasing His Factory's Fire Insurance By 462% Weeks Before It Burned.** According to NPR, a fire-analysis report commissioned by the St. Paul insurance company concluded the 1982 fire at Issa's Ohio manufacturing plant was "incendiary," citing "suspicious burn patterns" including "two separate major areas of origin" with no accidental heating source found at either. Issa had increased the company's fire insurance from $100,000 to more than $462,000 less than three weeks before the blaze, and a co-worker said Issa had removed the business's computer and financial files from the building beforehand. Issa was never charged. [NPR, [1/20/11](https://www.npr.org/2011/01/20/133071575/Past-Haunts-Rep-Issa-Head-Of-Investigative-Committee)]

**Issa Was Indicted For Grand Theft Auto In The Theft Of A Maserati From A Cleveland Car Dealership.** According to NPR, in 1972, Issa and his brother William were arrested for stealing a Maserati from a Cleveland car dealership after a witness reported seeing them "pushing the sports car down the street just before midnight." A grand jury indicted Issa on a larceny charge, but a prosecutor later dropped the case. [NPR, [4/16/12](https://www.npr.org/2012/04/16/150739985/house-investigator-issa-has-faced-allegations-as-well)]

**Issa Was Convicted Of Carrying A Concealed Weapon.** According to Media Matters, Issa was arrested and convicted of "possession of an unregistered handgun" in connection with the 1972 incidents, fined $100, and placed on three months' probation. Police found a handgun and a tear-gas gun with ammunition for both in his glove compartment. [Media Matters, [1/20/11](https://www.mediamatters.org/new-york-times/report-media-ignore-rep-issas-alleged-criminal-past)]
