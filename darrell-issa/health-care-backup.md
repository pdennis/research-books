# Rep. Darrell Issa (CA-48): Can't Be Trusted on Health Care

Rep. Darrell Issa has spent over a decade working to dismantle health care protections for his own constituents. Issa voted 17 times to repeal the Affordable Care Act, cast a decisive vote for the American Health Care Act that would have stripped coverage from 37,000 people in his district, and told a constituent at a town hall that if people "choose the freedom to not be insured... hopefully you don't get a condition." In 2025, Issa voted for the One Big Beautiful Bill Act's sweeping Medicaid cuts that are projected to leave more than 24,000 of his constituents uninsured, and he voted against a bipartisan proposal to reinstate ACA tax credits that 8,000 of his own constituents relied on. He has justified gutting the ACA by falsely claiming Medicaid enrolls "criminal illegals to the tune of billions of dollars every year" — a claim KPBS found to be false. When constituents tried to confront him, Issa refused to hold a town hall for eight years, prompting hundreds to attend "Empty Chair" events in his district.

### A Decade of Voting to Strip Health Care Coverage

**Issa Voted 17 Times To Repeal The Affordable Care Act.** According to American Journal News, "Issa voted 17 times to abolish the ACA" between 2011 and 2017, and "his mission to defund and dismantle the popular health care program continues." [American Journal News, [1/28/26](https://americanjournalnews.com/darrell-issas-long-record-of-voting-to-repeal-obamacare/)]

**Issa Cast A Decisive Vote For The American Health Care Act, Which Would Have Stripped Coverage From 37,000 Constituents.** According to The San Diego Union-Tribune, Issa voted yes on the American Health Care Act on May 4, 2017, helping it pass 217-213. The bill would have allowed states to opt out of protections that prevent insurers from charging people with pre-existing conditions more. [San Diego Union-Tribune, [5/4/17](https://www.sandiegouniontribune.com/opinion/the-conversation/sd-darrell-issa-votes-yes-on-obamacare-repeal-acha-health-care-20170504-story.html)]

**Issa Told A Constituent Whose Son Has Type 1 Diabetes To "Hopefully" Not Get A Condition.** According to the Center for American Progress Action, after voting for the AHCA, Issa told constituents at a town hall: "because we are getting rid of the personal mandate [...]  if you choose the freedom to not be insured [...] hopefully you don't get a condition." Carlsbad resident Nikki Faddick, whose son has Type 1 diabetes and was previously denied coverage for his pre-existing condition, called the response dismissive of families who need insurance as a medical necessity. [Center for American Progress Action, [5/4/18](https://www.americanprogressaction.org/article/my-son-has-type-1-diabetes-and-darrell-issa-voted-to-repeal-his-healthcare/)]

**Issa Explicitly Called For A "Real Phase Out Of Obamacare" On Fox Business.** According to American Journal News, in a December 9, 2025 Fox Business interview, Issa stated: "There will be less subsidy, there will be better scrutiny over fraud, and there's intended to be a real phase out of Obamacare." [American Journal News, [1/28/26](https://americanjournalnews.com/darrell-issas-long-record-of-voting-to-repeal-obamacare/)]

### Voting to Gut Medicaid for His Own Constituents

**Issa Voted For The One Big Beautiful Bill Act, Which Is Projected To Leave More Than 24,000 Of His Constituents Uninsured.** According to American Journal News, "an analysis by Democrats on the Joint Economic Committee found that more than 24,000 people in his district are likely to become uninsured as a result of the law. That number swells to more than 32,000 when the impact of expiring Obamacare tax credits is factored in." [American Journal News, [1/28/26](https://americanjournalnews.com/darrell-issas-long-record-of-voting-to-repeal-obamacare/)]

**Up To 62,000 Adults In Issa's District Could Lose Medi-Cal Due To Work-Filing Requirements.** According to The San Diego Union-Tribune, Medicaid work requirements in the bill force adults to prove monthly they are working or seeking work, and based on the experience of Arkansas — where 44% of recipients lost coverage due to complex documentation rules — up to 62,000 adults in District 48 may lose Medi-Cal coverage. [San Diego Union-Tribune, [5/30/25](https://www.sandiegouniontribune.com/2025/05/30/issa-ready-to-put-many-vulnerable-constituents-at-risk/)]

**Two Hospitals In Issa's District Face A Combined $14 Million Revenue Loss From His Health Care Votes.** According to American Journal News, hospitals in Murrieta and Temecula in Issa's district "are expected to see a combined $14 million dip in revenue" as a result of Medicaid cuts and expiring ACA tax credits Issa voted against extending. [American Journal News, [1/28/26](https://americanjournalnews.com/darrell-issas-long-record-of-voting-to-repeal-obamacare/)]

**Issa's Medicaid Cuts Could Cost San Diego County Up To 15,200 Jobs.** According to KPBS, the Medicaid provisions Issa voted for could "cost San Diego County 7,600-15,200 jobs" and eliminate coverage for more than 28,000 people in his district alone. [KPBS, [7/2/25](https://www.kpbs.org/news/politics/2025/07/02/congressman-issa-falsely-claims-criminal-illegals-are-enrolled-in-medicaid)]

### Blocking ACA Tax Credits His Constituents Depend On

**Issa Voted Against Reinstating ACA Tax Credits That 8,000 Of His Own Constituents Relied On.** According to American Journal News, Issa was one of 196 House Republicans to vote against a bipartisan proposal to reinstate Obamacare tax credits that "reduced the monthly cost of insurance plans for about 22 million Americans, including 8,000 of Issa's own constituents." Some constituents saw their monthly premiums quadruple after the credits expired on January 1. [American Journal News, [1/28/26](https://americanjournalnews.com/darrell-issas-long-record-of-voting-to-repeal-obamacare/)]

### Justifying Cuts With False Claims

**Issa Falsely Claimed Medicaid Enrolls "Criminal Illegals To The Tune Of Billions Of Dollars Every Year."** According to KPBS, Issa claimed Medicaid is "enrolling criminal illegals to the tune of billions of dollars every year," but KPBS found this claim to be false — immigrants without legal status are not eligible for federal Medicaid dollars, and Issa's office "provided no evidence supporting the claim." Rep. Mike Levin called Issa's claims "complete disinformation being pushed by Republicans who are eager to scapegoat immigrants." [KPBS, [7/2/25](https://www.kpbs.org/news/politics/2025/07/02/congressman-issa-falsely-claims-criminal-illegals-are-enrolled-in-medicaid)]

### Refusing to Face Constituents

**Issa Refused To Hold A Town Hall For Eight Years, Prompting Hundreds To Attend "Empty Chair" Events.** According to East County Magazine, hundreds of constituents gathered at an "Empty Chair Town Hall" at the California Center for the Arts in Escondido on March 23, 2025, organized by Indivisible North County San Diego "to bring attention to what they say is Issa's penchant to avoid meeting constituents in a Town Hall format." Issa had not held a traditional town hall since 2017. Attendees demanded answers on "why the government feels it has to slash billions of dollars from the Department of Health and Human Services, which manages Medicaid." [East County Magazine, [3/23/25](https://www.eastcountymagazine.org/%E2%80%9Cempty-chair%E2%80%9D-town-hall-rep-issa%E2%80%99s-constituents-air-concerns)]

### Impact on Everyday People

**A Carlsbad Mother Said Issa's Vote Threatened Her Son's Ability To Get Health Insurance.** According to the Center for American Progress Action, Nikki Faddick of Carlsbad, California said that after her son was diagnosed with Type 1 diabetes in 2006, the family was denied insurance coverage because of his pre-existing condition. The ACA changed that, but Issa's vote for the AHCA "would have permitted insurers to deny coverage for pre-existing conditions, potentially forcing her son to immediately seek full-time employment upon college graduation solely for health benefits." [Center for American Progress Action, [5/4/18](https://www.americanprogressaction.org/article/my-son-has-type-1-diabetes-and-darrell-issa-voted-to-repeal-his-healthcare/)]

**A San Diego County Doctor Warned Issa's Medicaid Cuts Threaten Local Hospitals And Clinics.** According to The San Diego Union-Tribune, a physician from rural east San Diego County warned that Issa's vote for Medicaid cuts "threaten San Diego County hospitals and clinics" that rely on Medicaid funding, "potentially reducing services or causing closures." The doctor noted that 40% of uninsured persons face a higher premature death risk. [San Diego Union-Tribune, [5/30/25](https://www.sandiegouniontribune.com/2025/05/30/issa-ready-to-put-many-vulnerable-constituents-at-risk/)]

**Constituents' Premiums Quadrupled After Issa Voted Against Extending ACA Tax Credits.** According to American Journal News, after Issa voted against reinstating ACA tax credits, some of his constituents "experienced monthly premiums quadruple" when the credits expired on January 1, 2026. [American Journal News, [1/28/26](https://americanjournalnews.com/darrell-issas-long-record-of-voting-to-repeal-obamacare/)]
