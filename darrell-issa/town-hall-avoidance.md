# Rep. Darrell Issa (CA-48): Refuses to Hold Town Halls

### Refusing To Face His Constituents

**Issa Has Not Held A Town Hall Since 2017, Prompting Hundreds To Attend "Empty Chair" Events.** According to East County Magazine, hundreds of constituents gathered at an "Empty Chair Town Hall" at the California Center for the Arts in Escondido on March 23, 2025, organized by Indivisible North County San Diego "to bring attention to what they say is Issa's penchant to avoid meeting constituents in a Town Hall format." Attendees demanded answers on "why the government feels it has to slash billions of dollars from the Department of Health and Human Services, which manages Medicaid." [East County Magazine, [3/23/25](https://www.eastcountymagazine.org/%E2%80%9Cempty-chair%E2%80%9D-town-hall-rep-issa%E2%80%99s-constituents-air-concerns)]

**A Democratic Congresswoman Hosted A Town Hall In Issa's District Because He Would Not.** According to East County Magazine, since Issa "repeatedly refused to hold a townhall," Congresswoman Sara Jacobs held "The Town Hall We Deserve" on July 27, 2025 at the California Center for the Arts in Escondido to speak with voters in Issa's district about critical issues including Medicare, Medicaid, Social Security, and veterans' services. [East County Magazine, [7/25](https://www.eastcountymagazine.org/congresswoman-sara-jacobs-hosts-town-hall-july-27-escondido-issa-wont-hold-live-session-his)]
