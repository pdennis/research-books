# Rep. Darrell Issa (CA-48): Self-Dealing Earmarks

### Self-Dealing: Earmarks Benefiting His Own Properties

**Issa Secured Nearly $815,000 In Earmarks To Widen Roads Adjacent To Commercial Properties He Owned.** According to KPBS, Issa requested $2 million and received $815,000 in federal earmarks to widen West Vista Way, add parking lots, bus stops, and sewer improvements near a medical plaza he purchased for $16.6 million in 2008. Issa also owned a commercial office building worth over $9 million and an adjacent retail office building about two miles down the same road. A firm representing Issa's real estate company advertised the medical building highlighting its "Excellent Access with Freeway Visibility." [KPBS, [8/16/11](https://www.kpbs.org/news/2011/aug/16/conflict-interest-questions-raised-about-sd-congre/)]

**A Liberal Advocacy Group Filed A Formal Ethics Complaint Over Issa's Self-Benefiting Earmarks.** According to CBS News, American Family Voices filed an ethics complaint with the House Office of Congressional Ethics alleging that Issa "secured millions of dollars in congressional earmarks to improve roads serving properties he owns." The New York Times had reported that "at least some of the congressman's government actions" raised "the potential for conflicts." [CBS News, [9/1/11](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/liberal-group-files-ethics-complaint-against-darrell-issa/)]
