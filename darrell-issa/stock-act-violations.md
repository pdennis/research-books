# Rep. Darrell Issa (CA-48): STOCK Act Violations and Financial Disclosure Failures

### STOCK Act Violations and Financial Disclosure Failures

**Issa Disclosed Up To $175 Million In Treasury Bond Sales Approximately 500 Days Late And Faced Only A $200 Fine.** According to Capitol Trades, Issa reported seven bond sales each valued between $5 million and $25 million — making it impossible to determine whether he sold $35 million or $175 million — approximately 500 days after they were required to be disclosed under the STOCK Act, which mandates reporting within 45 days. First-time STOCK Act offenders face only a $200 fine. [Capitol Trades, [10/2/24](https://www.capitoltrades.com/articles/congressional-stock-trades-raise-stock-act-violation-concerns-2024-10-02)]

**Issa Divested Nearly $355 Million In Treasury Bills In 2024, Breaching The STOCK Act Twice In One Week.** According to Finbold, Issa divested nearly $355 million in U.S. Treasury Bills before their maturity dates over several months in 2024, with the sales disclosed far past the 45-day deadline. The large reporting ranges — each sale listed as between $5 million and $25 million — made it "exceptionally hard to tell" the actual amounts, undermining the transparency the law was designed to provide. [Finbold, [10/1/24](https://finbold.com/congressman-breaks-insider-trading-law-twice-in-a-week-with-355-million-in-sales/)]
