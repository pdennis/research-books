# Rep. Darrell Issa (CA-48): Speaker Johnson Rental and Ethics Complaints

### Renting Property to Speaker Johnson's Campaign

**Issa Rented His Washington Townhouse To Speaker Johnson's Campaign, Drawing FEC And Ethics Complaints.** According to The San Diego Union-Tribune, House Speaker Mike Johnson's campaign paid $2,500 monthly since March 2025 to Greene Properties Inc., where Issa serves as CEO, for a Washington, D.C. property. The Campaign Legal Center filed complaints with the FEC and the Office of Congressional Conduct, arguing that "even if the campaign argues that the rent is for office space, lawmakers cannot live in campaign offices where the campaign subsidizes personal rent." Total disclosed payments were $12,500. [San Diego Union-Tribune, [8/16/25](https://www.sandiegouniontribune.com/2025/08/16/ethics-complaints-accuse-house-speaker-of-using-campaign-funds-to-pay-issa-rent/)]
