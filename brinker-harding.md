# How To Win Against Brinker Harding

Brinker Harding criticized politicians who optimized their own careers but admitted to prepping a congressional run in the event Don Bacon retired either in 2026 or two years after. He supported the Big Ugly Bill that took health care and food assistance from Nebraskans. He supported Trump’s economic policies that included punitive tariffs that were hurting Nebraska businesses and farmers. He supported the war in Iran despite the cost increases it was imposing on Nebraskans in the form of gas, diesel, and fertilizer prices. He can’t be trusted to represent all Nebraskans fairly as demonstrated by his refusal to delete an Instagram post that proliferated President Trump’s racist sombrero meme from last fall. He also drew criticism last September when, instead of addressing the root cause of homelessness and cost-of-living issues, he proposed an ordinance criminalize homelessness. Nebraskans in the second congressional district deserve better than the cost-raising Republican policies Harding intends to continue if elected to congress. 

### Harding Supported a bill That Would take Health coverage from nebraskans 

-   Harding [supported](https://www.instagram.com/p/DLs3KVruxuo/) the “One Big Beautiful Bill,” which cut health care for [17 million Americans](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2025/07/01/least-17-million-americans-would-lose-insurance-under-trump-plan/), including nearly [12 million Medicaid](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2025/07/01/least-17-million-americans-would-lose-insurance-under-trump-plan/) beneficiaries. More than [19,000 Nebraskans](https://www.rand.org/pubs/research_reports/RRA4098-1.html) were estimated to be at risk to lose health coverage from the “One Big Beautiful Bill” that Harding [defended](https://www.instagram.com/p/DN_0HvCjvmE/).
-   Harding advocated for [balancing the budget](https://www.facebook.com/share/p/1J47A2b2oh/) “[like we do in Omaha](https://nebraskaexaminer.com/2025/07/01/omaha-city-council-vp-brinker-harding-jumps-into-nebraska-2nd-district-u-s-house-race/)” if elected to Congress, which could [require](https://www.crfb.org/blogs/what-would-it-take-balance-budget-update)[cuts](https://www.cbpp.org/research/federal-budget/a-balanced-budget-amendment-doesnt-change-the-math) to Medicaid in order to achieve at the federal level. More than [114,000](https://www.americanprogress.org/article/medicaid-and-chip-coverage-mapped-by-119th-congressional-districts/) Nebraskans in the 2^nd^ District, relied on Medicaid for healthcare.

**Message:** Harding supported a bill that would take health care from Nebraskans, all to fund tax breaks for the super rich. 

### Harding Supported a bill That Would trigger $500 billion in Medicare cuts 

-   Harding [supported](https://www.instagram.com/p/DLs3KVruxuo/) the “One Big Beautiful Bill”, which would trigger nearly [$500 billion](https://www.cbo.gov/system/files/2025-05/61423-PAYGO.pdf) in Medicare cuts absent future congressional action. Nearly [390,000 Nebraskans](https://www.kff.org/medicare/state-indicator/total-medicare-beneficiaries/?currentTimeframe=0&selectedRows=%7B%22states%22:%7B%22nebraska%22:%7B%7D%7D%7D&sortModel=%7B%22colId%22:%22Location%22,%22sort%22:%22asc%22%7D) relied on Medicare for healthcare.
-   Harding has also advocated for [balancing the budget](https://www.facebook.com/share/p/1J47A2b2oh/) “[like we do in Omaha](https://nebraskaexaminer.com/2025/07/01/omaha-city-council-vp-brinker-harding-jumps-into-nebraska-2nd-district-u-s-house-race/)” if elected to Congress, which could [require](https://www.cbpp.org/research/federal-budget/a-balanced-budget-amendment-doesnt-change-the-math) cuts to Medicare to achieve at the federal level.

**Message:** Harding would be another vote to slash Medicare for Nebraska seniors. 

### Harding Supported The “One Big Beautiful Bill,” Which Made Significant Cuts To Food Assistance, After Saying He Thought Congress’s Job was To Protect The Programs

-   Harding [said](https://www.jewishomaha.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/JCRC-2026-Voter-Guide-FINAL.pdf) Congress’s job was to “protect those programs” when asked about SNAP, but [celebrated](https://www.instagram.com/p/DLs3KVruxuo/) Trump’s “One Big Beautiful Bill” that cut food assistance for [millions](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/07/10/trumps-big-beautiful-bill-cuts-snap-for-millions-of-families.html) of Americans. Nearly [23,000 households](https://www.fns.usda.gov/data-research/data-visualization/snap-community-characteristics-congressional-district-dashboard) in Nebraska’s 2^nd^ congressional district relied on SNAP and Nebraska had already seen a [10% decrease](https://www.cbpp.org/research/food-assistance/snap-tracker-people-are-losing-food-assistance-as-the-republican-megabill) in SNAP participation since the bill was enacted.

**Message:** Harding knows SNAP is important for Nebraskans but still supported a bill that cut food assistance all to give the super rich tax breaks. 

### Harding Was Eager to Support Trump’s AGENDA, which INCLUDED AN AGENCY THAT HAMSTRUNG SOCIAL SECURITY 

-   Harding was eager to [work with Trump](https://nebraskaexaminer.com/briefs/trump-gives-total-endorsement-to-brinker-harding-in-ne-02/), whose “Department of Government Efficiency” (DOGE) weakened the Social Security Administration. DOGE cuts caused longer [wait times](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2025/04/07/social-security-website-crashes-musk-trump/), frequent [website crashes](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2025/04/07/social-security-website-crashes-musk-trump/), and [mass layoffs](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2025/03/25/social-security-phones-doge-cuts/) in Social Security offices across the country. More than [107,000 Nebraskans](https://www.ssa.gov/policy/docs/factsheets/cong_stats/2024/ne.pdf) in the 2^nd^ congressional district relied on Social Security benefits.
-   Harding has also advocated for [balancing the budget](https://www.facebook.com/share/p/1J47A2b2oh/) “[like we do in Omaha](https://nebraskaexaminer.com/2025/07/01/omaha-city-council-vp-brinker-harding-jumps-into-nebraska-2nd-district-u-s-house-race/)” if elected to Congress, which could require significant [cuts](https://www.cbpp.org/research/federal-budget/a-balanced-budget-amendment-doesnt-change-the-math) to Social Security to achieve at the federal level.

**Message:** Harding was eager to support an agenda that hamstrung services seniors and Nebraskans rely on.

Trump’s [economic policy](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/01/31/trump-america-first-policy-tariffs-hostility-backfire-trade-deals.html) has been to impose punitive tariffs that [devastated](https://nebraskapublicmedia.org/en/news/news-articles/like-trying-to-get-taylor-swift-tickets-nebraska-businesses-struggle-with-tariff-refund-system/) Nebraska [businesses](https://www.kcur.org/2026-02-04/from-chocolate-to-farm-equipment-trumps-tariffs-are-pain-points-for-nebraska-manufacturers) and [farmers](https://www.omahamagazine.com/business/inflation-and-tariff-pressures-reach-from-farm-to-city/).

**Message:** Harding supported an agenda that raised costs on Nebraska businesses and farmers.  

### Harding supported The Iran war That Raised Gas, Diesel, and Fertilizer costs on Nebraskans 

-   Harding [supported](https://x.com/BrinkerHarding/status/2027887457560064484) Trump’s war in Iran and “peace through strength” approach to foreign policy.
-   Trump’s strike caused an [overnight](https://nebraskapublicmedia.org/en/news/news-articles/gas-prices-spike-in-nebraska-after-start-of-iran-conflict/) 16 cent spike in gas prices in Nebraska, with prices continuing to rise [29.1%](https://www.americanprogress.org/article/state-by-state-increases-in-gas-prices-since-trumps-war-on-iran/) through late April 2026. Diesel and fertilizer prices rose [nearly 50%](https://www.americanprogress.org/article/state-by-state-increases-in-gas-prices-since-trumps-war-on-iran/) in that time. Higher prices impacted gas for [schools and sheriffs](https://www.1011now.com/2026/04/29/higher-fuel-costs-could-soon-squeeze-budgets-nebraska-agencies/), hurt Nebraska [farmers](https://nebraskapublicmedia.org/es/news/news-articles/already-struggling-nebraska-farmers-face-war-fueled-cost-spikes/), and contributed to turmoil experienced by rural economies that were drifting into [recessions](https://nebraskapublicmedia.org/en/news/news-articles/more-than-half-of-rural-bank-ceos-believe-their-local-economies-are-in-a-recession/).
-   Trump’s war in Iran was projected to cost Americans an extra [estimated $235](https://responsiblestatecraft.org/gas-prices-iran-war/) to fill up their car with gas, and rising fuel costs caused corporations to begin [passing fuel surcharges](https://www.washingtonpost.com/business/2026/04/04/iran-war-global-economy/) onto American consumers. Americans were also [primarily concerned](https://www.pewresearch.org/global/2026/04/07/gas-prices-are-americans-top-concern-in-iran-war/) with how the war would impact their wallet, with a Ipsos poll [finding](https://www.washingtonpost.com/business/2026/04/04/iran-war-global-economy/) that 56 percent of Americas expected Trump’s war in Iran to have a “mostly negative impact” on their personal financial situation.

**Message:** Harding was willing to back Trump’s foreign policy that was raising prices on Nebraskans.

This caused [controversy](https://www.wowt.com/2025/09/23/omaha-city-council-holds-hearing-outlawing-homeless-camps-city-property/) at a public hearing. Harding [defended](https://www.wowt.com/2025/09/23/omaha-city-council-holds-hearing-outlawing-homeless-camps-city-property/) the proposal by suggesting it was about safety and public health.

**Message:** Instead of addressing the rising cost of living in Omaha or the root of homelessness, Harding wanted to criminalize homelessness.
