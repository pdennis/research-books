DOGE Slashed Grants To New Jersey’s AmeriCorps Program, Which Affected 500 Members Of 27 Organizations In The State, Including A Nonprofit That Runs A Food Pantry And Homeless Shelter. According the New Jersey Herald, "The task force overseen by Elon Musk under President Donald Trump's administration canceled about $400 million in grants handed out nationwide, a Washington Post report said. The money comes from AmeriCorps, a government organization that offers various programs to help address community services such as education, environmental conservation and poverty. The cuts affect roughly 500 members of 27 organizations based in New Jersey, including groups at Montclair State University and all three Rutgers campuses, in New Brunswick, Newark and Camden. Other local groups on the list include Zufall Health Center in Dover; the Social-Emotional Learning Alliance for New Jersey based in Morris Township; and St. Paul’s Community Development Corporation in Paterson. AmeriCorps participants from St. Paul's work at a food pantry, homeless shelter and elementary school program. The nonprofit corporation has 12 AmeriCorps participants for each year, with an additional 10 during the summer, said Executive Director Richard Williams." [New Jersey Herald, 4/29/25]
