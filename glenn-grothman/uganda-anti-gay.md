# Rep. Glenn Grothman (WI-06): Defended Uganda's Anti-Homosexuality Law

### Defended Uganda's Anti-Homosexuality Law

**Grothman Criticized The U.S. For Opposing Uganda's Anti-Homosexuality Law.** According to the Cap Times, after Uganda enacted a law carrying harsh sentences for homosexual acts in 2014, Grothman blasted Secretary of State John Kerry's response, saying: "What must God think of our country if now — again, rather than sending people to Uganda to explain better agricultural techniques, sending missionaries to Africa educating people on Christianity — we send scientists to Africa to say how wonderful the homosexual lifestyle is?" [Cap Times, [4/7/14](https://captimes.com/news/local/writers/steven_elbow/glenn-grothman-blasts-u-s-moves-against-draconian-ugandan-homosexuality-law/article_b77f921e-b93f-565f-9b65-9c6cc25bac56.html)]
