Overview

In 2025, Bobby Charles pushed an extreme anti-choice agenda, declaring himself “unapologetically pro-life.” Charles called for limiting abortion access and ending public funding for abortion. He also opposed pregnancy-related health care coverage for people under 21 and undocumented immigrants, framing access to care as something he would roll back if elected governor.

Bobby Charles Pushed An Extreme Anti-Abortion Agenda, Proposing Limits To Abortion Access And Ending Publicly-Funded Care

Charles Declared Himself “Unapologetically Pro-Life” And Called For Rolling Back Abortion Access And Ending Public Funding For Abortion

October 2025: Charles Said He Was “Unapologetically Pro-Life,” Supported “Rolling Back” Abortion Access, And Opposed Public Funding For Abortion Services. According to Bobby Charles at the 2025 Maine GOP Gubernatorial Candidate Forum, “CHARLES: I honor the faith expressed by everybody up here. As you may or may not know, Jesus Christ is my savior. He has saved my life many times, in many places. Faith is central to my life and the life of my family. I can think of three ways right off, first of all, beyond modeling that face every day as a governor. The first is we will never have mandates again that shut down people of faith. I will sign the bill and I think Reagan Paul introduced it, that shuts off emergency powers for a governor indefinitely. You can go 30, 60, 90 days, and then the legislature has to re-up it. So I will do that. Number two, we will respect the Christian schools and faith in our schools. And number three, I am unapologetically pro-life. Unapologetically. We will roll back the extreme late term abortions because 67% of Mainers agree with that. And 81% of the country agrees with that and there will be no taxpayer funded abortions.” [Bobby Charles, Maine GOP Gubernatorial Candidate Forum, 10/18/25] (VIDEO)

 

Charles Opposed Pregnancy Related Health Care Coverage For Undocumented Immigrants And People Under 21

December 2025: Bobby Charles Opposed Pregnancy Related Health Care Coverage For People Under 21 And Undocumented Immigrants And Said He Would “Stop” This As Governor. According to Charles’ Facebook, “Breaking: Janet Mills is offering healthcare to ‘pregnant people’ and illegals. This is an ad she’s running in Bangor. When I am Governor we will work with President Trump and ICE to stop this madness! Great work Maine Wire on the reporting.” 

[Facebook, Bobby Charles, 12/20/25]
