Overview

Rather than focusing on issues facing Maine, Bobby Charles spent part of his campaign mocking LGBTQ+ people. Charles compared transgender identity to children thinking they were "cats and dogs" and repeated the long-debunked litter box hoax. Experts have warned the litter box hoax has been used to ostracize LGBTQ+ students. Charles has also mocked LGBTQ+ Pride celebrations as a celebration of “alphabet soup” and complained there was no "heterosexual day parade.”
