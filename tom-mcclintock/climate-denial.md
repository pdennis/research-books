# Rep. Tom McClintock (CA-05): Climate Denial While His District Burns

### Questioned Whether Humans Cause Climate Change While His District Burned

**McClintock Received A 3% Lifetime Score From The League Of Conservation Voters.** According to the League of Conservation Voters, McClintock received a 3% score on its 2024 National Environmental Scorecard and holds a 3% lifetime score. He voted against environmental protections on nearly every tracked vote, including measures on clean water safeguards, wildlife protections, Arctic protections, and climate action. [League of Conservation Voters, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://www.lcv.org/moc/tom-mcclintock/)]

**McClintock Questioned Whether Human Activity Causes Climate Change, Citing Warming On Mars And Jupiter.** According to his own campaign website, McClintock stated that "increases in atmospheric carbon dioxide always follow increases in global temperatures by several hundred years" and pointed to "solar radiation" increases as an alternative explanation. In a 2009 speech at the Heartland Institute, he asked: "if global warming is caused by your SUV, why is it that we're seeing global warming on every other body in the solar system?" [Tom McClintock Campaign, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://www.tommcclintock.com/issues/global-warming-and-climate-change/)]

**McClintock Said The United States 'Should Not Hamstring Its Economy' To Combat Climate Change.** According to The Valley Citizen, McClintock stated: "Simply adding more regulations based on suspect science will not change natural climate patterns" and "I firmly believe that the United States should not hamstring its economy in an attempt to combat climate change." The author noted that his district experienced record-breaking wildfires consuming nearly 250,000 acres, with six of California's twenty largest fires occurring in a single year. [Valley Citizen, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://thevalleycitizen.com/congressman-tom-mcclintocks-deadly-denial-of-global-warming/)]

**McClintock Voted Against H.R. 3684, The Bipartisan Infrastructure Law, Which Included $3.4 Billion For Wildfire Risk Reduction.** According to NPR, the Infrastructure Investment and Jobs Act passed the House 228-206 on November 5, 2021, with bipartisan support — but McClintock voted no. The law included $3.4 billion for wildfire risk reduction programs, directly relevant to his fire-prone district. [NPR, [11/5/21](https://www.npr.org/2021/11/05/1048745258/the-house-passes-bipartisan-infrastructure-bill-sending-it-to-bidens-desk)]
