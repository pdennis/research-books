# Rep. Tom McClintock (CA-05): Carpetbagger -- Doesn't Live in His District

### Collected $300,000 In Per Diem While Living Near Sacramento -- Not In His District

**McClintock Collected Over $300,000 In Tax-Free Per Diem Payments As A State Senator While Living Year-Round Near Sacramento, Not In The Southern California District He Represented.** According to the Press Telegram, McClintock represented Ventura County in the state legislature "about 350 miles to the southwest" from where he actually lived in Elk Grove, near Sacramento. He "collect[ed] tax-free legislative per diem pay of more than $300,000 for maintaining a 'second home' far from the district he visited only occasionally." [Press Telegram, [6/8/08](https://www.presstelegram.com/2008/06/08/californias-carpetbaggers/)]

- **Campaign Ads Accused McClintock Of Not Owning Or Renting A Home In His State Senate District, Claiming His Mother's Thousand Oaks Home As His Residence.** According to The Union, opponent Doug Ose's campaign commercials argued that McClintock "did not own or rent a home in the 19th district, but claimed his mother's home in Thousand Oaks as his state senate district residence." [Union, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://www.theunion.com/news/carpetbagging-at-its-best/article_4ae77d31-8f39-579d-9216-a142972154c6.html)]

**After Redistricting, McClintock Became One Of Only A Few Members Of Congress Who Do Not Live In The District They Represent.** According to the East Bay Times, after the 2020 redistricting, "the redrawn district cut out the 4th's share of Sacramento County, including the part of Elk Grove that includes McClintock's home." While the Constitution only requires members to live in the state they represent, McClintock's residency outside his own district has been a recurring vulnerability. [East Bay Times, [2/12/10](https://www.eastbaytimes.com/2010/02/12/carpetbagger-losing-steam-as-campaign-strategy/)]
