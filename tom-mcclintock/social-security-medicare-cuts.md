# Rep. Tom McClintock (CA-05): Advocated for Social Security and Medicare Cuts

### Advocated For Social Security And Medicare Cuts

**A Local Newspaper Reported That McClintock 'Incessantly Advocated' For Cuts To Social Security And Medicare.** According to the Mountain Democrat, McClintock "has incessantly advocated for 'entitlement reform' which, in GOP parlance, means cuts in Social Security, Medicare, Medicaid and social programs aimed at the young, the old and the infirm." The author characterized his approach as seeking to "sacrifice our paid-for retirement and health insurance on the altar of more tax cuts for billionaires." [Mountain Democrat, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://www.mtdemocrat.com/letters/mcclintocks-plans-for-social-security-and-medicare/)]

**McClintock Voted Against H.R. 3, Which Would Have Lowered Drug Prices To 120% Of The Average Price In Comparable Countries.** According to a statement on McClintock's official website, he characterized the bill as "classic price controls by a different name" and argued that "price controls [...] consistently and unerringly produce a shortage of whatever commodity they're trying to control — in this case, life-saving drugs." [Rep. Tom McClintock, [12/12/19](https://mcclintock.house.gov/newsroom/vote-notes/hr-3-pharmaceutical-price-controls-no)]
