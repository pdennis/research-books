Overview

In 2020, Lindell promoted oleandrin as a treatment for coronavirus, which was derived from a what medical ethnobotanist Cassandra Quave called a “deadly plant poison,” and which Lindell stood to profit from.

Lindell met with Trump and his coronavirus task force to pitch oleandrin, which Lindell claimed Trump was “enthusiastic” about. Lindell also promoted the product on television and claimed he used it himself. 

Oleandrin was derived from the plant oleander, which was toxic and potentially fatal and known to cause heart problems. Oleandrin was not properly tested for treating coronavirus, and the only study showing potential effectiveness was not peer reviewed and authored by Robert Newman, the president of Phoenix Biotechnology, the company that manufactured the product. Additionally, Lindell served on the board of Phoenix Biotechnology, and therefore held a financial stake in the success of oleandrin.

HEADLINE: “Oleandrin Is A Deadly Plant Poison, Not A COVID-19 Cure” [Cassandra Quave Opinion – Conversation, 8/18/20]

Emory University Assistant Professor Of Dermatology And Human Health Cassandra Quave Opinion: Oleandrin Was The Chemical Causing The Oleander Plant To Be Poisonous And Cause Potentially Lethal Cardiac Arrhythmias And Other Effects On Heart Tissue. According to a Cassandra Quave opinion from The Conversation, “Nerium oleander is a highly toxic plant from the Apocynaceae family. Though renowned for its beauty and use in landscaping, this Mediterranean shrub is responsible for cases of accidental poisoning across the globe. All parts of the plant are poisonous. If eaten, it causes cardiac arrhythmias, or irregular heart rates, and can be lethal to both humans and animals. Oleandrin is the chemical that causes the plant’s lethal toxicity. It is known by scientists as a cardiac glycoside, a class of organic compounds with a common feature: They exhibit powerful effects on heart tissue, often with deadly consequences.” [Cassandra Quave Opinion – Conversation, 8/18/20]
