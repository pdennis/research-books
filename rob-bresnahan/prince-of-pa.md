If we want to restore trust in government and our political leaders, then Congress needs to lead with these policies.’ If elected, Mr. Bresnahan told voters, he would co-sponsor legislation to ban stock trading by members of Congress, a practice he said ‘needs to come to an end immediately.’" [[New York Times, 4/5/25](https://www.nytimes.com/2025/04/05/us/politics/congress-stock-trading-ban.html)]
