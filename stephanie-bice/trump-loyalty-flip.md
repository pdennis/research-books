# Rep. Stephanie Bice (OK-05): Trump Loyalty Flip-Flop

### Criticized Trump In 2016, Pledged "Complete Loyalty" When It Suited Her In 2020

**Bice Called Trump's Comments "Inappropriate And Offensive" And Endorsed An Opponent In 2016.** According to the DCCC, Bice criticized Donald Trump in 2016, calling his comments "inappropriate and offensive," and endorsed one of his primary opponents. [DCCC, [8/25/20](https://dccc.org/the-case-against-stephanie-bice/)]

**Bice Then Pledged Her "Complete Loyalty" To Trump When She Ran For Congress In 2020.** According to OpenSecrets, Bice completely reversed her position when she ran for Congress, pledging loyalty to Trump, attending his Tulsa rally, praising his handling of COVID-19, and claiming she would work with him on the border wall. [OpenSecrets, [8/25/20](https://www.opensecrets.org/news/2020/08/oklahoma-republican-primary-runoff)]
