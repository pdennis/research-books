# Rep. Stephanie Bice (OK-05): Anti-Teacher Votes in Oklahoma State Senate

### Voted To Let Schools Cut Teacher Pay Below State Minimums And Slash Teacher Benefits

**Bice Voted For A Bill Allowing School Districts To Opt Out Of Minimum Teacher Salary Requirements.** According to Tulsa World, Bice voted for Senate Bill 1187, which would have allowed school districts to opt out of minimum teacher salary requirements, providing health insurance, and the teachers' retirement system. The bill passed the Oklahoma Senate 25-20. Bice argued it was about "local control," saying, "This bill gives that local control back to the very people that are most invested in their community and schools." [Tulsa World, [2016](https://tulsaworld.com/news/education/senate-passes-controversial-optional-school-deregulation-bill/article_568216ec-cc9f-5293-9891-c2394665eb18.html)]

- **The DCCC Framed The Vote As Allowing Schools To Pay Teachers Less Than Minimums And Cut Benefits.** According to the DCCC, Bice "voted to let school administrators pay teachers less than statewide minimums, cut their health insurance benefits and underfund the teacher retirement system." [DCCC, [8/25/20](https://dccc.org/the-case-against-stephanie-bice/)]

**Bice Voted For A Budget That Cut $60 Million From Oklahoma's Public Education.** According to DCCC research, Bice voted in 2016 for a budget that cut $60 million from Oklahoma's public education. She later voted for education funding increases in 2018 and 2019. [DCCC, [8/25/20](https://dccc.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/08/8.25.2020-Neese_Bice-Backup-SAM-SR.pdf)]
