# Rep. Stephanie Bice (OK-05): STOCK Act Violations

### Violated The STOCK Act By Filing Financial Disclosures Up To A Year Late

**Bice Failed To Disclose Treasury Bill Purchases Within The Required Timeframe.** According to Newsweek, Bice reported two U.S. Treasury bill purchases — made on June 22, 2023, and September 27, 2023, each worth between $15,001 and $50,000 — on August 10, 2024, nine months to a year after the required 45-day disclosure window. [Newsweek, [2024](https://www.newsweek.com/stock-act-congress-violations-trading-shares-1947459)]

- **Bice Was Among Dozens Of Lawmakers Who Violated The STOCK Act.** According to Sludge, Bice was among members of Congress who violated the STOCK Act by failing to report financial transactions within the legally required timeframe. [Sludge, [8/16/24](https://readsludge.com/2024/08/16/fetterman-gallego-and-others-violate-ethics-law-by-failing-to-report-stock-trades/)]
