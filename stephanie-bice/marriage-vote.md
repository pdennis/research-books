# Rep. Stephanie Bice (OK-05): Voted Against Protecting Her Own Interracial Marriage

### Voted Against A Law That Protects Her Own Interracial Marriage

**Bice Voted Against The Respect For Marriage Act Despite Being In An Interracial Marriage.** According to KFOR, four of five Oklahoma U.S. representatives voted against the Respect for Marriage Act, which codifies federal protections for both same-sex and interracial marriages. Bice, the daughter of an Iranian immigrant, voted against the bill that would protect her own marriage. She said the legislation "withheld key protections for religious communities, charities, and individuals." [KFOR, [12/8/22](https://kfor.com/news/local/4-of-5-oklahoma-u-s-representatives-vote-against-respect-for-marriage-act-5th-did-not-vote/)]
