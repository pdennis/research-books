# Rep. Stephanie Bice (OK-05): Election Denial and January 6

### Voted To Overturn The 2020 Election, Then Tried To Have It Both Ways On January 6

**Bice Voted To Object To Certifying Arizona's And Pennsylvania's Electoral Votes On January 6.** According to ReadFrontier, Bice was among the majority of Oklahoma's delegation who voted to object to certifying the 2020 election results after the Capitol riot. She justified her vote by claiming "the executive and judicial branches in certain states acted in an unconstitutional manner by implementing changes to state election laws." [ReadFrontier, [1/7/21](https://www.readfrontier.org/stories/majority-of-oklahoma-members-object-to-bidens-election-after-riot-overtakes-u-s-capitol/)]

- **Republican Accountability PAC Gave Bice An "F" Democracy Score.** According to the Republican Accountability PAC, Bice received a grade of "F (Very Poor)" for her actions related to the 2020 election and January 6. She was one of 147 Republicans who voted to overturn election results. [Republican Accountability, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://accountability.gop/profile/rep-stephanie-bice/)]

**Bice Was The Only Oklahoma Delegate To Vote For The Bipartisan January 6 Commission.** According to OKC Fox, Bice was one of only 35 Republicans to vote for the bipartisan commission to investigate January 6, and the only member of Oklahoma's delegation to do so. She said, "The purpose of the commission is not to go after former President Trump, but to find out why the Capitol police and sergeants-at-arms were so unprepared." [OKC Fox, [5/19/21](https://okcfox.com/news/connect-to-congress/congresswoman-bice-backs-jan-6th-commission-other-ok-lawmakers-vote-against)]

**Bice Then Reversed Course And Dismissed The January 6 Investigation As Partisan.** According to a column by Rep. Bice, she characterized the House Select Committee investigation as "Democrats' Partisan Jan. 6th Committee," distancing herself from the investigation she had initially voted to create. [Bice.House.Gov, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://bice.house.gov/media/weekly-columns/democrats-partisan-jan-6th-committee)]
