# Rep. Stephanie Bice (OK-05): Hiding From Voters — Town Hall Avoidance

### Refused To Hold In-Person Town Halls, Then Scheduled One In The Wrong District

**A Constituent Confronted Bice On A Telephone Town Hall, Calling Her Remote Events "A Joke."** According to Oklahoma Voice, a constituent who got through Bice's telephone town hall screening pressed her on why she would not hold in-person events "where constituents could look her in the face, eye-to-eye, and answer tough questions." The caller said the telephone town halls "are a joke" and "carefully screened," and claimed he had to "pretend to be asking another question" to get his question through. [Oklahoma Voice, [7/31/25](https://oklahomavoice.com/2025/07/31/constituent-presses-rep-stephanie-bice-to-hold-in-person-town-halls/)]

- **Bice Defended The Format, Saying She Could Reach "Several Thousand" By Phone.** According to NOTUS, Bice said, "I can reach several thousand constituents when I dial out for these telephone town halls. I am never going to get that kind of engagement if I'm doing in-person town halls. I am lucky to get a couple hundred, and that's on a great day." [NOTUS, [7/31/25](https://www.notus.org/congress/rep-stephanie-town-halls-remote-call)]

**Bice Scheduled A "Meet And Greet" Outside Her Own Congressional District, Then Canceled It.** According to Raw Story, Bice scheduled a small town hall meeting in a location outside the boundaries of her congressional district. Her Democratic opponent pointed out the error, and Bice canceled the event. [Raw Story, [8/27/25](https://www.rawstory.com/stephanie-bice-town-hall/)]

- **The Lost Ogle Headlined The Story: "Stephanie Bice Is Doing A Great Job (Hiding From Angry Voters)."** According to The Lost Ogle, Bice's avoidance of constituents and the wrong-district gaffe became a running story in Oklahoma media, with the blog writing that she "agrees to meet n' greet in wrong Congressional district." [Lost Ogle, [8/1/25](https://thelostogle.com/2025/08/01/stephanie-bice-is-doing-a-great-job-hiding-from-angry-voters)]
