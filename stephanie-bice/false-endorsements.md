# Rep. Stephanie Bice (OK-05): False Endorsements and Unauthorized Logo Use

### Used A Pro-Life Group's Logo Without Permission And Falsely Claimed A Pence Endorsement

**Bice's Campaign Sent A Mailer Using The Oklahomans For Life Logo Without Authorization.** According to OpenSecrets, the Bice campaign sent a mailer including the Oklahomans for Life logo without the organization's permission during the 2020 GOP primary. The organization had not made an endorsement in the race. Bice acknowledged the group was not endorsing, saying she "wanted to make clear that I am pro-life." [OpenSecrets, [6/2020](https://www.opensecrets.org/news/2020/06/big-money-oklahoma-race/)]

**Bice Falsely Claimed Vice President Mike Pence Had Endorsed Her.** According to the DCCC, Bice claimed an endorsement from Vice President Mike Pence during the 2020 primary, but Pence had not endorsed either runoff candidate. [DCCC, [8/25/20](https://dccc.org/the-case-against-stephanie-bice/)]
