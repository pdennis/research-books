Marshall Took Money From Fertilizer Companies Under Investigation For Price Fixing

Marshall Claimed That The Fertilizer Market Was “Working Against” Kansas Farmers

Marshall Claimed That The Fertilizer Market Was “Working Against” Kansas Farmers. Drop Site News posted, “ROGER MARSHALL | KANSAS ‘Kansas farmers are getting hit by a fertilizer market that’s working against them,’ Marshall said in April. His campaign and leadership PAC have received a combined $97,000 from PACs affiliated with three companies named in Bloomberg’s reporting on the DOJ price-fixing investigation: $75,000 from Koch’s PAC since 2016. $17,000 from Nutrien’s PAC since 2020. $5,000 from Mosaic’s PAC since 2024. Those are totals across multiple election cycles. Marshall has introduced legislation to remove duties on Moroccan phosphate fertilizer, saying farmers are paying prices that threaten their bottom line. But he has also repeatedly opposed efforts to end the military operations disrupting fertilizer supplies. On June 23, he voted against an Iran withdrawal resolution that passed the Senate. On July 30, he joined Ricketts in blocking advancement of another withdrawal measure.” [Twitter, @DropSiteNews, 9/3/26]
