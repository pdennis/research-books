One Kansas Farmer Saw His Fertilizer Prices Double Due To The War

One Kansas Farmer Saw His Fertilizer Prices Double Due To The War. According to KWCH, “As the Iran war continues, it’s taking a toll here on the home front in Kansas. With rising input costs all across the board, things like fertilizer, fuel and labor put an even bigger dent in farmers’ wallets. Spring planting season is on the horizon for most, meaning it comes at a crucial time for farmers like Robert White in Sumner County. ‘Farming runs on a pretty narrow margin to begin with, and when you take the main components of input and triple them or quadruple them, we’ll see who survives the longest,’ said White. White saw the prices on fertilizer that he buys for his farm double after the war began. That’ll hurt his ability to make a profit this year. ‘Urea fertilizer was $370 a ton, four days later it was $850 a ton. That’s outlandish,’ said White.” [KWCH, 4/4/26]
