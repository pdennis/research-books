Overview

Roger Marshall takes taxpaying Kansans for chumps. With an air of impunity, Marshall used technicalities to expense substantial amounts of his 2020 campaign on the taxpayer’s dime, shocking campaign finance experts with his “deliberate violations of the law” and “efforts to cover them up.” The man central to Marshall’s unethical scheme, Chief of Staff Brent Robertson, has a long history of his own with flouting ethics rules. Robertson charged taxpayers tens of thousands of dollars for his “highly unusual” commutes and made nearly $16,000 in prohibited outside income during his tenure. Roger Marshall doesn’t blink twice when it comes to fleecing Kansas taxpayers, and he surrounds himself with people of similar ill character to make it happen.

Campaign Finance Experts Strongly Disputed Robertson’s Interpretation Of The Ethics Rules
