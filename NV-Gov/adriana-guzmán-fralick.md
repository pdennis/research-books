# How To Win Against Adriana Guzmán Fralick

Republican Nevada attorney general candidate Adriana Guzmán Fralick supported policies that harmed everyday Nevadans and showed she would put Trump’s wishes over the needs of her constituents. Guzmán Fralick wanted to implement barriers to voting, including voter ID requirements and restrictions for mail-in voting. Guzmán Fralick also opposed the Affordable Care Act and wanted to sue the Obama administration over its passing in 2010. Guzmán Fralick also condoned Trump’s federal immigration tactics that killed two Americans in Minnesota, and pledged to “stop suing” Trump as attorney general. 

### **Guzmán Fralick Would Make It Harder For Nevadans To Vote**

-   In 2026, Guzmán Fralick made [_“securing elections”_](https://web.archive.org/web/20260508194356/https://www.adrianafornevada.com/issues/) a main priority of her attorney general campaign, and specifically wanted to pass the [_“SAVE Nevada Act”_](https://x.com/AdrianaForNV/status/2033574213408178510?s=20) to replicate Trump’s “SAVE America Act.” Guzmán Fralick pushed for election reforms that included eliminating [_automatic voter registration_](https://web.archive.org/web/20260508194356/https://www.adrianafornevada.com/issues/) and [_mail-in voter registration_](https://thenevadaindependent.com/article/in-gop-primary-for-nevada-attorney-general-newcomer-challenges-familiar-face), ending [_universal mail-in voting_](https://web.archive.org/web/20260508194356/https://www.adrianafornevada.com/issues/), implementing [_voter ID requirements_](https://www.2news.com/townnews/politics/looking-at-the-two-republican-candidates-for-nevada-attorney-general/article_c5a45bc7-e02e-4d75-a690-b9b6c9fb80de.html) and [_proof of citizenship_](https://thenevadaindependent.com/article/in-gop-primary-for-nevada-attorney-general-newcomer-challenges-familiar-face) for voter registration, and [_counting all ballots on election day_](https://x.com/AdrianaForNV/status/2033574213408178510?s=20).
-   Despite her focus on election security, Guzmán Fralick criticized her Democratic counterparts’ efforts to ensure free and fair elections in Nevada. Guzmán Fralick said she thought Attorney General Aaron Ford pursued legal action against fake electors in the 2020 presidential election [_“too aggressively,”_](https://www.rgj.com/story/news/politics/elections/2026/05/07/republicans-guzman-fralick-tarkanian-run-for-nevada-attorney-general/89969280007/) and was not sure if it was [_“worth spending the resources and the time.”_](https://www.rgj.com/story/news/politics/elections/2026/05/07/republicans-guzman-fralick-tarkanian-run-for-nevada-attorney-general/89969280007/) When Secretary of State Cisco Aguilar [_expressed concern_](https://www.reviewjournal.com/news/politics-and-government/nevada/nevada-13-other-states-say-shifting-mail-ballot-deadline-will-complicate-november-elections-3728935/) over the U.S. Supreme Court changing mail-in voting rules during an election year and how they would inform voters of last-minute changes, Guzmán Fralick [_called him a hypocrite_](https://x.com/AdrianaForNV/status/2036568052733456644?s=20) because Democrats changed the rules during an election year in 2020 due to the COVID-19 pandemic.
-   Guzmán Fralick also [_accused Aguilar of retaliating against her_](https://www.rgj.com/story/news/politics/elections/2026/04/08/guzman-fralick-criticizes-nevada-sos-after-drivers-license-exposed/89508804007/?gnt-cfr=1&gca-cat=p&gca-uir=true&gca-epti=z113730p001850c001850e006800v113730d--37--b--37--&gca-ft=167&gca-ds=sophi) because of her positions on election reform. After Aguilar announced the Secretary of State’s office had accidentally published her private information on their website, Guzmán suggested the information was published on purpose and questioned the office’s ability to keep elections secure.

**Message:** Guzmán Fralick supported changes to Nevada’s election systems that threatened Nevadans’ voter access and sowed distrust in elections.

### **Guzmán Fralick Supported Overturning The Affordable Care Act**

-   In 2010, as general counsel to Nevada Governor Jim Gibbons, Guzmán Fralick called for Attorney General Catherine Cortez Masto to join 14 other states’ attorneys general in suing the Obama administration to overturn the Affordable Care Act. Guzmán Fralick said she thought the arguments against the Affordable Care Act were “sufficient to give basis for a lawsuit” and was not “frivolous.” More than [_104,000 Nevadans_](https://www.kff.org/affordable-care-act/state-indicator/open-enrollment-marketplace-plan-selections/?currentTimeframe=0&selectedRows=%7B%22states%22:%7B%22nevada%22:%7B%7D%7D%7D&sortModel=%7B%22colId%22:%22Location%22,%22sort%22:%22asc%22%7D) were enrolled in Affordable Care Act marketplace plans in 2026.
-   In 2026, Trump’s “One Big Beautiful Bill” made [_drastic changes to the Affordable Care Act_](https://www.ama-assn.org/system/files/aca-enrollment-changes-summary.pdf) to [_fund tax cuts_](https://www.cnn.com/2025/05/02/politics/what-is-in-trump-tax-spending-cuts-package) [_for the wealthy,_](https://www.cbpp.org/blog/house-republican-budget-takes-away-health-care-food-aid-to-pay-for-expanded-tax-cuts-for) including allowing premium tax credit subsidies to expire which was expected to cause 4.2 million enrollees to become uninsured. It also made changes that would increase out-of-pocket expenses, make it more difficult for enrollees to remain eligible, and cost states more than $7 million to implement. More than [_110,000_](https://www.americanprogress.org/article/the-one-big-beautiful-bill-act-will-increase-the-number-of-americans-without-health-coverage-in-every-state-and-congressional-district/#:~:text=Table_content:%20header:%20%7C%20State%20%7C%20Increase%20in,%7C%20Increase%20in%20uninsured%20population:%20+422%2C000%20%7C) were expected to lose health insurance by 2034 due to the bill.

**Message:** Guzmán Fralick supported ripping health care away from hundreds of thousands of Nevadans to fund tax cuts for the wealthy.

### **Guzmán Fralick Would Not Protect Nevadans From Trump’s Federal Overreach**

-   When federal immigration agents killed two Americans in Minnesota, Guzmán Fralick defended their actions, claiming they were [_“trying to do a job”_](https://nevadacurrent.com/2026/01/27/fed-violence-in-mn-prompts-reactions-from-nv-ag-governor-candidates-except-for-lombardo/) and following [_“orders.”_](https://nevadacurrent.com/2026/01/27/fed-violence-in-mn-prompts-reactions-from-nv-ag-governor-candidates-except-for-lombardo/) Guzmán Fralick argued the aggressive and deadly immigration tactics were Trump doing the job Americans [_“elected \[him\] to do.”_](https://nevadacurrent.com/2026/04/28/republican-ag-primary-pits-lombardos-choice-against-perennial-candidate-with-a-famous-name/)
-   Guzmán Fralick also made [_“stopping Sanctuary cities”_](https://web.archive.org/web/20260508194356/https://www.adrianafornevada.com/issues/) a main priority of her campaign, and said local law enforcement needed to [_“be free to work with federal immigration officials to keep dangerous criminals off our streets.”_](https://web.archive.org/web/20260508194356/https://www.adrianafornevada.com/issues/) This is despite the fact that when Guzman was 9, she immigrated to America from Mexico, an experience which she claimed [_“gave her empathy.”_](https://nevadacurrent.com/2026/04/28/republican-ag-primary-pits-lombardos-choice-against-perennial-candidate-with-a-famous-name/)

**Message:** Guzmán Fralick defended Trump’s federal immigration enforcement tactics that resulted in the deaths of two Americans.

### **Guzmán Fralick Suggested She Would Do Trump’s Bidding As Nevada’s Attorney General**

-   In her 2026 attorney general campaign, Guzmán Fralick worked to align herself with Trump, and said the first action she would take in office was to [_“stop suing our president.”_](https://soundcloud.com/abpac/adriana-guzman-fralick/s-R3d03Itaolj?si=1b486f90404e4e85979bd4996ae70abc&utm_source=clipboard&utm_medium=text&utm_campaign=social_sharing) Guzmán Fralick said she supported the actions Trump has taken in office so far, and said she believed Trump [_“knows way more than I do.”_](https://thenevadaindependent.com/article/in-gop-primary-for-nevada-attorney-general-newcomer-challenges-familiar-face)
-   Guzmán Fralick also attempted to paint her Republican primary opponent, Danny Tarkanian, as a never-Trumper. Guzmán Fralick [_attacked Tarkanian_](https://thenevadaindependent.com/article/in-gop-primary-for-nevada-attorney-general-newcomer-challenges-familiar-face) using an op-ed he shared in 2024 titled, “I hate Trump, but I’m glad he won.” Guzmán Fralick said the post showed that Tarkanian, [_and anyone who supported him_](https://www.rgj.com/story/news/politics/elections/2026/04/03/nevada-attorney-general-race-sparks-trump-clash-at-republican-forum/89452977007/?gnt-cfr=1&gca-cat=p&gca-uir=true&gca-epti=z113630p001950c001950e003600v113630d--36--b--36--&gca-ft=176&gca-ds=sophi), hated Trump, which would hurt him in the primary because Trump was [_“extremely popular with our party.”_](https://thenevadaindependent.com/article/in-gop-primary-for-nevada-attorney-general-newcomer-challenges-familiar-face)

**Message:** Guzmán Fralick’s statements suggested she would submit to Trump over protecting Nevada.
