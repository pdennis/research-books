Rep. Tony Wied (WI-08): Active Stock Trading While Voting on Market-Moving Policies

Active Stock Trader Voting On Policies That Move Markets

Wied Was One Of The Most Active Stock Traders In Wisconsin's Congressional Delegation. According to the Badger Project, Wied, "a wealthy businessman elected to Congress in 2024, is very active in the stock market compared to other members of Wisconsin's congressional delegation." His October periodic transaction report alone listed 11 transactions valued between $1.6 million and $6.5 million combined. [Badger Project, 1/13/26]

Wied Moved Between $1 Million And $5 Million Into Treasury Bills As Stock Market Turmoil Mounted. According to NEW Media, Wied purchased between $1 million and $5 million in U.S. Treasury bills in October — "a slow-growing but safe asset" — as congressional stock trading came under scrutiny following a surge of trades around President Trump's tariff announcements that briefly crashed the market. [NEW Media, 1/13/26]

  • Wied's Spokesperson Said His Investments Were "Solely Managed Through An Independent Financial Advisor." According to NEW Media, Wied's spokesperson stated his investments are "solely managed through an independent financial advisor" and that Wied does not participate in trading decisions. [NEW Media, 1/13/26]

Wied Sold Off Hundreds Of Thousands Of Dollars Worth Of Tariff-Exposed Stocks, While Vocally Supporting Trump’s Tariffs

2026: Wied Sold Off Between $395,000 And $950,000 In Tariff-Exposed Stocks

2026: Wied Sold Off Between $395,000 And $950,000 In Tariff-Exposed Stocks. According to Washington Sun, “Members of Congress sold off millions of dollars in shares of companies exposed to President Donald Trump’s tariffs this year, even as they publicly downplayed, praised or voted against ending the policies. […] Rep. Tony Wied (R-Wisconsin) dropped at least between $395,000 and $950,000 in tariff-exposed stocks this year. The majority came from companies that manufacture semiconductors or related equipment, including at least $150,001 in Lam Research and $100,001 in Advanced Micro Devices stock. The transactions were made through a jointly owned account.” [Washington Sun, 10/28/26]

Wied Sold Stocks In Companies That Manufactured Semiconductors, After Trump Levied A 25 Percent Tariff On The Industry In January 2026

The Majority Of The Tariff-Exposed Stocks Wied Sold Were In Companies That Manufactured Semiconductors, An Industry That Faced A 25 Percent Tariff Levied By Trump In January. According to Washington Sun, “The majority came from companies that manufacture semiconductors or related equipment, including at least $150,001 in Lam Research and $100,001 in Advanced Micro Devices stock. The transactions were made through a jointly owned account. In January, Trump levied 25% tariffs on the semiconductor industry and is reportedly considering enforcing new semiconductor tariffs. Wied sold the affected Lam Research stocks during a nine-day period in February. Lawmakers are only required to report the value of their transactions in broad ranges.” [Washington Sun, 10/28/26]

Wied Vocally Supported Trump’s Tariff Policies 

Wied Claimed Trump Was “Doing The Right Thing” When It Came To Tariffs And Suggested It Was What Was Best For The Country. According to Washington Sun, “‘I think he’s doing the right thing, utilizing different tools to be able to use those tariffs, again, as leverage to do what’s best for our country, and to grow our economy and make us safer and more secure,’ Wied told C-SPAN in February, weeks after selling semiconductor industry stocks.” [Washington Sun, 10/28/26]
