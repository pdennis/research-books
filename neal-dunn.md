# How To Win Against Neal Dunn

Neal Dunn markets himself as a doctor who understands health care, but his record reveals a wealthy physician-banker who consistently sides with industry donors over the struggling families of rural north Florida. He accepted over [$273,000 from health industry donors](https://www.opensecrets.org/members-of-congress/neal-dunn/industries?cid=N00037442) then voted against the [$35 insulin cap](https://clerk.house.gov/Votes/2022102), against [Medicare drug price negotiation](https://news.wfsu.org/wfsu-local-news/2022-08-17/rep-neal-dunn-explains-vote-against-inflation-reduction-act), and to [repeal the Affordable Care Act](https://dunn.house.gov/2017/5/dr-dunn-s-statement-repealing-and-replacing-obamacare) — all while his district includes counties where [uninsured rates top 40 percent](https://www.floridapolicy.org/posts/florida-small-towns-and-rural-areas-are-disproportionately-hurt-by-floridas-failure-to-expand-medicaid). He cast a decisive vote for the One Big Beautiful Bill Act, which [slashed $300 billion from SNAP and $700 billion from Medicaid](https://www.govtrack.us/congress/votes/119-2025/h145) — programs that nearly [3 million Floridians](https://www.wusf.org/politics-issues/2025-05-30/trump-big-beautiful-bill-cuts-medicaid-snap-what-that-means-for-floridians) depend on — in a district where [35.5 percent of children in Gadsden County are food insecure](https://www.wusf.org/health-news-florida/2025-02-21/food-insecurity-major-concern-florida-children-well-being). He voted to [keep Trump's tariffs on Canada](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/congress/house-votes-end-trump-tariffs-canada-rcna258661) that economists called a ["Category 5 economic hurricane" for Florida](https://www.newsweek.com/donald-trump-tariffs-impact-florida-jobs-economy-2055385), and he [violated the STOCK Act](https://www.opensecrets.org/news/2025/03/rep-neal-dunn-becomes-latest-lawmaker-to-violate-stock-act) by filing a late financial disclosure on a cryptocurrency-linked stock purchase. Now he has [announced his retirement](https://rollcall.com/2026/01/13/neal-dunn-republican-florida-retires-election/) and is [rumored to resign early](https://floridapolitics.com/archives/779479-neal-dunns-office-continues-to-swat-away-speculation-about-potential-early-exit-from-congress/) — checking out on the constituents whose health care, food assistance, and economic security he voted to gut.

### **HEALTH CARE: A DOCTOR WHO VOTED AGAINST HIS PATIENTS' INTERESTS**

Dunn voted to [repeal the Affordable Care Act](https://dunn.house.gov/2017/5/dr-dunn-s-statement-repealing-and-replacing-obamacare) and weaken pre-existing condition protections. He voted against the [$35 insulin cap](https://clerk.house.gov/Votes/2022102) and against the [Inflation Reduction Act's Medicare drug price negotiation](https://news.wfsu.org/wfsu-local-news/2022-08-17/rep-neal-dunn-explains-vote-against-inflation-reduction-act), dismissing it as "inflationary spending."
[Health professionals were his top industry donor](https://www.opensecrets.org/members-of-congress/neal-dunn/industries?cid=N00037442), contributing over $273,000. He held [$32,000 in pharmaceutical investments](https://www.opensecrets.org/personal-finances/neal-dunn/net-worth?cid=N00037442) while voting on health care legislation.
His rural north Florida district has uninsured rates [above 40 percent in counties like Jackson](https://www.floridapolicy.org/posts/florida-small-towns-and-rural-areas-are-disproportionately-hurt-by-floridas-failure-to-expand-medicaid), and [13 counties exceed the state poverty level](https://digitalcommons.unf.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1289&context=fphr).

**Message:** Dunn is a doctor who took $273,000 from the health care industry, then voted against insulin caps and drug price negotiation for his own patients — in a district where 4 in 10 people lack insurance.

### **AFFORDABILITY: BACKED TARIFFS AND GUTTED FOOD ASSISTANCE**

Voted to [keep Trump's tariffs on Canada](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/congress/house-votes-end-trump-tariffs-canada-rcna258661) that Florida TaxWatch warned could [raise grocery bills by 10 to 15 percent](https://floridapolitics.com/archives/746357-florida-taxwatch-state-consumers-will-see-higher-prices-as-a-result-of-donald-trumps-trade-tariffs/) and add [$10,000 to the cost of a new Florida home](https://www.businessobserverfl.com/news/2025/jul/11/report-tariffs-add-to-cost-florida-house-cars/).
Cast a decisive vote for the OBBBA, which would [more than double Florida's SNAP administrative costs from $89 million to $1.6 billion](https://www.wusf.org/politics-issues/2025-05-30/trump-big-beautiful-bill-cuts-medicaid-snap-what-that-means-for-floridians) and could threaten [nearly 1.75 million Florida families with children](https://www.splcenter.org/resources/hopewatch/one-big-beautiful-bill-act-florida-food-assistance/).
The Yale Budget Lab found the combined effect of tariffs and the OBBBA would [cut incomes for the bottom 10 percent by approximately 7 percent](https://budgetlab.yale.edu/research/combined-distributional-effects-one-big-beautiful-bill-act-and-tariffs-0) while giving the top 10 percent a 1.5 percent raise.

**Message:** Dunn voted for tariffs that raise grocery bills 10-15% and a spending bill that guts food assistance — in a district where more than a third of children go hungry.

### **JOBS AND ECONOMY: VOTED AGAINST EVERY MAJOR INVESTMENT**

Voted against the [bipartisan infrastructure law](https://www.govtrack.us/congress/votes/117-2021/h369) and the [CHIPS and Science Act](https://www.govtrack.us/congress/votes/117-2022/h404), blocking investments in roads, broadband, and domestic manufacturing for rural north Florida.
Backed tariffs that a University of Tampa economist called a ["Category 5 economic hurricane" for Florida](https://www.newsweek.com/donald-trump-tariffs-impact-florida-jobs-economy-2055385). Nationally, [72,000 manufacturing jobs were lost](https://www.commondreams.org/news/tariffs-and-us-manufacturing) in the eight months following the tariffs.
The OBBBA he voted for could [threaten 33,000 Florida jobs and blow a $3 billion hole in the state economy](https://www.wusf.org/politics-issues/2025-05-30/trump-big-beautiful-bill-cuts-medicaid-snap-what-that-means-for-floridians).

**Message:** Dunn voted against every major jobs bill, backed tariffs economists call a "Category 5 hurricane" for Florida, and supported a spending law that could destroy 33,000 Florida jobs.

### **ETHICS: STOCK ACT VIOLATION AND STAFF SCANDAL**

[Violated the STOCK Act](https://www.opensecrets.org/news/2025/03/rep-neal-dunn-becomes-latest-lawmaker-to-violate-stock-act) by filing a late financial disclosure on a MicroStrategy stock purchase — a cryptocurrency-linked firm that owned more than $40 billion worth of Bitcoin.
His [chief of staff resigned in 2018](https://thehill.com/homenews/house/376083-florida-reps-chief-of-staff-resigns-amid-sexual-misconduct-allegations-report/) after being named in a House Ethics Committee sexual harassment investigation.
As a multimillionaire who founded a [50-physician medical practice and a regional bank](https://dunn.house.gov/biography) with a [net worth over $9.5 million](https://www.opensecrets.org/personal-finances/neal-dunn/net-worth?cid=N00037442), Dunn is insulated from the economic pain his votes inflict on one of Florida's poorest districts.

**Message:** Dunn violated federal disclosure law, hired a chief of staff embroiled in a sexual harassment probe, and lives as a multimillionaire while voting to gut programs his poorest constituents depend on.

### **CHECKED OUT: RETIRING AND RUMORED TO RESIGN EARLY**

[Announced he would not seek reelection](https://rollcall.com/2026/01/13/neal-dunn-republican-florida-retires-election/) in January 2026. House Republican leadership [believes he will resign by July](https://floridapolitics.com/archives/779479-neal-dunns-office-continues-to-swat-away-speculation-about-potential-early-exit-from-congress/), further shrinking the GOP's razor-thin majority to 217-214.

**Message:** Dunn is already out the door — checking out on the constituents he spent a decade voting against.
