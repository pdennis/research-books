# How To Win Against Mario Diaz-Balart

### Diaz-Balart Voted To Gut Healthcare For Floridians

- Diaz-Balart [voted](https://clerk.house.gov/Votes/2025190) for a bill that would kick [17 million Americans](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2025/07/01/least-17-million-americans-would-lose-insurance-under-trump-plan/) off their health insurance, including nearly [11.8 million Americans](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2025/07/01/least-17-million-americans-would-lose-insurance-under-trump-plan/) on Medicaid. As a result of his vote, more than [179,100](https://www.americanprogress.org/article/medicaid-and-chip-coverage-mapped-by-119th-congressional-districts/) of his own constituents were at risk of losing coverage. 

- Diaz-Balart said he was [“proud”](https://mariodiazbalart.house.gov/media-center/press-releases/diaz-balart-president-trumps-one-big-beautiful-bill-will-boost-economic) to vote for the “Big Beautiful Bill” for many reasons. Diaz-Balart pretended he was someone who wanted to preserve and [protect](https://mariodiazbalart.house.gov/media-center/press-releases/diaz-balart-applauds-passage-president-trumps-one-big-beautiful-bill) Medicare for seniors. Then, he [voted](https://clerk.house.gov/Votes/2025190) for a bill that the Congressional Budget Office estimated would result in nearly [$500 billion](https://www.cbo.gov/system/files/2025-05/61423-PAYGO.pdf) in cuts to Medicare, absent future congressional action.

### Diaz-Balart Voted For The Largest Food Assistance Cut In History

- Diaz-Balart [voted](https://clerk.house.gov/Votes/2025190) for a bill that made the [largest SNAP cut](https://www.cbpp.org/research/food-assistance/by-the-numbers-senate-republican-leaderships-reconciliation-bill-takes) in history. His vote [threatened](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/07/10/trumps-big-beautiful-bill-cuts-snap-for-millions-of-families.html) food assistance for more than [64,000 households](https://www.politico.com/news/2023/12/23/mike-johnsons-florida-snap-problem-00132868) in the 26th district. 

### Diaz-Balart Backed The Iran War That Raised Prices For Floridians

- Diaz-Balart defended Trump’s war in Iran by saying he felt the conflict was [necessary](https://www.cbsnews.com/miami/news/florida-26-congressional-district-mario-diaz-balart-nicole-locklin-deborah-ann-meidinger-hosey/). In February 2026, he [commended](https://mariodiazbalart.house.gov/media-center/press-releases/diaz-balart-expresses-solidarity-iranian-people-and-commends-president) Trump for launching attacks on Iran. Diaz-Balart then [repeatedly](https://plus.cq.com/vote/2026/H/307) voted against ending the United States’ hostilities in Iran. The conflict was costly for Floridians who faced higher costs of [gas](https://www.mysuncoast.com/2026/07/13/florida-gas-prices-shoot-up-after-tensions-escalate-us-iran-conflict/), [diesel](https://www.azfamily.com/2026/07/17/gas-prices-continue-inch-up-us-iran-resume-fighting/) and [groceries](https://apnews.com/article/iran-war-prices-gasoline-groceries-flights-9c413bc111efcfa9bac53b20e9057738) as a result of the conflict. 

### Diaz-Balart Was The Deciding Vote To Protect Trump's Tariffs That Hurt Floridians

- Diaz-Balart voted to protect Trump’s tariffs six times, including in April 2025 when he was the [deciding vote](https://clerk.house.gov/evs/2025/roll094.xml) to protect them in Congress. Diaz-Balart commended the president for his tariffs and claimed he was [“standing up”](https://floridianpress.com/2025/04/buchanan-commends-trumps-renegotiation-of-european-trade-deals-after-eu-visit/) for American jobs. Tariffs contributed to [higher food prices](https://fortune.com/2026/04/08/iran-war-high-grocery-prices-getting-worse/) for Florida consumers and [“mega-layoffs”](https://www.washingtonpost.com/business/2025/11/06/job-cuts-accelerate-october-layoffs-trump/) across the nation.
