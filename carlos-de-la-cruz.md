# How To Win Against Carlos De La Cruz

Carlos De La Cruz thought he could win the TX-35  election on family ties alone. He coasted to victory in the primary without taking a stand on key issues important to Texans, instead relying on his sister’s name. One thing he made clear was that he was eager to be “Trump’s wingman” if elected to Congress. He has touted endorsements from President Donald Trump and Speaker Mike Johnson, even though their policies have caused pain for many Texans in the 35^th^ district, and he has also touted endorsements from numerous members of Congress who have faced various scandals. Texans in the new 35^th^ district deserve a congressman who will be honest with them about where they stand on key issues, not another self-serving politician who’s just another talking head for Washington party leaders. 

### De La Cruz Was Eager To Back Trump And Speaker Johnson’s Agenda, Which Included Cutting Health Care For 17 Million Americans

-   Trump and Speaker Mike Johnson were behind legislation that cut health care for [17 million Americans](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2025/07/01/least-17-million-americans-would-lose-insurance-under-trump-plan/), including nearly [12 million Medicaid](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2025/07/01/least-17-million-americans-would-lose-insurance-under-trump-plan/) beneficiaries. More than [4.4 million people](https://files.kff.org/attachment/fact-sheet-medicaid-state-TX) in Texas relied on Medicaid to obtain health insurance.

**Message:** De La Cruz wanted to be another vote in Congress to kick Texans off their health care all to give billionaires tax breaks. 

### De La Cruz supported Trump And Johnson who spearheaded a bill that will trigger major cuts to Medicare 

-   Trump and Johnson [fought](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2025/may/22/house-vote-trump-tax-bill) for passage of the One Big Beautiful Bill, which would trigger nearly [$500 billion](https://www.cbo.gov/system/files/2025-05/61423-PAYGO.pdf) in Medicare cuts absent future congressional action. [3.4 million Texans](https://www.tha.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/2025-Hospital-Payment-Sources.pdf) relied on Medicare. 

**Message:** De La Cruz would be another vote to slash Medicare for seniors. 

### De La Cruz Wanted To Wingman Trump and johnson as they Raised Prices AND DAMAGED TEXAS’ ECONOMY

-   Mike Johnson [worked](https://www.axios.com/2026/02/11/tariff-votes-congress-canada-mexico-democrats) around the clock to block legislative opposition to Trump’s costly tariffs. Johnson pulled several [legislative maneuvers](https://www.businessinsider.com/gop-leaders-mike-johnson-block-congress-action-trump-tariffs-2025-4) to ensure House Republicans [supported tariffs](https://www.reuters.com/world/us/house-republicans-block-congress-ability-challenge-trump-tariffs-2025-03-11/) that were raising prices on all Americans. In Texas, the tariffs were causing [worsening job growth](https://www.sacurrent.com/news/new-numbers-show-trumps-tariffs-mass-deportations-taking-a-toll-on-the-texas-economy-38270071/) and [increased prices](https://www.houstonpublicmedia.org/articles/arts-culture/food/2025/10/31/534764/restaurants-texas-trump-immigration-tariffs/) for local businesses and their customers. 

**Message:** De La Cruz was eager to back making Texans pay higher prices on groceries. 

### De La Cruz STOOD WITH Trump And Johnson AS THEY MADE SIGNIFICANT CUTS To Food Assistance texans relied on to afford groceries

-   Trump and Johnson were [behind](https://www.foxnews.com/politics/johnson-touts-beauty-unified-government) the single largest cut to SNAP in history. The “One Big Beautiful Bill” they pushed through Congress included [$186 billion](https://www.cbpp.org/research/food-assistance/by-the-numbers-senate-republican-leaderships-reconciliation-bill-takes) in cuts to food assistance. 

**Message:** De la Cruz would be another vote in congress for cuts to food assistance that Texans rely on to afford groceries. 

### De La Cruz Gave His Full Support To Trump’s Agenda, Which Included weakening the social security administration and slashing san antonio’s government contracts

-   Trump’s “Department of Government Efficiency” (DOGE) weakened the Social Security Administration and cost Texas hundreds of millions in federal contracts. DOGE cuts caused longer [wait times](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2025/04/07/social-security-website-crashes-musk-trump/), frequent [website crashes](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2025/04/07/social-security-website-crashes-musk-trump/), and [mass layoffs](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2025/03/25/social-security-phones-doge-cuts/) in Social Security offices across the country, making it harder for Texas seniors to access their benefits. San Antonio businesses lost an estimated [$375 million](https://www.expressnews.com/business/article/doge-contract-cuts-san-antonio-texas-583m-20281710.php) in federal contracts because of DOGE cuts, and Texas lost at least [five Veterans' Affairs contracts](https://www.expressnews.com/business/article/doge-contract-cuts-san-antonio-texas-583m-20281710.php).

**Message:** De La Cruz was willing to back an agenda that hamstrung services for seniors and cut contracts meant to help South-Central Texans.
