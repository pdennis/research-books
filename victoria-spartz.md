# How To Win Against Victoria Spartz

Victoria Spartz has built a record defined by chaos, hypocrisy, and capitulation. She declared herself a ["hard no"](https://newrepublic.com/post/192088/donald-trump-bullied-victoria-spartz-vote-budget-bill) on the budget reconciliation bill over deficit concerns, then flipped after Trump [screamed at her and called her "a fake Republican"](https://newrepublic.com/post/192088/donald-trump-bullied-victoria-spartz-vote-budget-bill) — voting for a law projected to [strip Medicaid from nearly 12 million people](https://www.chcs.org/resource/a-summary-of-national-medicaid-work-requirements/) and [cut $186 billion from food assistance](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/08/08/trumps-spending-bill-cuts-billions-in-snap-benefits.html), threatening benefits for [600,000 Hoosiers](https://usafacts.org/answers/how-many-people-receive-snap-benefits-in-the-us-every-month/state/indiana/).

She criticized Biden's [9-18% lumber tariffs](https://wibc.com/124170/spartz-says-white-house-plans-to-raise-lumber-tariffs-on-canada-are-coming-at-the-worst-time/) as coming at "the worst time," then [voted to protect Trump's far larger 25% tariffs on Canada](https://thehill.com/homenews/house/5732632-gop-rebuke-trump-tariffs/) — hours after saying Congress should "decide which tariffs are good." Those tariffs triggered [900 layoffs at Stellantis plants in Kokomo](https://www.wishtv.com/news/business/stellantis-announces-layoffs-at-kokomo-plant/), in her own district. 

The [House Ethics Committee investigated her](https://www.mediaite.com/politics/house-republican-faces-ethics-committee-probe-over-abuse-rage-and-general-toxicity-in-the-workplace/) for staff abuse including throwing furniture at aides, she was [charged with bringing a gun through airport security](https://www.cnn.com/2024/07/01/politics/victoria-spartz-weapons-charge-airport/index.html), and she [called President Zelensky a "moron"](https://www.kyivpost.com/post/50487) and said Ukraine should cede territory to Russia. The Indiana Capital Chronicle summed her up as ["simply unreliable, and that's the kindest description available."](https://indianacapitalchronicle.com/2024/06/11/rep-victoria-spartz-is-simply-unreliable-and-thats-the-kindest-description-available/)

### **HEALTH CARE: LIED ABOUT PREEXISTING CONDITIONS, THEN VOTED TO GUT MEDICAID**

Spartz [voted against protecting Hoosiers with preexisting conditions](https://dccc.org/spartz-continues-to-lie-about-health-care-in-latest-ad/) as a state senator, then ran ads claiming she "guaranteed protections for Hoosiers with pre-existing conditions." Her plan put [329,300 Hoosiers with preexisting conditions at risk](https://dccc.org/spartz-continues-to-lie-about-health-care-in-latest-ad/).

Spartz [voted against the Inflation Reduction Act](https://www.govtrack.us/congress/votes/117-2022/h420), which capped insulin at $35/month for seniors and gave Medicare the power to negotiate drug prices for the first time — [lowering costs for Indiana seniors](https://www.hhs.gov/sites/default/files/ira-rx-prices-indiana.pdf).

The reconciliation law Spartz voted for is projected to [cost Indiana $800 million in uncompensated care and threaten 12 hospital closures](https://www.indianahousedemocrats.org/big-ugly-betrayal), with [nearly 1.8 million Hoosiers covered by Medicaid or CHIP](https://www.indianahousedemocrats.org/big-ugly-betrayal) at risk.

**Message:** Spartz lied about protecting preexisting conditions, voted against $35 insulin, then caved to Trump and voted to gut Medicaid for nearly 2 million Hoosiers.

### **AFFORDABILITY: CRITICIZED TARIFFS UNDER BIDEN, THEN PROTECTED BIGGER ONES UNDER TRUMP**

Spartz called Biden's [9-18% lumber tariffs "the worst time"](https://wibc.com/124170/spartz-says-white-house-plans-to-raise-lumber-tariffs-on-canada-are-coming-at-the-worst-time/) to raise prices on consumers — then [voted to block Congress from repealing Trump's 25% tariffs on Canada](https://reason.com/2026/02/11/the-house-just-reclaimed-its-tariff-power-from-trump-now-congress-can-try-to-strike-down-his-canada-tariffs/), which are far larger. She said Congress should ["decide which tariffs are good, which tariffs are bad"](https://thehill.com/homenews/house/5732632-gop-rebuke-trump-tariffs/) — then hours later voted to prevent Congress from doing exactly that.

The [Yale Budget Lab estimates tariffs cost the average household $1,700/year](https://budgetlab.yale.edu/research/state-us-tariffs-november-17-2025), hitting the poorest families hardest. A Ball State economist said [Indiana is among the three most at-risk states](https://www.ibj.com/articles/tariffs-could-reach-deep-into-manufacturing-heavy-indiana) because manufacturing accounts for 26% of GDP.

**Message:** Spartz called tariffs bad under Biden but protected bigger ones under Trump — while Indiana families and factories pay the price.

### **JOBS AND THE ECONOMY: VOTED AGAINST EVERY MAJOR JOBS BILL, THEN BACKED TARIFFS KILLING JOBS**

Spartz voted against the [CHIPS Act (\$3.87B semiconductor investment in Indiana)](https://www.govtrack.us/congress/votes/117-2022/h404), the [Infrastructure Law (\$7.9B for Indiana roads and bridges)](https://www.govtrack.us/congress/votes/117-2021/h369), and the [Inflation Reduction Act ($7.8B in clean energy investment in Indiana)](https://www.govtrack.us/congress/votes/117-2022/h420) — opposing every bipartisan jobs bill that brought billions to her state.

The tariffs she voted to protect triggered [900 layoffs at three Stellantis plants in Kokomo](https://www.wishtv.com/news/business/stellantis-announces-layoffs-at-kokomo-plant/) — in her own district. [Indiana farm income is projected to drop over $1 billion in 2026](https://ag.purdue.edu/commercialag/home/resource/2026/02/farm-income-outlook-for-indiana/).

**Message:** Spartz voted against $19.5 billion in investment for Indiana, then backed tariffs that are laying off her own constituents in Kokomo.

### **CHAOS AND CHARACTER: ETHICS INVESTIGATION, GUN CHARGE, AND STAFF ABUSE**

The [House Ethics Committee investigated Spartz](https://www.mediaite.com/politics/house-republican-faces-ethics-committee-probe-over-abuse-rage-and-general-toxicity-in-the-workplace/) over allegations of "complete and total rage," with former aides saying she [threw furniture and shoes at staffers](https://www.rawstory.com/victoria-spartz-2668478720/). She has the [highest historical staff turnover of any active House member](https://www.legistorm.com/pro_news/3225/spartzs-staff-turnover-problem-continues-to-get-worse.html) — 3.5x the House average.

Spartz was [charged with a misdemeanor for bringing a gun through TSA at Dulles Airport](https://www.cnn.com/2024/07/01/politics/victoria-spartz-weapons-charge-airport/index.html).

She [announced her retirement](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/congress/republican-rep-victoria-spartz-says-will-retire-politics-2024-rcna69039), then [reversed course after nine Republicans had already filed to replace her](https://www.axios.com/2024/02/05/house-republican-victoria-spartz-retirement-walk-back), then [boycotted committees and the GOP caucus](https://www.wfyi.org/news/articles/spartz-to-boycott-committees-gop-caucus) after being denied her preferred assignment.

**Message:** Spartz's own staff can't work for her, her own party can't count on her, and her constituents can't trust her.
