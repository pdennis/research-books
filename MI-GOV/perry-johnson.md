# How To Win Against Perry Johnson

Failed gubernatorial and presidential candidate Perry Johnson built his campaign around supporting Trump’s harmful agenda, including backing the “One Big Beautiful Bill,” which would strip health care from hundreds of thousands of Michiganders. While Trump’s “One Big Beautiful Bill” would slash SNAP benefits relied on by Michigan families, Perry Johnson also denied Trump’s tariffs were raising grocery prices. Perry Johnson praised Trump’s “One Big Beautiful Bill,” which also threatened clean energy jobs and projects across Michigan.Perry Johnson repeatedly praised Trump’s tariffs and urged Americans to “get behind tariffs,” even as they drove up grocery prices and hurt Michigan farmers, families, and small businesses. Perry Johnson supported Trump’s Department of Government Efficiency and called himself the “Perfect DOGE Guy,” even as its cuts threatened social security services relied on by more than 2.3 million Michiganders and threatened thousands of federal workers across Michigan. Johnson supported extreme positions on reproductive rights, claimed “life begins at conception,” supported a six-week abortion ban, and opposed abortion exceptions for rape and incest. Perry Johnson spread conspiracy theories about the 2020 election and endorsed candidates who supported efforts to overturn the results. His previous gubernatorial campaign was tied to fraudulent petition signatures after election officials found more than 9,000 invalid signatures and removed him from the Republican primary ballot.

### Perry Johnson Supported Devastating Health Care Cuts

-   In 2025, Perry Johnson [supported](https://x.com/PJQualityGuru/status/1924547990099722737) Trump’s “One Big Beautiful Bill,” which was estimated to kick [17 million Americans](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2025/07/01/least-17-million-americans-would-lose-insurance-under-trump-plan/) off their health insurance, including [nearly 12 million Americans](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2025/07/01/least-17-million-americans-would-lose-insurance-under-trump-plan/) who rely on Medicaid. More than [424,000 Michiganders](https://www.jec.senate.gov/public/_cache/files/9cfdee90-dd29-41e8-97da-b5dcdc0317cf/updated-jec-fact-sheet-on-district-level-impacts-of-health-care-cuts.pdf) could lose their health insurance and hundreds of rural hospitals could close, including [at least three hospitals](https://www.michiganpublic.org/politics-government/2025-07-15/trump-medicaid-cuts-risk-closing-three-michigan-hospitals) in Michigan as a result of Trump’s tax bill. By 2034, [237,708 Medicaid recipients](https://www.jec.senate.gov/public/_cache/files/9cfdee90-dd29-41e8-97da-b5dcdc0317cf/updated-jec-fact-sheet-on-district-level-impacts-of-health-care-cuts.pdf) in Michigan were expected to lose their coverage under the “One Big Beautiful Bill.”
-   In 2025, Trump’s “One Big Beautiful Bill” also [failed to extend](https://www.ama-assn.org/health-care-advocacy/federal-advocacy/changes-medicaid-aca-and-other-key-provisions-one-big) enhanced Affordable Care Act subsidies that were set to expire after 2025, contributing to rising premiums and fewer Michiganders enrolling in Marketplace coverage. As of January 2026, [40,000 fewer](https://bridgemi.com/michigan-health-watch/40000-fewer-michiganders-seek-affordable-care-act-health-insurance/) Michiganders signed up for Affordable Care Act Marketplace plans compared to the previous year amid rising premiums and expiring subsidies.

**Message:** Perry Johnson supported ripping away health care from hundreds of thousands of Michiganders to fund tax cuts for the wealthy. 

### Perry Johnson Supported Massive Cuts To Medicare

-   In 2025, Perry Johnson [supported](https://x.com/PJQualityGuru/status/1924547990099722737) Trump’s “One Big Beautiful Bill,” which the Congressional Budget Office estimated would trigger [$500 billion](https://www.washingtonpost.com/business/2025/05/21/medicare-cuts-big-beautiful-bill-republicans-house/) in cuts to Medicare.

**Message:** Perry Johnson supported making massive cuts to health care services seniors rely on. 

### Perry Johnson Supported Trump’s Devastating Tariffs

-   In 2025 and 2026, Perry Johnson supported Trump’s tariffs and said they were “[necessary](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q-bKXgCOJYs)” because the United States had faced “[unfair](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=daicohBD8sY)” global trade relationships for years. While Trump’s tariffs were contributing to [higher food prices](https://www.axios.com/local/portland/2025/09/16/trump-tariffs-grocery-prices-rise-cpi), Perry Johnson [denied](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AFlte1ObTF8) they were raising grocery prices for Americans. As Trump’s tariffs were [hurting Michigan small businesses](https://bridgemi.com/business-watch/trump-tariffs-jolt-michigan-businesses-eye-cuts-shoppers-tighten-belts/), Perry Johnson said he **“**[didn’t know](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bzgt_rrql2o)**”** whether Trump’s tariffs were hurting small businesses, and claimed his multi-billion company was a small business. Trump’s tariffs were dooming Michiganders to a trade war, [driving up prices](https://bridgemi.com/business-watch/trump-tariffs-jolt-michigan-businesses-eye-cuts-shoppers-tighten-belts/), harming [farmers](https://www.thecentersquare.com/michigan/article_c41156e0-439e-4aff-ad5d-7b5dd9442e3d.html) and [families](https://www.newsfromthestates.com/article/whitmer-ordered-report-links-trumps-tariffs-rising-prices-and-slower-growth-across-michigan).
-   In February 2026, after the Supreme Court ruled that Trump [exceeded](https://www.nytimes.com/live/2026/02/20/us/trump-tariffs-supreme-court) his authority with global tariffs, Perry Johnson declared he “loved” Trump and urged Republicans to “[get behind tariffs](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3GxmoUov-NI).” After the Supreme Court rejected Trump’s tariffs, Michigan businesses that paid $3.8 billion under the policy faced [uncertainty](https://www.axios.com/local/detroit/2026/02/24/michigan-tariff-refunds-supreme-court-trump-3-8-billion) over whether they would recover their money.

**Message:** Perry Johnson supported Trump’s disastrous tariffs even though they would crush Michigan small businesses and ensure Michiganders will pay more for groceries.

### Perry Johnson Failed To Make The 2022 GOP Primary Ballot Due To Fraudulent Petition Signatures

-   In 2022, Perry Johnson was [removed](https://medium.com/the-left-is-right/to-the-gop-candidate-who-texted-me-023450473a01) from the Republican gubernatorial primary ballot after the [Michigan Bureau of Elections](https://www.detroitnews.com/story/news/politics/2022/05/23/james-craig-perry-johnson-shouldnt-make-ballot-michigan-gubernatorial-primary-state-bureau-elections/9895349002/) determined that more than [9,000](https://www.detroitnews.com/story/news/politics/2022/04/27/democrats-challenge-petitions-3-gop-governor-candidates/9549372002/) of his petition signatures were [invalid](https://www.detroitnews.com/story/news/politics/2022/05/23/james-craig-perry-johnson-shouldnt-make-ballot-michigan-gubernatorial-primary-state-bureau-elections/9895349002/), leaving him [below](https://www.detroitnews.com/story/news/politics/2022/05/23/james-craig-perry-johnson-shouldnt-make-ballot-michigan-gubernatorial-primary-state-bureau-elections/9895349002/) the 15,000 requirement to [qualify](https://www.michiganpublic.org/politics-government/2022-04-28/democrats-allege-dead-voters-signature-fraud-in-petitions-for-republican-gubernatorial-campaigns). Petition circulators tied to Johnson’s campaign were accused of [forging signatures](https://bridgemi.com/michigan-government/massive-forgery-scheme-claims-rock-michigan-elections-governors-race/), and Democrats accused Johnson of attempting to [cover up](https://bridgemi.com/michigan-government/james-craig-seeks-stay-michigan-governor-race-says-campaign-defrauded/) evidence of the fraud. Perry Johnson had [claimed](https://bridgemi.com/michigan-government/ten-michigan-gop-governor-candidates-file-signatures-make-ballot/) he submitted nearly 23,000 signatures and that more than 15,000 were valid. 
-   Michigan Attorney General Dana Nessel said it was “[incumbent on that candidate to make some level of inquiry](https://www.wxyz.com/news/political/michigans-attorney-general-says-she-expects-to-investigate-petition-fraud)” on whether submitted petition signatures were fraudulent. [The Detroit News Editorial](https://www.detroitnews.com/story/opinion/editorials/2022/05/26/gop-governor-signature-gathering-process-fair/9913916002/) agreed that it “remains on the candidates who decided to submit the signatures.” The Michigan Bureau of Elections and Michigan Board of State Canvassers said they were “confident” the signatures were [fraudulent](https://bridgemi.com/michigan-government/board-denies-craig-johnson-others-spots-michigan-ballot-lawsuits-next/) and said there was “[nothing to suggest that there are valid signatures in there](https://www.detroitnews.com/story/news/politics/2022/05/26/michigan-gop-governor-candidates-canvassers-ballot-eligibility/9926218002/).”
-   Perry Johnson filed a [lawsuit](https://apnews.com/article/2022-midterm-elections-lawsuits-michigan-detroit-gretchen-whitmer-145e2650a0b41c435ca4d1f1e8f1d5b2) asking the [Michigan Court of Appeals](https://bridgemi.com/michigan-government/james-craig-perry-johnson-sue-make-michigan-ballot-amid-forgery-scandal/) to restore his access to the Republican primary ballot, but a three-judge panel [unanimously rejected](https://www.detroitnews.com/story/news/politics/2022/06/01/perry-johnson-denied-spot-ballot-court-appeals-ruling-governor-candidate/7469031001/) his appeal. Perry Johnson appealed to the [Michigan Supreme Court](https://bridgemi.com/michigan-government/james-craig-loses-ballot-access-suit-johnson-appeals-michigan-supreme-court/) in a final attempt to restore his ballot access, but the court [declined to take up his case](https://www.freep.com/story/news/politics/2022/06/03/craig-johnson-lose-appeals-land-spot-primary-ballot/7482112001/?gnt-cfr=1&gca-cat=p&gca-uir=true&gca-epti=z115146e1156xxv115146d--57--b--57--&gca-ft=170&gca-ds=sophi).

**Message:** Perry Johnson could not be trusted as governor after his previous campaign was tied to fraudulent petition signatures.
