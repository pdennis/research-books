# How To Win Against Brad Knott

Brad Knott [voted for the One Big Beautiful Bill Act](https://www.wunc.org/politics/2025-09-19/telephone-town-hall-nc-congressman-brad-knott-guns-immigration-health) — cutting [$1 trillion from Medicaid](https://ncbudget.org/house-republicans-extreme-budget-plan-fails-north-carolina-families-children-and-communities/), nearly [$200 billion from SNAP](https://nciom.org/federal-changes-to-food-assistance-in-north-carolina/), and repealing [clean energy tax credits](https://www.bpr.org/bpr-news/2025-07-25/western-north-carolina-clean-energy-businesses-residents-might-take-a-hit-from-the-one-big-beautiful-bill-act) used by 89,000 North Carolina households — then [celebrated by claiming](https://knott.house.gov/media/press-releases/knott-votes-pass-president-trumps-one-big-beautiful-bill) Congress "removed illegal aliens and able-bodied young people from programs" without mentioning that the cuts threatened [496,000 North Carolinians'](https://ncbudget.org/house-republicans-extreme-budget-plan-fails-north-carolina-families-children-and-communities/) Medicaid coverage and [five rural hospitals](https://governor.nc.gov/news/press-releases/2025/07/02/governor-stein-urges-north-carolina-congressional-delegation-oppose-reconciliation-bill). He [voted against extending ACA subsidies](https://www.northcarolinahealthnews.org/2026/01/19/nc-health-insurance-consumers-left-in-limbo-by-congress/) and [refused to comment](https://www.northcarolinahealthnews.org/2026/01/19/nc-health-insurance-consumers-left-in-limbo-by-congress/), driving a [28.6% average premium spike](https://www.ncdoi.gov/news/press-releases/2025/10/29/aca-health-insurance-rates-released-2026-open-enrollment) for nearly one million North Carolinians. He [voted to protect Trump's tariffs](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/02/11/gop-trump-tariffs-canada.html) while [Johnston County farmers in his district](https://abc11.com/post/tariffs-us-china-nc-farmers-worried-soybeans-become-latest-victim-trade-war/17966147/) described existential anxiety and a John Locke Foundation report warned tariffs could wipe out [$695 million in farm income](https://ncnewsline.com/2026/01/16/tariffs-could-wipe-out-nearly-a-third-of-nc-farm-income-new-report-says/). Before Congress, Knott [voted in five elections from the wrong address](https://www.wral.com/story/a-republican-candidate-voted-from-the-wrong-address-will-it-affect-his-bid-for-congress/21305345/) — a potential Class I felony — and his campaign was launched with [over $700,000](https://www.wunc.org/politics/2023-12-14/brad-knott-republican-super-pac-nc-13th-congressional-district-wiley-nickel-democrat) from his brother's single-candidate super PAC.

### **KNOTT VOTED FOR DEVASTATING HEALTH CARE CUTS**

Knott voted for [Medicaid cuts](https://www.wunc.org/politics/2025-09-19/telephone-town-hall-nc-congressman-brad-knott-guns-immigration-health) that could strip coverage from [496,000 North Carolinians](https://ncbudget.org/house-republicans-extreme-budget-plan-fails-north-carolina-families-children-and-communities/) and the state's Medicaid director said the work requirements would [likely undo North Carolina's Medicaid expansion](https://medicaid.ncdhhs.gov/beneficiaries/impact-hr1-and-federal-changes-medicaid). Governor Stein warned the bill could cause [five hospitals to close](https://governor.nc.gov/news/press-releases/2025/07/02/governor-stein-urges-north-carolina-congressional-delegation-oppose-reconciliation-bill).
He [voted against extending ACA subsidies](https://www.northcarolinahealthnews.org/2026/01/19/nc-health-insurance-consumers-left-in-limbo-by-congress/) and refused to comment, driving premiums up [28.6% on average](https://www.ncdoi.gov/news/press-releases/2025/10/29/aca-health-insurance-rates-released-2026-open-enrollment) — one Charlotte couple saw their premium [quadruple from $7,225 to $31,446 per year](https://ncvoices.com/north-carolina-leaders-warn-aca-premiums-are-soaring-after-tax-credits-expire/).
NC Health News could not find [a single public statement](https://www.northcarolinahealthnews.org/govpack_profiles/brad-knott/) from Knott on Medicare, Medicaid, rural hospital funding, or prescription drug costs.

**Message:** Knott voted to gut health care for half a million North Carolinians, then refused to even talk about it.

### **KNOTT VOTED TO PROTECT TARIFFS THAT ARE DEVASTATING NORTH CAROLINA FARMS**

Knott [voted to protect Trump's 25% tariffs](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/02/11/gop-trump-tariffs-canada.html) while a John Locke Foundation report warned tariffs could wipe out [$695 million in farm income and 8,000 jobs](https://ncnewsline.com/2026/01/16/tariffs-could-wipe-out-nearly-a-third-of-nc-farm-income-new-report-says/). North Carolina soybean exports [collapsed from 72,000 tons to 1,800 tons in two weeks](https://abc11.com/post/tariffs-us-china-nc-farmers-worried-soybeans-become-latest-victim-trade-war/17966147/).
When the News & Observer surveyed the delegation on tariffs, Knott was identified as ["silent on the issue"](https://www.yahoo.com/news/under-dome-tariffs-divide-north-090000163.html). The Yale Budget Lab estimated tariffs cost the median household [$1,400 per year](https://budgetlab.yale.edu/research/state-us-tariffs-november-17-2025), with the burden falling hardest on lower-income families.

**Message:** Knott protected tariffs that are devastating North Carolina farmers and raising prices for families — and won't even talk about it.

### **KNOTT VOTED TO CUT FOOD ASSISTANCE AND RAISE ENERGY COSTS**

Knott voted for nearly [$200 billion in SNAP cuts](https://nciom.org/federal-changes-to-food-assistance-in-north-carolina/) threatening 1.4 million North Carolinians — including 600,000 children — while the bill shifted up to [$420 million in annual costs to the state](https://www.wral.com/news/nccapitol/north-carolina-lawmakers-trumps-snap-medicaid-cuts-2026/). The average SNAP family stood to lose [$234 per month](https://www.americanprogress.org/article/north-carolina-families-cost-of-living-would-increase-under-house-republicans-one-big-beautiful-bill-act/).
He voted to repeal clean energy tax credits used by [89,000 North Carolina households](https://www.bpr.org/bpr-news/2025-07-25/western-north-carolina-clean-energy-businesses-residents-might-take-a-hit-from-the-one-big-beautiful-bill-act), projected to raise average household energy costs by [$215 in 2026](https://www.americanprogress.org/article/north-carolina-families-cost-of-living-would-increase-under-house-republicans-one-big-beautiful-bill-act/).

**Message:** Knott voted to make food more expensive and energy bills higher for North Carolina families.

### **KNOTT VOTED TO KILL CLEAN ENERGY JOBS IN HIS OWN BACKYARD**

His vote is projected to [eliminate over 41,000 North Carolina jobs by 2030](https://energyinnovation.org/report/updated-economic-impacts-of-u-s-senate-passed-one-big-beautiful-bill-act-energy-provisions/). A [$1.4 billion battery factory near Rocky Mount was canceled](https://www.wral.com/story/battery-maker-natron-closes-shop-killing-plans-for-1-000-jobs-in-north-carolina/22144342/), killing 1,000 planned jobs, while North Carolina's clean energy sector was [growing six times faster](https://e2.org/releases/clean-jobs-north-carolina-2025-report/) than the rest of the state economy.
Knott said ["the federal government employs too many people"](https://justfacts.votesmart.org/candidate/key-votes/213703/brad-knott) while DOGE cuts devastated Research Triangle jobs — [RTI International slashed over 400 NC jobs](https://businessnc.com/dialing-back-federal-funding-strikes-the-triangles-acclaimed-research-complex/) and the EPA's RTP campus faced plans to [cut up to 75% of its research workforce](https://www.wral.com/business/epa-cuts-threaten-north-carolina-research-jobs-march-2025/).

**Message:** Knott killed clean energy jobs growing six times faster than the state economy and cheered while DOGE gutted Research Triangle employers.

### **KNOTT VOTED FROM THE WRONG ADDRESS AND WAS BANKROLLED BY HIS BROTHER'S SUPER PAC**

Knott [voted in five general elections](https://www.wral.com/story/a-republican-candidate-voted-from-the-wrong-address-will-it-affect-his-bid-for-congress/21305345/) while registered at his parents' address instead of his own home — a potential [Class I felony](https://www.wral.com/story/a-republican-candidate-voted-from-the-wrong-address-will-it-affect-his-bid-for-congress/21305345/) under North Carolina law. He dismissed it as "a paperwork issue."
His brother donated [over $700,000 to a single-candidate super PAC](https://www.wunc.org/politics/2023-12-14/brad-knott-republican-super-pac-nc-13th-congressional-district-wiley-nickel-democrat) that spent $500,000 on advertising before the primary to launch his campaign.

**Message:** Knott couldn't even follow the voting rules he's supposed to uphold — and his brother's money bought his seat.

### **KNOTT REFUSES TO FACE HIS CONSTITUENTS**

Veterans [protested Knott](https://cardinalpine.com/opinion/op-ed-nc-13s-veterans-deserve-answers-from-congressman-brad-knott/) for refusing to hold in-person town halls and ignoring repeated calls about mass VA firings. He holds only [telephone town halls](https://www.wunc.org/politics/2025-09-19/telephone-town-hall-nc-congressman-brad-knott-guns-immigration-health) where constituents must register in advance.
He accepted over [$84,000 from health care/insurance PACs](https://www.fec.gov/data/committee/C00855361/) and [$192,000 from agribusiness, manufacturing, and energy PACs](https://www.fec.gov/data/committee/C00855361/) — including [$10,000 from sugar industry interests](https://www.fec.gov/data/committee/C00855361/) that benefit from tariff protections raising food prices.

**Message:** Knott won't face his constituents but always has time for the industry lobbyists who fund his campaign.
