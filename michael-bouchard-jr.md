# How To Win Against Michael Bouchard, Jr.

Michael Bouchard Jr. thought he could win the MI-10 Republican nomination on family ties and supporting Trump’s agenda alone. One thing he made clear was that he was eager to be a rubber stamp for Trump’s agenda if elected to Congress, touting his support for Trump’s policies at every turn despite the detrimental impact they had on the 10^th^ district. He said he wanted to “continue” Trump’s economy that was hurting Michiganders’ wallets and crippling the state’s economy, supported Trump’s “peace through strength” approach to foreign policy that Americans openly opposed and caused gas prices to skyrocket. Bouchard was also close friends with a Michigan county prosecutor plagued by sexual harassment investigations, and while Bouchard claimed to be running on his “experience,” he was openly recruited to run by his father (an influential figure in Michigan) and a team of influential Michigan Republicans while he was still deployed overseas.

### Bouchard Was Eager To Back Trump’s Agenda, Which Included Cutting Health Care For Michiganders

-   Bouchard said he wanted to be Trump’s “ally” in congress. Trump was behind legislation that cut health care for [17 million Americans](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2025/07/01/least-17-million-americans-would-lose-insurance-under-trump-plan/), including nearly [12 million Medicaid](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2025/07/01/least-17-million-americans-would-lose-insurance-under-trump-plan/) beneficiaries. As many as [200,000 Michiganders](https://planetdetroit.org/2025/07/medicaid-cuts-threaten-michigan/) were at risk of losing their healthcare if the Medicaid cuts were enacted, including more than [35,000 Michiganders](https://www.jec.senate.gov/public/_cache/files/9cfdee90-dd29-41e8-97da-b5dcdc0317cf/updated-jec-fact-sheet-on-district-level-impacts-of-health-care-cuts.pdf) in the 10^th^ Congressional district.

**Message:** Bouchard wanted to be another vote in Congress to kick Michiganders off their health care all to give billionaires tax breaks. 

### Bouchard supported Trump, who spearheaded a bill that would trigger major cuts to Medicare 

-   Bouchard said he wanted to be Trump’s “ally” in congress. Trump [fought](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2025/may/22/house-vote-trump-tax-bill) for passage of the One Big Beautiful Bill, which would trigger nearly [$500 billion](https://www.cbo.gov/system/files/2025-05/61423-PAYGO.pdf) in Medicare cuts absent future congressional action. [22.4 percent](https://michiganadvance.com/2025/10/16/michigan-ranks-in-the-middle-of-the-pack-for-medicare-performance/) of Michiganders were enrolled in some sort of Medicare plan. 

**Message:** Bouchard wanted to go to congress to support Trump’s agenda, which included a bill that would cut Medicare. 

### Bouchard Supported Trump’s Agenda, Which Included weakening the social security administration and Cutting critical funding for Michigan 

-   Bouchard said he wanted to be Trump’s “ally” in congress. Trump’s “Department of Government Efficiency” (DOGE) weakened the Social Security Administration and cost Michigan hundreds of millions in federal contracts. DOGE cuts caused longer [wait times](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2025/04/07/social-security-website-crashes-musk-trump/), frequent [website crashes](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2025/04/07/social-security-website-crashes-musk-trump/), and [mass layoffs](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2025/03/25/social-security-phones-doge-cuts/) in Social Security offices across the country. Effects from DOGE-era cuts [lingered](https://www.businessinsider.com/why-social-security-offices-temporarily-closed-across-us-2026-4) at Social Security offices across the nation even a year later. DOGE cuts to Michigan agencies left the state [less able to respond](https://www.cbsnews.com/detroit/news/federal-workers-impacted-by-doge-cuts-speak-out-michigan-lawmakers/) to emergencies and pulled funding from an [estimated 196](https://www.mlive.com/politics/2025/06/doge-claims-to-have-saved-630m-in-michigan-some-say-the-numbers-dont-add-up.html) grants from Michigan University and Michigan State University. 

**Message:** Bouchard was willing to back an agenda that weakened services for seniors and cut funding meant to support Michigan universities.
