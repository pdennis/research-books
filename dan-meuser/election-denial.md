# Rep. Dan Meuser (PA-09): Election Denial and January 6th

### Voted To Overturn Pennsylvania's Own Election Results

**Meuser Signed An Amicus Brief Supporting A Texas Lawsuit To Invalidate Pennsylvania's 2020 Presidential Election Votes.** According to a press release from Meuser's office, he signed the amicus brief in December 2020, stating "There continue to be many unanswered questions related to the irregular actions taken in the past election across the Commonwealth." The Supreme Court rejected the case, finding Texas lacked standing. [Meuser.house.gov, [12/11/20](https://meuser.house.gov/media/press-releases/meuser-statement-amicus-brief-urging-supreme-court-review-election-processes)]

**Meuser Voted Against Certifying Pennsylvania's Electoral Votes On January 6, Then Said His Objection Was Never Meant To Overturn The Election.** According to Coal Region Canary, Meuser said "there was little to no chance that my objection to the Pennsylvania electors would overturn the results of the election" and that "my focus was never centered on unproven claims of fraud." He added that "last week's tragic events also did not sway my resolve to highlight the highly irregular actions taken by state officials in Pennsylvania." [Coal Region Canary, [1/14/21](https://coalregioncanary.com/2021/01/14/meuser-pennsylvania-election-investigation-supreme-court/)]

  - **Meuser Voted Against Creating An Independent Commission To Investigate The January 6 Attack And Against Holding Steve Bannon In Contempt Of Congress.** According to NBC News, Meuser voted against both the creation of an independent January 6 commission and holding Steve Bannon in contempt for defying a congressional subpoena. [NBC News, [10/14/21](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/congress/jan-6-committee-threatens-criminal-referrals-after-steve-bannon-snubs-n1281133)]
