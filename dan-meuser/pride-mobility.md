# Rep. Dan Meuser (PA-09): Pride Mobility — Undocumented Workers and Cheated Employees

### Ran A Company Fined For Hiring Undocumented Workers, Then Campaigned On Border Security

**Pride Mobility Was Fined $41,000 For 58 Immigration Violations — The Largest Fine Ever Levied On A Pennsylvania Manufacturing Business.** According to the Times Leader, Pride Mobility was fined $41,000 (reduced to $23,000 on appeal) for hiring undocumented workers in 1995. The Wayne County District Attorney noted the fine was the largest ever levied on a Pennsylvania manufacturing business. Meuser later said records from the case were gone. [Times Leader, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://www.timesleader.com/archive/272762/stories-records-on-fine-for-illegals-gone2c-meuser-says103673)]

**Meuser Still Sits On Pride Mobility's Board While The Company Donates To His Campaigns And Accepts Medicare And Medicaid Payments.** According to a letter to the Williamsport Sun-Gazette, Meuser "worked for his family's multimillion dollar mobility company, Pride Mobility Products" from 1988 to 2008 and "still sits on the company's board today." The letter noted that "Pride Mobility Products accepts government payments in the form of Medicaid and Medicare" and is "a high-level donor to Congressman Meuser's political campaigns." FEC records show Pride Mobility Products Corp and its PAC have contributed over $40,000 to Meuser's campaigns across all cycles. [Sun-Gazette, [3/12/25](https://www.sungazette.com/opinion/letters/2025/03/start-with-dan-meuser/); FEC.gov]

**Former Pride Mobility Employees Won A $2.1 Million Class Action Settlement After The Company Invested Their Retirement Funds Into 'Dismally Performing' Assets.** According to Bloomberg Law, former Pride Mobility workers negotiated a $2.1 million class settlement after alleging the company "invested funds from its employee stock ownership plan into dismally performing Treasury bills and cash equivalents." The settlement, approved June 17, 2025, benefited approximately 1,500 former employees. [Bloomberg Law, [6/17/25](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/employee-benefits/pride-mobility-stock-plan-cash-lawsuit-settles-for-2-1-million)]
