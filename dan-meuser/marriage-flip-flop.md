# Rep. Dan Meuser (PA-09): Same-Sex Marriage Flip-Flop

### Voted For Same-Sex Marriage, Then Flipped His Vote Three Months Later

**Meuser Was One Of Only 10 House Republicans To Vote For The Respect For Marriage Act In July 2022, Then Vote Against Final Passage In December 2022.** According to LebTown, Meuser said his July vote was "to affirm the current law and support the rights and freedoms of all Americans" and that "this vote was not difficult for me, as it does not infringe upon anyone's personal and religious rights." Three months later, he voted no on the Senate-amended version, claiming "the Senate version, unlike the original House version, includes language that puts religious freedoms in jeopardy and opens up organizations to civil suits." [LebTown, [7/25/22](https://lebtown.com/2022/07/25/meuser-says-yes-vote-for-respect-for-marriage-act-affirmed-law-upheld-rights/); Hill, [12/8/22](https://thehill.com/homenews/house/3768072-these-10-house-republicans-flipped-their-votes-on-the-same-sex-marriage-bill/)]
