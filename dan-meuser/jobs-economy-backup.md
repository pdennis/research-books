# Rep. Dan Meuser (PA-09): Jobs and the Economy

Rep. Dan Meuser talks about jobs and the economy but has voted against every major bipartisan investment in American workers and manufacturing — then championed tariff policies that are destroying the very jobs he promised to protect. As a member of the Problem Solvers Caucus, Meuser backed a bipartisan infrastructure framework, then voted against the $1.2 trillion Infrastructure Investment and Jobs Act that delivered $14.8 billion to Pennsylvania for roads, bridges, and broadband. He voted against the CHIPS and Science Act, which has since brought $172 million in semiconductor manufacturing investment and over 800 new jobs to the Lehigh Valley alone. Instead, Meuser championed tariffs he called the start of a "golden era in manufacturing" — but since those tariffs took effect, the U.S. has lost over 40,000 manufacturing jobs, including up to 350 at a single Mack Trucks plant in Pennsylvania. Before Congress, Meuser ran Pride Mobility Products, where workers had to sue to recover $2.1 million in retirement savings after the company invested their ESOP funds into "dismally performing" assets — and as Secretary of Revenue, he oversaw a botched $130 million computer modernization that wasted over $100 million in taxpayer money before the state cut ties with the contractor.

### Backed Bipartisan Infrastructure, Then Voted Against It

**Meuser Backed A Bipartisan Infrastructure Framework Through The Problem Solvers Caucus, Then Voted Against The Infrastructure Investment And Jobs Act.** According to The Hill, Meuser was listed among Problem Solvers Caucus members who said they would vote against the infrastructure measure. The $1.2 trillion bipartisan infrastructure bill passed the House 228-206 on November 5, 2021, with 13 Republicans voting yes — Meuser was not among them. He had previously endorsed the Problem Solvers Caucus's bipartisan infrastructure framework, which called for "leveraging private capital to revitalize communities and rebuild roads, bridges, and highways." [Hill, [11/5/21](https://thehill.com/homenews/house/573409-whip-list-how-house-democrats-say-theyll-vote-on-infrastructure-bill/)]

  - **The Law Meuser Voted Against Delivered $14.8 Billion To Pennsylvania For Roads, Bridges, Broadband, And Clean Energy Projects.** According to Keystone Newsroom, the Bipartisan Infrastructure Law and Inflation Reduction Act "provided funding for $14.8 billion in public infrastructure and clean energy projects throughout the commonwealth," including $11.3 billion for highway programs, $1.6 billion for bridge repairs, $1.16 billion for broadband expansion, and $2.8 billion for public transportation. [Keystone Newsroom, [2/12/24](https://keystonenewsroom.com/2024/02/12/heres-how-pennsylvanias-infrastructure-has-benefited-from-some-major-federal-investments/)]

  - **Infrastructure Law Funded Specific Projects In Meuser's District, Including A $4.2 Million Bridge Replacement In Schuylkill Haven And $6.1 Million In Highway Resurfacing In Schuylkill County.** According to the Reading Eagle, PennDOT's District 5 expected approximately $500 million worth of projects including resurfacing Route 61 at an estimated $6.1 million, resurfacing Route 209 at $2.1 million, and replacing the Route 443/Columbia Street Bridge over the Schuylkill River in Schuylkill Haven at $4.2 million. [Reading Eagle, [4/18/22](https://www.readingeagle.com/2022/04/18/penndot-u-s-sen-bob-casey-tout-infrastructure-law-funding/)]

### Voted Against The CHIPS Act, Which Brought Semiconductor Manufacturing To Pennsylvania

**Meuser Voted Against The CHIPS And Science Act Despite Having Called On Congress To Fund It As A Problem Solvers Caucus Member.** According to GovTrack, Meuser voted Nay on the CHIPS and Science Act, which passed the House 243-187 on July 28, 2022. The Problem Solvers Caucus, of which Meuser was a member, had previously issued a bipartisan statement calling on Congress to "fully fund the CHIPS for America Act" to "boost American semiconductor production and secure supply chain." [GovTrack, [7/28/22](https://www.govtrack.us/congress/votes/117-2022/h404); Problem Solvers Caucus, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://problemsolverscaucus.house.gov/media/press-releases/problem-solvers-caucus-fully-fund-chips-act)]

  - **The CHIPS Act Has Since Delivered $172 Million To Pennsylvania And Over 800 Manufacturing Jobs At Two Lehigh Valley Companies Alone.** According to the Pennsylvania Department of Community and Economic Development, Coherent received up to $79 million to expand its Easton facility, creating 320 manufacturing jobs and 40 construction jobs, while Infinera received $93 million for a new semiconductor facility in Bethlehem, creating approximately 500 manufacturing jobs and 1,200 construction jobs. [PA DCED, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://dced.pa.gov/newsroom/leading-the-way-in-innovation-shapiro-administration-celebrates-federal-investment-of-up-to-172-million-in-two-pennsylvania-technology-companies/)]

### Championed Tariffs That Are Destroying Manufacturing Jobs

**Meuser Praised Trump's Tariffs As The Start Of A 'Golden Era In Manufacturing' And Claimed They Would 'Bring Down Inflation.'** According to Newsmax, Meuser said "The more 'made in the USA', the more supply we create, that will match demand, which will bring down inflation." He called it a "golden era in manufacturing." [Newsmax, [4/3/25](https://www.newsmax.com/politics/dan-meuser-tariffs-trump/2025/04/03/id/1205525/)]

  - **Meuser Said Markets 'Needed A Reckoning' As Tariffs Drove Up Consumer Prices.** According to WVIA, Meuser told Newsmax that "We needed a reckoning. We needed a correction, and President Trump is bringing it." [WVIA, [4/3/25](https://www.wvia.org/news/local/2025-04-03/area-congressmen-offer-support-for-president-trumps-trade-tariffs)]

**Since Tariffs Took Effect, The U.S. Has Lost 72,000 Manufacturing Jobs — The Opposite Of What Meuser Promised.** According to CBS News, the manufacturing sector has shed jobs every month since tariffs were imposed in April 2025, with 42,000 positions lost through August. Employers shed 12,000 manufacturing jobs in August 2025 alone, and the sector lost 33,000 jobs total in 2025. [CBS News, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/jobs-manufacturing-trump-tariffs-economy/)]

**Mack Trucks Laid Off Up To 350 Workers At Its Lehigh Valley Plant, Blaming Tariffs Meuser Championed.** According to CBS Philadelphia, Mack Trucks announced it would lay off between 250 and 350 workers at its plant in Lower Macungie, approximately 10% of its workforce, citing "the impact of tariffs and other economic factors." Governor Shapiro called the layoffs "deeply troubling" and "another example of how tariffs are negatively impacting Pennsylvania." [CBS Philadelphia, [4/17/25](https://www.cbsnews.com/philadelphia/news/mack-trucks-layoffs-lehigh-valley-pennsylvania/)]

**Yale Budget Lab Found Tariffs Cost The Average American Household $1,700 Per Year, With Low-Income Families Hit Three Times Harder Than The Wealthy.** According to the Yale Budget Lab, "The price level rises by 1.2% in the short run, representing a loss of $1,700 for the average household and $900 for households at the bottom of the income distribution." The burden on the poorest ten percent was 2.4% of their income, compared to just 0.8% for the wealthiest. [Yale Budget Lab, [11/17/25](https://budgetlab.yale.edu/research/state-us-tariffs-november-17-2025)]

**Despite Meuser's Promise That Tariffs Would Lower Prices, He Later Acknowledged Grocery Costs Were Still Rising.** According to Newsmax, Meuser said in December 2025 that "We all go to the grocery store. People see it. There's no denying that" — acknowledging persistent price increases eight months after he predicted tariffs would "bring down inflation." [Newsmax, [12/8/25](https://www.newsmax.com/politics/danmeuser-economy-affordability/2025/12/08/id/1237493/)]

### Ran A Company That Cheated Workers Out Of Their Retirement Savings

**Former Pride Mobility Employees Won A $2.1 Million Class Action Settlement After The Company Invested Their Retirement Funds Into 'Dismally Performing' Assets.** According to Bloomberg Law, former Pride Mobility workers negotiated a $2.1 million class settlement after alleging the company "invested funds from its employee stock ownership plan into dismally performing Treasury bills and cash equivalents." The settlement, approved June 17, 2025, benefited approximately 1,500 former employees. [Bloomberg Law, [6/17/25](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/employee-benefits/pride-mobility-stock-plan-cash-lawsuit-settles-for-2-1-million)]

**Pride Mobility Was Fined For Hiring Undocumented Workers — The Largest Immigration Fine In Pennsylvania Manufacturing History — While Meuser Now Campaigns On Border Security.** According to the Times Leader, Pride Mobility was fined $41,000 for 58 immigration violations in 1995, the largest fine ever levied on a Pennsylvania manufacturing business. Meuser later said records from the case were gone. [Times Leader, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://www.timesleader.com/archive/272762/stories-records-on-fine-for-illegals-gone2c-meuser-says103673)]

### Wasted Over $100 Million In Taxpayer Money As Secretary Of Revenue

**As Secretary Of Revenue, Meuser Oversaw A $130 Million Computer Modernization Contract That Failed After The Contractor Spent Over $100 Million And Delivered Only A Partial Upgrade.** According to reporting cited during the 2018 campaign, Meuser hired Accenture for a $130 million computer modernization project despite the firm having been previously fired by Maryland for wasteful spending on a similar project. After spending over $100 million, Accenture only managed to upgrade the state's business tax system before the state cut ties in 2015. A replacement firm had to be hired at an additional $57 million cost to taxpayers. [On the Edge of the Precipice, [4/19/18](https://jmrodkey.wordpress.com/2018/04/19/who-is-dan-meuser-really/)]

**Meuser Also Pushed An Unconstitutional Lottery Privatization Scheme That Cost Taxpayers $4.6 Million Before Being Abandoned.** According to The Washington Post, Governor Corbett dropped the lottery privatization plan Meuser championed after the original $725,000 implementation cost ballooned to $4.6 million. Pennsylvania's Attorney General had previously declared the contract with a foreign company unconstitutional. [Washington Post, [1/2/14](https://www.washingtonpost.com/blogs/govbeat/wp/2014/01/02/pennsylvanias-corbett-will-drop-lottery-privatization-plan/)]

### Voted To Cut Food Assistance While Grocery Prices Soared

**Meuser Voted For The One Big Beautiful Bill, Which Will Strip SNAP Benefits From 8,185 People In His District While Pennsylvania Had The Nation's Highest Grocery Price Increases.** According to the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania, 8,185 people in Meuser's 9th Congressional District will lose SNAP benefits under the bill. Meanwhile, according to Supermarket News, Pennsylvania led the nation with an 8.2% year-over-year grocery price increase, with rural areas — the core of Meuser's district — paying 7.6% more compared to 5.6% for urban consumers. [Commonwealth of Pennsylvania, [7/2/25](https://www.pa.gov/governor/newsroom/2025-press-releases/fact-sheet--how-many-lose-medicaid-and-snap-as-a-result-of-recon); Supermarket News, [12/14/23](https://www.supermarketnews.com/finance/pennsylvania-has-nation-s-highest-grocery-price-hikes-report)]

  - **Meuser Dismissed Projections That His Constituents Would Lose Benefits As 'Complete, Utter Nonsense.'** According to ABC27, Meuser called Governor Shapiro's projections "complete, utter nonsense," arguing "It's not taking into account there are illegals on the rolls, there are those that are ineligible." [ABC27, [7/3/25](https://www.abc27.com/pennsylvania-politics/meuser-fires-back-at-shapiro-after-medicare-snap-callout/)]

### Impact on Everyday People

**Mack Trucks Workers In The Lehigh Valley Faced Layoffs Weeks After Meuser Called Tariffs A 'Golden Era' For Manufacturing.** According to the Lehigh Valley News, 250 to 350 employees at the Mack Trucks assembly plant in Lower Macungie were notified of layoffs on April 17, 2025 — just two weeks after Meuser's April 3 appearance on Newsmax celebrating tariffs. Local 677 of the United Auto Workers represents 2,300 of the plant's workers. [Lehigh Valley News, [4/17/25](https://www.lehighvalleynews.com/business/mack-trucks-to-lay-off-hundreds-of-lehigh-valley-workers-state-representative-says)]

**Rural Pennsylvanians In Meuser's District Paid The Highest Grocery Prices In The Nation, With Fewer Stores And Higher Supply Chain Costs.** According to Supermarket News, Pennsylvania led the nation with an 8.2% year-over-year grocery price increase, with rural areas experiencing 7.6% inflation compared to 5.6% in large cities — a gap attributed to "a lack of competition — there are far less grocery stores in rural areas than there are in metropolitan areas." [Supermarket News, [12/14/23](https://www.supermarketnews.com/finance/pennsylvania-has-nation-s-highest-grocery-price-hikes-report)]

**Feeding America Estimated The Law Meuser Voted For Could Take 6 Billion Meals Off The Table For SNAP Participants Every Year.** According to Feeding America Action, the budget reconciliation legislation Meuser supported "could take nearly 6 billion meals off the table for SNAP participants every year." The law cuts nearly $200 billion from SNAP over ten years. [Feeding America Action, [12/16/25](https://feedingamericaaction.org/wp-content/uploads/FAQ_OBBBA.pdf)]
