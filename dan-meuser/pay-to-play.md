# Rep. Dan Meuser (PA-09): Pay-to-Play Cabinet Appointment

### Bought A Cabinet Appointment, Then Oversaw Millions In Taxpayer Waste

**Meuser Donated Nearly $60,000 To Tom Corbett's Campaign Before Being Appointed To A $135,000-Per-Year Cabinet Post As Secretary Of Revenue.** According to the Daily Item, Meuser, "a former Republican candidate in the 10th Congressional District who donated nearly $60,000 to Tom Corbett's campaign, was named to a Cabinet post" as Secretary of Revenue at "$135,000 a year." [Daily Item, [1/18/11](https://www.dailyitem.com/news/135g-job-tails-60g-donation/article_3774b253-e87e-5c83-98a9-8be101ad81f0.html)]

  - **Meuser Was Part Of A Corbett Administration Where Nearly $9 Million Was Contributed By Transition Team Members And Their Employers.** According to PoliticsPA, 70% of Corbett's privatization panel members were Corbett donors, part of a broader pattern of pay-to-play appointments across the administration. [PoliticsPA, [5/7/12](https://www.politicspa.com/early-returns-70-of-privatization-panel-are-corbett-donors/28301/comment-page-1/)]

**As Secretary Of Revenue, Meuser Pushed An Unconstitutional Lottery Privatization Scheme That Pennsylvania's Attorney General Rejected.** According to the Levittown Patch, Attorney General Kathleen Kane declared the lottery privatization contract with Camelot Global Services unconstitutional in February 2013, citing a lack of statutory authorization and concluding that Camelot's management fees constituted an unconstitutional waiver of the state's sovereign immunity. [Levittown Patch, [2/5/13](https://patch.com/pennsylvania/levittown/pa-s-private-lottery-contract-unconstitutional-ag-says)]

  - **The Lottery Privatization Effort Cost Taxpayers $4.6 Million Before Being Abandoned Entirely.** According to The Washington Post, Governor Corbett dropped the lottery privatization plan in January 2014 after the original $725,000 implementation cost ballooned to $4.6 million. [Washington Post, [1/2/14](https://www.washingtonpost.com/blogs/govbeat/wp/2014/01/02/pennsylvanias-corbett-will-drop-lottery-privatization-plan/)]

**As Secretary Of Revenue, Meuser Oversaw A $130 Million Computer Modernization Contract That Failed After The Contractor Spent Over $100 Million And Delivered Only A Partial Upgrade.** According to reporting cited during the 2018 campaign, Meuser hired Accenture for a $130 million computer modernization project despite the firm having been previously fired by Maryland for wasteful spending on a similar project. After spending over $100 million, Accenture only managed to upgrade the state's business tax system before the state cut ties in 2015. A replacement firm had to be hired at an additional $57 million cost to taxpayers. [On the Edge of the Precipice, [4/19/18](https://jmrodkey.wordpress.com/2018/04/19/who-is-dan-meuser-really/)]
