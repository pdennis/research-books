# Rep. Dan Meuser (PA-09): Serial STOCK Act Violations

### Serial STOCK Act Violator — Hid Lucrative Trades Twice While Chairing Oversight Subcommittee

**Meuser's Family Made Up To $600,000 In Stock Purchases During The March 2020 COVID Market Crash — When Congress Was Receiving Classified Pandemic Briefings.** According to LegiStorm, between March 26 and March 31, 2020, Meuser's dependent children purchased between $107,023 and $555,000 worth of stock in Visa, Walt Disney, Microsoft, and Alphabet, while his wife purchased between $3,003 and $45,000 in Visa, Microsoft, and Alphabet stock. [LegiStorm, [8/18/21](https://www.legistorm.com/pro_news/2816/rep-meuser-didnt-report-lucrative-stock-buys-on-time.html)]

**Meuser Did Not Disclose The Trades Until August 2021 — More Than A Year After The STOCK Act's 45-Day Deadline — And Paid Only A $200 Fine.** According to the Republican Herald, Meuser called the failure to report "simply human error" and blamed his broker, stating "the mistake was made solely by my brokerage." He paid a $200 late filing fee. Alphabet stock alone appreciated over 150% since the March 2020 purchase, potentially netting the family "as much as $150,000 or more," according to LegiStorm. [Republican Herald, [8/18/21](https://www.republicanherald.com/2021/08/18/meuser-calls-failure-to-report-familys-purchase-of-stock-on-time-human-error/)]

**Meuser Violated The STOCK Act A Second Time In 2025, Failing To Disclose A $750,000-To-$1.5 Million NVIDIA Stock Sale For Nearly 10 Months.** According to NOTUS, Meuser disclosed the sale of NVIDIA stock — transacted on October 1, 2024 — on August 13, 2025, nearly 10 months after the STOCK Act's 45-day deadline. Meuser told NOTUS "The mistake was made solely by my brokerage and benefited me in no way" and "I am very unhappy that this preventable error occurred." [NOTUS, [8/13/25](https://www.notus.org/money/stock-act-violations-blame-financial-adviser)]

  - **Meuser Chairs The House Financial Services Subcommittee On Oversight And Investigations — The Subcommittee Responsible For Financial Oversight — While Being A Repeat STOCK Act Violator.** According to Benzinga, Meuser disclosed the NVIDIA sale while serving as chairman of the subcommittee tasked with oversight of the financial system. [Benzinga, [8/13/25](https://www.benzinga.com/insights/government/25/08/47126374/pennsylvania-rep-daniel-meuser-sold-up-to-1-50m-worth-of-nvidia-stock)]

Nearly half of Meuser's 2024 fundraising came from corporate PACs ($839K), including $95K from financial services firms he is supposed to oversee. [FEC.gov]
