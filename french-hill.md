# How To Win Against French Hill

French Hill is a millionaire former banker who now [chairs the committee that regulates the banking industry](https://arktimes.com/arkansas-blog/2018/10/14/french-hill-high-on-the-wall-street-flunky-list) and his record reflects it. He voted for up to [\$1 trillion in Medicaid cuts](https://arktimes.com/arkansas-blog/2025/07/03/arkansass-congressmen-all-vote-to-slash-their-constituents-health-insurance), then [told constituents he had "never voted to end Medicare or Medicaid"](https://www.arkdems.org/arkansas-democrats-slam-trump-and-republicans-attacks-on-health-care-call-out-rep-french-hill-for-lies-about-medicaid/). He [told a reporter he opposes across-the-board tariffs](https://katv.com/news/local/four-candidates-one-seat-french-hill-discusses-kitchen-table-issues) the day after he [voted to keep Trump's Canada tariffs in place](https://www.reuters.com/world/us/us-house-backs-bid-end-canada-tariffs-rare-rebuke-trump-2026-02-11/), the same tariffs that could [close 1 in 3 Arkansas farms](https://www.npr.org/2025/09/11/nx-s1-5535575/many-arkansas-farmers-seek-federal-support-as-agriculture-crisis-grows). He voted for [\$186 billion in SNAP cuts](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/08/08/trumps-spending-bill-cuts-billions-in-snap-benefits.html) in the state the USDA ranks [dead last for food insecurity](https://www.kait8.com/2026/01/09/arkansas-tops-nation-food-insecurity-usda-study-shows/).

 Voted against [letting Medicare negotiate drug prices](https://hill.house.gov/news/documentsingle.aspx?DocumentID=6502), and championed Medicaid work requirements that even before the national bill was put in place, had [stripped 18,000 Arkansans of their coverage](https://www.aradvocates.org/wp-content/uploads/AACF.ArkWorks2B.webfinal.3.26.19.pdf) on the state level. 

With a [\$20.4 million net worth](https://www.quiverquant.com/news/Fundraising+Update%3A+Representative+J.+French+Hill+just+disclosed+%24554.1K+of+new+fundraising), [only 0.73% of his donations from small donors](https://dccc.org/case-congressman-french-hill/), and [\$839,595 from Wall Street in the last cycle alone](https://www.opensecrets.org/members-of-congress/french-hill/summary?cid=N00035792), Hill's defining vulnerability is the gap between what he says and what he does.

### **HEALTH CARE: VOTED TO GUT MEDICAID, THEN LIED ABOUT IT**

Hill voted for the ["One Big Beautiful Bill"](https://arktimes.com/arkansas-blog/2025/07/03/arkansass-congressmen-all-vote-to-slash-their-constituents-health-insurance) that cuts up to \$1 trillion from Medicaid, potentially affecting [821,000 Arkansans](https://www.arkdems.org/arkansas-democrats-slam-trump-and-republicans-attacks-on-health-care-call-out-rep-french-hill-for-lies-about-medicaid/), then sent constituents a letter claiming he had ["never voted to end Medicare or Medicaid."](https://www.arkdems.org/arkansas-democrats-slam-trump-and-republicans-attacks-on-health-care-call-out-rep-french-hill-for-lies-about-medicaid/)

He voted against [allowing Medicare to negotiate prescription drug prices](https://hill.house.gov/news/documentsingle.aspx?DocumentID=6502) and let [ACA premium subsidies expire](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/congress/senate-rejects-aca-funding-republican-alternative-premiums-set-spike-rcna248497), doubling costs for 22 million Americans, while taking [\$141,500 from insurance PACs](https://www.opensecrets.org/members-of-congress/summary?cycle=2016&cid=N00035792&type=I) and [\$13,500 from pharma](https://kffhealthnews.org/news/campaign/).
[30 of Arkansas's 47 rural hospitals](https://arkansasonline.com/news/2025/jul/13/report-finds-30-of-arkansas-47-rural-hospitals-at) are at risk of closing, and his Medicaid work requirements already [stripped 18,000 Arkansans of coverage](https://www.aradvocates.org/wp-content/uploads/AACF.ArkWorks2B.webfinal.3.26.19.pdf).

**Message:** French Hill voted to gut Medicaid in a state where rural hospitals are already closing -- then lied to his constituents about it.

### **AFFORDABILITY: TARIFFS AND FOOD CUTS IN A STATE THAT CAN'T AFFORD THEM**

Hill [told a reporter he opposes across-the-board tariffs](https://katv.com/news/local/four-candidates-one-seat-french-hill-discusses-kitchen-table-issues) the day after he [voted to keep Trump's Canada tariffs](https://www.reuters.com/world/us/us-house-backs-bid-end-canada-tariffs-rare-rebuke-trump-2026-02-11/). He previously [called tariffs a "blunt instrument" he "regretted"](https://hill.house.gov/news/documentsingle.aspx?DocumentID=1553) in 2018.
The tariffs he backed have driven [Arkansas soybean exports to China down 43.7%](https://arkansasadvocate.com/2025/07/01/arkansans-paying-millions-more-in-tariff-costs-compared-to-2024/), tripled [Arkansas vehicle parts tariff costs from \$748K to \$3.5M](https://arkansasadvocate.com/2025/07/01/arkansans-paying-millions-more-in-tariff-costs-compared-to-2024/), and threatened to [close 1 in 3 farms](https://www.npr.org/2025/09/11/nx-s1-5535575/many-arkansas-farmers-seek-federal-support-as-agriculture-crisis-grows). Even [Walmart warned it cannot absorb tariff costs](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/politics/walmart-will-raise-prices-due-to-tariff-costs).
He voted for [\$186 billion in SNAP cuts](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/08/08/trumps-spending-bill-cuts-billions-in-snap-benefits.html) in the state with the [worst food insecurity in America](https://www.kait8.com/2026/01/09/arkansas-tops-nation-food-insecurity-usda-study-shows/), where [25% of children are food insecure](https://arkansasadvocate.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/SNAP-report.webfinal.1.29.25.pdf).

**Message:** French Hill says one thing on tariffs and does another, while voting to cut food assistance in the hungriest state in America.

### **JOBS AND THE ECONOMY: BROKEN PROMISES FROM A MILLIONAIRE BANKER**

Hill [promised the 2017 tax cuts would create 8,277 Arkansas jobs](https://hill.house.gov/news/documentsingle.aspx?DocumentID=1490) -- but when asked which CEOs planned to hire, [he had no answer](https://arktimes.com/arkansas-blog/2017/12/19/a-question-for-congressman-french-hill). Corporations spent [37 times more on stock buybacks than wage increases](https://patrioticmillionaires.org/perspectives/corporate-tax-cuts-help-no-one-but-the-wealthy/).
He voted against raising the minimum wage [three times](https://arktimes.com/arkansas-blog/2014/11/05/french-hill-voted-against-minimum-wage-increase) -- Arkansas voters [overruled him twice](https://www.arkansasonline.com/news/2018/oct/02/3-congressmen-oppose-measure-to-boost-s/). He voted against the [bipartisan infrastructure bill](https://www.arkansasonline.com/news/2021/nov/22/spending-spree/), the [PRO Act](https://hill.house.gov/news/documentsingle.aspx?DocumentID=8325), and the [American Rescue Plan](https://hill.house.gov/news/documentsingle.aspx?DocumentID=8309).
His tariff vote helped drive a [surge in Arkansas farm bankruptcies](https://www.uaex.uada.edu/media-resources/news/2025/july/07-07-2025-ark-farm-bankruptcies.aspx), while [Amazon closed its Little Rock fulfillment center](https://www.arkansasonline.com/news/2025/nov/21/amazons-little-rock-jobs-cuts-a-challenge-for/) affecting 1,850 jobs.

**Message:** French Hill promised tax cuts would create thousands of Arkansas jobs. They didn't -- and he's voted against every bill that actually would.

### **WALL STREET'S CONGRESSMAN: A BANKER REGULATING BANKS**

Hill was named to a ["Banker's Dozen" list of top Wall Street allies](https://arktimes.com/arkansas-blog/2018/10/14/french-hill-high-on-the-wall-street-flunky-list), and took [\$839,595 from securities and investment firms](https://www.opensecrets.org/members-of-congress/french-hill/summary?cid=N00035792) in the 2024 cycle alone.
He is now [pushing to eliminate a \$5 cap on bank overdraft fees](https://arktimes.com/arkansas-blog/2025/02/19/banker-french-hill-clears-path-for-bankers-to-charge-you-more) and [collected \$180,500 from the crypto industry](https://www.washingtonexaminer.com/policy/finance-and-economy/3472672/campaign-cash-buys-big-crypto-allies-on-capitol-hill/) while shepherding its deregulation bills through his committee.
He [oversaw CARES Act loans while owning up to \$5.25 million in Simmons Bank stock](https://arktimes.com/arkansas-blog/2020/07/20/french-hill-fox-in-the-coronavirus-henhouse).

**Message:** French Hill is a banker chairing the committee that regulates banks -- and his donors, not his constituents, come first.

### **SAYS ONE THING, DOES ANOTHER**

On Medicaid: Voted for up to [\$1 trillion in cuts](https://arktimes.com/arkansas-blog/2025/07/03/arkansass-congressmen-all-vote-to-slash-their-constituents-health-insurance), then [told constituents he "never voted to end Medicare or Medicaid"](https://www.arkdems.org/arkansas-democrats-slam-trump-and-republicans-attacks-on-health-care-call-out-rep-french-hill-for-lies-about-medicaid/).
On tariffs: [Called them a "blunt instrument" he "regretted"](https://hill.house.gov/news/documentsingle.aspx?DocumentID=1553), then [voted to keep them in place](https://www.reuters.com/world/us/us-house-backs-bid-end-canada-tariffs-rare-rebuke-trump-2026-02-11/).
On SNAP: [Said he wanted to "strengthen" safety net programs](https://katv.com/news/local/congressman-hill-addresses-snap-thefts-medicaid-work-requirements-and-big-beautiful-bill), then [voted for the largest SNAP cuts in a generation](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/08/08/trumps-spending-bill-cuts-billions-in-snap-benefits.html).

**Message:** On every issue that matters, French Hill says one thing in Arkansas and does the opposite in Washington.
