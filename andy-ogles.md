# How To Win Against Andy Ogles

Andy Ogles [fabricated his resume](https://www.newsweek.com/read-andrew-ogles-college-transcripts-confirm-lies-resume-1784176) — claiming to be an economist after earning a C in his only economics course — and is now the subject of an [active FBI criminal investigation](https://www.washingtonpost.com/national-security/2024/08/06/andy-ogles-fbi-phone-search-campaign-finance/) after reporting a [\$320,000 campaign loan that was actually \$20,000](https://www.axios.com/local/nashville/2024/05/23/congress-tennessee-ogles-amends-campaign-finance-reports), triggering a [unanimous 6-0 ethics referral](https://tennesseelookout.com/2025/01/02/u-s-house-ethics-board-calls-for-more-investigation-of-tennessee-5th-district-congressman/) for a full House investigation. Before Congress, he [led the Americans for Prosperity campaign that killed Medicaid expansion](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/elections/rebuke-tennessee-governor-koch-group-shows-its-power-n301031) for over 300,000 Tennesseans. He [raised nearly \$25,000](https://www.newschannel5.com/news/newschannel-5-investigates/what-did-congressman-andy-ogles-do-with-nearly-25-000-meant-for-child-burial-garden-he-wont-say) using a photo of his stillborn baby for a children's burial garden that was never built — and has never accounted for the money. He now lives in a [\$3.7 million Capitol Hill townhouse](https://www.propublica.org/article/roommates-steve-berger-mike-johnson-andy-ogles-right-wing-influence-townhouse) owned by his top donor, whose business interests align with Ogles's legislative agenda.

In Congress, his first bill sought to [repeal the \$35 insulin cap for seniors](https://ogles.house.gov/media/press-releases/rep-ogles-introduces-first-bill-inflation-reduction-act-2023-repeal-democrats), he publicly declared he wants to ["be the guy that kills" the ACA](https://www.wkrn.com/news/tennessee-news/it-puts-your-back-against-the-wall-aca-subsidy-deadline-looms-for-tennessee-families/), and he called Medicaid recipients ["losers in their mama's basement"](https://dccc.org/losers-in-their-mamas-basement-this-is-what-andy-ogles-thinks-of-his-constituents-he-just-voted-to-kick-off-medicaid/) the morning he voted for a bill that Nashville's mayor estimated would strip [62,000 of his own constituents](https://nashvillebanner.com/2025/06/09/ogles-criticism-impact-nashville/) of medical coverage. He voted to preserve tariffs projected to cost Tennessee [\$8 billion](https://www.herenashville.com/tennessee-tariffs-impact/) while Nashville families face a [40% surge in food insecurity](https://www.wsmv.com/2025/10/09/nashville-food-pantry-struggling-handle-40-surge-hungry-families/) and a [\$90,000 homeownership affordability gap](https://www.nashville.gov/sites/default/files/2025-09/UHS-Full-Report.pdf?ct=1759160211).

### **OGLES GUTTED HEALTH CARE FOR HIS OWN CONSTITUENTS**

Ogles [led the AFP campaign that killed Medicaid expansion](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/elections/rebuke-tennessee-governor-koch-group-shows-its-power-n301031) for 300,000+ Tennesseans, his [first bill repealed the \$35 insulin cap](https://ogles.house.gov/media/press-releases/rep-ogles-introduces-first-bill-inflation-reduction-act-2023-repeal-democrats), and he declared he wants to ["be the guy that kills" the ACA](https://www.wkrn.com/news/tennessee-news/it-puts-your-back-against-the-wall-aca-subsidy-deadline-looms-for-tennessee-families/) — which [643,000 Tennesseans](https://sycamoretn.org/aca-marketplace-subsidies/) rely on.
He called Medicaid recipients ["losers in their mama's basement"](https://dccc.org/losers-in-their-mamas-basement-this-is-what-andy-ogles-thinks-of-his-constituents-he-just-voted-to-kick-off-medicaid/) the morning he voted for [Medicaid cuts](https://www.yahoo.com/news/u-rep-ogles-joins-tn-224912125.html) projected to leave nearly 12 million Americans uninsured — [62,000 in his own district](https://nashvillebanner.com/2025/06/09/ogles-criticism-impact-nashville/).
His only sponsored health bills are [abortion restrictions](https://www.congress.gov/member/andrew-ogles/O000175) — zero bills to expand coverage, lower drug costs, or address the uninsured.

**Message:** Ogles has spent his career destroying health care protections his own constituents depend on — and has never proposed a single alternative.
