### Ogles Worked To Gut Tennesseans' Health Care

- Ogles [voted](http://clerk.house.gov/evs/2025/roll190.xml) for House Republicans’ budget bill, which would kick [17 million](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2025/07/01/least-17-million-americans-would-lose-insurance-under-trump-plan/) Americans off their health insurance, including 12 million Americans on Medicaid, all to give tax breaks to billionaires. [More than 30,000 Tennesseans](https://www.jec.senate.gov/public/_cache/files/9cfdee90-dd29-41e8-97da-b5dcdc0317cf/updated-jec-fact-sheet-on-district-level-impacts-of-health-care-cuts.pdf) in Ogles’ district were projected to lose their health coverage as result of his votes. 

- Even before getting elected to congress, Ogles [led the campaign](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/elections/rebuke-tennessee-governor-koch-group-shows-its-power-n301031) that killed Medicaid expansion in Tennessee, keeping access to health coverage away from [476,000 Tennesseans](https://www.aarp.org/states/tennessee/improving-access-to-health-care-for-tennesseans/). 

- Ogles also opposed the Affordable Care Act and said he wanted to be the one to [“kill”](https://www.facebook.com/RepAndyOgles/posts/pfbid02VuWhhq4CShbg82pYzPGbeqAgF4WHRSooev215QsjyVDBR1pR1sRsvey7Z6cGGFDsl) the law. Most recently, he voted against extending enhanced health care tax credits and as a result Tennesseans are facing [dramatic premium increases](https://www.newschannel5.com/news/aca-tax-credits-set-to-expire-leaving-tennessee-families-facing-quadrupled-premiums). 

**Message**: Ogles not only voted to take health care from Tennesseans, he led the charge against expanding Medicaid, which would have allowed more Tennesseans to get access to health insurance.
