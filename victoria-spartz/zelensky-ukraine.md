# Rep. Victoria Spartz (IN-05): Called Zelensky a "Moron" and Said Ukraine Should Cede Territory to Russia

### Called Zelensky A "Moron" And Said Ukraine Should Cede Territory To Russia

**Spartz Said Ukraine Should Cede Land To Russia And Called For Zelensky's Resignation.** According to the Kyiv Independent, Spartz said in an interview with The Telegraph that Ukraine was "not in a position to demand the return of all occupied territories" and that "the people of Ukraine should demand Zelensky's resignation." [Kyiv Independent, [4/7/25](https://kyivindependent.com/uss-only-ukrainian-born-lawmaker-says-ukraine-should-cede-land-to-russia-demand-zelenskys-resignation/)]

**Spartz Called Zelensky And His Administration "Morons And Crooks."** According to the Kyiv Post, Spartz issued an official statement saying, "if you have morons and crooks like Zelensky and his puppet parliament running your country, it's a lost cause." [Kyiv Post, [4/9/25](https://www.kyivpost.com/post/50487)]

- **Ukraine's Foreign Ministry Fired Back: "Unlike Victoria, All Of Our Land Has Always Been And Will Always Remain Ukrainian."** According to the Kyiv Independent, Ukraine's Foreign Ministry responded directly to Spartz's comments, and Verkhovna Rada Speaker Ruslan Stefanchuk accused Spartz of echoing Kremlin narratives. [Kyiv Independent, [4/9/25](https://kyivindependent.com/foreign-ministry-slams-ukrainian-born-us-lawmaker-claim-on-territorial-demands/)]

**Spartz Had Previously Voted Against $61 Billion In Aid To Ukraine Despite Earlier Saying "Only Weapons Can Bring Peace."** According to the Kyiv Independent, Spartz had been more pro-Ukrainian at the onset of Russia's 2022 invasion, voting for multiple bills bolstering Kyiv's defenses and saying "only weapons can bring peace." She then shifted her stance and was among the 112 Republicans who voted against the $61 billion Ukraine aid package in April 2024. [Kyiv Independent, [4/20/24](https://kyivindependent.com/ukrainian-born-republican-congresswoman-spartz-says-she-wont-vote-for-ukraine-aid-bill/)]
