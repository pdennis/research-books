# Rep. Victoria Spartz (IN-05): Tariff Flip-Flop — Criticized Biden's Tariffs, Protected Trump's Bigger Ones

### Criticized Biden's Tariffs Then Protected Trump's Bigger Ones

**Spartz Criticized Biden Administration Lumber Tariffs As Coming At "The Worst Time" For Consumers.** According to WIBC, Spartz opposed the Biden administration's proposal to raise tariffs on Canadian lumber from 9% to 18%, saying, "That is the worst time when we already have an increase in prices." [WIBC, [1/17/22](https://wibc.com/124170/spartz-says-white-house-plans-to-raise-lumber-tariffs-on-canada-are-coming-at-the-worst-time/)]

**Spartz Said Congress Should "Decide Which Tariffs Are Good" — Then Hours Later Voted To Block Congress From Doing Exactly That.** According to The Hill, Spartz told reporters, "I think it's the job of Congress to take this vote and decide which tariffs are good, which tariffs are bad, and that's just we have to take votes." She then reversed and voted for a rule blocking Congress from voting to terminate Trump's 25% tariffs on Canada — far exceeding the Biden-era tariffs she had criticized. [Hill, [2/10/26](https://thehill.com/homenews/house/5732632-gop-rebuke-trump-tariffs/)]

### Impact On Everyday People

**Stellantis Laid Off 900 Workers At Three Plants In Kokomo Due To The Auto Tariffs Spartz Voted To Protect.** According to WISH-TV, Stellantis temporarily laid off 900 workers, including employees at the Indiana Transmission Plant, Kokomo Transmission Plant, and Kokomo Casting Plant, after President Trump's 25% auto tariffs triggered production pauses at assembly plants in Canada and Mexico. [WISH-TV, [4/3/25](https://www.wishtv.com/news/business/stellantis-announces-layoffs-at-kokomo-plant/)]

**A Ball State Economist Said Indiana Is Among The Three Most At-Risk States From The Tariffs Spartz Voted To Protect.** According to the Indianapolis Business Journal, Michael Hicks, a Ball State University economics professor, stated, "If we're not the most at-risk state, we're one of the three most at-risk states because of tariffs." Manufacturing represents 18% of Indiana's workforce and nearly 26% of GDP. [Indianapolis Business Journal, [Viewed 2/16/26](https://www.ibj.com/articles/tariffs-could-reach-deep-into-manufacturing-heavy-indiana)]
