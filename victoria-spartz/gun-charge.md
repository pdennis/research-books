# Rep. Victoria Spartz (IN-05): Charged With Bringing a Gun Through Airport Security

### Charged With Bringing A Gun Through Airport Security

**Spartz Was Charged With A Misdemeanor After TSA Found A Firearm In Her Carry-On At Dulles Airport.** According to CNN, TSA officers detected an unloaded .380-caliber firearm in Spartz's carry-on bag during security screening at Washington Dulles International Airport on June 28, 2024. Spartz was charged with a Class 1 misdemeanor under Virginia law, which can carry a sentence of up to one year in jail and a $2,500 fine. The charge was later dismissed after Spartz completed a gun safety course. [CNN, [7/1/24](https://www.cnn.com/2024/07/01/politics/victoria-spartz-weapons-charge-airport/index.html)]
