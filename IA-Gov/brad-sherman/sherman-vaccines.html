Overview

Brad Sherman campaigned on “medical freedom” and sowed distrust in public health initiatives and vaccines, accusing the “medical establishment” of “tyranny” during the COVID-19 pandemic. Sherman promoted misinformation about the COVID-19 vaccine, and he compared public health efforts during the pandemic to the Holocaust and forced medical experiments in Nazi Germany. He opposed all vaccine requirements, and received the endorsement of the anti-vaccine organization Stand for Health Freedom during his 2022 campaign for the Iowa House. In 2026, Brad Sherman Signed A Pledge With Americans for Health Freedom To Oppose COVID Vaccines.
