Overview

Despite running to be governor of Iowa, as of 2026, Lahn maintained a home in Kansas and frequently flew to Kansas using his private plane. Lahn maintained a $2 million mansion in Wichita, never claimed his Iowa property as his primary residence, and regularly flew from Kansas to campaign events in Iowa. The Des Moines Register found that Lahn’s private plane took 37 trips to Wichita and spent 75 nights in Wichita between October 2025 and May 2026. Lahn said that if he wins the gubernatorial election, he would “be in Iowa as much as humanly possible.” Lahn re-registered to vote in Iowa in October 2024, barely qualifying for Iowa’s residence requirement to run for Iowa governor, after living in Kansas and voting in Kansas elections from 2018 to 2022. In 2024, Lahn’s wife purchased a home near Wichita and declared it her primary residence before selling it to an LLC in 2025. 

Lahn’s 2025 travel records fueled further carpetbagger accusations after he claimed he had lived full time in Iowa since 2023. Strava data showed four Kansas bike rides beginning and ending at Lahn’s family home, in Kechi, Kansas while flight records indicated he may have spent weeks in Kansas around the same period. Lahn denied the report and claimed his “actual account” showed more than 90 percent of his rides were in Iowa, but he did not publicly release that account for review.

Prior to living in Kansas, Lahn also lived in Montana, working for Montana’s Americans for Prosperity chapter and Steve Daines. He also attended college in Colorado and worked for Cory Gardner.
