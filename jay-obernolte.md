# How To Win Against Jay Obernolte

Jay Obernolte voted for the [largest Medicaid cut in the program's 60-year history](https://www.cbo.gov/publication/61510) -- $911 billion -- in a district where [48% of residents depend on Medi-Cal](https://calmatters.org/health/2025/03/medicaid-cuts-republican-districts/), then [claimed "no one is being forced off Medicaid"](https://obernolte.house.gov/Medicaid) despite the CBO projecting 7.5 million will lose coverage. He [voted to shield Trump's tariffs from congressional oversight](https://thedispatch.com/article/congress-house-republicans-tariff-resolutions/), initially voted against the rule, then [flipped under pressure from GOP leadership and refused to explain why](https://www.washingtontimes.com/news/2025/sep/16/house-move-turn-lawmakers-authority-overturn-trump-tariffs-causes/) -- while the Inland Empire [lost 26,000 logistics jobs](https://calmatters.org/economy/2025/05/inland-empire-jobs-loss/) and economists projected the tariffs would cost households [$1,700 per year](https://budgetlab.yale.edu/research/state-us-tariffs-november-17-2025). He voted for the [most severe SNAP cuts in the program's history](https://www.cafoodbanks.org/statement/congress-slashes-snap/), projected to strip CalFresh benefits from [395,000 Californians](https://www.cafoodbanks.org/what-we-do/policy/calfresh-changes-hr1/), then called it a ["generational opportunity"](https://www.bigbeargrizzly.net/news/obernolte-yes-on-one-big-beautiful-bill-iran-and-military-deployment-on-u-s-soil/article_663f4a8d-0adc-484d-8425-901426fcc914.html). His first act in Congress was to [object to the 2020 election results hours after the January 6 attack](https://www.sbsun.com/2021/01/13/san-bernardino-county-democrats-call-for-rep-jay-obernolte-to-resign/), and when 200 constituents [booed him off stage at a town hall](https://www.kvcrnews.org/local-news/2025-02-24/yucca-valley-town-hall-turns-raucous-as-rep-jay-obernolte-defends-doge) in February 2025, he [canceled future in-person events](https://www.sbsun.com/2025/02/28/southern-california-gop-lawmaker-booed-at-town-hall-for-supporting-elon-musk/).

### **HEALTH CARE: VOTED TO GUT MEDI-CAL WHILE HALF HIS DISTRICT DEPENDS ON IT**

Obernolte voted for the [$911 billion Medicaid cut](https://www.cbo.gov/publication/61510) despite [48% of his district](https://calmatters.org/health/2025/03/medicaid-cuts-republican-districts/) relying on Medi-Cal. California stands to lose [$30 billion per year](https://www.kff.org/medicaid/how-will-the-2025-reconciliation-law-affect-the-uninsured-rate-in-each-state/) in federal Medi-Cal funding, and up to 1.7 million Californians could become uninsured.
He voted against the [Inflation Reduction Act's $35 insulin cap and Medicare drug price negotiation](https://www.govtrack.us/congress/votes/117-2022/h420), dismissed ACA subsidies as ["temporary bailouts"](https://www.ocregister.com/2025/12/24/lowering-health-care-costs-starts-with-real-reform-not-temporary-subsidies/) in an op-ed, and took [health industry PAC money](https://www.fec.gov/data/receipts/?committee_id=C00720078&two_year_transaction_period=2024&data_type=processed) including $5,000 from Blue Shield of California.
Constituents in Republican districts said they felt ["betrayed"](https://calmatters.org/health/2025/03/medicaid-cuts-republican-districts/), with one saying "Some people will die without it." The [California Hospital Association warned](https://kffhealthnews.org/news/article/health-costs-spending-affordability-hospitals-california-one-big-beautiful-bill/) of longer ER wait times, reduced access, and higher prices.

**Message:** Jay Obernolte voted for the largest Medicaid cut in history while half his district depends on Medi-Cal -- then claimed no one would lose coverage.

### **AFFORDABILITY: FLIPPED HIS TARIFF VOTE UNDER PRESSURE, THEN REFUSED TO EXPLAIN**

Obernolte [initially voted against blocking tariff oversight](https://thedispatch.com/article/congress-house-republicans-tariff-resolutions/), then flipped to "yes" under pressure from leadership and [refused to explain why](https://www.washingtontimes.com/news/2025/sep/16/house-move-turn-lawmakers-authority-overturn-trump-tariffs-causes/). He later [voted to keep Trump's 25% Canada tariffs](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/congress/house-votes-end-trump-tariffs-canada-rcna258661) in place.
The tariffs hit his district directly: [1 in 5 San Bernardino County jobs](https://iebusinessdaily.com/inland-empire-could-be-particularly-vulnerable-to-high-tariffs/) depend on logistics, and the Inland Empire [lost 26,000 warehouse and transportation jobs](https://calmatters.org/economy/2025/05/inland-empire-jobs-loss/) in six months. The [Inland Empire Economic Partnership CEO warned](https://calmatters.org/economy/2025/03/tariffs-inland-empire-economy/) tariffs will drive up consumer prices in the region.
He voted for the [most severe SNAP cuts in history](https://www.cafoodbanks.org/statement/congress-slashes-snap/), projected to strip benefits from [395,000 Californians](https://www.cafoodbanks.org/what-we-do/policy/calfresh-changes-hr1/) and cut up to $5.1 billion in annual federal food assistance. Food banks [warned they cannot fill the gap](https://www.cafoodbanks.org/statement/congress-slashes-snap/).

**Message:** Jay Obernolte flipped his tariff vote under pressure from party leadership, refused to explain why, and backed policies that cost Inland Empire families $1,700 a year.

### **JOBS AND THE ECONOMY: VOTED AGAINST JOB-CREATING BILLS, FOR BILLS THAT HURT WORKERS**

Obernolte voted against both the [Bipartisan Infrastructure Law](https://www.govtrack.us/congress/votes/117-2021/h369) and the [CHIPS and Science Act](https://www.govtrack.us/congress/votes/117-2022/h404) -- two landmark bills investing in roads, broadband, semiconductors, and research jobs -- despite claiming to champion technology and innovation.
He voted for the OBBBA, which the CBO found will [cut income for the poorest 10% by 3.1% while increasing income for the richest 10% by 2.7%](https://www.medicarerights.org/medicare-watch/2025/08/14/cbo-report-on-distributional-effects-of-the-budget-act-finds-richest-gain-and-poorest-face-cuts). The law delivered [$1 trillion in tax cuts to the top 1%](https://frac.org/blog/snap-cuts-in-obbba-h-r-1-billionaires-win-working-families-lose) while cutting $197 billion from food assistance.
His district has a [12.5% poverty rate](https://indicators.sbcounty.gov/income/overall-poverty/) exceeding state and national averages, median household income that falls [34% short](https://indicators.sbcounty.gov/income/median-household-income-and-cost-of-living/) of what families need, and [5% of employed residents living in poverty](https://indicators.sbcounty.gov/income/overall-poverty/) despite working.

**Message:** Jay Obernolte voted against every bill that creates jobs and for a bill that cuts income for the poorest while giving $1 trillion in tax breaks to the richest.

### **ELECTION DENIAL: OBJECTED TO THE 2020 RESULTS HOURS AFTER JANUARY 6**

Obernolte's first act in Congress was to [object to certifying the 2020 election results](https://www.sbsun.com/2021/01/13/san-bernardino-county-democrats-call-for-rep-jay-obernolte-to-resign/) in both Arizona and Pennsylvania -- hours after the Capitol attack. The San Bernardino County Democratic Party called on him to resign.
He voted against [impeaching Trump](https://accountability.gop/profile/rep-jay-obernolte/) for inciting the attack, against establishing a [January 6 commission](https://accountability.gop/profile/rep-jay-obernolte/), and against holding Steve Bannon in contempt. Republican Accountability gave him an ["F" grade](https://accountability.gop/profile/rep-jay-obernolte/) for "repeatedly failing to defend our democracy."

**Message:** Jay Obernolte's first act in Congress was to object to the 2020 election results -- hours after the January 6 attack on the Capitol.

### **CONSTITUENT BACKLASH: BOOED OFF STAGE, THEN CANCELED TOWN HALLS**

Over 200 constituents [booed Obernolte off stage](https://www.kvcrnews.org/local-news/2025-02-24/yucca-valley-town-hall-turns-raucous-as-rep-jay-obernolte-defends-doge) at a Yucca Valley town hall after he defended DOGE and Elon Musk. The crowd chanted "Do your job!" and "No kings!" A constituent told him, ["This is a fucking food desert, and that resource is a vital resource to this community."](https://www.deserttrumpet.org/p/town-holler-rep-obernolte-meets-resistance)
Obernolte then [canceled future in-person events](https://www.sbsun.com/2025/02/28/southern-california-gop-lawmaker-booed-at-town-hall-for-supporting-elon-musk/), citing "inclement weather," as GOP leaders urged lawmakers to stop holding town halls entirely.

**Message:** When 200 constituents confronted Jay Obernolte at a town hall, he didn't listen -- he canceled future events.

### **AI CONFLICTS OF INTEREST: SELLING ACCESS WHILE SHAPING POLICY**

Obernolte [launched a paid lobbying coalition](https://www.axios.com/2025/10/10/ai-startups-dc-advocate-obernolte) charging AI startups up to $3,500/month for access to Washington policymakers -- while leading the House's AI policy efforts.
He simultaneously [owns a tech company](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/newsletters/2024-08-09/how-jay-obernolte-a-video-game-developer-from-california-went-to-congress) with Sony, Microsoft, Google, and Apple as clients while chairing the House Science Committee's Research and Technology Subcommittee.

**Message:** Jay Obernolte is charging AI startups for access to the policymakers he leads -- while his own tech company counts Big Tech giants as clients.
