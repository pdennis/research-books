# Rep. Abe Hamadeh (AZ-08): Immigration Hypocrisy

### Hardline Immigration Stance Contradicted By Family History

- **Hamadeh's Father Overstayed His Visa By Approximately Seven Years While Hamadeh Campaigns On Hardline Border Security.** According to the Arizona Daily Star, Hamadeh's father "arrived in New York City on May 28, 1989, with permission to stay six months, till Nov. 27 that year, court documents show. Like millions of other foreign travelers, before and after him, he didn't leave." He remained undocumented for approximately seven years, filing a lawsuit in May 1996 demanding release from immigration detention. Hamadeh claimed "my parents proudly came to the United States LEGALLY in 1989," but the columnist noted this statement "obscures the messy reality" of his parents' prolonged undocumented status.
