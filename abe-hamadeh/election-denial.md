# Rep. Abe Hamadeh (AZ-08): Serial Election Denial and Court Sanctions

### Serial Election Denier Sanctioned By Arizona Supreme Court

**Hamadeh Filed Four Separate Lawsuits To Overturn His 280-Vote Loss In The 2022 Attorney General Race, Losing Nine Times Across Trial And Appellate Courts.** According to Democracy Docket, Hamadeh "filed four separate lawsuits attempting to overturn the election results — resulting in nine total court losses including appeals." His fourth attempt was dismissed as "untimely" because it exceeded the five-day filing window required by Arizona law. [Democracy Docket, [7/22/24](https://www.democracydocket.com/news-alerts/court-rejects-fourth-attempt-from-arizona-election-denier-to-overturn-2022-race/)]

**Hamadeh Was Sanctioned By The Arizona Supreme Court For Misrepresenting Facts In Court.** According to Democracy Docket, "in August 2023, Hamadeh along with fellow election-denying candidates Kari Lake and Mark Finchem were sanctioned by the Arizona Supreme Court for 'misrepresenting facts in court.'" [Democracy Docket, [7/22/24](https://www.democracydocket.com/news-alerts/court-rejects-fourth-attempt-from-arizona-election-denier-to-overturn-2022-race/)]

**Hamadeh Claimed 9,000 Ballots From The 2022 Race Were "Burned," A Claim A Former Secretary Of State Staffer Called "Delusional."** According to AZ Free News, Hamadeh stated: "No, they stole [the election]. Burned 9,000 uncounted ballots." Garrett Archer, a former Arizona Secretary of State employee and current ABC15 reporter, responded: "Because this take is delusional." [AZ Free News, [6/25](https://azfreenews.com/2025/06/former-sos-staffer-and-mainstream-media-reporter-disputes-claims-2022-ballots-were-destroyed/)]
