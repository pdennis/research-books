# Rep. Abe Hamadeh (AZ-08): Abortion Extremist

### Pledged To Uphold Near Total Abortion Ban In Arizona

**2022: Hamadeh Pledged To Enforce Arizona's Near-Total Abortion Ban As Attorney General.** According to Cronkite News, Hamadeh stated: "I currently agree with Gen. Brnovich's decision that the law is the law. I don't want to make the law. That's the job of the legislature. I enforce the law." [Cronkite News, [10/12/22](https://cronkitenews.azpbs.org/2022/10/12/arizona-attorney-general-abe-hamadeh-republican-running-against-leftist-mob/)]

- **2024: Hamadeh Later Dismissed The Same Ban As "A Complete Win For Democrats" And Said Its Effects Would Not Be "Actually Going To Be That Significant."** According to NBC News, when asked about the Arizona Supreme Court's reinstatement of the 1864 near-total abortion ban, Hamadeh's campaign called the ruling "a complete win for Democrats" and said the effects would not be "actually going to be that significant." Political strategists noted that both Hamadeh and Blake Masters "barely mentioned the abortion ban at all" during the primary, preferring to emphasize Trump loyalty and border security. [NBC News, [5/24](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/2024-election/arizona-abortion-ban-gop-congressional-primary-rcna150246)]
